\songtitle{Orinoco Crown}

\tag*{2022}\tag{symphonic-ska}\tag{original-source}\tag{african-empire}
\begin{notes}
Part 3 of a five-part historical epic. Lyrics by Suno based on Carlos Thompson's historical summary of the \emph{African Empire} alternate-history universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
symphonic ska ballad, slow march pulse, clear English lead vocal with chantable gang vocals and choir, bass guitar-led verses with kora and syncopated trumpet phrases, hand drums and west African percussion in stripped pre-choruses, strings opening the choruses with sax, trombone, rise cymbals, shekere fills, brief horn breaks, punchy reggae-ska backbeat, latin jazz and Andean jazz accents, wide cinematic mix with vivid orchestral depth
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
No great turn in Persia’s old road,\\
Central Asia kept its line.\\
Black posts woke in India’s markets,\\
Small at first, then gold did shine.\\
North fell under Mongol banners,\\
South held fast and learned to pray,\\
Old ways stayed inside the new ways,\\
Till both sides bowed to one sunray.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
From the forests to the coast,\\
From the river mouths to stone,\\
Little fires became a trade web,\\
Then a kingdom of their own.

\songpart{Chorus}
From the Orinoco\\
to the Andes gold,\\
From Panama\\
to the rivers cold.\\
Black hands built the road,\\
Black hands held the land,\\
From the Orinoco\\
to the Andes gold.

\songpart{Verse 2}
By eleven sixty, African sails\\
Found the south and set their posts.\\
Woods and labor, salt and river goods,\\
Trading from the coast to coast.\\
Then the mountains spilled their silver,\\
And the gold began to call,\\
Amazon and Paraná answered,\\
Drums were heard above them all.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Up the river, down the shore,\\
Over isthmus, into flame,\\
By twelve seventy, Panama was opened,\\
And the map would not stay tame.

\songpart{Chorus}
From the Orinoco\\
to the Andes gold,\\
From Panama\\
to the rivers cold.\\
Black hands built the road,\\
Black hands held the land,\\
From the Orinoco\\
to the Andes gold.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Peru was taken from the jungle,\\
Then from Panama they came,\\
And the Andes lost their nobles\\
To a new and harder name.\\
North of the line they ruled in colonies,\\
South they planted fields and fort,\\
Rio de la Plata, western Bolivia,\\
Uruguay and Paraguay's port.

\songpart{Bridge}
The Amazon stayed a road of water,\\
Shore by shore and reach by reach,\\
Mesoamerican drums kept beating\\
When the black banners touched the beach.\\
Then the French-Basques came for Cuba,\\
For the gold beneath the sky,\\
Trading posts along the coasts burned bright,\\
And every crown had eyes.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
From the Orinoco\\
to the Andes gold,\\
From Panama\\
to the rivers cold.\\
Black hands built the road,\\
Black hands held the land,\\
From the Orinoco\\
to the Andes gold.

\songpart{Outro}
In North America, faith kept moving,\\
Dane and Scot went west no more,\\
Basque flags faded back to islands,\\
Mission bells rang off the shore.
\end{lyrics}
