\songtitle{Condor Rising}

\tag*{2026}\tag{trance}\tag{reggaeton}\tag{dubstep}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{infrastructure}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:condor-rising}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok from the same free-verse seed by Carlos Thompson as \emph{The Condor Line} (page \pageref{sec:the-condor-line}), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the Thomas Holdings Andean railway brands: Condor high-speed on top, Toucans intercity, Macaws regional, and the cargo trains that keep rolling behind them. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Trans anthem with reggaeton accents in the bridges; rising synth layers, hard kicks, dubstep subbass; sidechain-pumped synths and roomy, unprocessed vocals; baritone male rap against crooning legato soprano melodies, soaring repetitive hooks; fast, rave-driven builds into euphoric drops
\end{style}
