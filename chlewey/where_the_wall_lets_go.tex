\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on \emph{Gr\ae ville} 2 (1992), a fragment by Carlos Thompson -- ghosts that cannot be trusted, the Platonic trap of light and shadow, and the wall that can only be crossed by knowing it. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Deep house, two intimate high, light-register voices trading verse lines and stacking harmonies on the hooks; warm 122 BPM four-on-the-floor pulse; rounded bass, glass bells, slowly shifting sidechain-pumped pads, wordless choir; beat strips back for spoken bridge, full groove returns, ending mid-conversation over fading bells.
\end{style}
