\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok from the same draft by Carlos Thompson as \emph{Too Smart for Your Own Good} (page \pageref{sec:too-smart-for-your-own-good}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 18 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dark dubstep, layered vocals with melodic soprano verses in crooning legato phrasing, whispered ad-libs between lines, bass baritone vocals in drops and bridge; heavy half-time groove; sidechain-pumped bass layers and a dry bridge build; heavy electronic synth stabs, sparse sub-bass lead and glitch clicks, ending on residual high noise and a distant alarm tone.
\end{style}
