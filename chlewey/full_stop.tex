\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on \emph{Gr\ae ville} 6 (1992), a fragment by Carlos Thompson -- an argument over "Why shouldn't I kill him?", physical laws against human ones, ending in a full stop. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Deep house with new age textures; sparse glass bells lead over warm sub bass, steady four-on-the-floor kick, and slow uneasy choir pads at 122 BPM. High, light male and female leads argue in close-miked overlapping verses, with stacked harmonies on a simple descending chorus melody. Strip the beat for the final exchange; cut all sound on the last word.
\end{style}
