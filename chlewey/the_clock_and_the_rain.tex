\songtitle{The Clock and the Rain}

\tag*{1992}\tag{deep-house}\tag{new-age}\tag{original-source}\tag{graeville}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on \emph{Gr\ae ville} 4 (1992), a fragment by Carlos Thompson -- Eneli and Idemó trapped in her childhood room, afraid for Ámila and for themselves, a love neither dares to act on, the rain against the window and the ticking clock. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Deep house with new age textures; dark plate reverb; soft four-on-the-floor kick, warm sub bass, muted glass bells, slow ambient pads, rain-on-window percussion and quiet clock ticks; intimate female and male leads in a high, light upper register with crooning legato phrasing, trading lines then overlapping in the chorus, with a spoken bridge over a dropped kick; 120 BPM, unhurried four-on-the-floor pulse, ending on rain, clock and breath.
\end{style}
