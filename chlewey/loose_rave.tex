\songtitle{Loose (rave)}

\tag*{2026}\tag{eurodance}\label{sec:loose-rave}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{Loose} (page \pageref{sec:loose}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, restructured as a rave anthem with added rap lines. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Eurodance, long white-noise risers over a filtered-pad breakdown and snare-roll builds; warm mid-range female vocals, hushed and close-mic in the verses, opening into melodic hooks; punchy offbeat rap verses, euphoric supersaw drops with arpeggios and octave countermelodies, four-on-the-floor kick, pumping sidechain bass; driving 140 BPM rave groove, whole-step final-key lift.
\end{style}
