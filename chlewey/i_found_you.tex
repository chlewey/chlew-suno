\songtitle{I Found You}

\tag*{2026}\tag{bardcore}\tag{medieval-ballad}\tag{original-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson. \emph{You Found Me} answers it. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 5 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Bardcore medieval ballad, male tenor vocals with clear, earnest phrasing; fingerpicked fiddle, laúd drones, flute counterlines, pulsing frame drum and sub-bass drone; steady 118 BPM, chantable chorus with stacked gang echoes; earthy acoustic textures and a warm, focused mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro — Instrumental, flutes and finger-picked fiddles}

\songpart{Verse 1}
I was hypnotized, they say, not in my mind,\\
when I followed the path that led them behind.\\
A path that brought me to the place you stood,\\
and all I could think is that that was all good.\\
I remember every step and every choice I made—\\
I was not lost, I was sure and far from afraid.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The path I chose, that led me to you (you)\\
I wasn't aware but somehow I knew (knew)

\songpart{Chorus}
I found you, I found you, my dear,\\
the truest thing I've ever done.\\
I found you, and everything was clear:\\
my real life had just begun.

\songpart{Verse 2}
They fear you, like they fear the unknown,\\
they say it's something they feel in their bone.\\
Of course they do, you're not everybody's cake—\\
they can't understand you are not a mistake.\\
You're too perfect for mere mortals, I realize;\\
they can't grasp the soul revealed in your eyes.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus }
The choice I made in taking you (you)\\

\songpart{Verse 3}
Some call you a demon, some call you a fae,\\
some call you a witch, something not of this day.\\
Afraid they are of your deep purple eyes,\\
your crown of antlers you bear as a prize,\\
The fangs that reveal themselves as you smile,\\
the dress of foliage and moss you wear in style.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The decision I made always for you (you)\\

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I found you, I found you, my dear,\\
the truest thing I've ever done.\\
I found you, and everything was clear:\\
the right person with whom I'll run\\
to wherever you choose, I got no fear—\\
my real life had just begun.
