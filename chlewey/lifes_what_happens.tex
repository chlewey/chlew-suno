\songtitle{Life's What Happens}

\tag*{2026}\tag{candy-pop}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{autobiographical}\label{sec:lifes-what-happens}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- an uplifting, comforting message drawn out of small vignettes of his own life: weather, errands, late nights, agents doing the job, the news cycle, and the cat in the meeting. Adapted by Claude as \emph{Life's What Occurs} (page \pageref{sec:lifes-what-occurs}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Candy pop, clean production with airy vocal layers; a light, bouncing midtempo groove; female vocals in a low, dark lower register, breathy and close in the verses, opening into doubled choruses; plucked keyboards and rounded 808 bass, with a pared-back outro.
\end{style}
