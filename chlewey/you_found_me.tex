\songtitle{You Found Me}

\tag*{2026}\tag{bardcore}\tag{medieval-ballad}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok, as a response to \emph{I Found You}. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Bardcore medieval ballad, steady 118 BPM pulse; clear, earnest female soprano with a chantable chorus and stacked gang echoes; earthy acoustic textures in a warm, focused mix; fingerpicked fiddle, laúd drones, featured flute counterlines, pulsing frame drum, and sub-bass drone.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro — Soft drones, distant strings, a single low flute}

\songpart{Verse 1}
You say the steps were yours, each one deliberate, clear,\\
that nothing clouded the moment you drew near.\\
You stood before me certain, unafraid, awake—\\
yet certainty can be the softest kind of mistake.\\
I watched you choose the turning, watched you claim the road;\\
I neither called nor cautioned. I simply let it unfold.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You found the place I stood (stood)\\
and called it good, and called it good

\songpart{Chorus}
You found me, you found me, you say,\\
the truest thing you’ve ever done.\\
You found me, and the old life fell away—\\
but every finding cuts two ways at once.

\songpart{Verse 2}
They name me demon, fae, or something worse by far,\\
they read the antlers, fangs, the eyes that hold the dark.\\
You wear their warnings lightly, brush them off like dust,\\
and call the danger beauty, call the risk a trust.\\
I never promised safety. I never promised kind.\\
I only stood where moonlight leaves the ordinary behind.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The bet you placed on me (me)\\
still sits unanswered, quietly

\songpart{Verse 3}
You speak of purple eyes, the crown, the moss-green dress,\\
the smile that shows the edge of something feral, less\\
or more than human. You call it perfect, call it true.\\
I wear the shape the forest gave; I did not make it new.\\
Whether I am the answer or the question still unasked\\
is not a thing I settled when you chose to take the path.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You say you’ll always choose (choose)\\
the same again—yet choice can bruise

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You found me, you found me, my dear,\\
the truest thing you’ve ever done.\\
You found me, and the way ahead looks clear—\\
but clarity and freedom are not always one.\\
Go if the weight grows heavier than the thrill;\\
I never locked the gate. I only held still.
\end{lyrics}
