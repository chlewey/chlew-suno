\songtitle{Dreaming Is Cheap}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dream-pop}\tag{glam-metal}\tag{original-lyrics}\label{sec:dreaming-is-cheap}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- dreaming of an integrated multimodal transit system: bus, regional train, metro and tram on one ticket, a few high-speed hubs, a cable car into the mountains and BRT where rails can't reach. Tightened by Grok as \emph{Urban Signal} (page \pageref{sec:urban-signal}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 4 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
dream pop, glam metal, 132 BPM, mezzo-soprano vocals, electric guitar power chords, chiming guitar arpeggios, synth bass drone, analog polysynth pads, string ensemble swells, tom-heavy fills, gated reverb snare, tape saturation, plate reverb, stereo delay throws, four-on-the-floor kick, half-time prechorus, bittersweet longing, nocturnal uplift
\end{style}

System in, system out, smooth like a drop.
