\songtitle{Battle for the Throne}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dubstep}\tag{cats}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a specific prompt by Carlos Thompson: two of his cats competing for space on his lap.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
industrial electronic anthem, aggressive dubstep drops, distorted mid-bass growls, pounding four-on-the-floor intro, metallic percussion, siren synths, tense risers, half-time drop sections, cinematic impact hits, texture-rich male vocals with gritty layered chants, alternating vocalists for comic rivalry, polished loud modern mix with controlled sub bass
