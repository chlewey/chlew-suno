\songtitle{Miguel, Corazón Sereno}

\tag*{2024}\tag{ska}\tag{ricochets}\tag{miguel-roa}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
From Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}. Character summary of Miguel Roa by Claude (2026) from lore established by Carlos Thompson (2024). Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Upbeat Colombian ska fused with light reggaeton and Latin pop: brisk offbeat guitar, warm electric bass, punchy brass stabs, accordion flourishes, alegre percussion, and a playful call-and-response chorus, Male Spanish vocals with clear conversational phrasing, affectionate family energy, and a slightly mischievous delivery, Arrange with a compact verse, rising pre-chorus, big horn-led chorus, a short instrumental break, and a final celebratory chorus, Clean, lively mix with tight drums and bright brass
\end{style}
