\songtitle{Go}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{victoria-ospina}\tag{fragment}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Character profile of Victoria Ospina by Carlos Thompson and Claude (2026). Fragment. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Children’s pop-rock with candy-pop texture: punchy live drums, handclaps, bright crunchy electric-guitar chords, bouncy bass, toy piano accents, glockenspiel sprinkles, and clean energetic lead vocals with playful group responses, Start with a catchy guitar-and-clap hook, keep verses nimble and conversational, lift into a huge singalong chorus, add a brief quieter bridge about helping people recover, then finish with stacked harmonies and a joyful final refrain, Polished, colorful, upbeat mix with crisp transients and warm low end; motivating rather than frantic
\end{style}
