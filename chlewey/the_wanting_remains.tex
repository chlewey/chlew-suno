\songtitle{The Wanting Remains}

\tag*{2026}\tag{trance}\tag{dubstep}\tag{partial-lyrics}\tag{longing-is-safe}\label{sec:the-wanting-remains}
\begin{notes}
Partial authorship: the three builds by Carlos Thompson, unchanged from \emph{Longing Is Safe} (page \pageref{sec:longing-is-safe}); intro, drops and bridges by Grok, extending it -- the names never said out loud, and the wanting kept quiet so the rest of a life could go on. \emph{Those Kinds of Crushes} (page \pageref{sec:those-kinds-of-crushes}) adapts the same free verse. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 2 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Trance, sub-bass drone beneath sparse piano and pads; wide reverb, gentle sidechain, dry up-front lead vocals with stacked harmonies on the hooks; 85–95 BPM, gradual builds into rising dubstep drops; soft intimate female or androgynous bass-baritone vocals, almost spoken in the builds, melodic in the bridges, with long vowel hooks and soft choir-like stacks.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro — soft female melodic}
I still carry the names\\
I never said out loud.\\
Some loves stay in the body\\
long after the world stops looking.

\songpart{Build 1 — bass baritone, rap/almost spoken}
Those kinds of crushes\\
that you bring through decades.\\
You liked them as a teen;\\
now you like them still\\
as we both approach\\
our mutual middle age.

\songpart{Drop 1 — soft intimate female}
Still here.\\
Still mine.\\
They never asked\\
and I never told.
(long open vowels and soft hmmm)

\songpart{Bridge 1 — soft female melodic}
There are maps I never drew.\\
There are doors I left closed\\
not out of fear,\\
but out of care\\
for the life I already built.

\songpart{Build 2 — bass baritone, rap/almost spoken}
The transgression\\
that won’t happen.\\
The comfort zone\\
I won’t leave.\\
Longing is safe,\\
no energy wasted.\\
Longing is safe—\\
no attempt, no fail.

\songpart{Drop 2 — soft intimate female}
I kept the wanting quiet\\
so the rest of me could live.\\
That was not surrender.\\
That was strategy.
(long open vowels and layered hmmm)

\songpart{Bridge 2 — soft female melodic}
Some of us learn early\\
how to hold two truths at once:\\
the one the world can see\\
and the one that never left the room.

\songpart{Build 3 — bass baritone, rap/almost spoken}
Those kinds of crushes\\
that you bring through decades.\\
Age ain’t invisible,\\
but the core’s still there.\\
But they won’t know\\
that they ever were,\\
that they still are.

\annotation{Final Drop — soft intimate female}\\
They won’t know.\\
We still are.\\
The core remains.\\
The wanting remains.\\
We remain.
(long sustained vowels, soft choir stacks, fading)
\end{lyrics}
