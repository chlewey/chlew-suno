\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the profile of Erika Czajka in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Vispop with nyckelharpa and fiol, mid-tempo with a bouncing Nordic groove; verse rides sparse plucked strings and soft hand percussion, pre-chorus lifts with rising violin swells, chorus lands on layered female vocals and a chantable hook, Bridge strips to nyckelharpa, low drones, and intimate lead with stacked harmonies blooming back in, Bright, clear, and slightly cinematic mix with crisp strings and warm vocal air, punta, nostalgic, environmental, low, dynamics
\end{style}
