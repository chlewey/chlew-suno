\songtitle{Noviembre}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{punk}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{spanish-lyrics}\label{sec:noviembre}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- a cold November night in the city: a twenty-block walk home after a party, a stop moved by roadworks, a bus that won't stop, and everything changing, from day to night, from sun to rain. Adapted by Claude into English as \emph{It Changes} (page \pageref{sec:it-changes}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
% TODO: year of the original lyrics unverified (tagged 2026)
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop-rock with a driving, brisk pulse; wide gang-chorus overdubs; Colombian male tenor with punk-edged theatrical delivery, clipped rhythmic staccato phrasing, roomy and unprocessed; synth-led arrangement with punchy electric guitars and tight drums.
\end{style}
