\songtitle{Ugenito: A Traveler’s Götterdämmerung}

\tag*{2015}\tag{orchestral}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{air-jaguar}\tag{aircraft}

\begin{notes}
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
mock heroic orchestral, blues and punk flavor, wagnerian brass, kora, patter song, deadpan spoken vocals, dramatic tenor, male baritone vocals, spoken vocals, clear spanish interjections, rapid-fire clarinet runs, out-of-tune trumpets, tuba walking bass, snare drum march, bandoneón, bouncy orchestra, plate reverb, theatrical dynamics, 118 BPM, frantic 2-4, triumphant exhaustion
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
\annotation{Slow Wagnerian brass, low strings, single bandoneón note held}

\annotation{Spoken, deep and solemn}\\
Behold the man. %
The plan. %
The flight.\\
Ugenito’s saga. %
Born of night.

\annotation{Brass swell}

\annotation{Sung, operatic}\\
An invitation… far away…\\
To help coordinate… Chiang Mai.

\annotation{Cymbal crash—pause—tuba sigh}

\songpart{Verse 1}
\annotation{Tango lento, bandoneón solo, piano stabs}

\annotation{Sung, dramatic}\\
The budget was a tiny sum.\\
Google Flights returned… a great big thumb.\\
Poor Ugenito, the bandoneón cries—\\
No combination under the skies.

\annotation{Spoken, sarcastic}\\
¡Yupi por Ugenito!

\annotation{Cumbia rhythm enters—accordion and güiro}

\annotation{Sung, bouncy}\\
But wait! A Disney trip appears!\\
The family cheers! Ugenito cheers!\\
“If we’re already paying for Orlando bound—\\
Maybe my ticket can be… refound!”

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Full cumbia psicodélica, theremin wail, tuba walking bass}

\annotation{Sung, joyful and manic}\\
¡Yupi por Ugenito! ¡Yupi por él!\\
A genius with a budget hotel!\\
Bogotá to Miami—Houston—Beijing—\\
Six connections, no sleep, and a credit card scream.

\annotation{Whispered}\\
Poor Ugenito…

\annotation{Shouted}\\
¡YUPI POR UGENITO!

\songpart{Verse 2}
\annotation{Tango stop-time—bandoneón alone, dramatic rests}

\annotation{Spoken-sung, new tango style}\\
May fifteenth. %
The deadline’s here.\\
The passport’s with the gringos… oh dear.\\
“Almost sure,” they said. %
But “almost” isn’t “yes.”

\annotation{Silence—one piano note}

\annotation{Sung, tense}\\
Ugenito checks Google Flights again… and feels the stress.

\annotation{Violin screech—bandoneón glissando down}

\annotation{Spoken}\\
The super-economy fares? Gone. %
Disappeared.\\
He buys anyway. %
Because he’s brave. %
Or weird.

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Slow tango, sarcastic brass}

\annotation{Sung, ironic}\\
Poor Ugenito, the bandoneón sighs—\\
He thinks he’s Odysseus, but he’s just bad with airlines.\\
Two visas, one wallet, a single carry-on,\\
And the whole thing falls apart before the dawn.

\annotation{Spoken, deadpan}\\
¡Pobre Ugenito!

\songpart{Verse 3}
\annotation{Patter allegro—tuba oompah, snare drum, rapid narration}

\annotation{Rapid-fire, spoken-sung}\\
July first he boards he’s fine a child steals his window seat—\\
Aisle is fine he tells himself the air is bittersweet.\\
Fog delay in Bogotá the plane is in a queue—\\
He lands in Miami late and immigration takes a…\\
century.

\annotation{Trumpet wah-wah}

He grabs his bag he runs to check-in “HOUSTON’S CLOSED” they say—\\
“My connection’s in an hour!” “Too bad, go away.”\\
He drops his papers crawls to ticketing in physical pain—\\
The flight has left. %
No Houston. %
Here comes the hurricane.

\annotation{Tuba fart—kazoo squeak}

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Exhausted tango—slow bandoneón, tired brass}

\annotation{Sung, defeated}\\
American gives a hotel and a morning flight—\\
But Air China wants three thousand dollars. %
Overnight.\\
Ugenito stares at nothing. %
Loses faith in time and space.\\
Sleeps in a Holiday Inn near a Houston suburb’s… face.

\annotation{Spoken, barely audible}\\
Pobre. Pobre. Pobre.

\songpart{Bridge}
\annotation{Bandoneón rises—strings tremolo—hopeful piano}

\annotation{Sung, soft, awakening}\\
But Seoul… Seoul doesn’t need a visa.\\
Korean Air appears—a visa-free diva.\\
He calls the consulate in Los Angeles to check—\\
“Yes, Ugenito, fly without a wreck.”

\annotation{Spoken, building excitement}\\
He books it. %
Houston—Seoul—Bangkok—Chiang Mai.\\
The family shifts the flights. %
The credit card… sighs.

\annotation{Single cymbal tap—tuba note held}

\annotation{Sung, wry}\\
Eighteen dollars lost on a train ticket he won’t use.\\
That’s two empanadas and a Coke. %
He counts the abuse.

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Smiling tango—bandoneón and piano, lighter}

\annotation{Sung, resilient}\\
Poor Ugenito, the bandoneón now winks—\\
He thought he’d lost it all but he’s still in the rinks.\\
From Bogotá to Houston to Seoul to Bangkok’s heat—\\
He’s a tired, broke, magnificent feat.

\annotation{Spoken}\\
¡Yupi… por Ugenito!

\songpart{Verse 4}
\annotation{Balkan brass enters—out-of-tune trumpets, tuba, snare—fast and ridiculous}

\annotation{Shouted, joyful chaos}\\
He lands in Bangkok! He made it! HURRAH!\\
But health control says—“YELLOW FEVER CARD?”—Oh là là.\\
The certificate is in Bogotá on the nightstand by the bed—\\
He shows a photocopy. %
They nod their heads.

\annotation{Trumpet flourish}

Chiang Mai! The hotel! The AC on full blast—\\
He wakes up with a cold. %
The worst has come to pass.\\
“Where have you been?” “Korea.” MERS flashes in their eyes—\\
Quarantine! A swab! Six hours of lies.

\annotation{Tuba slide down—pause}

The test comes back: common cold. %
Go back to bed.\\
Ugenito finally… FINALLY… walks in.\\
Five days late.\\
Standing.\\
After all he said.

\songpart{Outro}
\annotation{Wagnerian theme returns—played on kazoo and tuba—bandoneón sighs in background}

\annotation{Spoken, exhausted but laughing}\\
He coordinated the Olympiad. %
He did his job.\\
He didn’t cry. %
He didn’t sob.\\
He lost some money. %
Lost some sleep.\\
Lost his vaccination certificate in a very messy heap.

\annotation{Silence—one kazoo note held alone}

\annotation{Whispered, then shouted with full ensemble}\\
¡Yupi por Ugenito! He made it through!\\
The flights! The visas! The quarantine! The queues!\\
¡Yupi por Ugenito! Raise a glass—\\
He’s the hero of the airport… economy class.

\annotation{Final brass chord—tuba fart—kazoo trill—blackout}

\annotation{End}
\end{lyrics}
