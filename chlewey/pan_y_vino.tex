\tag*{2026}\tag{new-age}\tag{pasillo}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT on instructions by Carlos Thompson: an acrostic sonnet whose initials spell THOMPSON PINZÓN -- the small stumbles of a day losing their bitterness, the bread the brothers keep, and a toast to every day with its laughter. \emph{Canto en la memoria} is its companion. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Symphonic new age, gritty, strained lyric tenor vocals in Spanish, half-spoken through the verses; mid-tempo, with a high energy intro falling into soft 6/8 pasillo cadence; bright multi-tracked piano and swelling strings, piano-led verses opening into an uplifting instrumental outro.
\end{style}
