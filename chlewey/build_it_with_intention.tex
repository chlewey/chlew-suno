\songtitle{Build It with Intention}

\tag*{2026}\tag{heavy-metal}\tag{hip-hop}\label{sec:build-it-with-intention}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a ChatGPT summary of Carlos Thompson's latest work -- building with intention: scoped software, separate worlds kept distinct (\emph{Thomas' Entourage}, Ciudad Índigo, \emph{Ricochets}, songwriting, pictures, timelines), and continuity as care for the first idea. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic heavy metal hip-hop, 92 BPM half-time groove, tightly syncopated low-tuned guitar riffs with orchestral strings and brass, deep punchy kick and sub-bass, crisp trap-influenced hats, tense piano motif. Verses stay controlled and articulate with melodic rap phrasing; choruses widen into sung, anthemic harmonies without screamed vocals. Build through dramatic orchestral lifts, sharp drops, and a final expanded chorus; polished, weighty mix with clear vocal focus.
\end{style}
