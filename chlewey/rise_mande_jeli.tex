\songtitle{Rise, Mande Jeli}

\tag*{2022}\tag{griot-ballad}\tag{original-source}\tag{african-empire}
\begin{notes}
Part 1 of a five-part historical epic. Lyrics by Suno based on Carlos Thompson's historical summary of the \emph{African Empire} alternate-history universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Epic West African griot ensemble, traditional kora arpeggios, ngoni ostinatos, resonant balafon patterns, layered djembe and calabash percussion, broad gang choir answering the lead in the chorus, commanding male baritone lead, ceremonial call-and-response, gradual historical sweep from intimate prophecy to vast royal procession, organic room ambience, earthy low mids, crisp hand percussion, dramatic pauses before each chorus, final chorus expanding with dense communal harmonies and triumphant cadence
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
Hear the dust remember\\
Hear the old road turn\\
A seed beneath the baobab\\
A lamp the night will learn

\songpart{Verse 1}
A stranger crossed the red earth\\
With a book beneath his arm\\
He spoke beside the river bend\\
Of mercy stronger than harm\\
The king watched the morning gather\\
On the bronze of shield and spear\\
Then opened wide the palace gate\\
And called the distant word near

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
From one small flame in the court\\
A hundred lamps were raised\\
Hands that once guarded the border\\
Built houses where prayers were praised

\songpart{Chorus}
Rise, Mande jeli, tell the turning\\
Sing the names the years preserve\\
Kora bright and ngoni burning\\
Beat the road beneath our nerve\\
Many tongues beneath one banner\\
Many paths beneath one sun\\
When the drum calls every village\\
We remember what we’ve done

\songpart{Verse 2}
Stone walls climbed the highland ridges\\
Monks marched beside the throne\\
Farmers brought their grain at harvest\\
Smiths made iron bright as bone\\
When the desert banners gathered\\
Our riders met them west\\
Not for one altar only\\
But for every neighbor’s rest

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The river carried iron\\
The harbor learned the tide\\
Black sails crossed the western water\\
With the old world at their side

\songpart{Chorus}
Rise, Mande jeli, tell the turning\\
Sing the names the years preserve\\
Kora bright and ngoni burning\\
Beat the road beneath our nerve\\
Many tongues beneath one banner\\
Many paths beneath one sun\\
When the drum calls every village\\
We remember what we’ve done

\songpart{Verse 3}
Then came teachers from the desert\\
And the crescent found a home\\
Beside the cross and village altar\\
No one road stood alone\\
The council shared the water\\
The market shared the law\\
A child could learn three blessings\\
And still know the drumbeat’s call

\songpart{Bridge}
Westward went the shipwrights\\
Round the southern sea\\
Beyond the islands’ blue-green chain\\
New shores rose free\\
Gold was weighed in quiet rooms\\
Maps were drawn by fire\\
Every coast became a question\\
Every mast climbed higher

\songpart{Verse 4}
Across the ocean, red soil waited\\
Under unfamiliar rain\\
Across the eastern archipelago\\
Our songs returned again\\
In northern courts, dark horsemen\\
Guarded walls and sacred gates\\
Merchants wore the royal colors\\
Envoys opened iron states

\songpart{Chorus}
Rise, Mande jeli, tell the turning\\
Sing the names the years preserve\\
Kora bright and ngoni burning\\
Beat the road beneath our nerve\\
Many tongues beneath one banner\\
Many paths beneath one sun\\
When the drum calls every village\\
We remember what we’ve done

\songpart{Verse 5}
But a crown can split like dry wood\\
When the hands beneath it fight\\
Local lords reclaimed the valleys\\
Old alliances lost their light\\
Still the smith remembers fire\\
Still the mother names the line\\
Still the griot keeps the ledger\\
Still the broken beads can shine

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Rise, Mande jeli, keep the turning\\
Sing the names the years preserve\\
Kora bright and ngoni burning\\
Let the whole wide earth respond\\
Many tongues beneath one banner\\
Many paths beneath one sun\\
Though the kingdom breaks to rivers\\
What we carried isn’t gone

\songpart{Outro}
A child strikes one calabash\\
Another answers from afar\\
The road is long, the tale is living\\
We are more than where we are
\end{lyrics}
