\songtitle{Wrong Enough}

\tag*{2026}\tag{electronic-country}\tag{ricochets}\tag{john-carlson}\tag{armando-perez}\label{sec:wrong-enough}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene in chapter 19 of \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson: Carlson and Perez and the DEA jet dressed in Army colors. \emph{Keep Your Eyes Open} (page \pageref{sec:keep-your-eyes-open}) sets the same scene. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Electronic country, mid-tempo 4/4 with a clipped train-beat kick and snare, rubbery sub-bass under bright banjo-like plucks and steel-guitar bends. Start spare and observant; add tight electronic percussion and a pulsing synth bass through the verses, then open into a broad, hooky chorus with layered harmonies. Low, weathered male lead, conversational phrasing that turns wry and uneasy; crisp modern mix, dry close vocal, spacious guitar tails.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Challenger on the runway, Army green and brown\\
Canadian-built wings with the wrong coat painted on\\
Lines don't fit the fuselage, the badges sit askew\\
Fresh paint catching morning light where old wear ought to show through

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
From the road it passes, official as a seal\\
But look a little closer and you feel what isn't real

\songpart{Chorus}
This is wrong on so many levels\\
Wrong paint, wrong place, wrong kind of crew\\
Dark jackets, DEA across the chest\\
No one pretending to be what they ain't\\
Perez waits inside, says, “Take it easy”\\
A faint smile, like he knows the joke\\
Close enough to fool the passing eye\\
Wrong enough to make the careful look twice
