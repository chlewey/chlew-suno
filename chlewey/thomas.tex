\songtitle{Thomas}

\tag*{2026}\tag{rap-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a specific prompt by Carlos Thompson, regarding Thomas Garcia's impostor syndrome behind the billionaire image.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Hip-hop rock, English lyrics, male baritone lead, restrained confessional verses over hard live drums, distorted electric guitar riffs, punchy bass, tight head-nod groove, occasional half-time drops, dynamic build from intimate rap-sung delivery to a huge gang choir chorus repeating the name Thomas, polished but gritty modern mix, clear vocal diction, vulnerable first-person performance
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I sign my name where the skyline starts,\\
Buy the whole block, still misplace my heart.\\
Three companies humming, a billion on the page,\\
But I freeze at a doorway, rehearsing what to say.\\
They call me a builder, a king with a plan,\\
I know every exit, not the touch of a hand.\\
A room full of laughter, I study the cues,\\
Smile half a second late, then disappear in the news.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I can move a market, I can move a crowd,\\
But a quiet hello comes out too loud.\\
Every win wears a mask I can’t remove,\\
They see the proof; I feel the fool.

\songpart{Chorus}
Thomas! Thomas!\\
Tell me I belong here.\\
Thomas! Thomas!\\
Make the distance disappear.\\
Thomas! Thomas!\\
All the gold can’t hold us close.\\
Thomas! Thomas!\\
I’m the man they praise the most.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I’ve got summer houses, numbers with commas,\\
A different beautiful stranger in every new drama.\\
They say I’m fearless, say I collect the night,\\
I leave before the morning so nobody looks too close at the light.\\
I learned how to navigate people by sound,\\
Map the little changes when a mood turns around.\\
If the room tilts left, I can steady the frame,\\
Then spend the ride home wondering why I came.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I can close a deal with a room full of knives,\\
But I lose my words when somebody asks about my life.\\
Every trophy whispers, “You slipped through the door,”\\
So I build one more floor, then I build one more.

\songpart{Bridge}
No, I’m not broken, I’m calibrated strange,\\
I read the weather in a stranger’s face.\\
I need a minute when the whole world shines,\\
I need plain words and a little more time.\\
If success is a costume, it’s cutting my skin,\\
If I’m an impostor, let the honest man in.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Thomas! Thomas!\\
I don’t have to prove it.\\
Thomas! Thomas!\\
Let the room see through it.\\
Thomas! Thomas!\\
Billionaire, builder, ghost—\\
Thomas! Thomas!\\
I’m still learning to come close.

\songpart{Outro}
I sign my name, then I breathe.\\
For once, the man they see is me.
