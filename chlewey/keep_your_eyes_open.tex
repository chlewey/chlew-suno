\songtitle{Keep Your Eyes Open}

\tag*{2026}\tag{electronic-country}\tag{ricochets}\tag{john-carlson}\tag{armando-perez}\label{sec:keep-your-eyes-open}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene in chapter 19 of \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Carlson: Perez's DEA jet dressed in Army colors. \emph{Wrong Enough} (page \pageref{sec:wrong-enough}) sets the same scene. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
electronic country, tense and unsettling, sung vocals; a clipped, watchful lead voice with dry twang and a restrained edge, set against a sparse electronic pulse and country guitar phrases
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Keep your eyes open, Carlson, count the seams.\\
I tell myself: don’t let the paint decide.\\
A Challenger wears Army colors on its sides,\\
Canadian-built, civilian bones beneath.\\
The proportions miss the fuselage by inches,\\
Too fresh in spots, insignia placed just off.\\
Far off, it passes for official.\\
Up close, I know what’s wrong.

\songpart{Chorus}
What’s wrong? This is wrong on so many levels.\\
Who says? Perez, with a faint smile: relax.\\
I hear myself ask; I hear myself answer—\\
Just relax. I don’t.

\songpart{Verse 2}
The crew make no military claim at all;\\
plain dark jackets, DEA across the chest.\\
That honesty makes the whole thing stranger:\\
a false skin, honest staff, one double mismatch.\\
Perez is waiting inside, already seated.\\
He says they seized it from a Mexican narco two years back.\\
He always wondered what the most dramatic use could be.\\
Now it carries a witness. I count each seam again.

\songpart{Outro}
Keep your eyes open, Carlson. Don’t look away.
\end{lyrics}
