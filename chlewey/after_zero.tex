\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on \emph{Gr\ae ville} 3 (1992), a fragment by Carlos Thompson -- Ámila, Eneli and Idemó face to face with the All and the Nothing, the one and the zero, and what comes after being. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 November 2026).

\begin{style}
Deep house with new age textures, close-miked production; warm four-on-the-floor kick, rounded sub bass, glass bells, slowly shifting pads and piano; 122 BPM, restrained four-on-the-floor pulse; three high, light voices in an upper register, crooning legato phrasing, entering in turn and stacking on the chorus, echoed counting in the drop, dry spoken dialogue over a beatless piano breakdown, restrained full-groove return.
\end{style}
