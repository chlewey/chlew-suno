\songtitle{Alone or Only Alone}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-ballad}\tag{soledad-y-compania}\label{sec:alone-or-only-alone}
\begin{notes}
English translation by Grok of \emph{Soledad y Compañía} (page \pageref{sec:soledad-y-compania}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Mellow piano-led pop ballad, intimate warm male baritone with clear natural phrasing, restrained verses over felt piano and soft ambient strings, subtle low percussion enters in the first chorus, fuller drums and layered harmonies lift the final chorus, dynamic but understated arrangement, close vocal, spacious polished mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Solitude\\
A song for a life lived free\\
Without watching who they want me to be\\
Or the version they pretend to see\\
Solitude\\
A cry into the empty fire\\
No one to break the chain that pulls me higher\\
Into every heavy theme, every darker desire
\songpart{Bridge}
Solitude\\
So loved and so hated, solitude
\songpart{Chorus}
Alone, or only alone in company\\
The loneliness that arrives so empty\\
Company like a weight I carry\\
Any feeling turns bitter and heavy
\songpart{Verse 2}
Company\\
The longing to share a story worth keeping\\
A beautiful moment that leaves us both smiling\\
And makes the heart remember why it’s still beating\\
Company\\
The daily drain on the mind\\
Walking that shadowed road, always looking behind\\
Afraid of the thing that could leave me confined
\songpart{Bridge}
Company\\
So loved and so hated, company
\songpart{Chorus}
Alone, or only alone in company\\
Loneliness becomes the only guide I see\\
Company like a shelter I run to\\
One or the other leads me back to joy, it’s true
\songpart{Breakdown}
The solitude that sets me free\\
And kills me slowly, silently\\
The company that covers me\\
And wears me down so easily
\songpart{Final Chorus}
Alone, or only alone in company\\
The loneliness that arrives so empty\\
The loneliness that becomes my guide\\
Company like a weight I carry\\
Or just another bitter feeling\\
Company like a shelter I run to\\
One or the other leads me back to joy
\end{lyrics}
