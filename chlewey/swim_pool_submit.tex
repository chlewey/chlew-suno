\songtitle{Swim Pool Submit}

\tag*{2026}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{ricochets}\tag{nestor-mojica}\tag{miguel-morocho}
\begin{notes}
Co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude, based on a scene from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.

Verses and Counter-Verses by Claude, edited by Thompson. Chorus by Thompson.
\end{notes}
