\songtitle{Urban Signal}

\tag*{2026}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{dreaming-is-cheap}\label{sec:urban-signal}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Grok from \emph{Dreaming Is Cheap} (page \pageref{sec:dreaming-is-cheap}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, with the meter tightened. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 4 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber hip-hop, gritty baritone lead with strained crooning legato phrasing and a gently held chorus refrain; sparse kick snare, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, muted upright bass, brushed toms, tremolo piano and string quartet accents; close-mic dry vocal with restrained dynamics, tape saturation, plate reverb and stereo delay throws; measured 88 BPM groove and wistful resolve.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
A bus stop stands two blocks from home,\\
Three minutes more to catch the train;\\
A few stops back from where I roam,\\
The rapid ride that brings no pain.

Downtown arrives in minutes eight,\\
A short walk or one metro ride;\\
No separate tickets at the gate—\\
The system flows on every side.

\songpart{Chorus}
Just dreaming costs us nothing dear;\\
To share a dream can strike the spark\\
Of some new urban signal clear\\
That lets us leave a lasting mark.

\songpart{Verse 2}
The bus, the metro, and the tram,\\
The regional lines that serve the fringe:\\
No need to join the traffic jam\\
Or suffer transit’s crushing hinge.

High speed is not for daily run,\\
Yet cities need a handful hubs\\
So local lines can share the ton\\
And lighten all the heavy subs.

\songpart{Chorus}
Just dreaming costs us nothing dear;\\
To share a dream can strike the spark\\
Of some new urban signal clear\\
That lets us leave a lasting mark.

\songpart{Bridge}
A cable climbs the mountain face,\\
BRT goes where no rail can reach;\\
Wide promenades and public space\\
Replace the beach with green and speech.

\songpart{Outro}
To share a dream can strike the spark\\
Of some new urban signal clear.\\
Keep dreaming—dreaming lights the dark;\\
Keep dreaming, for the key is here.
\end{lyrics}
