\songtitle{It Changes}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soul}\tag{noviembre}\label{sec:it-changes}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude into English from \emph{Noviembre} (page \pageref{sec:noviembre}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Slow-burn urban soul, smoky baritone with clipped, rhythmic staccato phrasing and light falsetto at the end of choruses; sparse backing vocals echo the hook. Warm Rhodes chords, round electric bass, brushed hi-hats and muted horn pads; tape saturation and a dry, up-front lead. At 72 BPM, the spoken bridge pulls back before the final chorus lifts.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
A cold night in November,\\
a dark street past the door,\\
a party that wasn't forever,\\
the drizzle you've known before.

Twenty blocks, no hurry, you walk slow\\
through a nightlife you don't know,\\
crowding on certain corners,\\
another life, and different tones.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And you keep walking, past blocks that feel hostile,\\
past corners you can't read,\\
down streets you've known for years\\
but with doors you've never seen.

\songpart{Chorus}
And it changes (changes),\\
from day to night, it changes,\\
from sun to rain, it changes,\\
your life and your state, they change too.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A cold night in November,\\
the office work won't end,\\
the street still full of people,\\
the bus won't stop, won't understand.

The stop improvised for roadworks,\\
today one block, yesterday the other side,\\
your eyes following the maneuvers,\\
the taxi that won't change its sign.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And you wait for the bus that takes you home,\\
for the taxi that won't say,\\
for a change in your life\\
that isn't a new life, anyway.

\songpart{Chorus}
And it changes (changes),\\
from day to night, it changes,\\
from stop to stop, it changes,\\
your life and your state, they change too.

\songpart{Bridge, spoken, drums drop out}
You change your house.\\
You change your job.\\
The friends you go out with,\\
suddenly, in one cut.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
And it changes (changes),\\
from day to night, it changes,\\
from stop to stop, it changes,\\
from sun to rain, it changes,\\
your life and your state,\\
that changes too.
\end{lyrics}
