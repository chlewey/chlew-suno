\songtitle{Too Smart for Your Own Good}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-folk}\label{sec:too-smart-for-your-own-good}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude from a draft by Carlos Thompson -- the organizer by the bed, notes to the future self, and the habit of making a habit that slips away: every solution comes with its excuse. \emph{Too Smart for Your Own Good} (page \pageref{sec:too-smart-for-your-own-good-dubstep}) sets Grok's lyrics from the same draft. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 18 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie folk, fingerpicked acoustic guitar with a pad widening the pre-choruses, soft bass and brushed drums entering at the first chorus; nearly bare verses, then a voice-and-guitar bridge and a final chorus that fades out; steady 92 BPM pulse; male baritone vocals, nasal, reedy, hushed and close-mic.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You keep an organizer by the bed,\\
Write notes to your future self,\\
Set the alarms, repeat what they said,\\
Till the habit's on the shelf.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But the habit of making a habit\\
Is a habit that slips away,\\
And the plan to fix the plan\\
Is a plan that fades by day.

\songpart{Chorus}
You're too smart for your own good,\\
For every solution, an excuse.\\
You study your own cheat codes\\
And you break the rules you chose.

\songpart{Verse 2}
They say hack yourself, gamify,\\
You thrive on what is new,\\
But once the task becomes a duty,\\
The thrill slips out of view.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
So make the duty novel, call it bliss,\\
It lasts a week or two,\\
Then you spot the trick behind the trick\\
And the same old wall comes through.

\songpart{Chorus}
You're too smart for your own good,\\
For every solution, an excuse.\\
You study your own cheat codes\\
And you break the rules you chose.

\songpart{Bridge}
Tell somebody, let them hold you to it,\\
Let their watching pull you through.\\
It matters some, but barely moves you,\\
And now there’s one more thing to do.\\
So don’t tell a soul, don’t tell a soul,\\
Fail in silence, not out loud.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You’re too smart for your own good,\\
Too aware to trick yourself.\\
Their disappointment’s real enough,\\
It’s just too faint to help.
\end{lyrics}
