\songtitle{Paths of Grief}

\tag*{2026}\tag{medieval-epic}\tag{partial-lyrics}\tag{african-empire}\tag{monukae}\tag{gladiel}\tag{abulon}\tag{mombuto}\label{sec:paths-of-grief}
\begin{notes}
Partial authorship: intro, bridges and outro by Carlos Thompson; verses by Claude, adapting Carlos Thompson's free verse into iambic pentameter -- from the \emph{Monukae} saga, narrated by Gladiel: no tears for her own kin, but grief for Abulon, her captor and mentor, the father of Mombuto, the man she chose to wed. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 10 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Medieval epic with kora ostinato, ngoni plucks, goje drones, patterned balafon, calabash percussion and djembe hand drum beneath flute counterlines and orchestral strings; wide choir-pad layers; female soprano, clear and plaintive with sustained phrases; measured, processional pulse building to dark majesty.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
I have walked the mysterious paths of grief\\
in ways the girl I was wouldn't understand.

\songpart{Verse 1}
When father died, I found I could not grieve:\\
He and my brother were my last true tie,\\
And then my brother cut it, took his leave.\\
No tear for Mac Ghilt, none for days gone by.

I resent the man whose greed set the stage,\\
And scorn the men who slaughtered all I knew.\\
I had no life left in this golden cage,\\
No life left. None; and nothing to pursue.

\songpart{Bridge}
I have walked the mysterious paths of grief\\
not for my kin, but for those who crushed it.

\annotation{Varse 2}\\
My mentor died; I could not hold it in.\\
He gave me back the life I thought was done.\\
I loved him more than I had loved my kin,\\
And wept the tears my father never won.

My husband wept beside me, bowed with grief;\\
I lost a mentor, he his father, too.\\
I mourned a man I’d hated past belief,\\
Held by the one I loathed, who held me through.

\songpart{Bridge}
I have walked the mysterious paths of grief\\
and grief make sense the way joy does not.

\songpart{Verse 3}
I was his captive in a golden cage;\\
He took the only life I’d ever known.\\
He slew my kin, yet like a patient sage\\
He taught me worth my kin had never shown.

My warden, he who left my kin all dead,\\
My mentor, teacher, he who made me strong,\\
The father of the man I chose to wed,\\
Whose house became the place I now belong.

\songpart{Outro}
I have walked the mysterious paths of grief\\
and the mysterious path of love,\\
and I am both.
\end{lyrics}
