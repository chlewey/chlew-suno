\songtitle{Those Kinds of Crushes}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{longing-is-safe}\label{sec:those-kinds-of-crushes}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from the free verse of \emph{Longing Is Safe} (page \pageref{sec:longing-is-safe}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, recast as rhymed verse-and-chorus pop. \emph{The Wanting Remains} (page \pageref{sec:the-wanting-remains}) extends the same lyrics. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 2 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop, low, dark lower-register male vocals, warm and rounded, rising to a gang chorus; layered distorted guitars build into the final refrain while the melody ends on an unresolved note; punchy drum kit, electric bass, keyboard chords; fast-paced d-minor drive.
\end{style}
