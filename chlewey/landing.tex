\songtitle{Landing}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alt-pop}\tag{i-understand}\label{sec:landing}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{I Understand} (page \pageref{sec:i-understand}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, tightened into rhymed, metered quatrains. \emph{Landing (soft rock)} (page \pageref{sec:landing-soft-rock}) sets the same lyrics. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Alt-pop, 2020s; sparse pulsing electronic drum machine in the verses, opening into a bigger layered chorus; layered chorus vocal stacks; close-miked midrange voice; steady midtempo pulse.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I understand. I know my place,\\
I know the way I take up space,\\
I know what all my actions do,\\
and how my presence lands on you.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But knowing's not the problem, though;\\
it doesn't make it real.\\
The knowledge never reaches down,\\
I'm numb behind the wheel.

\songpart{Chorus}
The question isn't understanding;\\
my mind can handle that.\\
The question is the truth just landing,\\
and that's where I fall flat.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I understand. Just tell me how\\
it is, and I will get it now.\\
I read the facts the way they are\\
as quick as you could set the bar.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But somewhere deep, my gut insists\\
you've got a hidden aim,\\
that no one cares about my mood\\
unless there's gain to claim.

\songpart{Chorus}
The question isn't understanding;\\
my mind can handle that.\\
The question is the truth just landing,\\
and that's where I fall flat.

\songpart{Bridge}
My head can see what instinct won't:\\
that you can simply be\\
right here, and truly like the time\\
you spend along with me.

I can't say where the hurt began,\\
some few things, all aligned.\\
It's simpler not to let you in:\\
you leave, I never signed.

I understand.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
The question isn't understanding;\\
my mind has handled that.\\
The question is the truth just landing,\\
and what if it falls flat?
\end{lyrics}
