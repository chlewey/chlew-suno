\songtitle{Say Wall}

\tag*{1992}\tag{deep-house}\tag{new-age}\tag{original-source}\tag{graeville}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on \emph{Gr\ae ville} 5 (1992), a fragment by Carlos Thompson -- the wall that is new every moment, Zeno, the "never" said twice, and the wall as the tower of Babel. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Deep house with new age textures; warm pads, rounded bass, glass bells and distant wordless choir, shifting synth arpeggio; dry, close spoken exchanges with warm mid-range male and female leads trading clipped, rhythmic staccato phrases; soft shifting 122 BPM four-on-the-floor, kick cut in the breakdown and restored beneath the final call-and-response chorus.
\end{style}
