\songtitle{Crazy Town Recap}

\subsection{Notes}

\subsection{Style}
Eurodance with industrial synth stabs, dubstep drops, and a subtle vallenato-merengue bounce; driving four-on-the-floor verses, a stripped pre-chorus with accordion-like synth licks and clipped callouts, then a huge chorus with chopped gang vocals, distorted bass hits, and bright hook synths. %
Male vocals in an intimate close-mic verse, stacked doubles on the hook, vocal delay throws on key names, metallic risers and reverse swells into each drop, crisp and punchy mix with a gritty low end., merengue, industrial, vallenato, eurodance, fresh, dubstep
