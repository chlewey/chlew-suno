\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Aurora's psychological profile of Thomas, written by Claude from notes and interactions by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe (the same source as \emph{Explicit Agreements} (page \pageref{sec:explicit-agreements})) -- two years on the record, no reassessment, and Aurora's open questions before her leave. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 10 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
adult contemporary with restrained glam-metal elements, mid-tempo steady arena-rock pulse, polished piano and clean electric guitar in the verses, broad melodic electric-guitar chorus with controlled drums and rounded bass, female lead vocal delivered matter-of-factly with precise diction and cool emotional restraint, subtle harmony doubles at section peaks, spacious but focused 1980s-leaning production, dynamics rise modestly into the chorus and settle for the final verse
\end{style}
