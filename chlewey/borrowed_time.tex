\songtitle{Borrowed Time}

\tag*{2026}\tag{rap-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{zahra-moradi}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{liis-sepp}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a story by Carlos Thompson: a late reflection by Thomas Garcia on his romantic history, naming Gabi, Zahra, Kadri, Liis, Erika, Sylvia, Claire and Greca among others. Characters: Thomas Garcia, Zahra Moradi, Kadri Kuznetsova, Liis Sepp.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Hip-hop rock with regilaul-inspired textures: minor-key electric guitar riffs, punchy live drums layered with restrained boom-bap percussion, low warm bass, hand-played frame-drum accents, sparse plucked folk strings, and subtle Estonian choral vowel textures, Male baritone lead, conversational and reflective in verses, forceful melodic hook, intimate close-mic vocal, occasional doubled phrases, Start with a stark guitar-and-percussion pulse, build into dense rock choruses, strip back for a confessional bridge, then finish with unresolved tension, Organic room sound, gritty midrange guitars, controlled sub-bass, clear vocal-forward mix
\end{style}
