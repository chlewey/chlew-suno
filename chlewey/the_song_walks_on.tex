\songtitle{The Song Walks On}

\tag*{2022}\tag{desert-blues}\tag{original-source}\tag{african-empire}
\begin{notes}
Part 5 of a five-part historical epic. Lyrics by Suno based on Carlos Thompson's historical summary of the \emph{African Empire} alternate-history universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Tuareg desert blues and West African takamba, slow rolling 12/8 groove, dry electric guitar with hypnotic pentatonic riffs, kora arpeggios, ngoni ostinatos, balafon answering phrases, deep djembe and calabash polyrhythms, sparse desert ambience, weathered male lead vocal, epic grieving delivery, call-and-response chorus, gradual arrangement from intimate verse to powerful communal climax, earthy warm mix with wide hand percussion and restrained saturation
\end{style}

Across the salt road, the old names rise\\
A black sun burns behind the eyes

\songpart{Outro}
