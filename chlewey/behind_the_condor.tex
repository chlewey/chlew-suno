\songtitle{Behind the Condor}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:behind-the-condor}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{Detrás del Cóndor} (page \pageref{sec:detras-del-condor}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- carried into English in an attempt to mimic Carlos Thompson's style. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock, mid-tempo steady snare groove; dry baritone with crooning legato phrasing, gang-vocal chorus, and lightly slapbacked voice; dry close-miked drums; tres and acoustic guitar carry the arrangement, with a spoken rhythmic breakdown.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
The gossip press knows the artist from Germany,\\
her circle of women, nothing confirmed.\\
The political press has no story, no theory;\\
the financial press can't read the firm.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The businessman who builds\\
and rarely shows his face.\\
The press knows he has reach\\
and can't yet make it plain.

\songpart{Chorus}
Thomas, Thomas García,\\
behind the Condor flying over the peaks.\\
Thomas, Thomas García,\\
owner of MACAW, of Índigo, of every feat.

\songpart{Verse 2}
The biggest private jet in Latin America:\\
the press reviewed it and still has no idea.\\
Ciudad Punta Espada, where the border ends.\\
Ciudad Índigo in Bogotá, and the new rail lines.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The businessman who builds\\
and keeps his life from view.\\
The press knows he has reach\\
and holds no cards, no clue.

\songpart{Chorus}
Thomas, Thomas García,\\
behind the Condor crossing the plain.\\
Thomas, Thomas García,\\
owner of MACAW, of Índigo, of every name.

\songpart{Breakdown, rapped}
His mother was a diplomat.\\
We found that in the public record.\\
An ex-wife, and children growing up\\
in the anonymity of Ciudad Índigo.\\
The German artist in his life\\
went from New York to Barcelona,\\
and a young Sardinian woman is in his care.\\
Not much is known about the person.

\songpart{Chorus}
Thomas, Thomas García,\\
behind the Condor crossing the whole map.\\
Thomas, Thomas García,\\
owner of MACAW, of Índigo, of Punta Espada, and what guides it.

\songpart{Outro, instruments out}
And of who he is,\\
we still know little.
\end{lyrics}
