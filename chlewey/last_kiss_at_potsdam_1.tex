\songtitle{Last Kiss at Potsdam}

\tag*{2026}\tag{rap-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Based on \emph{The Platform} by Carlos Thompson (2026). Characters: Thomas Garcia, Gabriele Wiater. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Mid-tempo hip-hop rock, around 94 BPM, tense live drums with a dry kick and cracking snare, gritty overdriven bass, muted electric-guitar stabs in the verses, widening distorted guitar chords in the chorus, sparse piano accents, and a restrained cinematic lift at the bridge, Male baritone vocals sit low and partially buried in the mix, intimate and conversational in verses, pushing into rough melodic phrasing for the chorus; use tape saturation, narrow verse ambience, wider chorus guitars, and a slightly raw room sound, Build from minimal groove to a heavy final refrain, leaving space between phrases and avoiding polished pop gloss
\end{style}
