\songtitle{Dear Thomas}

\tag*{2026}\tag{britpop}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{victoria-ospina}\tag{erika-czajka}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Vicky, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, following \emph{What Could Go Wrong} and before \emph{Night-long Embrace} (October 2026) -- writing to Thomas about Erika naming them girlfriends at his parents' table in Bogotá, a word that changed nothing, and why she keeps coming back to his arms. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 27 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Britpop with r\&b cadence, close-miked vocal and light tape saturation; relaxed midtempo backbeat with syncopated accents; female singer with Australian accent, warm mid-range register, husky, smoky phrasing; acoustic piano, chiming electric guitar, rounded bass and crisp drums; pre-chorus lift, bridge pullback, full final chorus.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
For long Erika was the one refusing to name it.\\
We were so easy together—why wouldn’t she commit?\\
Or maybe it was easy because we didn’t frame it:\\
different cities, open arrangement—that was our fit.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But for long I was afraid, my dear Thomas,\\
maybe she loved you more than she loved me.

\songpart{Chorus}
And Bogotá came, she and I together\\
with the blood and the chosen tribe,\\
and the question came on whether\\
we would sing in our mutual vibe.

\songpart{Verse 2}
And right at your parents’ table—lovely, by the way—\\
quietly she told me how she loved to introduce me\\
as her girlfriend, and she my girlfriend all the way.\\
And I said yes to the glitter, prior to landing what that’d be.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Because you should know, my dear Thomas,\\
she looks for you more than she looks for me.

\songpart{Chorus}
In your hometown, she and I together\\
with the blood and the chosen tribe,\\
then the question came on whether\\
we would sing in our mutual vibe.

\songpart{Bridge}
That was a well-sounding whim,\\
not a true commitment by her.\\
She liked the word with a rim,\\
the idea of stepping forth.

She went back to her lil’ town,\\
left me in London on my own.\\
Nothing changing, a word worn down—\\
just naming it, carving a stone.

Little changed, and yet my pace\\
feels now strained and unfree,\\
so I came back to your space\\
as a little need to flee.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
We left Bogotá, she and I together,\\
left the blood and the chosen tribe.\\
The question answered on whether\\
we would sing in our mutual vibe.

\songpart{Outro}
And here in your arms I am, dear Thomas—\\
my silent and safe way to resist,
’cause she sees in you what she can’t see in me.
\end{lyrics}
