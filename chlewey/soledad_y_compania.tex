\songtitle{Soledad y Compañía}

\tag*{2026}\tag{bolero}\tag{blues}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{spanish-lyrics}\label{sec:soledad-y-compania}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- solitude and company, each one so loved and so hated. Translated into English by Grok as \emph{Alone or Only Alone} (page \pageref{sec:alone-or-only-alone}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cuban bolero, close-miked vocal with room bleed and a delta blues texture; unhurried bolero sway, upright bass tumbao under fingerpicked acoustic guitar, deep baritone Spanish vocals, raspy, gravelly, powerful full-voice belt.
\end{style}
