\songtitle{The Steady Hand}

\tag*{2026}\tag{punk-rock}\tag{ricochets}\tag{nicolas-fuentes}
\begin{notes}
From Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}. Character summary of Jhon Nicolás Fuentes by Claude from the novel and further lore by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
anthemic alternative punk rock, fast driving live drums, gritty overdriven guitars, punchy bass, tight palm-muted verse riffs, wide melodic chorus with gang harmonies, rap bridge over half-time drums and bass, male lead vocal shifting from urgent punk phrasing to warm tuneful singing, raw but polished band mix, dynamic final chorus
\end{style}
