\songtitle{The Bridge Before I Named It}
\setin{2013-04-17}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{autobiographical}\label{sec:the-bridge-before-i-named-it}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-produced excerpt of \emph{A Life in World Events}, the memoir of Carlos Thompson -- from a Catholic childhood to the fence of agnosticism, and the April post, written in Spanish on the seventeenth, that made plain he no longer believed, while Rome announced a new pope. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 13 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Reflective pop-rock in D minor at 124 BPM, male lead vocal, steady driving rock groove with punchy live drums, melodic electric bass, clean electric-guitar arpeggios and restrained distorted guitars opening into broad choruses. Start intimate and measured, build through the verses with subtle keys and layered guitars, then widen the chorus with stacked harmonies and full-band lift; create a brief stripped-back bridge before a decisive final chorus and resolved instrumental ending. Expressive, connected vocal phrasing, nuanced rather than forceful, polished organic band mix with clear vocal focus.
\end{style}
