\songtitle{Longing Is Safe}

\tag*{2026}\tag{trance}\tag{dubstep}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{autobiographical}\label{sec:longing-is-safe}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- the crushes carried through decades into middle age, and longing as the safe comfort zone: no transgression, no attempt, no fail. Extended by Grok as \emph{The Wanting Remains} (page \pageref{sec:the-wanting-remains}) and adapted by Claude as \emph{Those Kinds of Crushes} (page \pageref{sec:those-kinds-of-crushes}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 1 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Trance anthem, driving four-on-the-floor builds into heavy dubstep drops; sub-bass drone, arpeggiated synths and soft harmony pads under the chorus; low baritone register, powerful full-voice belt with long vowel calls; boxed, distorted vocals punctuate instrumental bridges and the final drop.
\end{style}
