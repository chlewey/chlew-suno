Lyrics by SUNO based on a except from \emph{My Grabriele} from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.

\begin{style}
Electronic R\&B built around a restrained midtempo groove at roughly 86 BPM: clipped drum-machine kick and rim, rounded sub bass, sparse glassy synth chords, muted electric piano, and occasional granular vocal textures, Female lead vocal is intimate, close-miked, emotionally controlled in the verses, opening into layered harmonies in the chorus, Arrange as detailed narrative verses, a tense pre-chorus with rising bass and filtered percussion, a spacious chorus, then a stripped bridge for the December studio scene before a warmer final chorus, Keep the mix dry and near-field in the verses, widen the chorus subtly, and end with a quiet unresolved instrumental tail
\end{style}
