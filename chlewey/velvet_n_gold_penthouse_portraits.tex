\begin{style}
Cinematic lounge jazz with soft piano, brushed drums, and velvety upright bass; slow-bloom arrangement that opens from intimate spoken-word vignette into warm, brassy ensemble swells. %
Smoky penthouse-bar atmosphere, close-mic female vocal delivery with subtle reverb and late-night room tone. %
Choruses build with layered female harmonies, muted trumpets, and gentle handclaps, while verses stay storytelling and jazz-inflected over sparse chords. %
Overall feel is elegant, low-lit, and narrative-driven with a graceful key-lift in the final chorus and a hushed piano-and-brushes outro.
\end{style}
