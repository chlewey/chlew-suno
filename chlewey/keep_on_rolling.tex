\songtitle{Keep On Rolling}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{original-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- a night-out anthem: the outfit, the friends and the dancefloor, with the refrain ``just keep on rolling''. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Theatrical pop rock and roll, 1950s grand-orchestra sweep; tape saturation, plate reverb and stereo delay throws; layered unison vocals with gang-choir echoes and call-and-response; driving 132 BPM backbeat; brass fanfares, timbales and conga groove over electric rhythm guitars and upright bass, with dynamic verse-to-chorus scene shifts.
\end{style}
