Lyrics by ChatGPT based on \emph{Gr\ae ville} 1 (1992), a fragment by Carlos Thompson -- Ámila's Friday morning: half asleep under the covers while her mother says the cocoa is getting cold, the school bus she will not miss again, and her hungover sister Eneli coming round the same morning. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 23 November 2026).
