\songtitle{The Condor Line}

\tag*{2026}\tag{electronic-pop}\tag{pasillo}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{infrastructure}\label{sec:the-condor-line}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude from a free-verse seed by Carlos Thompson, with specific instructions for the chorus hook, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the branding of the Thomas Holdings Andean railway plans: Condor for high-speed passenger service, Toucan for intercity, Macaws for the regional operators, and freight hauling beneath them all. \emph{Condor Rising} (page \pageref{sec:condor-rising}) sets Grok's lyrics from the same seed. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Electronic pop, lilting 6/8 pasillo cadence; powerful full-voice clear mezzo-soprano belt with whispered ad-libs between lines; Andean flutes, marimbas, and quenas answering between stanzas over crisp synths and programmed drums; polished corporate jingle production with bright hooks and clean, concise arrangement.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
The Condor, bird of Colombia’s sky,\\
Of Ecuador, Bolivia, Perú,\\
Became the name for the railway high\\
That Andean nations would run through.

I started where my country lay,\\
For nearness made the first step plain;\\
Venezuela I tried, in spite of the sway\\
Of politics that split our reign.

\songpart{Bridge}
Peru and Bolivia still have trains\\
That run their rails on mountain ground;\\
The Condor name, on high-speed lanes,\\
Would be the brand where all is found.

\songpart{Chorus}
Fly, Condor, fly along the Andes line,\\
Toucans in the valleys, Macaws in the sky,\\
Steel on the mountain, a wing on every sign,\\
One long road of rails where the birds go by.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Yet underneath, the real tracks run,\\
For cargo up the mountainside,\\
From port to port beneath the sun,\\
The working horse, the steady guide.

Two tiers more for those who ride:\\
The Toucan brand, the intercity line;\\
Each region’s operator, with pride,\\
Would choose a name, a brand, a sign.

\songpart{Bridge}
So Condors, Toucans, Macaws take wing,\\
Great birds, great brands, in rail and air,\\
High-speed, intercity, everything,\\
With freight behind them, hauling there.

\songpart{Chorus}
Fly, Condor, fly along the Andes line,\\

Steel on the mountain, a wing on every sign,\\

\end{lyrics}
