\songtitle{Tiny Suits}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pasillo}\tag{ricochets}\tag{nestor-mojica}\tag{miguel-morocho}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude, based on a scene from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.

Verses and Pre-Chorus by Claude, edited by Thompson. Chorus by Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
pasillo folk rock, theatrical pop-rock, gang vocals, male lead tenor, spoken word bridge, corporate speech recitative, accordion arpeggios, nylon-string guitar, brushed snare, bass guitar ostinato, harmonized chorus, call-and-response vocals, plate reverb, tape saturation, 132 BPM, syncopated march feel, bittersweet irony, suspended unease
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1 — male, pasillo}
La piscina cristalina\\
hoy me espera de manera\\
tan tranquila y placentera\\
que ninguna prisa domina.\\
Bajo el sol que no lastima\\
floto en calma, en el pasado,\\
donde nada ha terminado;\\
Don Pablo, siempre paciente,\\
guarda un trago diferente\\
para el hombre que ha llegado.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus — female, corporate speech}
Weston observes through glass and wire,\\
A pool of men who should not sit\\
Together, bound by common fire —\\
Rivals now, or so we're writ.

\songpart{Chorus — gang, pop-rock}
Enemies in a swimming pool\\
Sharing whisky in tiny suits\\
Observed but not recorded\\
Enemies in a swimming pool

\songpart{Verse 2 — male, pasillo}
Vincent pregunta en el viaje
—¿qué se supo del envío?—\\
Yo respondo con sigilo,\\
sin mostrar ningún coraje:
—Bard aceptó el hospedaje\\
de la trampa que le dieron;\\
en Miami ya mordieron\\
el anzuelo de Carlson,\\
sin saber que en la balanza\\
otros nombres se pusieron.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus — female, corporate speech}
The camera counts what it can see:\\
Alcántara, Arbeláez near,\\
Vincent Smith, and Morocho, free\\
To float where no old lines appear.

\songpart{Chorus — gang, pop-rock}
Enemies in a swimming pool\\
Sharing whisky in tiny suits\\
Observed but not recorded\\
Enemies in a swimming pool

\songpart{Verse 3 — male, pasillo}
Cuando Mojica ya llega\\
saludamos en silencio,\\
pues aquí todo es silencio\\
aunque el odio no se niega.\\
Rivalidad que navega\\
bajo el agua, oculta y dura,\\
pacto que nadie asegura\\
pero que el lujo sostiene;\\
cada cual su cuenta tiene\\
y la paga será oscura.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus — female, corporate speech}
Then Mojica arrives at last,\\
Completing what should not exist.\\
No sound to tell us what has passed —\\
Only the shape of an alliance, missed.

\songpart{Final Chorus — gang, pop-rock}
Enemies in a swimming pool\\
Sharing whisky in tiny suits\\
Observed but not recorded\\
Enemies in a swimming pool\\
Things will change, we soon will see\\
Enemies in a swimming pool
\end{lyrics}
