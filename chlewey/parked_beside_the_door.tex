\songtitle{Parked Beside the Door}
\setin{2003/2013}

\tag*{2026}\tag{goth-metal}\tag{autobiographical}\label{sec:parked-beside-the-door}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-produced excerpt of \emph{A Life in World Events}, the memoir of Carlos Thompson -- 2003 and after: a mathematics course at the Javeriana chosen over work, the classroom door never reached, help never asked for, and the late diagnosis in January 2013, adult attention deficit disorder. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 12 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Gothic metal ballad, male baritone lead, intimate and restrained verses opening into a broad, aching chorus. Slow 6/8 pulse; low piano and clean guitar begin alone, then measured toms, deep bass, and layered distorted guitars build gradually. Add a brief orchestral string lift at the emotional turn, then fall back to piano and voice for the final image. Expressive melodic singing with clear diction, controlled dynamics, subtle backing harmonies, dark spacious production, weighty but not aggressive.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
At Javeriana, Cuartas showed me numbers\\
That would not fit the engineer’s design;\\
Not Olympiad victories, not blueprints,\\
But a stranger, deeper order in the line.\\
In 2003, I kept the networks running,\\
Taught a little, held another door ajar;\\
I cleared my second term of teaching hours\\
To follow mathematics where it was.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Then the contract ended, nothing offered back.\\
The year arrived with two roads on the map.

\songpart{Chorus}
I parked beside the place I meant to enter,\\
Close enough to know the classroom door.\\
I had the address, had the hour, had the reason—\\
Still I stayed where I was before.\\
A whole life leaning toward the next step,\\
A hand that would not reach the handle there.\\
I parked beside the place I meant to enter,\\
And let the day go on without me there.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I chose half-time, kept a search for work alive;\\
I thought I’d leave the other half to learn.\\
The fee was due a month into the classes;\\
I lost that month before I knew the terms.\\
By then the lectures had moved on without me,\\
And I arrived already out of time.\\
The job search filled the hours on the screen,\\
While message boards became a hiding place.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Some days I crossed the campus, reached the lot,\\
And sat beside the lesson I had not.

\songpart{Chorus}
I parked beside the place I meant to enter,\\
Close enough to know the classroom door.\\
I had the address, had the hour, had the reason—\\
Still I stayed where I was before.\\
A whole life leaning toward the next step,\\
A hand that would not reach the handle there.\\
I parked beside the place I meant to enter,\\
And let the day go on without me there.

\songpart{Bridge}
I thought: ask for help. The university had a room.\\
I never crossed its threshold either.\\
Then came the years of trying to explain\\
Why wanting did not make the doing easier.\\
Depression was a name that nearly fit;\\
Dysthymia, another offered thread.\\
In January, 2013, the words changed:\\
Adult attention deficit disorder.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I parked beside the place I meant to enter,\\
Now I can name more of what held me there.\\
The years of flattened color, stalled intentions,\\
The distance between promise and repair.\\
A different name does not erase the silence,\\
Or make that younger man less lost, less human.\\
I parked beside the place I meant to enter—\\
And at last I see him sitting there.

\songpart{Outro}
Near the classroom.\\
Knowing the way.\\
Still in the car.\\
Still there that day.
\end{lyrics}
