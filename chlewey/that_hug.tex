\songtitle{That Hug}

% -> internal.tex (Form and Craft > Adaptations III: Cross-language, translated from Kvar i mig/draft); pop.tex (R\&B > Everything Else)
\tag*{2026}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{original-source}\tag{kvar-i-mig}
\begin{notes}
English-language adaptation by Claude, drawn partly from \emph{Kvar i mig} and partly from the same detailed draft by Carlos Thompson behind it -- a hug that's stayed in memory for years, love never confessed at the time, and a chance encounter years later that confirms it's still unresolved. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
R\&B cinematic soul ballad, slow to mid-tempo with a restrained half-time bridge, Rhodes piano chords, muted electric guitar, warm sub bass and brushed snare, intimate close-mic male baritone with restrained phrasing, tape saturation, plate reverb and stereo delay
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
That hug's been living in my head for years,\\
felt like the last time I would see your face,\\
and I was way too scared to say it then —\\
how much I really cared.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You became something I couldn't explain,\\
but I let the silence win (mmm)

\songpart{Chorus}
I healed every time I looked into your eyes,\\
lost myself in all my silence,\\
I healed just from thinkin' 'bout you,\\
lost myself again 'cause I never made a move.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Didn't feel it right away, the first time that we met,\\
but the next time, girl, you had me gone,\\
and the summer let me finally know you,\\
by the fall I was all in.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You became the one inside my heart,\\

\songpart{Chorus}

\songpart{Bridge — half-time feel}
But winter came,\\
and my time was up,\\
I kinda knew I wasn't comin' back,\\
and we hugged goodbye.

Years went by, we crossed paths again,\\
just a moment, just enough to remember,\\
just a moment, just enough to know —\\
you're still somewhere in me.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I healed every time I looked into your eyes,\\
lost myself in all my silence,\\
I healed, eventually,\\
lost myself so many more times,\\
healed one part of me through you,\\
lost a little more in our story,\\
and that hug —\\
still living in my heart.
