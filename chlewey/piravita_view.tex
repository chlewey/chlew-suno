\songtitle{Piravita View}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pasillo}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{piravita}\tag{padre-peter}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Piravita} short story by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on the story's characters and events -- Father Peter and Pastor Miguel Jara.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
pasillo, acoustic guitars, arpeggiated nylon-string guitar, two male voices, church choir, baritone lead, close harmony chorus, 86 BPM
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
A small town of agricultural roots,\\
Weekends flooded by city folks.\\
Traditions of the old religion\\
Dying under new gospels and secular yokes.

Father Peter comes to town brand-new\\
To lead an almost empty church.\\
Pastor Miguel Jara fills his place\\
With promises of prosperity—little urge.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Piravita is a showcase\\
Of what the country is becoming:\\
Old faiths waning, new ones surging,\\
And modernity just humming.

\songpart{Chorus}
Father Peter, the task’s not easy—\\
Leading a shrinking community.\\
Father Peter, a beacon of faith\\
That few still need, not even he.\\
Father Peter, learn to navigate\\
What’s not taught in the seminary.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Meet the mayor, meet the police chief,\\
The leaders of the town—one still left.\\
Pastor Miguel Jara invites Father Peter:\\
Professional courtesy, get it off his chest.

A delicate performance of a family man,\\
Soft drinks on the table, a perfect picture.\\
A warm home-cooked meal, everyone in place—\\
Traditional values not quite in Scripture.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The dinner is over,\\
Time for the men to talk.\\
Glasses of whiskey in a private study,\\
The agenda coming out of the lock.

\songpart{Chorus}
Father Peter, the task’s not easy—\\
Leading a shrinking community.\\
Father Peter, a beacon of faith\\
That few still need, not even he.\\
Father Peter, learn to navigate\\
What’s not taught in the seminary.

\songpart{Bridge}
The degradation of society,\\
Modern ideas taking root.\\
A common front of people of faith\\
Against what progressives pushed through.

Father Peter knows the drill:\\
A test of conservatism, a purity test.\\
The Pastor delivers a rehearsed talk;\\
The Priest is doubting his own quest.

\songpart{Chorus}
Father Peter, the task’s not easy—\\
Leading a shrinking community.\\
Father Peter, a beacon of faith\\
That few still need, not even he.\\
Father Peter, learn to navigate\\
What’s not taught in the seminary.

Father Peter, with Matthew in his chest,\\
Conservative values against social doctrine.\\
Father Peter, the leader of faith\\
Against weaponized prayers that are empty in fact.
\end{lyrics}
