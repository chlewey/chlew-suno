\songtitle{Yet In Her Hands}

\tag*{2026}\tag{vispop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{erika-czajka}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the profile of Erika Czajka in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Vispop with pulsing midtempo groove, bright nyckelharpa and sweet fiol riffs threading through synth bass; verse stays intimate and conversational, pre-chorus lifts with stacked female harmonies and short string runs, chorus opens wider with layered doubles and a chantable anchor, Add delicate bell ticks, reverse string swells into each chorus, and a soft handclap lift before the bridge, Glossy but close-mic, airy and emotional mix, distinct, emotional, drama, romantic
\end{style}
