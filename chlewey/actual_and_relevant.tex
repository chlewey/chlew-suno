\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on part of a Quora post by Carlos Thompson -- an argument that evolution didn't create atheists out of theists, it just made agnostics out of deists, since doubt about a personal god predates Darwin. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Funk, polished live-band mix, driving midtempo syncopated groove, tight syncopated bass, clipped rhythm guitar, dry punchy drums, sharp horn stabs, organ accents, conversational male lead with call-and-response backing vocals, verse-forward arrangement with pre-choruses, memorable chorus, bridge, break, final chorus, and outro
\end{style}
