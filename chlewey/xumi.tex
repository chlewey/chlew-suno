\songtitle{Xumi}

\tag*{2025}\tag{jazz-fusion}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
Written with Claude, based on a short story from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Fast-paced modern jazz with upbeat electronic fusion, driving brushed drum grooves and walking upright-style bass lines, bright piano comping and nimble solos, tight muted guitar riffs, airy synth arpeggios accenting transitions, and playful vocal-style chops tucked into the background, Energetic, café-club feel with a propulsive swing, syncopated horn-like keyboard stabs, light sidechain movement on pads, and glossy, wide stereo space that keeps the narrative vocal front and clear
\end{style}
