\songtitle{Life's What Occurs}

\tag*{2026}\tag{chamber-pop}\tag{lifes-what-happens}\label{sec:lifes-what-occurs}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{Life's What Happens} (page \pageref{sec:lifes-what-happens}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, recast in rhymed, metered quatrains. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber pop, warm rounded baritone with stacked harmonies on the hooks; felted grand piano, buoyant saxophone solo, and chamber strings; close-miked strings with an intimate, clear mix; gently lilting midtempo pulse.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
A sunny day that warms the room,\\
A chilly afternoon before,\\
Then sudden showers break the gloom—\\
Umbrellas, hoods, or getting soaked once more.

\songpart{Chorus}
Life’s what occurs while you complain,\\
The little moments, small frictions too,\\
Mishaps and joys that still remain,\\
Reality or fiction, all come true.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A walk to buy some milk or eggs,\\
You leave the office late at night,\\
While agents handle half the dregs,\\
You spend the weekend setting both things right.

\songpart{Chorus}
Life’s what occurs while you daydream,\\
The little moments, small frictions too,\\
Mishaps and joys that still remain,\\
Reality or fiction, all come true.

\songpart{Verse 3}
The news cycle lays the world bare,\\
Exceptions deemed newsworthy still,\\
New trends that flood the social air,\\
And deeds too young for you to thrill.

\songpart{Chorus}
Life’s what occurs while coffee brews,\\
The little moments, small frictions too,\\
Mishaps and joys that still remain,\\
Reality or fiction, all come true.

\songpart{Verse 4}
The people whom you like live on,\\
Sun through the window at your post,\\
The cat demands attention strong—\\
They call it cute; you spit its hair the most.

\songpart{Chorus}
Life’s what occurs while you endure,\\
The little moments, small frictions too,\\
Mishaps and joys that still remain,\\
Reality or fiction, all come true.
\end{lyrics}
