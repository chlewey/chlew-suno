\songtitle{The Country That Never Was}

\tag*{2022}\tag{epic-alt-rock}\tag{original-source}\tag{african-empire}
\begin{notes}
Part 4 of a five-part historical epic. Lyrics by Suno based on Carlos Thompson's historical summary of the \emph{African Empire} alternate-history universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
symphonic epic alt-rock with raw grunge guitar texture, cinematic strings and brass, pounding taiko-style toms, frame drums, deep tribal percussion, Native American cedar flute and hand drum colors, West African kora and djembe accents, modal melodies, call-and-response ensemble vocals, forceful mixed choir, weathered lead vocal, dynamic verses into enormous choruses, organic live-room distortion, wide dramatic mix, solemn historical grandeur, rising final coda
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
Before the crowns, before the charts,\\
The rivers kept the oldest marks.\\
A syllable carried through rain and stone,\\
A borrowed script made flesh and bone.

\songpart{Verse 1}
Across the salt, the longships came,\\
With iron oaths and Danish names.\\
They planted grain where forests stood,\\
And bound the harvest to the wood.

No bloodline law could close the gate,\\
No single color sealed a fate.\\
The chief took seal, the stranger swore,\\
And every hand was counted more.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
From cedar smoke to castle wall,\\
New tongues rose where old ones called.\\
The land was written, claimed, and scarred,\\
Each promise paid, each burden hard.

\songpart{Chorus}
This is the country that never was,\\
Forged in the thunder, shaped by us.\\
North wind, red earth, river stone,\\
Many crowns, but not one throne.\\
We carry the names the maps erased,\\
We inherit the wound and the gathered grace.\\
Raise every banner, let every bell ring—\\
A continent learning what freedom brings.

\songpart{Verse 2}
In Louisiana, English sails,\\
In Texas, French beneath the gales.\\
Basque lords rode the southern plain,\\
Scots kept watch through dust and rain.

A dark-skinned duke by the ocean tide,\\
A native king with a book at his side,\\
Servants from every shore and tongue,\\
Their years were pledged, their lives unsung.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No chain was born to mark the skin,\\
But law still chose who lost and win.\\
A softer word can hide a blade,\\
A measured debt can still be paid.

\songpart{Chorus}
This is the country that never was,\\
Forged in the thunder, shaped by us.\\
North wind, red earth, river stone,\\
Many crowns, but not one throne.\\
We carry the names the maps erased,\\

Raise every banner, let every bell ring—\\

\songpart{Bridge}
Da’aket walked where the high snows bend,\\
With a reed-marked page and a stranger friend.\\
He spoke of one breath beneath many skies,\\
Of mercy with hands, not words or replies.

Black signs traveled from coast to coast,\\
Maya ink crossed the southern post.\\
The flute answered the iron choir,\\
The drum made a road through ash and fire.

\songpart{Breakdown}
Write it in bark.\\
Write it in clay.\\
Write what the conquerors\\
Could not take away.

Call to the mountain.\\
Call to the sea.\\
No kingdom owns\\
What the living can be.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
This is the country that never was,\\
Forged in the thunder, shaped by us.\\
North wind, red earth, river stone,\\
Many crowns, but not one throne.\\
We carry the names the maps erased,\\
We inherit the wound and the gathered grace.\\
Raise every banner, let every bell ring—\\
A continent learning what freedom brings.

\songpart{Outro}
When the last lord falls silent,\\
When the old seals fade,\\
The syllables remain\\
In every road we made.\\
The syllables remain,\\
The syllables remain.
\end{lyrics}
