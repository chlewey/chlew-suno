\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the profile of Erika Czajka in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Vispop with lilting nyckelharpa and bright fiol over a midtempo pulse, buoyant but restrained; verse 1 stays intimate and conversational, pre-chorus lifts with filtered strings and stacked female harmonies, chorus opens into layered hooks and handclap-like percussion, bridge drops to bowed folk textures then returns with fuller harmony, Close-mic lead, airy doubles, soft delay throws, crisp wide mix, natural, punta, world, warm, dynamic, dynamics, gradual
\end{style}
