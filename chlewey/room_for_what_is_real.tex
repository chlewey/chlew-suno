\songtitle{Room for What Is Real}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{erika-czajka}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the profile of Erika Czajka in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Nordic vispop with traditional nyckelharpa and fiol leading the arrangement, intimate acoustic guitar, warm upright bass, brushed frame percussion, and subtle piano, Mid-tempo 6/8 folk sway, Clear female lead vocal with carefully layered female harmonies entering on choruses and key final phrases; conversational verses, composed delivery, bittersweet but unsentimental tone, Begin sparse, add bowed strings through the second verse, widen into a harmony-rich chorus, then strip back for a reflective bridge before a full acoustic final chorus, Natural room ambience, detailed string bow texture, warm analog mix, restrained dynamics
\end{style}
