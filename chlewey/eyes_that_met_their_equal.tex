\songtitle{Eyes That Met Their Equal}

\tag*{2026}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{my-gabriele}\tag{mariain-schumer}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{fragment}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Based on \emph{My Gabriele} by Carlos Thompson (2026). Character: Mariain Schumer. Fragment. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Slow-burn contemporary R\&B ballad at 68 BPM, minor-key piano and muted electric piano, deep restrained sub-bass, sparse brushed percussion, occasional suspended guitar notes, and low cello swells entering the pre-chorus, Breath-forward mezzo-soprano vocal, intimate close-mic verses with fragile cracks and controlled lamentation, widening into fuller but still restrained choruses, Arrangement should build gradually: bare piano and voice, add bass and brushed pulse, then warm harmonies and cello in the final chorus, Modern spacious mix, dry intimate lead with short dark plate reverb, soft tape saturation, rounded low end, no theatrical belting
\end{style}
