\songtitle{Havana-Bound}

% -> personal.tex (Biographical); stories.tex (Self); rockola.tex (Confessions & Small Defeats)
\tag*{2026}\tag{rock}\tag{original-source}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a short story extracted from Carlos Thompson's memoir \emph{A Life in World Events}, cowritten with Claude -- senior year 1989, a wrong turn on the bus to a senior-retreat in Bucaramanga, whose last day landed in conflict with Carlos's own trip to Havana for the Iberoamerican Math Olympiad, and the cassette that scored the whole detour. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 26 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Argentinian rock protest pop with tape-saturated cassette textures and plate reverb, an 89 BPM danceable rock groove, clipped rhythm guitar, tight syncopated bass and live-room drums with driving tom fills, abrupt sectional shifts and a rising anthem-chorus lift; male baritone lead with a gang-chant refrain.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Senior year, eighty-nine, three dates on the page:\\
Cuba, the retreat, the exam to take.\\
I made the team for Havana-bound flight,\\
But the school bell and the timetable fought.

We asked for a bargain, found a way through:\\
The science and math crowd would sit the test too.\\
Then Bucaramanga, a few hours behind,\\
A small late convoy with the whole road in mind.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
We said, "It'll be fine, we can make up the time,"\\
With our bags in the back and a plan drawn in lines.

\songpart{Chorus}
Turn it around, turn it around,\\
We're still on the road, we're still Havana-bound.\\
Side A, side B, the same refrain,\\
Sing it again as we cross the plain.\\
We came in late, we left too soon,\\
But we found our way beneath a different moon.\\
Turn it around, turn it around--\\
That wrong turn couldn't turn us down.

\songpart{Verse 2}
One Miguel Mateus cassette in the dash,\\
Side A, side B, then the same songs back.\\
The tune settled in like the dust on our shoes,\\
A traveling chorus we couldn't refuse.

At Tunja the driver took one road too far,\\
The tape kept spinning like nothing was wrong.\\
Duitama greeted us, good town, wrong direction,\\
Two, maybe three hours added to the collection.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
We checked the road signs, checked the time,\\
The little delay grew a longer line.

\songpart{Chorus}
Turn it around, turn it around,\\
We're still on the road, we're still Havana-bound.\\
Side A, side B, the same refrain,\\
Sing it again as we cross the plain.\\
We came in late, we left too soon,\\
But we found our way beneath a different moon.\\
Turn it around, turn it around--\\
That wrong turn couldn't turn us down.

\songpart{Bridge}
I caught the last of the retreat,\\
A few bright hours, a half-known week.\\
Then back to Bogotá, bags in hand,\\
Panama first, then Havana land.\\
Silver in Cuba, worth every mile--\\
Though that cassette could still call the next song by its smile.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Turn it around, turn it around,\\
We're still on the road, we're still Havana-bound.\\
Side A, side B, the same refrain,\\
Now every old chorus brings it back again.\\
We came in late, we left too soon,\\
But we found our way beneath a different moon.\\
Silver in hand, the long road behind--\\
I know what song comes next every time.

\songpart{Outro}
Side A, side B, that road rolls on,\\
Tunja to Duitama, then the wrong turn's gone.
\end{lyrics}
