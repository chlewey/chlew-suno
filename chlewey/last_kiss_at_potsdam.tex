\songtitle{Last Kiss at Potsdam}

\tag*{2026}\tag{rap-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Based on \emph{The Platform} by Carlos Thompson (2026). Characters: Thomas Garcia, Gabriele Wiater. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Hip-hop rock with mid-tempo breakbeat drums, distorted palm-muted guitars, and buried male baritone vocals tucked under stacked shouts; verse rides a dry kick-snare pocket with tense bass guitar, pre-chorus strips to half-time claps and a filtered guitar scrape, chorus hits with gang doubles on the hook and a rough sung-rap lift, Use vinyl crackle, reversed cymbal swells into each chorus, and a final drop where the drums slam wider, Mix is gritty, punchy, and intimate with a dark room tone, rock, romantic, happy, hip-hop, light
\end{style}
