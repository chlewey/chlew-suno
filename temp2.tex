\songtitle{Actual and Relevant}
\tag*{2026}\tag{funk}\tag{quora-posting}

Lyrics by SUNO based on part of a Quora post by Carlos Thompson -- an argument that evolution didn't create atheists out of theists, it just made agnostics out of deists, since doubt about a personal god predates Darwin. Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 September 2026).
\end{notes}

Funk, polished live-band mix, driving midtempo syncopated groove, tight syncopated bass, clipped rhythm guitar, dry punchy drums, sharp horn stabs, organ accents, conversational male lead with call-and-response backing vocals, verse-forward arrangement with pre-choruses, memorable chorus, bridge, break, final chorus, and outro
\end{style}

So don't confuse the map with the road

You can kneel, you can doubt, you can pray, you can leave

[Verse 2]
There are Christians in the laboratory light

Pagans finding wonder in the living chain

And there were doubters long before
The word "evolution" crossed the door
No grand new theory had to arrive
To make a god feel absent from their lives

[Pre-Chorus]
The issue isn't how the branches grew
It's whether something divine feels real to you

[Chorus]
Evolution didn't make atheists out of theists
That story's wearing borrowed clothes

You can kneel, you can doubt, you can pray, you can leave

[Bridge]
What does a person need to say "I'm free"?
Not a war with biology
Not a fight with every sacred name
Just no god felt actual, no god felt relevant
No presence answering the frame

[Break]
Actual and relevant
Actual and relevant
That's the line beneath the argument
Actual and relevant

[Final Chorus]
Evolution didn't make atheists out of theists
Faith has never been one single road
