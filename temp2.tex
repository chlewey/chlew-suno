\begin{lyrics}
[Intro: fingerpicked guitar, fiddle underneath, spoken]
Summer, twenty-nineteen.
A man with a rule and a rented car.

[Verse 1: sung, sparse, acoustic]
Liis said “don’t come to the ceremony,”
so I dressed up, kissed her, and I went.
A week rolled by, a meme on Telegram,
and that was the last thing she ever sent.
I sent my report, walked home in the sun,
spent the night at Kadri’s, no plans to be done,
and my kids touched down in Tallinn at half past one.

[Rap 1: context and backstory, rapid-fire, drums kick in under the guitar]
Rewind: shy kid, couldn’t read a room, first “no” hit like a verdict,
carried it for years, turned the hurt to a grudge, called it fate and lived in it,
learned to talk, learned to try, got engaged, got married, got caged,
comfortable cage, ups and downs, I kept my place on the stage,
business broke, money came, doors opened, and temptation had a key,
double life, half-hearted, kept the marriage afloat, let it be,
then the rupture: all of it came down, and I swore in the wreckage, no more,
no promises, no futures, no names: one night, one tour, a season, then the door.

[Chorus A: the rule, full band, singalong]
Well, I wrote me some rules on a hotel pad,
keep it easy, keep it moving, that’s all I had.
It was only supposed to be a small season,
now the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

[Verse 2: sung]
Three nights Tallinn, three in Riga,
a ferry in the dark to Stockholm’s shore.
We drove down through Sweden, met Erika’s father,
her brothers, same age as my kids and more.
Hamburg sent my children home, and she and I stayed in,
thirty-six hours behind a door, Bremen never had a chance to begin.
Then Rotterdam, July eight, and Claire booked two beds:
“Don’t touch me till I ask.” She said it with a smile,
and the morning I reached out, she said no, I hadn’t been asked,
and I’ll admit that stung: not her, just my own expectations, my past.

[Rap 2: the hunt, faster, snare snapping]
Rotterdam to Teterboro, refuel, new guests come on board,
influencer, physician, young engineer, scanned them, weighed them, shut the door,
Claire flew back to London, Fernando to Houston, me in a RAV4 with a GPS,
Amanda, Brown’s assistant, toured me, nights were hers, no stress,
Bob’s ranch near Wichita Falls, separate rooms, his sister-in-law’s a blip,
Austin bar, one escort, not worth the tip,
hotel ceiling asking “is this worth it?” the hunt had lost its shine,
the ones worth it, Gabi, Amanda, never needed a chase, never needed a sign.

[Chorus B: the hunt goes hollow, slower]
Well, the bars and the airports, they all run the same,
you chase the whole night and you don’t know their name.
It was only supposed to be a small season,
now the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

[Verse 3: sung, steel guitar enters]
In New York I wasn’t sure about Gabi,
a feeling she wouldn’t be safe.
Holding hands in Queens, no sex, no overnights,
too domestic, I thought, for the chase.
Then the night before my flight, a kiss became more,
and I asked, “Come to Bogotá.” She said yes before I knew what for.
Six hours in a tube, with Claire on crew in the front,
Gabi cold in the Guaymaral night, so I gave her my jacket for the stunt.
She asked me, “Did you sleep with Claire?” I told her, “Yes, I did.”
She said something in German, “lucky bastard” or “you fool,” I never knew which it said.

[Rap 3: the plural, rapid-fire, rhythm locks in]
Overnight flight back, Claire in the cabin, Gabi asleep, she turned to me:
“How serious are you about Gabi?” and I had no reply, just a plea,
Carroll Gardens Friday, smoke in the walls, she doesn’t smoke, her friends do,
artists, promoters, Susan, Michael, José, I nod, I eat, I get through,
Mariain, her promoter, ex-girlfriend, the anchor in the room,
Gabi touching both of them, no hiding, no gloom:
“We said we’re intrinsically plural, in Tallinn, in Bogotá, this is her turn,”
she showed me her people, took me to her bed, and morning, I flew back, I learned.

[Chorus C: the rule cracks, melody lifts, harmonies]
I said “come with me” without a thought,
she said yes the same, and the rule came unbought.
It was only supposed to be a small season,
now the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

[Bridge: slow, spare, almost spoken-sung, one guitar]
Before takeoff, Gabi texted:
Forever, or for as long as we can bear each other. I’m in.
Claire showed me her phone: Take care of him.
That polite smile you can’t read.
Tallinn night, a small, unperformed cuddle.
And Claire in the morning: “You take care of yourself.
For her, for me, for your kids.”

[Chorus D: the table, full band, stacked harmonies, slower]
Now there’s different hearts at the same long table,
nobody running, everybody able.
It was only supposed to be a small season,
now the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

[Outro: one guitar, spoken]
Kadri wrote that night: “Coffee? Off campus. Three thirty.”
Thumbs up. Marked.
Another small season. Maybe.
