Four million lives, two hundred twenty sails.
Tomorrow’s leaving, secrets still unpublished—
A week compressed in whispers, ink, and ale.

[Pre-Chorus: Tenor]
Behind the study door, the future waited.
Forecast named the ship; I knew it too.

[Pre-Chorus: Soprano]
“Promise me you won’t inherit by commotion.”
A measured glass, an old accord made new.

[Chorus: Duet]
At six, the ships will sever every tether,
And carry all their histories away.
We sign our names and say we stand together;
We keep our separate truths until the day.

[Verse 2: Tenor]
Sixteen years, my hand stayed at my side;
No threat came close enough to draw the steel.
Then Ban was laughter in the guarded corridors,
A secret neither of us chose to reveal.

[Verse 2: Soprano]
Two years held between a look and leaving,
While Jhon’s displeasure sharpened in the air.
I thought the papers needed Tokyo’s seal;
I never knew the reason for his stare.

[Bridge: Duet]
He wanted her near, and hated that he wanted.
He knew their secret, weighed it like a cost.
Desire could not turn the course of business;
Still, something shifted when her glance was lost.

[Verse 3: Tenor]
Next morning, one transporter, not together.
At the airfield, London was her way.
“Take this flight. Keep clear of what is coming.”
No reason offered; no more he would say.

[Verse 3: Soprano]
I’ll see him when we land, I told myself.
The papers must be witnessed in Tōkyō.
The order felt like distance without meaning;
I boarded with a question left below.

[Final Chorus: Duet]
At six, the ships will sever every tether,
Four million carried past the harbor’s rim.
One leaves for London, one returns to Tokyo;
One watches as the evening gathers in.
We sign our names and say we stand together,
While love and envy travel out of view.
The ships depart; the unspoken stays behind—
And none of them can tell what others knew.
