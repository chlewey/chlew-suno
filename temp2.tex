\songtitle{I Understand}
\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{original-lyrics}\label{sec:i-understand}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- understanding is not the problem; internalizing it is. Adapted by Claude as \emph{Landing} (page \pageref{sec:landing}) and \emph{Landing (soft rock)} (page \pageref{sec:landing-soft-rock}). Retrieved from Carlos Th's Suno page (V6-MINI, 18 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop rock, male vocals, narrative verses, pre-chorus lift, repeated chorus hook, dynamic arrangement, scene-setting intro, layered guitars, piano countermelody, analog synth pads, string swells, tape-saturated rock, plate reverb, stereo delay throws, half-time breakdown, anthem chorus, introspective ache, road-bound melancholy. Start with a restrained piano-and-guitar scene-setting intro, keep verses conversational and close, build through a rising pre-chorus into a broad melodic chorus with layered guitars and vocal harmonies. Let strings and synth pads widen the bridge, drop to half-time beneath its most vulnerable lines, then return to a full-band final chorus and resolve with an intimate vocal tag. Warm tape texture, clear lyric-forward mix, expressive male lead, dynamic but controlled delivery.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
[Verse 1]
I understand.
Human condition is something I can understand.
I can know well where I stand.
I can know well what my actions do,
how my presence and my doings affect you.

I understand.
But understanding is not the problem here.
Understanding doesn't make the room clear
'cause spontaneously I just can't feel
how the heck I'm steering the wheel.

[Chorus]
The question is not understanding.
My mind can process that.
The question is internalizing,
which I'm provenly not good at.

[Verse 2]
I understand.
You can tell me what it is and I can get it.
Just expose it and give me credit.
I understand things as they are.
I get fast reality if that's your bar.

I understand.
But my gut and my body refuse to believe
there's no hidden agenda under the sleeve,
that nobody cares for me or my mood
if they can't get a profit plain and crude.

[Chorus]
The question is not understanding.
My mind can process that.
The question is internalizing,
which I'm provenly not good at.

[Bridge]
My intellect can know
what my instinct refuses to see:
that you can just be there
and really enjoy being with me.
I don't know where the trauma
came from into my life, my mind.
Not one event, just a few,
but somehow they all aligned.
It became easier for me to ignore
that you genuinely are with me
'cause if you ever leave
I can feel I didn't ever agree.

I understand.

[Chorus / Outro]
The question is not understanding.
My mind has processed that.
The question is internalizing,
like that could land flat.
\end{lyrics}
