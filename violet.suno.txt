[Intro — spoken or half-sung, dry, slightly ironic]
He said it was about carbon guilt —
it was the way he engineered rendezvous.

[Verse 1]
Moving west to start again,
manifest before the gate.
One name caught — a Berlin artist,
arranged the aisle, adjusted fate.

[Verse 2]
She was lighter than her sculptures,
eyes like something between tides.
What the profile couldn't carry
came out somewhere over the divide.

[Pre-Chorus]
She could've stayed in Rotterdam —
she chose to stay and talk instead.
And somewhere over open water
something shifted that I can't explain.

[Chorus]
I set the table, pulled the chair,
I thought I knew what I was doing there.
But she arrived and broke the frame —
the plan remained, the plan became
something I didn't engineer,
something I couldn't have prepared.
I made the calculation, she made it wrong —
she made it something more.

[Verse 3]
Three days walking stone and copper,
talking past the point of talk.
What we called exploration
felt like more than I'd allow.

[Pre-Chorus 2]
I filed it neatly, labeled it,
told myself I understood —
but Tallinn has a way of loosening
the things you've held too good.

[Chorus — repeat]

[Bridge]
No name for what the mornings were,
no name for what we didn't say.
Just city light and something building
I kept almost looking away.

[Pre-Chorus — resolution]
Platform, coat, the train already breathing —
she turned and kissed me like a door.
Not the kisses of the week before.
The kind that tells you what you're for.

[Chorus / Outro — extended]

[Intro — spoken or half-sung, dry]
He said it was about carbon guilt —
it was the way he engineered rendezvous.

[Verse 1]
Manifest before the gate,
moving west to start again.
One name caught between the others —
Berlin girl, a New York name.
Rearranged the aisle seating,
chose the window, left the space.
Most interesting person boarding —
he just helped her find her place.

[Verse 2]
She was lighter than the steel
that she bent and welded back in Queens.
Potsdam bones and Hudson angles,
something quiet in between.
Those eyes held what the profile missed —
a mind that didn't need to prove.
Somewhere over the Atlantic
the conversation found its groove.

[Pre-Chorus]
Rotterdam was never in her plans —
she chose to stay and talk instead.
Something shifted over open water,
something not yet named, not yet said.

[Chorus]
He set the table, she arrived —
the plan worked better than designed.
She exceeded what he'd calculated,
and left the calculation behind.

[Verse 3]
Three days walking stone and amber,
talking past the need for talk.
What they called a loose arrangement
felt like more with every walk.
Coffee turning into evening,
evenings turning into night —
what he'd filed under interesting
kept refusing to stay filed right.

[Pre-Chorus 2]
He told himself he understood the terms,
told himself he'd seen this before —
but Tallinn has a way of loosening
the things you think you've got a name for.

[Chorus — repeat]

[Bridge]
No name for what the mornings were,
no name for what they didn't say.
Just copper light and something building
he kept almost looking away.

[Pre-Chorus — resolution]
Platform coat, the train already breathing —
she turned and kissed him like a door.
Not the kisses of the days before.
The kind that tells you what you're for.

[Chorus / Outro — extended]
He set the table, she arrived —
the plan worked better than designed.
She exceeded what he'd calculated,
and left the calculation behind.
(repeat, fading:)
She exceeded what he'd calculated.
Left the calculation behind.
Left the calculation.
Behind.
