\songtitle{An Island and a Northwest}

\setin{1787}
\tag*{2003}\tag{folk-ballad}\tag{original-source}\tag{british-louisiana}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on a scene of \emph{British Louisiana} (2003), an alternate history by Carlos Thompson (Part 3, \emph{The United States of America}) -- a delegate's report on the states' land claims, the federal Northwest and the island in the Potomac. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Broadside ballad, late-1780s New England; unaccompanied tenor, bright clear tone with clipped rhythmic staccato phrasing; solo verses and a small singing-school chorus in rough four-part harmony, tenor carrying the melody; dry wooden-meetinghouse room sound; steady walking tempo, verses sharing one simple tune, final refrain beginning in unison and opening into harmony on the last two lines.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1 - Solo}
I took my place among the states\\
And heard their claims that day.\\
Each laid a finger on the map\\
And drew the lines his way.

\songpart{Verse 2 - Solo}
From river bend to inland lake\\
They marked their claims in ink.\\
I said, “Let all the Union hold\\
The land.” What did you think?

\annotation{Refrain - Four-part chorus}\\
An island and a Northwest too,\\
How much will serve your turn?\\
We shook a king from off our backs.\\
What lesson did we learn?

\songpart{Verse 3 - Solo}
At home I told them what I’d done\\
Beside the meeting door.\\
A cooper wiped his hands and said,\\
“What did you ask it for?”

\songpart{Verse 4 - Solo}
I said, “The great would take the whole\\
And leave us what they spare.”\\
He said, “When Congress holds the deed,\\
Who speaks for my good share?”

\annotation{Refrain - Four-part chorus}\\
An island and a Northwest too,\\
How much will serve your turn?\\
We shook a king from off our backs.\\
What lesson did we learn?

\songpart{Verse 5 - Solo}
“Potomac has an isle,” he said,\\
“With room enough to meet.\\
What need have they of all that land\\
To give the House a seat?”

\songpart{Verse 6 - Solo, quieter}
I folded up the map that night\\
And set it by the grate.\\
The island vanished in the fold.\\
The West would not fold straight.

\annotation{Final Refrain - Unison, then four-part harmony}\\
An island and a Northwest too,\\
How much will serve your turn?\\
We shook a king from off our backs.\\
What lesson did we learn?
\end{lyrics}
