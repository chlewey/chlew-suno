\songtitle{After the Bliss}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-folk}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{mariain-schumer}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Thomas, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- a night with Mariain while she still misses Gabi: comfort, not passion, and a nudge out of her New York--New Haven lane. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 19 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie Folk / Folk-Pop, unhurried steady sway, strummed acoustic and lightly chiming electric guitars with warm bass, restrained drum kit and melodic harmonica, hushed close-mic male baritone with dry up-front lead and subtle gang echoes, clean 2020s production.
\end{style}

\songpart{Verse 1}
Undoing your blouse, I realized\\
I've never been with you alone.\\
We should likely have this revised—\\
make this a thing of our own.

But you weren't ready, I knew.\\
You still missed her, I can tell.\\
She'd be in July passing through;\\
she'd told me she misses your smell.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}

more weird than it is.\\

You need to abandon your lane,\\
get your back into your fashion.
