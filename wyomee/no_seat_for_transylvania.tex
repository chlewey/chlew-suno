\songtitle{No Seat for Transylvania}

\setin{1776}
\tag*{2003}\tag{folk-ballad}\tag{original-source}\tag{british-louisiana}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by ChatGPT based on a scene of \emph{British Louisiana} (2003), an alternate history by Carlos Thompson (Part 5, \emph{Transylvania}) -- Virginia and North Carolina deny Transylvania a seat in July 1776, and Boonesborough turns to the Crown. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
1770s Kentucky frontier ballad; one plainspoken singer in a low, dark lower register with Anglo-Scots lilt, answering the fiddle’s phrases; unvarnished voice-and-string balance, dry fiddle tone; dry, open-string fiddle carries the lead; steady two-beat stride, narrow verse tune, lift on “No seat for Transylvania,” fiddle answers between stanzas, final verse and refrain unaccompanied.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1 - Solo}
I rode east from Boonesborough\\
With mud upon my coat.\\
I thought I’d stand beside the rest\\
And speak before the vote.

\songpart{Verse 2 - Solo}
Virginia laid her finger down\\
Along a river blue.\\
The man from Carolina said,\\
“We have a claim there too.”

\annotation{Refrain - Solo}\\
No seat for Transylvania,\\
No room upon the floor.\\
They found the room to claim our land,\\
A hundred miles and more.

\annotation{Short fiddle interlude}

\songpart{Verse 3 - Solo}
Back home they asked what word I’d brought.\\
I told them what I’d heard.\\
They passed the jug from hand to hand.\\
Nobody said a word.

\songpart{Verse 4 - Solo}
The king would ease our tax, they said,\\
And help us hold our ground.\\
I gave my word to back the Crown.\\
It had a bitter sound.

\annotation{Refrain - Solo}\\
No seat for Transylvania,\\
No room upon the floor.\\
They found the room to claim our land,\\
A hundred miles and more.

\annotation{Short fiddle interlude}

\songpart{Verse 5 - Unaccompanied}
I spread my map upon the board\\
And set my candle near.\\
I put a mark for Boonesborough\\
And said, “We’re still right here.”

\annotation{Final Refrain - Unaccompanied}\\
No seat for Transylvania,\\
No room upon the floor.\\
They found the room to claim our land,\\
A hundred miles and more.
