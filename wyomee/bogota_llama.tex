\songtitle{Bogotá Llama}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ebm}\tag{original-source}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on 2024 notes by Carlos Thompson on tourist circuits -- ten tours leaving from Bogotá, from London and the Baltic to the Andes, Africa and the Trans-Siberian. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 26 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Futuristic EBM with a tight four-on-the-floor pulse, sequenced analog bass, clipped industrial percussion, metallic synth accents, and dark cinematic pads. Spanish lead vocal, expressive and melodic with clear Central Colombian diction; short spoken-style route calls folded into sung phrasing. Build from a spare dispatch-like intro into driving verses, a wide anthemic chorus, a tense mid-song breakdown, then a final layered chorus. Precise, punchy club mix with controlled distortion and spacious synth depth.
\end{style}
