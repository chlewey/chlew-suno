\songtitle{Breakfast in My Bed}

\tag*{2026}\tag{industrial-pop}\tag{ebm}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:breakfast-in-my-bed}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Gabi and Sylvia}, a short story by Carlos Thompson (2026), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Gabi -- their reunion in June 2021: the question left from her studio, the line she drew and crossed, and the morning after in Carroll Gardens. \emph{Breakfast on a Tray} (page \pageref{sec:breakfast-on-a-tray}) tells it from Sylvia's side. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 27 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
German-style industrial pop with a driving EBM pulse and cinematic tension. Open with a clipped, intimate female vocal over low mechanical synth pulses; build into tight electronic drums, distorted bass, metallic percussion, and cold synth chords. Use restrained, melodic phrasing with standard American English pronunciation, clear rhotic consonants, and theatrical dynamics without shouting. Verses feel controlled and confessional; the chorus opens into a memorable, aching hook. Strip back briefly for the hand-taking and kiss, then swell into a warm, sly morning-after coda. Polished, punchy low end, sharply defined percussion, dark tonal palette with a touch of tenderness.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
“Last year, in your studio…”\\
You paused and watched me wait\\
Was it work, was it December,\\
Was it something we couldn’t name?\\
Then you asked if you had kissed me,\\
Would I have kissed you back?\\
I gave you my firmest answer,\\
Like a line I couldn’t crack

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
At lunch I let the guardrail down,\\
Told you what the past had done\\
You wore that open, guileless face—\\
And for a moment, you had won

\songpart{Chorus}
You were too young to hold that kind of power,\\
Too kind to make a weapon of it then\\
I pulled away and made a lesson of my fear,\\
Drew a line I had no right to draw again\\
You said, “I know you would—but it wouldn’t be right,\\
Me taking advantage of you”\\
Then you held your hand out, quiet as a dare—\\
And I let myself choose

\songpart{Verse 2}
I left your hand there for a few long seconds,\\
Then took it, looking in your eyes\\
“You’re evil, Sylvia,” I said softly,\\
No surprise left to disguise\\
I brought your fingers to my mouth\\
And told you what I’d tried to hide:\\
“No, I wouldn’t have kissed you back—\\
I’d have gone the whole way, against my mind”

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You closed the space between us,\\
Your face a breath from mine\\
“You’re right,” you said, “I’m evil”—\\
And we crossed the careful line

\songpart{Chorus}
You were too young to hold that kind of power,\\
But you knew what it could mean\\
The kiss was ours, the choice was ours,\\
No one else inside that scene\\
We undressed without a promise,\\
Let the night unfold its truth\\
In my modest castle in Corall Gardens,\\
I lost my guard with you

\songpart{Bridge}
Morning found you at my bedside,\\
Making breakfast from my cupboards’ odds\\
Things I never knew were waiting,\\
Made to taste like something warm\\
You improvised with what you found there,\\
I watched you move through quiet rooms\\
All that fierce restraint behind me—\\
And your hand still fitting into mine

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You were too young to hold that kind of power,\\
Still so innocent to see\\
How one honest word could undo me,\\
How a choice could set me free\\
I drew the line, then crossed it willingly,\\
With my eyes wide open, too\\
You were evil, Sylvia—and I knew it;\\
I still reached back for you

\songpart{Outro}
Breakfast in my bed,\\
Your bright, untroubled eyes\\
I thought I knew my kitchen,\\
You proved me otherwise
\end{lyrics}
