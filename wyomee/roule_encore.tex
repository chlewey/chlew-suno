\begin{style}
Eurodance, driving four-on-the-floor at 132 BPM; male rap builds into a female melodic drop with a French hook chant; sidechain pumping, tape saturation, plate reverb; punchy kick, distorted synth bass, staccato synth brass, arpeggiated lead synth, syncopated pre-chorus lift, hypnotic break, big drop fading out.
\end{style}
