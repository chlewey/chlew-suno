\songtitle{Cantar di Gladiel e Mombutu}

\tag*{2026}\tag{mediterranean-folk}\tag{sabir-lyrics}\tag{african-empire}\tag{monukae}\tag{gladiel}\tag{mombuto}
% -> stories.tex (African Empire -- Monukae)
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on the \emph{Monukae} saga within the \emph{African Empire} alternate-history universe by Carlos Thompson, written as a Mediterranean ballad in Sabir (the Mediterranean lingua franca): Gladiel and Mombuto from the burning of Cork to the false ring that becomes a vow. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 15 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Mediterranean folk ballad, low female voice and male baritone in an intimate Sabir duet, warm and restrained, almost spoken in the verses with whispered ad-libs between lines; a powerful full-voice belt lifts the choruses. Slow 70 BPM minor modal sway, melancholic and dramatic; intimate close-miked sound with warm room tone. Solo oud opens, saz layers in the chorus, distant ney enters, and frame drum shifts from steady pulse to ticking bridge notes; hushed bridge, dramatic build, unresolved ending.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
\annotation{Instrumental: solo oud, soft frame drum, distant ney}

\songpart{Verse 1}
\annotation{Female vocal, low, almost spoken}\\
Fogo grande, notte nero,\\
Cork star cenere, padre star captivo.\\
Ella vidir, ella non plorar,\\
ella dir: "mi non scordar."

\songpart{Verse 2}
\annotation{Male vocal, warm baritone, restrained}\\
Ellu star fiu di duca,\\
ellu non sabir odiar.\\
Ella star in bentana,\\
ellu mirar, ellu non parlar.

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Duet, voices join, oud and saz, frame drum steady}\\
Amor star catena? Amor star chiave?\\
Mi non sabir, mi non sabir.\\
Ti tenir mi, mi tenir ti,\\
mar andar, mar tornar.

\songpart{Verse 3}
\annotation{Male vocal}\\
Ellu andar Perù, ellu tenir sposa,\\
ma cor star qui, cor non andar.
\annotation{Female vocal}\\
Ella aspettar in casa di nemico,\\
ella aspettar, ella non sabir perché.

\songpart{Verse 4}
\annotation{Female vocal, then duet, ney enters}\\
Francia star freddo, casa star pietra,\\
ella star sposa, ma anillo star falso.
\annotation{Male vocal}\\
Prete metir aqua, nome vecchio morir,\\
nome nuovo star porta, nome nuovo star chiave.

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Duet, fuller, added saz layers}\\
Amor star catena? Amor star chiave?\\
Mi non sabir, mi non sabir.\\
Ti tenir mi, mi tenir ti,\\
mar andar, mar tornar.

\songpart{Bridge}
\annotation{Stripped back, ticking pulse, low frame drum, hushed voices}\\
Imperador dir: "mi dar ti doi mese."\\
Tre porta star, tuti star mal.\\
Mi cercar quarta porta,\\
quarta porta, quarta porta.
\end{lyrics}
