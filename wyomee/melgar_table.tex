\songtitle{Melgar Table}

\tag*{2026}\tag{blues-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{sylvia-lai}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{lucia-perez}\label{sec:melgar-table}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Jan 2026} by Carlos Thompson, Thomas' version of Sylvia's 2025--2026 diaries, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- an easy New Year's Eve at the Melgar villa: his sister and his folks up from Barranquilla, Lucía down from Bogotá with the kids and Pilar, Gabi and Sylvia flying in, beds shifting when his aunt arrives, Pilar's tent in the yard, and a shared table with no outside help. \emph{She Notices} (page \pageref{sec:she-notices}) sets the same New Year from Sylvia's side, and \emph{Sin complicaciones} also passes through it.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Blues rock with a mid-tempo shuffle and loose backbeat, warm guitar bite and organ swells; verse rides a sparse groove with brushed snare, bass, and one overdriven riff, pre-chorus opens with tambourine and rising fills, chorus hits wider with gang vocals and stop-time turns. %
Lead vocal is gritty and close-mic with doubled lines, cracked ad-libs, and short delay throws on the hook. %
Ear candy: porch-noise fade-in, handclaps into the chorus, and a final guitar squeal lift. %
Mix is warm, roomy, and lived-in., blues, slow, rock, simple
\end{style}
