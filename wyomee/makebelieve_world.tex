\songtitle{Make-Believe World}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dream-pop}
\begin{notes}
Adapted into English by Claude from \emph{Perderme en el anhelo} (page \pageref{sec:perderme-en-el-anhelo}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 7 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dream-pop, cinematic alt pop; an unhurried pulse with a gradual swell, wide layered overdubs and spacious stereo, close-mic male baritone confession with restrained verses opening into layered choruses, shimmering arpeggiated electric guitar over sustained synth pads and soft, steady drums.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Lost in the longing, cheap escape,\\
an easy way to kill the day.\\
Lost in the longing, loving things\\
that can't come near my way.\\
It's safe, 'cause none of it is real,\\
a waking dream that's far away.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Lost in the longing, dreaming up\\
pretend things, bright and fine,\\
seeing the beauty from afar,\\
watching the days unwind.

\songpart{Chorus}
A make-believe world inside my head,\\
impossible wishes, dreams cast aside.\\
A world that isn't there, but I'm not nuts, I said:\\
wishes dreamed, run off, worn out, kept inside.

\songpart{Verse 2}

a dream's not what I pursue.\\

it's just a wish that never came through.

\songpart{Verse 3}
I watch the beauty from afar, play safe:\\
no broken heart if I promise nothing more.\\
But still it breaks behind my silence\\
each time they leave, like times before,\\
my longings living lives I'm not in,
'cause staying quiet means I never give.
