\songtitle{When It Falls Apart}

\tag*{2026}\tag{metal-ballad}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{xumi}\tag{camila-alexander}\label{sec:when-it-falls-apart}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions, based on \emph{Shoulders} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Barcelona, November 2026: Xumi weighing Pere against Karim, and asking whether his shoulder will still be there if it falls apart. From the same story as \emph{Mis brazos, no a mí} (page \pageref{sec:mis-brazos-no-a-mi}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Metal ballad with brushed percussion, finger-picked electric guitars, and distorted bass; male baritone with clear pronunciation, clipped rhythmic staccato phrasing, and gang-vocal shouts on the hook; close-mic production; unhurried ballad pulse.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Coffee in Barcelona, November in the air,\\
Xumi tells me Sylvia's finding her place there.\\
A few rough edges, but she's learning to stand,\\
building something of her own, with her future in her hands.

Then she speaks of Pere, of something taking shape,\\
a door that might be opening, a chance she might embrace.\\
And Karim lives in London, somewhere out of reach,\\
the one she thought she'd choose, if he were free to meet.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
First Natalia, now Xumi,\\
another line I hadn't seen.\\
I ask her what she really wants,\\
she doesn't know what that could mean.

\songpart{Chorus}
If it falls apart, will you come back to me?\\
Will I still be here, the place you need?\\
You don't have to promise, you don't have to stay,\\
just leave a little room for me along the way.

If it falls apart, when the lights go down,\\
when the words run out and there's no one around,\\
will you find my shoulder in the aftermath?\\
Will I still be there when you look back?

\songpart{Verse 2}
A couple of days later, tea around the room,\\
Claire beside me, Vicky claiming space as usual.\\
Fiona's somewhere west, another time, another place,\\
and for a little while, we're all in the same space.

Then Claire and Vicky leave, the evening slips away,\\
Xumi stays beside me, with something left to say.\\
She doesn't think that Pere is the one who'll make it last,\\
but she won't close the door before she's seen it through at last.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
She could let it fail on its own,\\
instead of pulling it apart.\\
She's afraid of being wrong,\\
but more afraid to guard her heart.

\songpart{Chorus}
"If it falls apart, will you come back to me?\\
Will I still be here, the place you need?\\
I don't want to stop it just because I'm scared,\\
but I need to know that you'll still be there."

And I don't have an answer\\
that could make the future kind.\\
So I let her rest beside me,\\
and I keep the question in my mind.

\songpart{Bridge}
Karim was the name she carried,\\
Pere is the chance she's taking.\\
One is somewhere in the distance,\\
one is here, and worth the risking.

She doesn't ask me to decide,\\
doesn't ask me to be right.\\
Only whether, when it hurts,\\
I'll still be there that night.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
If it falls apart, will you come back to me?\\
Will I still be here, the place you need?\\
You don't have to promise, you don't have to stay,\\
just leave a little room for me along the way.

If it falls apart, when the lights go down,\\
when the words run out and there's no one around,\\
you can find my shoulder in the aftermath.\\
I'll still be here when you look back.

\songpart{Outro — Spoken, Thomas}
Funny thing about November.

Sylvia wanted my arms.\\
Xumi wanted my shoulder.

I used to think people came to me for what I could offer,\\
for the money, the opportunities, the things I could arrange.

But maybe sometimes they just need somewhere to lean.

And I wonder...

Do they want me there?\\
Or just the shoulder?
\end{lyrics}
