\songtitle{You Owe Me Nought}

\tag*{2026}\tag{bedroom-pop}\tag{partial-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Partial authorship: chorus, pre-chorus and final chorus by Carlos Thompson; verses and bridge by Grok. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Bedroom pop with fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed percussion and detuned synth bass; hushed, androgynous low-register vocal; 92 BPM with a stripped verse building into a dropped chorus release; intimate bathroom-room tone and small-room vocal bleed.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You grumble soft that life with me\\
weighs heavy on your chest each night,\\
yet still you nod and take my hand\\
and follow where I lead the light.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You're not trapped\\
in the scheme of things;\\
you still have your wings.

\songpart{Chorus}
Feel free to leave—\\
you owe me nought.\\
Feel free to choose\\
your path, your thought.

\songpart{Verse 2}
You mutter low of chains that bind\\
and how the years have worn you thin,\\
yet every time the choice arrives\\
you keep complying, and give in.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Nobody's keeping you\\
against your will;\\
you don't owe me a bill.

\songpart{Chorus}
Feel free to leave—\\
you owe me nought.\\
Feel free to choose\\
your path, your thought.

\annotation{Break}\\
You grumble that the days with me\\
feel long.\\
You claim the weight has worn you thin\\
and wrong.\\
You mutter how you long to leave\\
this place.\\
Yet still you nod and keep the pace\\
I trace.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Feel free to leave—\\
you owe me nought.\\
Feel free to choose\\
your path, your thought.\\
Feel free to be\\
instead of to rot.
\end{lyrics}
