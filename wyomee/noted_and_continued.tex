\songtitle{Noted and Continued}

\tag*{2026}\tag{glam-metal}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{mariain-schumer}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\label{sec:noted-and-continued}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a summary plan by Claude for sections 3 to 7 of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Texas, then Gabi's New York, Bogotá in August, her Carroll Gardens crowd, and Tallinn waiting at the summer's end. Covers the ground of \emph{Just the Road} (page \pageref{sec:just-the-road}) through \emph{The Night Say What It Knew} (page \pageref{sec:the-night-say-what-it-knew}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Narrative glam metal with late-1980s arena-rock swagger, punchy snare and tom fills, distorted melodic guitar riffs, harmonized lead-guitar hooks, driving bass, and theatrical but controlled male lead vocals. Build from a lean, observant verse into a wide, singable chorus; shift the verse imagery across Texas, New York, Bogotá, and Tallinn, with a brief guitar solo before the final return. Polished, vivid, high-contrast production; emotionally candid, playful, and restless.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Two weeks in Texas, Dallas kept the score,\\
Austin meetings, then a ranch beyond the door.\\
Wichita Falls, long weekend, investor’s wide domain;\\
I learned the shape of distance from the miles across the plain.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Then New York, twenty-sixth of July,\\
Daytime names and numbers, then your studio at night.\\
No grand entrance, no bouquet to prove;\\
I walked your blocks and learned the places where you move.

\songpart{Chorus}
I can’t promise forever, I can give you tonight,\\
A chair beside your window, a walk home in the light.\\
You heard the truth I brought you; you let it stand its ground.\\
Noted, and continued—while the world kept turning round.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Tallinn was a spark that wouldn’t stay a spark,\\
By week’s end we had crossed into a different kind of dark.\\
Then Bogotá, August, two days in the cold,\\
Thin air in the morning, stories layered, streets untold.\\
The altitude caught you; I watched you find your feet,\\
And said what I could not give before we crossed the street.

\songpart{Chorus}
I can’t promise forever, I can give you tonight,\\
A chair beside your window, a walk home in the light.\\
You heard the truth I brought you; you let it stand its ground.\\
Noted, and continued—while the world kept turning round.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Carroll Gardens, following week, the room was yours to name,\\
Your friends, your former lovers, every thread of your own frame.\\
Across the crowded evening, I found a place to stand,\\
Unbothered by the currents, meeting each new face at hand.\\
You watched me work the room, then smiled across the floor:\\
“I can work with this”—and opened one more door.

\songpart{Bridge}
Mariain Schumer, August ninth, a note beneath the din,\\
I met her, marked the moment, let the evening carry in.\\
And Kadri stayed a quiet line the summer couldn’t close;\\
By August’s end, Tallinn waited where the semester rose.

\annotation{Guitar Solo}

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I can’t promise forever, I can show up tonight,\\
At your studio, your corner, the places you set right.\\
You heard the truth I brought you; you let it stand its ground.\\
Noted, and continued—now the miles keep circling round.

\songpart{Outro}
Dallas to Bogotá, Brooklyn to Tallinn town,\\
No grand gesture, just returning when the sun goes down.
\end{lyrics}
