\songtitle{Seven Bright Banners}

\tag*{2026}\tag{symphonic-ska}\tag{kingdom-of-columbia}\tag{ai-based}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Kingdom of Columbia} alternate history by Carlos Thompson (2021). Based on Claude's attempt to identify several flags of the Kingdom of Columbia. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Symphonic ska with a brisk  ska groove, punchy offbeat electric guitar, walking bass, tight drums, bright trumpet and trombone fanfares, lush cinematic strings, French horns, and dramatic orchestral swells, Verses stay nimble and rhythmic; choruses expand into a bold brass-and-strings anthem with counter-melodies, Energetic mixed-gender gang responses, charismatic lead vocal, crisp vintage-inspired production with modern low-end, short room ambience, and a triumphant parade-like finish
\end{style}
