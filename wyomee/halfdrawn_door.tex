\songtitle{Half-Drawn Door}

\tag*{2026}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\label{sec:half-drawn-door}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a summary of Aurora and Thomas's first sessions (storyline by Carlos Thompson, written by Claude, summary by Grok), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dark contemporary R\&B, slow swung half-time groove with muted kick, dry rim clicks, deep rounded sub-bass, sparse minor-key electric piano, and occasional breathy synth haze. Female lead vocal, intimate low register with a controlled Northeast American accent, precise conversational phrasing and subtle melodic turns. Verses stay close and minimal; pre-chorus adds a pulse and layered harmony; chorus opens into wider stacked vocals without losing restraint. Polished close-mic vocal, dark spacious mix, understated dynamics, emotionally conflicted and clinical in tone.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You came across the border with a bottle and a date\\
Said, “I need the same prescription,” like the answer couldn’t wait\\
I said, “Not before I know you, not before we take the time”\\
Your English held steady; your first language crossed your mind

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Anxiety beneath the practiced calm\\
Old attention trails, a mind that runs on plans\\
When the noise got quiet, other patterns came in view\\
You build the map by hand; the room won’t hand it to you

\songpart{Chorus}
I can name the pattern, I can hold the line\\
Hear the parts you edit, leave the rest behind\\
You say you want closeness, then you keep the door half-drawn\\
I’m the one who asks what you have never said out loud

\songpart{Verse 2}
Since thirteen, the medicine has helped you steer the day\\
When focus stopped consuming you, the other traits stayed plain\\
A bright mind making systems where instinct might have been\\
You count each social angle, then you check the count again

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Your former wife, still kind across the years\\
A New York love who gets some guarded truths to hear\\
And Claire, beside your work, a name you can’t define\\
You fear you’ll wound her if you promise what you can’t supply

\songpart{Chorus}
I can name the pattern, I can hold the line\\
Hear the parts you edit, leave the rest behind\\
You say you want closeness, then you keep the door half-drawn\\
I’m the one who asks what you have never said out loud

\songpart{Bridge}
You told me there were many beds, but none that felt like home\\
You wanted something tender you could speak in, not perform\\
I offered cues; you watched me, let your interest show\\
Then covered it with careful words, as if I wouldn’t know

I asked for your consent before we crossed that frame\\
You said yes, and named the terms; we started all the same\\
You opened up; your mood came back from somewhere low\\
I wrote the change beside the dates you’d choose when you were in New York

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I can name the pattern, but I won’t call it fate\\
You can want a life that doesn’t make you lie or wait\\
You say you want closeness; now the words come less restrained\\
I can hear you clearer when you don’t erase your name

\songpart{Outro}
You’ll book around your travels; we’ll take it as it comes\\
I’ll keep the room for truth, and leave the choice as yours
\end{lyrics}
