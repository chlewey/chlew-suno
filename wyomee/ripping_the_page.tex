\songtitle{Ripping the Page}

\tag*{2026}\tag{punk}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Gemini, from a prompt instructing it to follow the style of Carlos Thompson as a songwriter. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 12 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Punk anthem, forceful baritone lead with stacked gang-chant refrains; tape saturation, plate reverb, sidechain pumping; distorted electric guitars, syncopated synth bass, metallic percussion, four-on-the-floor kick and snare backbeat; driving pace, pre-chorus lift, bridge breakdown, abrupt dropouts.
\end{style}
