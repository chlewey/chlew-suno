\songtitle{By the Office Door}

\tag*{2026}\tag{estonian-folk}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-guided story on Kadri Kuznetsova's account of her relationship with Thomas, story and guidance by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Estonian folk ballad, slow 6/8 pulse, minor-modal fiddle and kannel-like plucked strings, sparse bowed bass, dry frame drum entering gradually, intimate female lead vocal with a gentle Russian accent, careful consonants and slightly rounded vowels, narrative verses, restrained ornamentation, widening harmony in the refrain, raw close-miked room sound, bittersweet and observant, no modern pop drums
\end{style}
