\songtitle{Mark of 87}

\tag*{2026}\tag{epic-pop}\tag{eiran}\tag{cartographer}
\begin{notes}
From the narrative Eiran, created by Claude to make sense of a series of curated images by Carlos Thompson.\\
Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Epic pop ballad with medieval color, mid-tempo 6/8 sway and driving quarter-note percussion; verse rides lute, harp arpeggios, and low bass, pre-chorus strips to voice, fiddle, and hand drums, chorus opens with full percussion, flute lines, and soaring stacked harmonies. %
Lead vocal stays close and urgent in verses with doubles on key phrases, then widens into chant-like gang harmonies on the hook. %
Use rising swells, reverse cymbal breaths, and a ringing bell hit into each chorus. %
Mix is wide, cinematic, and bright with a polished emotional sheen., harp, medieval, low, tropical, pop, jungle, flat, ballad, world
\end{style}
