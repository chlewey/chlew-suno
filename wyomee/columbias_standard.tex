\songtitle{Columbia's Standard}

\tag*{2026}\tag{orchestral}\tag{kingdom-of-columbia}\tag{ai-based}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Kingdom of Columbia} alternate history by Carlos Thompson (2021). Based on a clean chronology of the Kingdom of Columbia generated by Claude. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic patriotic orchestral anthem with a distinctly Southern American brass-band character: stately snare cadence, warm French horns, trumpets, trombones, low strings, piano, and restrained gospel-inflected choir, Begin with a solemn historical overture, build through marching verses, expand into a triumphant broad chorus, then pass through a reflective bridge about reform and religious freedom before a grand final reprise, Clear commanding lead vocal, mixed choir responses, polished film-score production, noble rather than militaristic, with tasteful Americana and royal ceremony
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
From tidewater halls to the western plain,\\
A young crown rose through fire and rain.\\
Six old provinces, one steadfast name,\\
Columbia called through the years of change.

\songpart{Verse 1}
Parliament spoke when the old ties broke,\\
A winter of powder, a nation awoke.\\
Across the river, across the bay,\\
They held their ground and made their way.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
When distant thrones turned their faces away,\\
A child took the oath in a new-born state.

\songpart{Chorus}
Columbia, lift your standard high,\\
From Williamsburg to Memphis sky.\\
Through every trial, through every age,\\
A people write their living page.\\
Columbia, with a steadfast heart,\\
Many roads, yet not apart.\\
Under the crown and the common law,\\
We remember what we fought for.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Charles wore the ring in an uncertain dawn,\\
John Charles held fast when the years moved on.\\
From river courts to the western gate,\\
The kingdom widened its halls and fate.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Then chains fell silent by Parliament's decree,\\
And guarded doors opened toward liberty.

\songpart{Chorus}
Columbia, lift your standard high,\\
From Williamsburg to Memphis sky.\\
Through every trial, through every age,\\
A people write their living page.\\
Columbia, with a steadfast heart,\\
Many roads, yet not apart.\\
Under the crown and the common law,\\
We remember what we fought for.

\songpart{Bridge}
When the old state bell no longer ruled the room,\\
Faith found room to breathe and bloom.\\
Every conscience, every prayer,\\
Found a place beneath the open air.\\
From Italy's fields to the years ahead,\\
We carry the names of the living and dead.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Columbia, lift your standard high,\\
Let every honest voice reply.\\
From coastal dawn to prairie flame,\\
A changing kingdom keeps its name.\\
Columbia, with a steadfast heart,\\
Many roads, yet not apart.\\
Under the crown and the common law,\\
We remember what we fought for.

\songpart{Outro}
John the Third beneath the morning sun,\\
A newer chapter has begun.\\
Columbia, hold your promise true—\\
The future belongs to all of you.
\end{lyrics}
