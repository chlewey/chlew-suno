\songtitle{The Unfinished Line}

\tag*{2025}\tag*{2026}\tag{country}\tag{disco}\tag{ai-portrait}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{mariain-schumer}\label{sec:the-unfinished-line}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Stable Diffusion portrait of Mariain (2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- a gallery, a purple sleeve and silver bracelets, and the words kept unsaid. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 10 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Country-disco, danceable four-on-the-floor kick with a rounded bass groove, twangy clean electric guitar and pedal-steel flourishes over crisp disco strings and handclaps. Begin with a poised, intimate verse, widen into a bright, bittersweet chorus, then add a brief instrumental steel-and-string break before the final chorus. Expressive adult female lead, warm and self-possessed with a shy, yearning edge; polished, lively mix with clear vocals and subtle harmony layers.
\end{style}

You held your hand beside the painted blue\\
Explaining all the shapes as if they knew\\
Your purple sleeve caught every gallery light\\
I smiled like I was only there for art that night

But I’m still holding what I couldn’t tell
