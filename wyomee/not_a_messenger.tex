\songtitle{Not a Messenger}

\tag*{2026}\tag{metal-ballad}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{erika-czajka}\label{sec:not-a-messenger}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions, based on \emph{Shoulders} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Utrecht, November 2026: he arrives with words from Vicky, Erika refuses to hear them through him, and they take a train to London together. From the same story as \emph{Mis brazos, no a mí} (page \pageref{sec:mis-brazos-no-a-mi}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
pop metal ballad, male baritone singer, clear pronunciation, close-mic, brushed percussion, finger-picked electric guitars, distorted bass
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I came to Utrecht, passing through,\\
With words from Vicky meant for you.\\
But there you were, against my chest,\\
And I had no idea what to say next.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
May and October, now November,\\
Another shoulder to remember.\\
I tried to speak, but you said no:
"Tyst, Thomas. Just don't."

\songpart{Chorus}
I'm not your messenger,\\
I'm not here to take sides.\\
I'm just the shoulder where\\
You can let the tears subside.\\
You're my friend, my lover,\\
But not the one I call my own.\\
And Vicky needs to know that\\
Before we make it home.

\songpart{Verse 2}
"Not a whim," you said to me,
"When I told her I was ready."\\
You stopped me when I tried to speak:
"Don't bring her words to me."

Then you looked me in the eye:
"My friend and fuckboy, that's your place.\\
Not my partner, not my anchor.\\
Vicky needs to know the space."

\songpart{Chorus}
I'm not your messenger,\\

I'm just the shoulder where\\

You're my friend, my lover,\\

\songpart{Bridge}
"I need you," you said, "but something's wrong.\\
I need her to know I meant it all along."

I checked the trains, the flights to go.
"Pack your things. We're off to London."

"But..."

"Is it skating?"

"No."

"Then come along."

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I'm not your messenger,\\
I'm not here to take sides.\\
I'm just the shoulder where\\
You can let the tears subside.\\
No promises, no answers,\\
No pretending we know why.\\
Just a train out of Utrecht\\
And London by tonight.

\songpart{Outro}
I came to Utrecht for business.\\
I left with Erika.\\
Somehow, that's become\\
A familiar story.
\end{lyrics}
