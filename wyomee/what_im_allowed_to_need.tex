\songtitle{What I'm Allowed to Need}

\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{folk}\tag{original-source}\label{sec:what-im-allowed-to-need}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a 2026 reflection by Carlos Thompson -- not knowing what one is allowed to need, and missing being held without a trial. \emph{A Body Without a Name} (page \pageref{sec:a-body-without-a-name}) comes from the same prompt. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber adult contemporary confession in English, singer-songwriter folk, intimate and candid, with a restrained, expressive lead vocal and chamber strings framing a spare acoustic guitar. Let the arrangement breathe around the uneasy tenderness of the confession.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Put down the verdict for a minute.\\
I learned to call wanting something wrong,\\
then learned the newer words for why\\
my wanting might be dangerous.\\
Somewhere between those lessons,\\
I stopped knowing how to ask.

\songpart{Verse 2}
There are emergencies everywhere.\\
There are harder things between us.\\
I can make a case for silence\\
and call the case compassion.\\
I can make my hunger small enough\\
to fit beneath the door.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But you can be lonely\\
in a crowded room.\\
You can miss a hand\\
and blame yourself for missing it.

\songpart{Chorus}
Mm-ah, mm-ah,\\
I don't know what I'm allowed to need.\\
Mm-ah, mm-ah,\\
I don't know what I can ask you for.\\
I don't want a body like a purchase;\\
I want the ease of being welcome there.\\
Mm-ah, mm-ah,\\
I don't know what I'm allowed to need.

\songpart{Verse 3}
I want to touch without a script,\\
to learn the weather of your shoulder,\\
to let my hands arrive uncertain\\
and not be punished for the tremor.\\
You might still find joy in me\\
when I am anxious, slow, unsure.\\
I know that has been true.\\
I know it can be true again.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Still, where would I find you?\\
And what could I bring?\\
I keep the question folded\\
like a note I won't deliver.

\songpart{Chorus}

\songpart{Bridge}
I thought about paying for the shape of it,\\
for an hour where nobody has to choose me.\\
But if I buy the door, do I get the room?\\
If I buy the touch, does it touch me back?\\
I open the app; the bright faces pass.\\
I measure my face against a row of faces.\\
Then the fake names, the offers, the quick exits.\\
And I call it their failure, then mine.

\songpart{Verse 4}
No. I have been unfair.\\
Some good things came near,\\
and I was the one still holding the latch.\\
I wanted to be freer than I was.\\
I wanted you to know without my saying.\\
I wanted the risk to make no sound.

\songpart{Outro}
Mm-ah, mm-ah,\\
I don't know what I'm allowed to need.\\
Maybe I can say it plainly now:\\
I miss being held without a trial.\\
I miss being wanted as a whole person.\\
You, if you were here, would you understand?
\end{lyrics}
