\songtitle{Delicate Swan}

\tag*{2026}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{original-lyrics}\label{sec:delicate-swan}
\begin{notes}
Written by Carlos Thompson based on characters and stories from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Confessional indie / alternative singer-songwriter\\
male baritone vocals, breathy, piano, sax, acoustic guitars, bass, bass drum, snare, high hats
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I couldn’t help but notice\\
the roundness through the unbuttoned blouse.\\
I pretend I could keep my gaze unnoticed,\\
but I know that’s not the case.

I knew you were doing it on purpose.\\
I didn’t know why,\\
and you were becoming bolder and bolder,\\
testing if I would fly.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Your unconventional methods,\\
a secret we both could keep.\\
Conventional practice says it’s a sin,\\
but sins I bury deep.

\songpart{Chorus}
A delicate swan in your prescription note,\\
but that’s not the way you kept my mind clear.\\
The keeper of my words who keeps me afloat,\\
the warm body I can speak to her ear.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Then came the day you said, “Let’s try something new.”\\
You asked if you could touch.\\
I closed my eyes, accepting what I knew—\\
something I dreamed so much.

Your pale skin against my tanned being,\\
those round teasers in full display.\\
You know what you have, and I was agreeing:\\
beauty porcelain, soft plush every day.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Your unconventional methods\\
that not everyone could understand.\\
Conventional practice says it’s a sin—\\
defying sins is my brand.

\songpart{Chorus}
A delicate swan in your prescription note,\\
but that’s not the way you kept my mind clear.\\
The keeper of my words who keeps me afloat,\\
the warm body I can speak to her ear.

\songpart{Bridge}
The system saw\\
an unforgettable sin.\\
They threw you;\\
I brought you in.

Not everyone around me\\
would accept,\\
but I proudly see\\
what I kept.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
A delicate swan (Aurora Swansen)\\
in your prescription note,\\
but that’s not the way (is there a way?)\\
you kept my mind clear.\\
The keeper of my words (keeper of my sins)\\
who keeps me afloat,\\
the warm body (the gorgeous figure)\\
I can speak to her ear.
\end{lyrics}
