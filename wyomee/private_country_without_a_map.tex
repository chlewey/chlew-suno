\songtitle{Private Country Without a Map}

\tag*{2026}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a summary of Aurora and Thomas's first sessions (storyline by Carlos Thompson, written by Claude, summary by Grok), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- companion to \emph{Half-Drawn Door} (page \pageref{sec:half-drawn-door}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
dark alternative R\&B, female lead vocal, northeastern American English diction, slow nocturnal groove, sub-heavy rounded bass, sparse swung drums, minor-key electric piano, glassy synth pads, intimate close-mic verses, restrained melodic chorus, tense spacious production, subtle vocal doubles, polished but emotionally uneasy mix
\end{style}
