\songtitle{Steady as the Line}

\tag*{2025}\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{ai-portrait}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{pilar-venegas}\label{sec:steady-as-the-line}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Stable Diffusion portrait of Pilar Venegas (2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of her principal -- at the front doors, down the corridor and at the range: steady, skilled and composed. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 10 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock storytelling ballad, measured mid-tempo groove with brushed snare and steady kick, warm acoustic guitar and rounded bass supporting a restrained electric-guitar shimmer. Begin intimate with fingerpicked guitar and close, thoughtful male vocal; let the verses add subtle percussion and low piano, then open the chorus into full-band harmonies without losing its calm, observant character. Clear narrative phrasing, nuanced melodic delivery, organic room sound, polished but unshowy mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
At the front doors, morning gathers on the glass,\\
Pilar checks the papers, lets the hurried people pass.\\
A question comes, a schedule slips, a phone begins to ring;\\
She takes each thing in measured hands and sets it right again.

\songpart{Chorus}
Pilar, steady as the line drawn through a page,\\
You make a crowded day feel simpler, calm and clear.\\
I see the skill behind the quiet way you work,\\
And the grace you carry with you everywhere.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Down the corridor, the briefcase at your side,\\
You know which detail matters and which worry can subside.\\
At the range, your careful focus holds; your judgment stays exact,\\
Then back among the working hours, you bring that balance back.

\songpart{Chorus}
Pilar, steady as the line drawn through a page,\\
You make a crowded day feel simpler, calm and clear.\\
I see the skill behind the quiet way you work,\\
And the grace you carry with you everywhere.

\songpart{Bridge}
I’m your principal; I keep my praise sincere,\\
There’s respect in what I notice, and I’ll leave it there.\\
Those bright, thoughtful eyes, that composed and open face—\\
Beauty needs no grand announcement; it is simply part of your grace.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Pilar, steady as the line drawn through a page,\\
You make a crowded day feel simpler, calm and clear.\\
For the care, the poise, the work you do so well,\\
I’m grateful for the steadiness you bring here.

\songpart{Outro}
The doors swing wide; the day moves on.\\
You meet it with your usual calm.
\end{lyrics}
