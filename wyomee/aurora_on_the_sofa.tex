\songtitle{Aurora On The Sofa}

\tag*{2023}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Difussion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing a concept of a femme fatale redhead psychotherapist %
that would be incorporated into the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe as Aurora Swansen.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sofisti-pop, bedroom pop with a slow swaying groove and soft syncopated drums; verse stays close and minimal with muted Rhodes, thumb bass, and brushed snaps; pre-chorus opens into stacked airy harmonies and a rising string pad; chorus lands on glossy synth blooms and tight doubles on the hook, Breathier lead vocal, intimate close-mic verses, silk-smooth ad-libs, delay throws on key lines, chiming transitions, wide cinematic but polished mix, natural, pop
\end{style}
