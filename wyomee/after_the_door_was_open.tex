\songtitle{After the Door Was Open}

\tag*{2026}\tag{country-noir}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:after-the-door-was-open}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Thomas, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- Tokyo, Mariko, and a door that would not close again. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 13 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Singer-songwriter, americana noir; tape-saturated mix with plate reverb and a dry, close vocal; dusty acoustic guitar and fingerpicked twelve-string lead, brushed snare, muted electric bass, pedal steel fills, and restrained Hammond organ; male baritone with a spoken intro, narrative verses, rising pre-chorus, and repetition-heavy chorus; bittersweet midtempo at 92 BPM.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I wasn’t a faithful husband.\\
I just lacked opportunities to cheat.\\
Not that cheating was in my nature—\\
rather, singularity was not my beat.

My online life was full of longing,\\
low-key flirtation, passive stalking,\\
testing waters I was afraid to sail.\\
Fear of rejection just left me gawking.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And Tokyo came, and my new friends.\\
When in Rome, do as the Romans do and blend.\\
Mariko, they said, likely not her real name.\\
I took the door—no one else to blame.

\songpart{Chorus}
My life was turning, my life was changing.\\
I was learning a different arrangement,\\
a new line of credit, time for free-ranging,\\
my personal life through newer engagement.

\annotation{Post-Chorus}\\
Once the door was open,\\
it wouldn’t close again.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Mariko taught me to feel free at it.\\
It was her job, and she did it just right:\\
be gentle and careful, and not be afraid,\\
feel free with myself and care for her tight.

She taught me my arms made a woman safe.\\
She showed me how to listen to what they said.\\
I never felt she felt wrong with me.\\
I can’t tell she wasn’t—no signs in my bed.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And Tokyo passed, time to move on.\\
The Bay Area called and Mariko was gone.\\
I couldn’t find her my next time in Japan—\\
no thank-you that I could bring in a can.

\songpart{Chorus}
My life was turning, my life was changing.\\
I was learning a different arrangement,\\
a new line of credit, time for free-ranging,\\
my personal life through newer engagement.

\annotation{Post-Chorus}\\

\songpart{Bridge}
Catherine-Anne Russo—\\
that name was real.\\
L.A. corporate lawyer\\
at my sponsors in the Bay.\\
She responded to my flirting;\\
we ended up in bed.\\
She would go back to her husband;\\
I would go back to my wife—\\
a woman I loved\\
but to whom I could not commit.\\
After the door was open,\\
no way for me back.

\songpart{Chorus}

I was learning a different arrangement,\\
a new line of credit, time for free-ranging,\\
my personal life through newer engagement.

\songpart{Outro}
I opened the door with Mariko\\
and ensured it stayed open with Russo.\\
After the door was open,\\
no way for me back.
