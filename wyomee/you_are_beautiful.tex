\songtitle{You Are Beautiful}

\tag*{2025}\tag*{2026}\tag{alternative-metal}\tag{ai-portrait}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{xumi}\tag{camila-alexander}\label{sec:you-are-beautiful}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Stable Diffusion portrait of Xumi (2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- a leaf-shaped silver pendant, freckles and a loose hair in the park: the spark held without asking, friendship first. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 10 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
slowburn alternative metal ballad, intimate male baritone with standard American English diction, restrained melodic delivery and no screaming; clean picked guitar and low piano open sparsely, warm bass and measured toms enter in the first verse, choruses widen with sustained distorted guitars and layered vocal harmonies, a brief instrumental lift before the bridge, final chorus reaches a broad emotional peak then resolves to exposed guitar; organic dynamics, clear vocal-forward mix, dark warm guitar tone
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You lean against the kitchen door,\\
leaf-shaped silver at your throat.\\
You tell me how the garden fared;\\
I catch each detail, miss my note.\\
The freckles scattered over your cheeks,\\
the half-smile you forget to hide—\\
I notice more than I should notice,\\
then let the moment pass beside.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
There’s a line I won’t make you cross,\\
a quiet promise I won’t spend.\\
Some truths can stay beneath the tongue\\
and still be true between two friends.

\songpart{Chorus}
You are beautiful; I know it.\\
That isn’t all I see in you.\\
I’d rather keep the talks that wander,\\
the honest things you trust me with, too.\\
I can hold the spark without asking\\
for a fire you never offered me.\\
I want you close, and I want you free—\\
most of all, I want you here with me.

\songpart{Verse 2}
At the park, your hair comes loose,\\
autumn caught against your sleeve.\\
You laugh about the dirt on your hands;\\
I laugh, and keep what I believe.\\
When you tell me who you’re hoping for,\\
I make room and hear you through.\\
It stings a little, then it settles;\\
your good news still feels good to you.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
There’s no debt inside your kindness,\\
no secret promise in your smile.\\
I can want what I won’t ask for\\
and still walk with you a while.

\songpart{Chorus}
You are beautiful; I know it.\\
That isn’t all I see in you.\\
I’d rather keep the talks that wander,\\
the honest things you trust me with, too.\\
I can hold the spark without asking\\
for a fire you never offered me.\\
I want you close, and I want you free—\\
most of all, I want you here with me.

\songpart{Bridge}
If someday you ask what I was thinking,\\
I’ll tell you plainly, without a claim:\\
I loved your face, your laugh, your courage—\\
but friendship mattered more than naming it a flame.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You are beautiful; I know it.\\
And there’s more to you than that.\\
I’d rather be the one you call on,\\
the steady chair beside your path.\\
I can hold the spark without asking,\\
leave the open door where it should be.\\
I want you close, and I want you free—\\
most of all, I want you here with me.

\songpart{Outro}
So tell me how the garden’s growing.\\
I’ll listen, glad you came to me.
\end{lyrics}
