\songtitle{Breakfast on a Tray}

\tag*{2026}\tag{synth-pop}\tag{italian-pop}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\label{sec:breakfast-on-a-tray}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Gabi and Sylvia}, a short story by Carlos Thompson (2026), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Sylvia -- the same June 2021 reunion as \emph{Breakfast in My Bed} (page \pageref{sec:breakfast-in-my-bed}), ending with breakfast made from whatever she found in Gabi's cupboards. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 27 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Italian-style synth-pop, sleek late-night pulse with crisp programmed drums, rounded analog bass, glassy arpeggiated synths and restrained piano accents. Young female lead with a clear, intimate delivery; British English pronunciation, with subtle Italian inflection in phrasing. Build from poised, close-miked verses into a memorable, emotionally charged chorus; add delicate harmony layers and a brief instrumental lift before the final refrain. Polished, cinematic production with warm low end and spacious but controlled vocals.
\end{style}
