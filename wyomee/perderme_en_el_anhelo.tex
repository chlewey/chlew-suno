\begin{notes}
Written by Carlos Thompson -- losing himself in longing as a cheap, safe escape: romanticizing the impossible precisely because it will never become real, never risking a broken heart by never promising a future. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 27 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
dream-pop, cinematic alt pop with wide layered overdubs, male baritone lead in a close-mic confession with clear central Colombian registry, pronunciation and cadence, layered harmony vocals, unhurried pulse with a tension-release arc, clean bass synth, spacious pads, restrained drums and a widening final chorus
\end{style}
