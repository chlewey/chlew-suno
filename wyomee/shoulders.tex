\begin{notes}
Lyrics by DeepSeek based on \emph{Shoulders} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- Thomas's visit to Barcelona and Utrecht in November 2026: Sylvia, Gabi in her own flat in Sant Martí, Xumi and Erika all come to him not for his money or his name but for a shoulder. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock, high light upper-register vocals opening into powerful full-voice belts, layered through the chorus; tape-saturated production; strummed acoustic guitar, electric guitar, bass and live drums, with bright synths and a pulsing four-on-the-floor beat in instrumental eurodance bridges; steady midtempo rock groove.
\end{style}
