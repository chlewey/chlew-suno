\songtitle{Columbia, Ride On}

\tag*{2026}\tag{march}\tag{kingdom-of-columbia}\tag{ai-based}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Kingdom of Columbia} alternate history by Carlos Thompson (2021). Based on a clean chronology of the Kingdom of Columbia generated by Claude. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Grand Southern marching anthem with 19th-century brass-band color, brisk two-step parade groove, banjo and acoustic guitar doubling the harmony, snare rolls, tuba bass, bright trumpets, and a memorable communal singalong refrain, Begin with a ceremonial brass fanfare, move through narrative verses with restrained percussion, then expand each chorus into full marching-band counterlines, Warm clear lead vocal, mixed choir on the refrain, polished modern recording with period-inspired arrangement, triumphant but dignified rather than militaristic
\end{style}
