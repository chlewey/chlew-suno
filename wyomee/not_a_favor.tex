\songtitle{Not a Favor}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-soul}\tag{bossa-nova}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{abu-dhabi}\tag{camila-alexander}\tag{xumi}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:not-a-favor}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude, fed with expanded lore, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Xumi -- her side of \emph{Abu Dhabi} (page \pageref{sec:abu-dhabi}): slow capital, her own terms, and no boss and no favour. The \emph{Abu Dhabi} section of \emph{Dubai} by Carlos Thompson was written after the song. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 25 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop-soul, unhurried bossa sway in the verses, restrained stereo strings, British female singer with a wide range rising into top notes and stacked hook harmonies, warm bass and crisp understated drums, Arabic-tonal-scale instrumental bridges.
\end{style}
