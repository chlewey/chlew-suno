\songtitle{Warm Hidden in the Cold}

\tag*{2026}\tag{metal-ballad}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\label{sec:warm-hidden-in-the-cold}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Thomas, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the start of his relationship with Kadri in 2018: finding her alone at a party one chilly October night, the boundary she set afterwards, and the warmth hidden in her cold. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 1 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Metal ballad, warm rounded male baritone, hushed close-mic delivery; warm analog tape saturation; slow, restrained ballad pulse; brushed percussion, electric guitars with sustained chords and restrained distortion, distorted bass anchoring the low end.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
A chilly October night, past midnight,\\
whether booking a ride or walking tight.\\
I’ve just made love to the warm in you,\\
hidden in the cold that protects you through.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You asked me to leave, and I complied—\\
no need to insist I’d be at your side.\\
We found each other for a warm exchange,\\
not an oddity, not something strange.

\songpart{Chorus}
The warm hidden in the cold (cold),\\
the rough diamond shining through (through).\\
Katya —Kadri—, that night revealed\\
the kind of fire you hide in you.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I found you alone on the party floor,\\
wanting to be, wanting to hit the door.\\
The need of connection, and also the fear\\
of failing to read the room, my dear.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I reached to you with no major hope:\\
I could get into you, or maybe nope.\\
Most words were excess, our bodies talked;\\
we went to your place and you I unlocked.

\songpart{Chorus}
The warm hidden in the cold (cold),\\
the rough diamond shining through (through).\\
Katya —Kadri—, that night revealed\\
the kind of cautious you were too.

\songpart{Bridge}
You set a boundary after the fact:\\
I’d leave, I wouldn’t insist—a pact.\\
I went home that chilly October night;\\
I kept for me I brought you to my light.

You called me later, coffee, off-walls,\\
a full debrief, inners down to balls.\\
I know you need me to be safe for you,\\
and I’m glad I passed—I liked you too.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
The warm hidden in the cold (cold),\\
the rough diamond shining through (through).\\
Katya —Kadri—, that night revealed\\
you were complex, fire, cold—the two:\\
the inviting body under crass view,\\
the broken shielded gal I’d care for too.
\end{lyrics}
