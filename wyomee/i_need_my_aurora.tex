\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{The Talk and the Warm} (page \pageref{sec:the-talk-and-the-warm}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- condensed to the chorus hook, with Gabi as the only other name left. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 19 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sophistipop with fretless bass, Rhodes piano chords, clean electric guitar, synth brass stabs, conga tumbao and brushed snare; 128 BPM, restrained late-night groove; breathy male baritone crooning in airy legato phrases, narrative verses and stacked backing vocals; tape saturation, plate reverb and sidechain pumping.
\end{style}
