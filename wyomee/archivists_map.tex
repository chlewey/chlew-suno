\songtitle{Archivist's Map}

\tag*{2026}\tag{fantasy-ballad}\tag{eiran}\tag{cartographer}
\begin{notes}
From the narrative Eiran, created by Claude to make sense of a series of curated images by Carlos Thompson.\\
Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Fantasy ballad with intimate male vocals, slow swaying 6/8 feel, fingerpicked acoustic lute and soft frame drum; verse stays sparse and story-led, pre-chorus opens with rising strings and breathy doubles, chorus blooms with layered harmony on the hook phrase. %
Add a low bowed drone, page-turn foley, and a warm harp glint before the last line. %
Mix is wide, antique, and candlelit, world, ballad, deep, warm
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
In the bones of the tower
He learned the old stone names
Three hundred winters passing
And not one shot astray

Eiran, small as a candlestick
Slate-fur, silver beard
Coat of dark earth, elbow-thread
By parchment, map, and spear

Twenty-two in the quiver
Each feather borrowed strange
The yew bow in his hand
Like an old friend knows your face

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Then there on the gallery
The Archivist's great chart
All the seas turned inside out
All the lands torn apart

He leaned in close to study it
And felt the room go still
Something old in cartographer blood
Said, "Look again, you will"

\songpart{Chorus}
The map was true
The map was true
The world was wrong in him

The map was true
The map was true
Warm at the edge of skin

He touched the page
He touched the page
And every lie fell thin

\songpart{Verse 2}
North where forests should be
Stood white as winter bone
The hooked peninsula was waiting
Where no chart had ever known

Triple islands, central plateau
All shuffled, mirrored, lost
A blue gulf eating solid ground
At a scholar's careful cost

He'd traced these bones for decades
In the hush before first light
But now the ink was breathing
And the shadows felt like sight

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Not drift, not error, not decay
Not some fool's cracked report
This was the shape beneath the shape
The deeper, truer sort

His clawed hand reached slowly
Parchment heat against his palm
And the silence in the tower
Fell heavy, deep, and calm

\songpart{Chorus}
The map was true
The map was true
The world was wrong in him

The map was true
The map was true
Warm at the edge of skin

He touched the page
He touched the page
And every lie fell thin

\songpart{Bridge}
Three hundred years of misses
Never once a broken aim
If the chart could speak a language
It knew his patient name

A frozen waterfall beard
A chest full of dust and dawn
He'd been living in the margins
While the real world turned wrong

\songpart{Final Chorus}
The map was true
The map was true
The old stone finally sing

The map was true
The map was true
And truth came back with wings

He touched the page
He touched the page
And found the hidden ring

The map was true
The map was true
The world began to ring
\end{lyrics}
