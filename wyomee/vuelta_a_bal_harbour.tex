\songtitle{Vuelta a Bal Harbour}

\tag*{2024}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{ricochets}\tag{fabian-muller}\tag{sandra-roa}\tag{spanish-lyrics}

\begin{notes}
Written by Suno based on a draft scene for \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Fast-paced R\&B with rap bridges, syncopated mid-tempo bounce and tight drum programming; verse rides sleek bass and clipped keys, pre-chorus opens with filtered harmonies and snapped ad-libs, chorus lands on stacked vocal hooks and a short chantable refrain. %
Rap bridges turn conversational and tense. %
Close-mic lead vocal, doubled hook lines, airy backing harmonies, quick risers, vinyl crackle hits, glossy and punchy mix., r\&b, clean, rap, light
\end{style}
