\songtitle{A Small Season}

\tag*{2026}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{alt-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{liis-sepp}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{diana-naji}\tag{erika-czajka}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{lucia-perez}\tag{mariain-schumer}\label{sec:a-small-season}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions, based on \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson and additional context, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- the whole summer in four rapid-fire verses, Tallinn to Rotterdam, Texas, New York to Bogotá, Carroll Gardens to Tallinn, and the rule of ``one night, one tour, a small season'' cracking along the way. \emph{Small Season} (page \pageref{sec:small-season}) is a second set from the same instructions. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 2 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Hip-hop anthem with alt-rock musicality; layered drum-machine and live-kit punch, tight bass, a harder head-knocking groove, sampled breakbeat drums, crunchy electric-guitar riffs and ringing chords; rapid-fire rap verses, half-spoken talk-sing across a wide range, more melodic chorus climbing into top notes, stacked harmonies swelling in the final refrain.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro, spoken, low}
Summer, twenty-nineteen.\\
Rules still on the wall. Let’s see who reads them.

\songpart{Verse 1: Tallinn to Rotterdam}
Liis said don’t come to the ceremony, fine, I dressed up, kissed her, gone,\\
Wednesday was the last time, then a meme on Telegram, done.\\
Last message, door shut soft, I don’t live in that room,\\
report’s sent, sun’s out, Kadri texts, I’m in a cab by noon.\\
Stayed the night, café, stroll, summer mood on a loop,\\
laptop, plans on plans, Telegram hole, Diana’s a no-show, no loop.\\
Six a.m. adrenaline, kids land one-thirty in Tallinn,\\
three nights, Riga, three, ferry, Stockholm, I’m balancing\\
Erika into the route, Jönköping, met her dad, her brothers,\\
my kids’ age, nobody blinks, we’re easy with each other.\\
Malmö, Copenhagen, Hamburg, kids on a plane to Bogotá,\\
thirty-six hours, Erika, Bremen scrapped, no say.\\
Utrecht for her, Rotterdam for me, plane waiting at the end,\\
every stop a small season, that’s the rule, my friend.

\songpart{Chorus A: the rule – melodic, easy, confident}
Keep it light, keep it moving, leave the window wide,\\
nobody chasing, nobody tied.\\
Rules on the wall, wrote them myself:\\
One night, one tour, a small season,\\
but the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

\songpart{Verse 2: Rotterdam, Dallas, Austin}
Rotterdam, July eight, Claire, double queen, two beds, one law:\\
don’t touch till I say so, she undressed like it’s a draw.\\
Morning she kisses me, I grab, she stops, “I didn’t ask,”\\
dressed, breakfast, cafeteria, ego cracked, that’s a task.\\
Wheels up, influencer on board, cute, vacuous, pass,\\
Teterboro, a physician, headphones, nap, thanks, last.\\
Dallas hotel, she finally says yes, next day she’s London-bound,\\
Fernando to Houston, me in a RAV4, lost in a town.\\
Amanda tours me, her nights, Bob’s ranch by Wichita Falls,\\
separate rooms, sister-in-law’s a blip, wasn’t into me at all.\\
Austin: bar, an escort, not worth paying for, no,\\
ceiling stare: was this worth it? Gabi, Amanda, so,\\
a sticker to Diana, “you crossed my mind,” no more,\\
she answers in a minute, then nothing, shut door.

\songpart{Chorus B: the hunt goes hollow – same melody, slightly slower, less sure}
Bars and airports, hunting in the dark,\\
all that chasing never left a mark.\\
The ones who were worth it weren’t the ones I chased:\\
One night, one tour, a small season,\\
but the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

\songpart{Verse 3: New York to Bogotá}
New York, I’m worried about Gabi, intuition says she’s not safe,\\
Claire’s across the river in Jersey, professional, in her place.\\
Young engineer on the flight, good banter, nothing more,\\
I could feel Claire’s disappointment through the cabin floor.\\
Gabi’s studio: Susan, Michael, José, hand in hand,\\
walks through Queens and Brooklyn, domestic, damn, unplanned.\\
Night before the flight she kisses me, it’s more, I’m too weary till I’m not,\\
“Come to Bogotá,” no thinking, “yes,” no thinking, on the spot.\\
Teterboro, six hours, tube, three of us, conference seats,\\
rear-facing takeoff, Claire hands me a bag, composure, no defeats.\\
Guaymaral cold, my jacket on her, Pilar brings one for me,\\
penthouse: “Did you sleep with Claire?” I said yes, honestly.\\
German back at me, “lucky bastard,” maybe “you fool,”\\
lunch with my parents, Gabi and Lucía talk, I don’t ask, that’s the rule.\\
Overnight home, Claire in the cabin, Gabi’s asleep, and she asks me,\\
“How serious are you about Gabi?” I couldn’t answer, it’s just me.

\songpart{Chorus C: the rule cracks – melody lifts, harmonies enter}
Said “come with me,” no thinking, she said yes the same,\\
the rule cracked quiet, nobody to blame.\\
I didn’t plan a future, I just planned a flight:\\
One night, one tour, a small season,\\
but the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

\songpart{Verse 4: Carroll Gardens to Tallinn}
Carroll Gardens, Friday, smoke in the walls, she doesn’t smoke,\\
artists, promoters, I nod, I snack, I keep my face from a choke.\\
Mariain, ex and promoter, Gabi’s anchor, I knew,\\
Michael stays too, embraces, touching faces, nothing to hide, nothing to do.\\
We’d already said it in Tallinn and in Bogotá,\\
intrinsically plural, this is her time to show me hers, okay.\\
She brings me to the bedroom, no follow-up, no test,\\
morning I tidy the house, noon I’m gone, no rest.\\
Before takeoff: “Forever, or for as long as we can bear each other, I’m in,”\\
Claire shows me her phone: “Take care of him,” a polite and unreadable grin.\\
Tallinn, my place, she cuddles, small, domestic, unperformed,\\
morning: “You take care of yourself. For her, for me, for your kids.” Warned.\\
Campus, Kadri: formalities, then a text that night,\\
“Coffee? Off campus. 15:30.” Thumbs up. Marked. Alright.

\songpart{Chorus D: the table – fullest version, stacked vocals, slower}
Different hearts at the same long table,\\
nobody chasing, everybody able.\\
I came to play and I’m staying to stay:\\
One night, one tour, a small season,\\
but the season won’t leave, and I’ve run out of reasons.

\songpart{Outro, spoken, over a stripped beat}
Rules on the wall. Still there.\\
Just nobody reading them anymore.
\end{lyrics}
