\songtitle{Keep the Thread}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-guided story on Kadri Kuznetsova's account of her relationship with Thomas, story and guidance by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Slowburn Estonian indie rock with female lead vocals, English lyrics, subtle regilaul fusion, Begin with sparse clean electric guitar, restrained floor toms, and intimate close-miked alto vocals; build gradually through bowed bass, hand percussion, and layered communal call-and-response syllables inspired by Baltic runo-song phrasing, Keep the verses conversational and emotionally controlled, then widen the choruses with suspended guitars and harmonies without becoming arena rock, Tallinn winter atmosphere, dry detailed verses, roomy live-band drums in the final third, bittersweet unresolved ending, organic analog mix, moderate tempo with a patient pulse
\end{style}
