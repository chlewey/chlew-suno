\songtitle{Her Own Place}

\tag*{2026}\tag{metal-ballad}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\label{sec:her-own-place}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions, based on \emph{Shoulders} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Barcelona, November 2026: Gabi playing matchmaker with Sylvia, then showing him the place she has made her own in Sant Martí. From the same story as \emph{Mis brazos, no a mí} (page \pageref{sec:mis-brazos-no-a-mi}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Metal ballad with finger-picked electric guitars, brushed percussion, and distorted bass; male baritone shifting from clear, close-mic verses into a powerful full-voice belt, with gang-vocal shouts on the hook; unhurried, joyful sway; intimate vocal detail against a warm, weighty guitar mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
She was laughing at the two of us,\\
As if she'd seen the ending first.\\
A little nudge, a knowing look,\\
A story she was pleased to write.\\
She seemed quite happy with the thought\\
Of Sylvia and me together,\\
Not trying to keep the pieces still,\\
But letting them fall where they will.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
She knows the ways I move through life,\\
The people who come close to me.\\
And rather than draw lines around us,\\
She makes a joke and lets me be.

\songpart{Chorus}
But this is her place, her own place,\\
Not somewhere borrowed in my world.\\
A room to breathe, a space to grow,\\
A life that's hers and hers alone.\\
And when I see her standing there,\\
I understand what brought her home.\\
She doesn't need another place beside me;\\
She needs a place that she can call her own.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Then she took me to Sant Martí,\\
To show me what she'd made of it.\\
The walls, the rooms, the little things,\\
The quiet pride of settling in.\\
I'd known she wanted somewhere hers,\\
Not just a corner of our story.\\
And seeing her become herself there\\
Made me happier than I could say.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
It wasn't distance, wasn't leaving,\\
Wasn't a door she closed on me.\\
She needed ground beneath her feet,\\
A place where she could simply be.

\songpart{Chorus}
Because this is her place, her own place,\\
Not somewhere borrowed in my world.\\
A room to breathe, a space to grow,\\
A life that's hers and hers alone.\\
And when I see her standing there,\\
I understand what brought her home.\\
She doesn't need another place beside me;\\
She needs a place that she can call her own.

\songpart{Bridge}
And maybe that's what love can look like:\\
Not holding on, not pulling near.\\
Sometimes it's watching someone settle\\
And being glad that they're right here.\\
She can send me off with Sylvia,\\
She can laugh and play the matchmaker.\\
Then take my hand and lead me home\\
To somewhere that belongs to her.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
This is her place, her own place,\\
And I am welcome, not the owner.\\
No need to make it part of mine,\\
No need to turn it into ours.\\
I see her there, I see her smile,\\
And something settles into place.\\
I think I finally understand\\
Why she needed her own space.

\songpart{Outro — spoken-sung}
I texted her, half-jokingly:

“Do you like my arms and shoulders\\
more than you like me?”

She sent a laughing face.

Then an eggplant.

Deleted it.

Sent a flexed biceps.

Deleted that too.

Another eggplant.

And then she asked:

“Xumi too?”
\end{lyrics}
