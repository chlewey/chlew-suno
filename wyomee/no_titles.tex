\songtitle{No Titles}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{abu-dhabi}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{camila-alexander}\tag{xumi}\label{sec:no-titles}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{Abu Dhabi} (page \pageref{sec:abu-dhabi}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the same story evened out into five-line stanzas, with a closing verse of its own. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 20 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Atmospheric rock with warm tape saturation, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, hushed nasal reedy male baritone close-miked with intimate verses and a fuller chorus, mid-tempo folk pacing.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Abu Dhabi—\\
we booked two rooms, we only used one.\\
You feared the coming doom that night;\\
I simply offered you my hand\\
and held it till the morning light.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Slow capital—\\
that’s all you said you ever wanted.\\
It was the only offer that I made.\\
Your mind stayed haunted by the weight,\\
the heavy size of what we’d trade.

\songpart{Chorus}
Xumi—\\
I told you I trust you, and I do.\\
You want this, deep down you know.\\
Slow capital under you right now—\\
you can make it gently flow.

\songpart{Verse 3}
That promise—\\
“Promise me something, Thomas, please:\\
you’ll never try to be my boss.”\\
I looked at you and gave my word,\\
a vow that wouldn’t come with cost.

\songpart{Verse 4}
In that room—\\
a promise made in a hotel bed,\\
sealed with blood and a borrowed plume.\\
An honest deed between us two\\
to keep you free inside the room.

\songpart{Chorus}

I told you I trust you, and I do.\\
You want this, deep down you know.\\
Slow capital under you right now—\\
you can make it start to grow.

\songpart{Verse 5}
No titles—\\
I won’t be your boss or overseer.\\
You’ll keep full control of your own life.\\
I’ll only stay your friend with benefits,\\
no heavier role, no quiet strife.

\songpart{Chorus}
