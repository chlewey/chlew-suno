\songtitle{Black Stone Map}

\tag*{2026}\tag{eiran}\tag{carthographer}
\begin{notes}
From the narrative Eiran, creaded by Claude on making sense to a series of curated images by Carlos Thompson.\\
Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Medieval epic ballad with slow triple-meter pulse, frame drum and bowed harp under low drone; verse rides sparse lute and solitary voice, pre-chorus opens with hand drum and higher fiddle, chorus swells with a small choir, hammered dulcimer, and open fifths. %
Lead vocal is close-mic and grave with doubled chant on key lines, low male harmonies on the hook, and half-whispered prophecy accents. %
Wind chimes, reversed cymbal swells, and distant horn calls bridge sections; mix is wide, dark, and cinematic with a cold stone-room resonance., reading, light, medieval, ballad, rock, world
\end{style}

\songpart{Verse 1}
A letter burned in green fire bright
And left me ash by candlelight
The Lich had signed the summons there
So I took bow and blade and prayer

Three weeks through fen and fir I rode
Till cracked earth split the pilgrim road
Then black rock rose above the mist
And all my blood grew pale and stiff

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I saw her robes, the night made cloth
Her cold reach out and take the warmth
The stones were singing in my bones
Old as the first of iron thrones

\songpart{Chorus}
Black stone map, show me the gate (show me the gate)

The copy blinks, the original calls
Hold the tear when the shadow falls

Black stone map, I bear your name (bear your name)
Black stone map, the worlds both claim
If something walks where both meet thin
Let my arrow find its skin

\songpart{Verse 2}
She never turned to face my fear

Her robes were dusk with teal-lit runes
A wound of cloth beneath two moons

A book she held with steady hand
Though no torch burned on that dead land
The glyphs cast green from seam to seam
Like drowned stars waking in a dream
