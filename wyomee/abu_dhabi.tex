\songtitle{Abu Dhabi}

\tag*{2026}\tag{chamber-punk}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{camila-alexander}\tag{xumi}\label{sec:abu-dhabi}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Thomas, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- two rooms booked and one used in Abu Dhabi, the ``slow capital'' Xumi wanted, and his promise never to be her boss. Expanded by Claude as \emph{No Titles} (page \pageref{sec:no-titles}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 20 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber punk-rock, roomy string-ensemble production with a restrained room mix; deliberate, unhurried pulse; husky, smoky, hushed close-mic male baritone; baritone saxophone answering distorted electric guitar, bass, and tight drums.
\end{style}
