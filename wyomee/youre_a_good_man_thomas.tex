\songtitle{You're a Good Man, Thomas}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alt-rock}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\label{sec:you-re-a-good-man-thomas}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Claire Jones -- London, November 2026, after the events of \emph{Shoulders} (page \pageref{sec:shoulders}): if he were dark he would have groomed Sylvia, dropped Xumi and taken Erika in Utrecht; he is a good man. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
British alt rock, steady mid-tempo backbeat, warm mid-range female half-spoken talk-sing with posh British pronunciation, layered guitar overdubs and close, clear studio mix, offset electric guitar driving the chords and melodic hooks.
\end{style}
