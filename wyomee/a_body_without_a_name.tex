\songtitle{A Body Without a Name}

\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{original-source}\label{sec:a-body-without-a-name}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a 2026 reflection by Carlos Thompson, from the same prompt as \emph{What I'm Allowed to Need} (page \pageref{sec:what-im-allowed-to-need}) -- wanting closeness rather than a purchased night, and learning to say so. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber adult contemporary singer-songwriter, intimate confessional baritone, restrained piano and close-miked acoustic guitar, warm cello and viola responses, subtle brushed percussion entering late, patient 6/8 pulse, nuanced melodic phrasing, gradual lift into a vulnerable but unresolved final chorus, organic room sound and clear vocal-forward mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I learned to fold my wanting small,\\
To call the body something wrong;\\
Then every lesson changed its clothes,\\
And taught me which desire to oppose.

The world is carrying heavier things,\\
So mine stays quiet, tucked beneath;\\
And when we speak of all that hurts,\\
I swallow what I need.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I have made a home of not asking,\\
A practiced ease with the empty space;\\
But silence isn't the same as peace,\\
And I still miss a tender place.

\songpart{Chorus}
I don't want a body without a name,\\
Or a night I can purchase and leave unchanged;\\
I want the comfort of being near,\\
To touch without bracing, to be met without fear.\\
If I falter, if wanting makes me unsure,\\
Could you stay with the person, not measure the score?\\
I know that kind of kindness can be real—\\
I've felt it once. I still remember how it feels.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I open the app; the old contest begins,\\
Their polished faces, my second-guessing;\\
Some doors are traps, some good things pass,\\
And I have helped the good ones close too fast.

I wonder if paying buys the illusion,\\
A borrowed closeness, a brief solution;\\
But what would I bring that could make someone stay?\\
I hate how that question takes my voice away.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Maybe I call it being considerate,\\
Maybe I'm scared to be clearly seen;\\
I want to give more than a guarded hand,\\
But freedom has not come easily.

\songpart{Chorus}
I don't want a body without a name,\\
Or a night I can purchase and leave unchanged;\\
I want the comfort of being near,\\
To touch without bracing, to be met without fear.\\
If I falter, if wanting makes me unsure,\\
Could you stay with the person, not measure the score?\\
I know that kind of kindness can be real—\\
I've felt it once. I still remember how it feels.

\songpart{Bridge}
I am not owed an answer,\\
I know another heart is free;\\
But I am not less human\\
For naming what is missing in me.\\
I can be honest without demanding,\\
I can want without making a claim;\\
Maybe the first door I open\\
Is the one where I say it plain.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I don't want a body without a name,\\
Or a night I can purchase and leave unchanged;\\
I want the comfort of being near,\\
To touch without bracing, to be met without fear.\\
If I falter, if wanting makes me unsure,\\
Could you stay with the person, not measure the score?\\
I don't know where that meeting will appear—\\
But I can stop pretending I don't want it here.

\songpart{Outro}
I can stop pretending.\\
I can say it here.
\end{lyrics}
