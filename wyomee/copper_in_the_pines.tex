\begin{style}
Indie-folk ballad with 92 BPM fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed frame drum, upright bass swells, and glowing string pads; verse stays intimate and close-mic, pre-chorus opens with harmony lift and soft hand percussion, chorus blooms with stacked female vocals and a chantable hook, bridge drops to bare voice and guitar before a final lift. %
Ear candy: reversed breathy swells into the chorus, delicate bell tones between lines, rainstick shimmer in transitions. %
Mix is warm, wide, and cinematic yet earthy., soft, natural, romantic, simple, warm, tone, light
\end{style}
