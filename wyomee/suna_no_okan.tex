\songtitle{\textjapanese{砂の王冠} (Suna no Ōkan)}

\tag*{2026}\tag{anime-rock}\tag{japanese-lyrics}\tag{multilingual}\tag{african-empire}\tag{monukae}\tag{abulon}
% -> stories.tex (African Empire -- Monukae)
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on the \emph{Monukae} saga within the \emph{African Empire} alternate-history universe by Carlos Thompson, written as an anime theme song for Abulon: the heir crossing the sea at seventeen, captivity, the return, and the crown he refuses while ruling from the throne's shadow. Japanese with Yoruba and Quechua lines. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 14 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Anime trailer song, cinematic orchestral anime rock; African kora intro over low strings, rock band with distorted guitars and bass, percussion and war drums, strings and mixed choir with West African and Andean color; cinematic string layering builds to a full-orchestra chorus, drops out for the bridge, returns at a key change, then leaves the kora unresolved; measured march rising into heavy rock pulses; raspy, gravelly male baritone lead vocals, stacked harmonies on hooks, Mostly Japanese with two Yoruba lines and one Quechua line.
\end{style}
