\songtitle{Il più bello del liceo}

\tag*{2026}\tag{italian-pop}\tag{italian-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:il-piu-bello-del-liceo}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok from a free-verse seed by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Sylvia Lai -- her high-school years before Thomas (ca. 2019--2020): a bet with Alessia over Matteo, the best-looking boy in school, won and then found to be a faulty trophy. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Italian pop, late-2010s radio pop; driving 128 BPM four-on-the-floor pulse, tape saturation, plate reverb, sidechain pumping and a polished radio mix; young female vocals in a high, light upper register, cinematic verse lead rising into stacked harmonies and a chant chorus; synth bass ostinato, drum-machine kick, snapped snare, glossy keys and distorted guitar accents.
\end{style}
