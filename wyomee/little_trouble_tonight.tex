\tag*{2026}\tag{country-noir}\tag*{english-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{una-locura}
\begin{notes}
English-language adaptation of \emph{Una locura}, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson -- the hotel-lobby proposition scene between Thomas and Pilar Venegas. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 13 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Modern country noir, midtempo brushed two-step groove, acoustic guitar and muted electric twang, warm upright bass, restrained drums, subtle pedal steel answering vocal lines, cinematic hotel-lobby tension, conversational male-female duet, sultry but tasteful delivery, close-miked lead vocals, stacked thirds and low octave harmonies on choruses, dry intimate verses opening into wider harmony, polished Nashville mix
\end{style}
