\songtitle{Beyond the Frame}

\tag*{2026}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-guided story on Kadri Kuznetsova's account of her relationship with Thomas, story and guidance by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
soft metal power ballad with restrained verses and a wide, dramatic chorus; operatic female lead with controlled low-register intimacy, rising into clear sustained belts; piano and clean electric guitar opening, swelling string pads, melodic distorted guitars, spacious live drums, warm bass, dynamic half-time lift in the chorus, tasteful guitar solo, polished cinematic mix with vocal clarity and emotional restraint
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
October wore a borrowed face\\
Music and bodies, a crowded room\\
I crossed the distance on my terms\\
No accident, no waiting room

I took him home; the night was brief\\
I gave the order: leave\\
He did not stage a wounded scene\\
No little wound for me to grieve

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I wrote it down beneath his name\\
A fact, a mark, a clean procedure\\
Then watched the ink begin to move\\
As if the page had learned to breathe

\songpart{Chorus}
You are not what I can classify\\
Not every door obeys a key\\
I built a file to keep me safe\\
And found your pulse inside the debris\\
Acceptable risk, proceed with care\\
Two separate columns, truth and fear\\
But when you turned and disappeared\\
I knew you had already entered here

\songpart{Verse 2}
Late October, coffee off campus\\
Neutral ground beneath the rain\\
I asked about Bogotá\\
The unfinished marriage, children, names

I asked about Liis without a prompt\\
You looked at me as if I’d crossed a line\\
Tallinn is small, the office smaller\\
I read the signs; I read the time

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But this is more than reading faces\\
More than training, more than skill\\
A compulsion dressed in credentials\\
A frightened heart made practical

\songpart{Chorus}
You are not what I can classify\\
Not every door obeys a key\\
I built a file to keep me safe\\
And found your pulse inside the debris\\
Acceptable risk, proceed with care\\
Two separate columns, truth and fear\\
But when you asked what brought me there\\
I nearly gave you something real

\songpart{Bridge}
My mother called my silence strength\\
And praised the armor on my skin\\
I learned to name the threat precisely\\
So nothing living could get in

But bodies are not just answers\\
And wanting is not evidence\\
I know the cost of misdirection\\
I know the weight of consequence

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You are not what I can classify\\
No clean report can hold your shape\\
I built a file to keep me safe\\
Then watched the margins rearrange\\
Acceptable risk, proceed with care\\
The words still stand; I still pretend\\
But one cold framework cannot tell me\\
Where the person starts, and fear must end

\songpart{Outro}
October, coffee, Bogotá\\
Liis, the children, doors, the rain\\
I called it data, called it caution\\
And learned your name beyond the frame
\end{lyrics}
