\songtitle{Hold Still A Moment}

\tag*{2024}\tag*{2026}\tag{synth-pop}\tag{ai-portrait}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:hold-still-a-moment}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Stable Diffusion portrait of Sylvia Lai (2024), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- teal hair, freckles, hazel eyes, posed beside a bear: the living moment over the picture. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 10 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Intimate synthpop with a measured, swaying midtempo pulse; glassy analog arpeggios and soft drum-machine ticks under warm rounded bass. Open with close, breath-near female vocals and a few isolated keys, then widen into a gently lifting chorus with layered harmonies, glossy pads, and crisp restrained percussion. Keep the verses confessional and detailed, the chorus affectionate and memorable; polished studio mix with clear vocal intimacy and a soft-focus, photographic finish.
\end{style}
