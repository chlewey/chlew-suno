\songtitle{Natalia}

\tag*{2023}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{natalia-kyou-park}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Difussion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing a concept of a green-eyed Asian %
that would be incorporated into the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe as Natalia Kyou Park.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic anime power ballad with intimate piano and clean electric guitar in the verses, restrained live drums, warm bass, and sweeping strings entering for the choruses, Midtempo  mu 6/8 pulse, gradual arrangement lift from close verse to soaring final chorus, expressive adult female lead vocal with clear diction and a soft-to-anthemic arc, polished wide mix with bright vocal presence and emotional orchestral peaks
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Natalia Kyou Park walks past the bakery door\\
Brown hair resting softly on her shoulders\\
Green strands catch the afternoon breeze\\
And every passerby becomes a little slower

You glance back with those green eyes\\
Rosy cheeks, red lips half-parted\\
A silver pendant moves against your choker\\
And suddenly my careful heart is started

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I know the corner where the buses turn\\
I know the blue of your worn jeans\\
You make an ordinary avenue\\
Feel like the finest scene I've seen

\songpart{Chorus}
Natalia, you carry the day so beautifully\\
Sneakers tapping out a bright refrain\\
I want to walk beside you, close enough to say\\
Your name without disguising what it means\\
Natalia, when you tease me with that sideways smile\\
The whole wide city falls away\\
If admiration is a road, I'll follow every mile\\
Just to keep you in my view today

\songpart{Verse 2}
Black T-shirt, silver at your ears\\
A tiny spark beneath the passing traffic\\
You tilt your head and ask me where I'm going\\
As if my answer could become a habit

The crosswalk counts down in little lights\\
A taxi rolls like distant thunder\\
You laugh and tuck your side bangs back\\
And I forget the years I've spent staying under

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No grand parade, no perfect line\\
Just your hand brushing mine\\
You make the crowded avenue\\
Feel like a place we could define

\songpart{Chorus}
Natalia, you carry the day so beautifully\\
Sneakers tapping out a bright refrain\\
I want to walk beside you, close enough to say\\
Your name without disguising what it means\\
Natalia, when you tease me with that sideways smile\\
The whole wide city falls away\\
If admiration is a road, I'll follow every mile\\
Just to keep you in my view today

\songpart{Bridge}
At the corner, evening gathers\\
On the windows, gold and gray\\
You say, "Come on, don't lose me"\\
So I choose the longer way\\
No need to promise forever\\
No need to make the moment grand\\
I only want this gentle distance\\
Closing as we walk hand in hand

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Natalia, you carry the day so beautifully\\
Every avenue remembers your name\\
I want to walk beside you, honest and unafraid\\
And let this admiration find its shape\\
Natalia, with your green eyes turned toward mine\\
The city opens like a door\\
If admiration is a road, I'll follow every mile\\
And still be grateful when we reach the shore

\songpart{Outro}
Natalia Kyou Park, the evening starts to sway\\
Keep walking, I'll be right beside you today
\end{lyrics}
