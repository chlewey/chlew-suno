\songtitle{Claire Jones, My Love}

\tag*{2023}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Difussion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing a concept of a redhead private jet stewardess %
that would be incorporated into the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe as Claire Jones.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Tender Welsh folk-pop ballad with a gentle 6/8 sway, intimate solo female vocal, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft piano, restrained brushed percussion, warm cello entering in the chorus, and subtle close harmonies, Begin sparse and reflective, widen gently for the chorus, then fall back to a quiet final verse, Cinematic but natural mix, clear vocal, warm low mids, understated cabin-like intimacy, bittersweet rather than dramatic
\end{style}
