\tag*{2026}\tag{dark-rnb}\tag*{english-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{una-locura}\tag{little-trouble-tonight}
\begin{notes}
Another English-language adaptation of \emph{Una locura}, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson -- the hotel-lobby scene between Thomas and Pilar Venegas, rewritten as a solo female vocal in dark R\&B. Companion to \emph{Little Trouble Tonight}. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 13 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dark r\&b, raspy female lead vocals, close-miked and sultry with intimate breath detail, late-90s r\&b phrasing, midtempo groove with sidechain pumping, analog synth bass, detuned synth pads, distorted drum machine, chopped percussion, gated snare, tape saturation and plate reverb
\end{style}
