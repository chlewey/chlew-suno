\songtitle{Winter Moves, and I Remain}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-metal}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-guided story on Kadri Kuznetsova's account of her relationship with Thomas, story and guidance by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Estonian folk metal, melodic female lead vocals, mid-to-fast tempo, galloping double-kick drums, punchy distorted guitars, bowed folk strings, kannel-like plucked motifs, occasional bagpipe color, minor-key verses opening into bright anthemic choruses, clear expressive mezzo-soprano delivery, tight modern metal mix with organic folk texture, dynamic seasonal transitions
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}

\songpart{Verse 1}
He went home for Christmas, I knew it by the thinning thread\\
Bogotá, then Mexico, then Bogotá instead\\
No itinerary requested, no claim upon his days\\
I do not count a man's departures or confuse them with a maze

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Still, absence has a shape\\
A blank where the page turns strange\\
Not grief, not a broken promise\\
Just less of him inside the frame

\songpart{Chorus}
Winter moves, and I remain\\
Cold white miles across the plain\\
I name the space, I let it be\\
No wound requires a tragedy\\
When the silence takes its place\\
I do not chase, I do not erase\\
Winter moves, and I remain\\
Clear-eyed beneath the falling rain

\songpart{Verse 2}
He came back late, blamed the weather, spoke as though no time had passed\\
I answered in the same old manner, made the ordinary last\\
Neither lied, neither opened up the door beneath the words\\
Some truths survive by staying quiet, undisturbed

\songpart{Verse 3}
Through March and April, threads returned, sporadic as before\\
A Swedish girl named Erika had crossed his winter door\\
I filed the name and left it there; I gave it no design\\
To make a fact become a question takes a choice, and it was mine

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I reached as often as he did\\
Perhaps a little more\\
I liked the shape of what we had\\
No deeper oath required

\songpart{Chorus}
Spring moves, and I remain\\
Green shoots splitting through the grain\\
I name the want, I let it stand\\
No hidden verdict in my hand\\
When the silence takes its place\\
I do not chase, I do not erase\\
Spring moves, and I remain\\
With open eyes and steady veins

\songpart{Bridge}
Not every clean line is deceit\\
Not every answer needs a wound\\
Sometimes a thing is simply what it is\\
And habit makes a maze around the room\\
My training reaches past its bounds\\
Then I call it back by name\\
The mind can measure what it finds\\
Without inventing blame

\songpart{Verse 4}
Then summer emptied out for weeks, Texas, New York, some business\\
Other people filled the days; I did not call it weakness\\
I never chase what will not turn, the data has been clear\\
There are always roads beyond one door, and other people here

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Summer moves, and I remain\\
Warm dust rising from the plain\\
No pursuit and no disguise\\
Just the honest weather of a life\\
When the silence takes its place\\
I do not chase, I do not erase\\
Summer moves, and I remain\\
And nothing lost is mine to name

\songpart{Outro}
Winter, spring, summer pass\\
I keep my footing in the grass\\
No promise made, no promise broken\\
Only what was left unspoken
