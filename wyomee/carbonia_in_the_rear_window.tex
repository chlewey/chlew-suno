\songtitle{Carbonia in the Rear Window}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{tallinn-arc}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{greca-piras}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, from the narrative universe \emph{Thomas' Entourage} -- Pilar Venegas drives Thomas from Carbonia to the Cagliari airport at the end of the Sardinia villa visit, needling him about Greca Piras along the way. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 13 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop ballad with a polished European electronic feel, close-mic male baritone vocals, restrained and intimate delivery, slow-building roadtrip pulse, pulsing synth bass, glassy arpeggiators, muted electronic drums, warm pads, and a widening final chorus with dark autumnal reverb
\end{style}
