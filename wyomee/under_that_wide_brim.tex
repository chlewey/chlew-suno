\songtitle{Under That Wide Brim}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ricochets}\tag{david-harris}\tag{marion-gebhart}
\begin{notes}
Written by Claude from specific prompts by Carlos Thompson, based on characters and events from the novel \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
torch-song noir, spoken intro, male tenor lead, baritone counterline, 92 BPM, half-time swing, brushed snare kit, upright bass pizzicato, muted trumpet, Rhodes piano chords, tremolo guitar, tape saturation, plate reverb, mono vocal close-mic, cinematic strings, noir suspense, urban nightscape, dramatic pre-chorus, story-driven arrangement, spoken intro and outro
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
A folder left in a stranger's box,\\
no name but mine on top —
"Eyes only." A warning wrapped in silence.\\
Photographs I never asked to stop.

\songpart{Verse 1}
I buried her in silence, called it grief,\\
never let the doubt get free.\\
A bullet with my name on it, I always thought —\\
now here's a woman watching me.\\
No badge, no warrant, just an envelope,\\
and a warning wearing my own name.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus 1}
Who calls a meeting and won't say why,\\
picks the corner, picks the hour?\\
I went not knowing who I'd find there —\\
only that she'd asked, and had the power.

\songpart{Chorus}
Marion, Marion Gebhart, under that wide brim,\\
sunglasses hiding where you've been.\\
Costume anywhere but here in Miami,\\
mysterious ways, and I let you in.

\songpart{Verse 2}
She talked while we walked, never once slowing,\\
counted the doors we passed by.
"Divorces, mostly," she said, "domestic work —\\
your Carlson hired me since mid-'03."\\
I did the math in the space she left me —\\
months before the case ever came to me.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I said MCT and something cracked in her —
"this was a mistake," she said.\\
Gone in the crowd before I could stop her,\\
just a hat, and the words in my head.

\songpart{Chorus}
Marion, Marion Gebhart, under that wide brim,\\
sunglasses hiding where you've been.\\
I checked your name — the sheriff's office knew it,\\
mysterious ways, but nothing I could pin.

\songpart{Bridge}
I reached for a card in a drawer left forgotten —\\
a detective's name, a case gone cold.\\
Two months dead. His brakes just failed him\\
on a road he drove too many times before.\\
He'd asked the same things I was asking.\\
Maybe that's why you ran.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Marion, Marion Gebhart, under that wide brim,\\
what did you know that I still don't?\\
Mysterious ways, or just afraid —\\
either way, Marion, you won't.

You won't come back. You won't explain it.\\
Mysterious ways, and I let you go.

\songpart{Outro}
I never proved a thing connects them —\\
a dead detective, a woman gone.\\
Just a feeling that follows me now,\\
that paranoids have enemies, and I might be one.
\end{lyrics}
