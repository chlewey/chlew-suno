\songtitle{The Talk and the Warm}

\tag*{2026}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{diana-naji}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:the-talk-and-the-warm}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Thomas, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- why he needs Aurora: neither Gabi, Claire, Diana nor Sylvia can take his darkest ghosts. Condensed by Claude as \emph{I Need My Aurora} (page \pageref{sec:i-need-my-aurora}). Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 19 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft metal ballad with wide, double-tracked vocal production; bright, clear-toned male baritone, hushed and close-miked; overdriven electric guitar in sustained chords and controlled melodic bends; slow, steady ballad pulse.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Sometimes I just need to discharge, to unload\\
without their mood being part of my mental load.\\
Sometimes what I need is to talk my mind out\\
freely, unpressured, not to be worried about\\
if what I said was rightly said and wouldn't hurt,\\
but rather would help me express my deep art.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Sometimes I need both, a shrink and an escort—\\
not a lover, a partner, or a friend with a heart.\\
Or maybe I just need my pale redhead with green eyes,\\
a friendly geography between her chest and her thighs.

\songpart{Chorus}
I need my Aurora, I need her too bad—\\
my time at your practice, the prescription pad.\\
I need my Aurora, the talk and the warm,\\
the rightful uneasiness, the calm and the storm.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Gabi is my partner, my equal, my accomplice,\\
someone who challenges, unapologetic and pompless.\\
But I can't burden her with my darkest inners—\\
the terrors, the cravings, the unfinished spinners.\\
Claire won't handle my darkest and deepest ghosts,\\
or maybe I'm afraid that will be something she hosts.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Diana knows many of my secrets but won't give advice.\\
Sylvia can handle, but I can't tell what I've promised twice.\\
Although even if I could remove what's wrong and not to allude,\\
I still need to care if we are aligned in the mood.

\songpart{Chorus}
I crave my Aurora, I crave her so bad—\\
my time at your practice, the prescription pad.\\
I crave my Aurora, the talk and the warm,\\
the rightful uneasiness, the calm and the storm.

\songpart{Bridge}
It might be arousal, it might be plain lust,\\
or things in my throat that brittle my crust.\\
It might be herself, 'cause I can't lie—\\
she is just lovely and easy on the eye.

Or maybe it is just that she can understand\\
the unique wiring in my mind like the back of her hand.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I lust my Aurora, I lust her quite bad—\\
my time at your practice, the prescription pad.\\
I lust my Aurora, the talk and the warm,\\
the rightful uneasiness, the calm and the storm,\\
the norm,\\
the dorm.
\end{lyrics}
