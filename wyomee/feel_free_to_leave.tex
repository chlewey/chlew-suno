\songtitle{Feel Free to Leave}

\tag*{2026}\tag{blues}\tag{partial-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Re-genre of \emph{You Owe Me Nought}, same lyrics (chorus, pre-chorus and final chorus by Carlos Thompson; verses and bridge by Grok), as slow blues. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Blues with Hammond B-3 organ chords and restrained fills, deep male baritone delivered in a weary, close-mic style, slow 67 BPM blues pulse, roomy plate reverb.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You grumble soft that life with me\\
weighs heavy on your chest each night,\\
yet still you nod and take my hand\\
and follow where I lead the light.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You're not trapped\\
in the scheme of things;\\
you still have your wings.

\songpart{Chorus}
Feel free to leave—\\
you owe me nought.\\
Feel free to choose\\
your path, your thought.

\songpart{Verse 2}
You mutter low of chains that bind\\
and how the years have worn you thin,\\
yet every time the choice arrives\\
you keep complying, and give in.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Nobody's keeping you\\
against your will;\\
you don't owe me a bill.

\songpart{Chorus}
Feel free to leave—\\
you owe me nought.\\
Feel free to choose\\
your path, your thought.

\annotation{Break}\\
You grumble that the days with me\\
feel long.\\
You claim the weight has worn you thin\\
and wrong.\\
You mutter how you long to leave\\
this place.\\
Yet still you nod and keep the pace\\
I trace.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Feel free to leave—\\
you owe me nought.\\
Feel free to choose\\
your path, your thought.\\
Feel free to be\\
instead of to rot.
\end{lyrics}
