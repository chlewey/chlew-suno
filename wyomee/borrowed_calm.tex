\songtitle{Borrowed Calm}

\tag*{2026}\tag{neo-folk}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-guided story on Kadri Kuznetsova's account of her relationship with Thomas, story and guidance by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Estonian neo-folk and modern regilaul fusion, mid-tempo around 96 BPM, female lead vocal with clear restrained alto delivery and subtle Baltic folk ornamentation, hand percussion and frame drum, plucked kannel-like strings, bowed fiddle drones, low warm bass, sparse chamber synth texture, verses carried by repetitive additive folk phrasing, chorus opening into layered women’s call-and-response, gradual arrangement swell from exposed voice to grounded ensemble, organic close-miked production with winter air and controlled dynamics, English lyrics
\end{style}
