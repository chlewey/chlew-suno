\songtitle{Parked Outside the Classroom}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alternative-metal}\tag{original-source}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a short story extracted from Carlos Thompson's memoir \emph{A Life in World Events}, cowritten with Claude -- a math master's application that started in 2003, left unfinished outside a classroom door in 2004, and the January 2013 adult ADHD diagnosis that finally named why. Retrieved from Wyomee Dank's Suno page (V6-MINI, 26 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Candy-pop opening with bright piano, handclaps, buoyant major-key bass, and close male lead; gradually darken into a slow-burn alternative metal ballad with low-tuned guitars, restrained toms, wide sustained chords, and a cathartic final chorus. Clear narrative vocal, intimate and conversational in the verses, building to a raw but controlled tenor. Arrangement pivots from sugary, compact verse phrasing into spacious half-time weight; polished mix that grows grainier and more dynamic as the song descends.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Cuartas drew the numbers clean,\\
A different kind of proof.\\
Two thousand three, the campus hum,\\
My desk beneath the roof.\\
I left my teaching hours blank,\\
Kept one door in my mind:\\
A mathematics master's course,\\
A road I meant to find.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Then the contract reached its end,\\
No renewal, no next day.\\
I had half a plan, half of a life,\\
And let the months decide my way.

\songpart{Chorus}
I was parked outside the classroom,\\
Key turned off, my hands held still.\\
I could see the door from where I sat,\\
I knew the stairs, I knew the hill.\\
I had a name for what I wanted,\\
I had every chance to start--\\
But the engine cooled around me\\
While I stayed there in the car.

\songpart{Verse 2}
The fee came due a month in,\\
I lost that month asleep to screens.\\
Forums filled the borrowed hours;\\
I called it searching, called it means.\\
I'd reach the campus, find a space,\\
Watch students cross the yard.\\
The room was there. The work was there.\\
Getting in was hard.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I told myself I'd ask for help,\\
The student service knew my name.\\
I never crossed that waiting room.\\
I let the silence do the same.

\songpart{Chorus}
I was parked outside the classroom,\\
Key turned off, my hands held still.\\
I could see the door from where I sat,\\
I knew the stairs, I knew the hill.\\
I had a name for what I wanted,\\
I had every chance to start--\\
But the engine cooled around me\\
While I stayed there in the car.

\songpart{Bridge}
Years later, January, twenty-thirteen,\\
A careful test gave shape to years unseen.\\
Attention deficit--adult, at last,\\
A lens across the present and the past.\\
The engineering years, the dulled-out drive,\\
The distance between knowing and doing alive.\\
Not laziness, not some simple flaw--\\
A life caught in a friction I could not name before.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I was parked outside the classroom,\\
Now I know what held me there.\\
Not a lack of want or promise,\\
Not a door beyond repair.\\
I can grieve the man who couldn't\\
Make his body leave the car.\\
I can meet him without blame now,\\
Say, "I know how hard you are."

\songpart{Outro}
The classroom's still behind me.\\
The road is not erased.\\
I sit beside that younger man,\\
And let him take his place.
\end{lyrics}
