Lyrics by Grok in an invented pseudo-language, written for sound rather than meaning. \emph{Kimi sela} (page \pageref{sec:kimi-sela}) sets the same lyrics as punk-rock. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 18 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
German hip-hop trap, punchy half-time groove with crisp rolling hi-hats and deep 808 bass, tense minor-key synth motif and clipped piano accents. Intimate, rhythmically precise female rap verses; pre-chorus lifts with layered backing vocals, chorus opens into a bold gang-choir response. Bridge strips back to sparse keys and voice, breakdown uses tightly articulated syllables, then final chorus returns wider and harder. Gritty vocal texture, controlled low end, upfront vocals, polished modern mix.
\end{style}
