\songtitle{Rain Check}

\tag*{2026}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{folk}\label{sec:rain-check}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions -- a map of a hundred pins against the warm couch and the kettle: the master of the rain check, cashing one tonight. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 2 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Singer-songwriter, fingerpicked guitar in Key of D, with a slight strum lift in verse three; slow 6/8 sway; husky, smoky singer with close-mic’d, intimate verses and call-and-response backing voices in the chorus; a nearly bare bridge leaves one guitar chord suspended, while the final chorus turns softer and almost spoken.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I’ve got a map with a hundred pins,\\
Lisbon rooftops, a red-lit door,\\
a stranger’s laugh in a language I don’t speak,\\
a summer I haven’t lived before.\\
But the kettle’s on and the couch is warm,\\
and the night is calling, and I’m so tired,\\
so I tell the wanderer in my chest,\\
maybe soon, but not tonight.

\songpart{Chorus}
Oh, I want the fire, I want the fever,\\
I want the skin of the world on mine.\\
But I’m a master of the rain check,\\
a poet of the “next time.”\\
I say I’m waiting on the right moment,\\
the right shoes, the right sky,\\
and the moment comes, and I wave it by.\\
Oh, I wave it by.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I write the scene where I say yes,\\
where I cross the room and take her hand,\\
where I miss the train on purpose\\
and let the night go as it planned.\\
Then the phone lights up, it’s a quiet text,\\
you coming out? and I type I’m beat.\\
Funny how the bravest man alive\\
lives only in my sheets.

\songpart{Chorus}

\songpart{Bridge – drop to just voice and one chord}
Is it fear? Is it just being kind to myself?\\
Where’s the line between rest and a cell?\\
I built a house out of good reasons,\\
and I decorated every wall.\\
Now I’m shouting from the window\\
at a life I never called.

\songpart{Verse 3 – build, a little more strum}
So tonight I’ll leave the kettle singing,\\
leave the light on, leave the door unlocked,\\
walk out with no plan and no excuses,\\
or at least as far as the end of the block.\\
And if I turn back at the corner,\\
well, at least I saw the stars.\\
The distance from the couch to the courage\\
is only ever ours.

\songpart{Final Chorus – softer, almost spoken}
Oh, I want the fire, I want the fever,\\
I want the skin of the world on mine.\\
I might still be the king of rain checks,\\
but I’m cashing one tonight.\\
Just one tonight.
\end{lyrics}
