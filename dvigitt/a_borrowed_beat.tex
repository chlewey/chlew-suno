\songtitle{A Borrowed Beat}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sea-shanty}\tag{salsa}\label{sec:a-borrowed-beat}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a prompt written for Gemini Notebook about genre purism -- nobody here has hauled a sail, yet the old work songs found a newer dock. \emph{Make a Harbor in Your Room} (page \pageref{sec:make-a-harbor-in-your-room}) comes from the same prompt. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Synthetic folk with a brisk sea-shanty work-song pulse fused to Latin salsa clave and contemporary chamber-folk colors. Frame the verses with handclaps, frame drum, acoustic guitar and a nimble accordion; bring in piano montuno, bass, bright brass accents, and fiddle for the choruses. Keep the groove communal and danceable, with a clear mezzo-soprano lead, intimate narrative phrasing, and small group response vocals. Build from a spare, almost-work-song verse into a fuller cross-rhythmic chorus, then a contrasting reflective bridge before a buoyant final refrain. Warm, crisp production with handmade textures and an intentionally synthetic folk arrangement.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Pull together, pass the line,\\
Salt on sleeves and borrowed time.\\
No one here has hauled a sail,\\
Still we know the captain’s tale.

We learned the deck from Saturday reruns,\\
A tiny boat and comic storms;\\
A sponge in trousers, gulls that squawk—\\
Old work songs found a newer dock.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The hands may change, the tune can bend,\\
A song still helps us pull as friends.

\songpart{Chorus}
Who gets to say what folk can be?\\
A borrowed beat, a family tree.\\
New hands, old roots, a different shore—\\
We make the song, then make some more.\\
Sing it for the ones who came before,\\
And leave the door wide open for more.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A salsa band can tell the truth\\
Of hard goodbyes and squandered youth.\\
A family’s ache, a life gone wrong—\\
The dancers carry every song.

The horns don’t make the sorrow small;\\
The clave keeps time while burdens fall.\\
A crowded floor can hold a grief\\
And give a tired heart relief.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}

\songpart{Bridge}
A fiddle met a drum one day,\\
A stranger’s melody came their way.\\
They used the tools they had at hand,\\
And made a sound their town could understand.\\
No style arrived untouched or sealed;\\
Each new tradition learned to yield.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Who gets to say what folk can be?\\
A borrowed beat, a family tree.\\
New hands, old roots, a different shore—\\
We make the song, then make some more.\\
Sing it for the ones who came before,\\
And leave the door wide open for more.

\songpart{Outro}
Pull together, pass the line—\\
Your voice, my voice, a changing time.
\end{lyrics}
