\songtitle{Ero Cras}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gregorian-chant}\tag{christmas-carol}\tag{latin-lyrics}\label{sec:ero-cras}
\begin{notes}
The O Antiphons in their original Latin, with an added call, ``Veni, veni, Emmanuel'', put together by Carlos Thompson; nothing original. \emph{Come to Our Souls} (page \pageref{sec:come-to-our-souls}) adapts them into English. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dark ecclesiastical synthbass, unhurried processional pulse, androgynous high-register chant voice with sustained phrases and layered responses, dark drone layering, deep synthbass tones with slow-moving sub-bass pulses.
\end{style}
