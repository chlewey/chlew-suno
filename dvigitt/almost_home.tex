\songtitle{Almost Home}

\tag*{2026}\tag{new-age}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a list of song titles -- a borrowed night in Tallinn, the ferry clock and Viru Square in the rain: let it stay sweet, this almost-home. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
new-age synth-pop, nocturnal and introspective, steady midtempo pulse with rounded sequenced bass and glassy analog pads; begin with a close, breath-soft mezzo-soprano over sparse piano and distant bell tones, add crisp restrained electronic percussion and warm low synth through the verses, open into a wide melodic chorus with subtle wordless backing harmonies, then strip back to piano and voice for a reflective bridge before a final fuller chorus; polished spacious mix, clear intimate lead vocal
\end{style}
