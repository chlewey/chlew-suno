\songtitle{Let You Sit With Me}

\tag*{2026}\tag{bedroom-pop}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{original-source}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a 2026 reflection by Carlos Thompson -- knowing what needs doing and not being able to start, and asking someone just to sit nearby. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Intimate bedroom singer-songwriter, close soft mezzo-soprano vocal with natural conversational phrasing, fingerpicked acoustic guitar and restrained felt piano, subtle room noise and warm tape texture. Start spare and confessional, let a gentle bass pulse and brushed percussion enter gradually, open the chorus with a little more harmony, then strip back to voice and guitar for the unresolved final lines. Human, understated mix with audible breath and no glossy polish.
\end{style}
