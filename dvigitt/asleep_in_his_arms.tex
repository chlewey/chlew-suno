\begin{notes}
Written by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Sylvia Lai from \emph{Thomas' Entourage} — the security of falling asleep in Thomas's arms, plainly not love, and her easy, unnamed complicity with the other woman who shares him. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 27 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dubstep, synthetic-textured female vocals with close, breathy build phrases and chopped, gated drop hooks; heavy sub-bass pulses and distorted bass drops; dense soundwall mix with hard-edged synth layers; half-time, lurching groove.
\end{style}
