\songtitle{Too High to Say}

\tag*{2026}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:too-high-to-say}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on \emph{Christmas 2024} and \emph{Full House} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- the full house for Christmas, the fireworks and the tamales on his shoulders, Jairo, Claire and Pilar catching what he lets fall: hold the ladder till I come down. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft metal power ballad: clean arpeggiated guitar, piano, warm bass, swelling pad and light cymbal roll build into distorted guitars, big drums and a melodic soaring solo. Bright, clear-toned male baritone croons in legato phrases; wide stacked chorus harmonies. Around 72 BPM, intimate verses rising to a full, powerful chorus, with the final chorus shifting up a whole step. Polished, spacious mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1 (clean guitar and piano, intimate, almost spoken)}
Morning in the cabin, laptop glowing,\\
Calling it work while I’m drawing the sky.\\
Burying highways, rebuilding a city,\\
A world’s worth of ideas behind my eyes.\\
A desert, a port, a promise to the clans,\\
A dozen dreams too big for my hands.

\songpart{Verse 2 (bass and brushed drums enter)}
Full house for Christmas, the whole family landing,\\
Planes in the dark and a hundred loose ends.\\
I promised the fireworks, the tamales, the surprise,\\
And I’m awake all night over currents and wind.\\
Nobody asked me to carry it all,\\
But I never learned how to want it small.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus (pad swells, cymbal roll builds, voice rising)}
And I don’t know how to tell you when it’s heavy,\\
I’m good at drawing maps, not at saying where it hurts.\\
So if I’m quiet, if I’m smiling too long,\\
Look closer. Look closer.

\songpart{Chorus (full band, huge open chords, stacked harmonies)}
When I reach too far, and I will,\\
When the sky I’m building is too tall to climb,\\
Don’t wait for me to say the words,\\
Just see me, just see me in time.\\
I’ll keep on dreaming, I’ll keep on chasing,\\
It’s the only way I know to feel alive,\\
But when I’m over the edge of what I can hold,\\
Hold the ladder till I come down.

\songpart{Verse 3 (drop back to clean guitar, tender)}
Jairo took the cargo before I had to ask,\\
Claire on the ground making sure there was air.\\
Pilar reads me like weather, never missing a sign,\\
And the staff cooked my grandmother’s recipe with care.\\
And someone sat beside me, saying nothing at all,\\
Just there in the quiet while I drifted off the wall.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus 2 (same build, more urgent, heavier drums)}
And I walk past the places where the ground remembers,\\
Don’t know what the chill means, just know where it sits.\\
Every dream has a price somewhere on the map,\\
And I’m learning what it costs me, bit by bit.

\songpart{Chorus (repeat, with a guitar countermelody)}
When I reach too far, and I will,\\
When the sky I’m building is too tall to climb,\\
Don’t wait for me to say the words,\\
Just see me, just see me in time.\\
I’ll keep on dreaming, I’ll keep on chasing,\\
It’s the only way I know to feel alive,\\
But when I’m over the edge of what I can hold,\\
Hold the ladder till I come down.

\songpart{Bridge (music drops to voice and a single sustained guitar note, then builds)}
Maybe I’m not as invisible as I think,\\
Maybe the quiet is louder than I know.\\
Maybe the people who love me have been reading\\
All the things I never let show.

\annotation{Guitar Solo (melodic, soaring, over the chorus chords; let it sing rather than shred)}

\songpart{Final Chorus (key change up a whole step, full band, gang vocals on the last lines)}
When I reach too far, and I will,\\
Thank you for the hands that catch what I let fall.\\
I’ll keep on dreaming, I’ll keep on chasing,\\
But I’ll learn to say it when I can’t hold it all.
(slow down, clean guitar alone, voice soft)\\
Hold the ladder. Hold the ladder till I come down.
\end{lyrics}
