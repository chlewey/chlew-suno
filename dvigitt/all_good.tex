\songtitle{All Good}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{claire-jones}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on Claire Jones's monologue in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Electronic pasodoble at a brisk, march-like 124 BPM: proud minor-key brass fanfares, clipped nylon-string guitar figures, castanets, snare-led two-step rhythm, deep modern synth bass, and precise club percussion, Begin with sparse aircraft-cabin ambience implied through filtered pads, then build into sharply accented verses; make the chorus broad and ceremonial with stacked female vocals, octave doubles, and answering harmonies, Use intimate, clear lead singing in the verses, confident ensemble vocals in the refrain, dramatic pauses before cadences, and a polished wide mix with tight low end, dry rhythmic transients, and restrained plate reverb, The emotional arc moves from completed duty and exhaustion toward composed self-possession in a warm Barcelona morning
\end{style}
