\songtitle{Stars Keep Watch}

\tag*{2026}\tag{doo-wop}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:stars-keep-watch}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno following specific instructions -- a Spanish-style Christmas carol about the little shepherds carried into English: the shepherd children at the manger, a blanket, a sprig of green, and a song beside the bed. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 7 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Children’s doo-wop Christmas carol, jaunty slow-swing groove with a gentle walking upright bass, brushed snare and handclaps, plucky piano, and soft guitar. Bright small-group backing harmonies answer a clear, friendly lead vocal with simple ba-ba syllables between lines. Keep the verses story-forward and intimate; open the chorus into an easy singalong, then finish with a sweet close-harmony tag. Vintage, cozy, clean production, light and tender rather than grand.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
Ba-doo, ba-doo, ba-doo-wah\\
Come along, come along

\songpart{Verse 1}
Little shepherd children heard the angels call,\\
Gathered up their cloaks and hurried, one and all.\\
Past the sleepy hillside, through the quiet night,\\
They found a manger filled with gentle light.

\songpart{Chorus}
Sleep, little baby, safe and warm,\\
While the stars keep watch above the farm.\\
Ba-doo, ba-doo, we’re here to see,\\
The tiny child in the manger, peacefully.

\songpart{Verse 2}
One brought a woolly blanket, one a sprig of green,\\
One just stood there smiling at the sweetest scene.\\
Mary softly thanked them; Joseph bowed his head,\\
And the little shepherds sang beside the bed.

\songpart{Chorus}
Sleep, little baby, safe and warm,\\
While the stars keep watch above the farm.\\
Ba-doo, ba-doo, we’re here to see,\\
The tiny child in the manger, peacefully.

\songpart{Bridge}
No grand palace, no golden throne,\\
Just a quiet stable, love enough for everyone.\\
Small hands folded, kind hearts near,\\
They welcomed the baby with a song of cheer.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Sleep, little baby, safe and warm,\\
While the stars keep watch above the farm.\\
Ba-doo, ba-doo, our voices agree,\\
Welcome, dear baby, we’re glad you’re here with me.

\songpart{Outro}
Ba-doo, ba-doo, ba-doo-wah,\\
Rest now, little one, la-da-da.
\end{lyrics}
