\songtitle{The Door Left Open}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-ballad}\label{sec:the-door-left-open}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno following specific instructions -- a feral dog setting out to win over a grumpy old man who keeps his door shut, until the door is left open. \emph{Two Hard Hearts} (page \pageref{sec:two-hard-hearts}) comes from the same prompt. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 1 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic folk ballad with a slow, deliberate pulse; intimate piano and low cello open sparsely, then dulcimer, bowed strings, and restrained frame-drum accents gradually build beneath a warm, expressive solo vocal. Clear narrative verses, a memorable rising chorus, and a softened final refrain as the man gives in; close natural vocal, earthy acoustic textures, dynamic but uncluttered mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You came through the pines with a burr in your coat,\\
One torn ear folded, one paw held afloat.\\
I kept my door shut; I’d seen strays before,\\
You curled by the woodpile and slept by the door.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I said, “You’ll get nothing. Move on from my land.”\\
You laid your chin softly beside my boot hand.

\songpart{Chorus}
You were a wild thing with a brave little heart,\\
I was an old fool who’d mastered the art\\
Of keeping the kettle, the curtains, the key—\\
Then you left muddy pawprints where no one could see.\\
You asked for no promise, no name, no embrace;\\
You just kept on choosing the same little place.

\songpart{Verse 2}
At dawn you stole bacon, then guarded my chair,\\
You chased off a crow and left feathers everywhere.\\
I grumbled at breakfast; you thumped your rough tail.\\
You walked at my heel down the frost-bitten trail.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I told you, “Don’t follow. I’m fine on my own.”\\
You waited all evening beside the cold stone.

\songpart{Chorus}
You were a wild thing with a brave little heart,\\
I was an old fool who’d mastered the art\\
Of keeping the kettle, the curtains, the key—\\
Then you left muddy pawprints where no one could see.\\
You asked for no promise, no name, no embrace;\\
You just kept on choosing the same little place.

\songpart{Bridge}
That winter the creek swallowed half of the track,\\
I slipped on the bank and couldn’t climb back.\\
You barked till the pines sent my neighbor awake,\\
Then pressed your warm side to my shivering shape.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You were a wild thing with a brave little heart,\\
You found the locked room and unfastened its parts.\\
Now two bowls sit beside the old stove each night,\\
Your paws by the bed, your coat in the light.\\
I still say “Get down” when you claim my chair—\\
You sigh, settle in, like you know I don’t care.

\songpart{Outro}
The wind worries the pines; the kettle sings low.\\
I leave the door open. You already know.
\end{lyrics}
