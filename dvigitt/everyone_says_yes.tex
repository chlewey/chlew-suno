\songtitle{Everyone Says Yes}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alt-rock}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:everyone-says-yes}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on \emph{Christmas 2024} and \emph{Full House} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- everyone says yes to the man who pays: is it love or manners, and what if the quiet was never agreement? Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 12 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Alt-rock / pop-rock, palm-muted guitar riff, tight drums and synth pulse; driving 120 BPM in A major, around 3:20; male vocals with a wide range, gritty and strained as they climb into the top notes; bridge drops to voice and one guitar, then the final chorus lifts a whole step with gang vocals.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Engines humming on the strip,\\
Every light is on for me.\\
Bags are carried, doors held wide,\\
Nobody asks what I need.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And I read your smile from across the room,\\
Never ask you out loud.

\songpart{Chorus}
’Cause everyone says yes to the man who pays,\\
Everyone smiles when I walk in.\\
Is it love, or is it manners?\\
I can’t tell where they end.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I hand out plans I didn’t draw,\\
Call it family, call it mentoring.\\
Who gets to say no to the boss?\\
Who’s brave enough to be the first?

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And I count the room by who’s still smiling,\\
Never hear what’s underneath.

\songpart{Chorus}
’Cause everyone says yes to the man who pays,\\
Everyone smiles when I walk in.\\
Is it love, or is it manners?\\
I can’t tell where they end.

\songpart{Bridge (drums out, voice and one guitar)}
What if the quiet was never agreement?\\
What if “no problem” meant “keep the peace”?

\songpart{Final Chorus (key up a whole step, gang vocals)}
Everyone says yes to the man who pays,\\
So I’ll ask, and I’ll listen this time.\\
Say it again, say what you mean,\\
Say it again, and I’ll hear you.
\end{lyrics}
