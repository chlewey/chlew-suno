\songtitle{Rosario House}

\tag*{2023}\tag{ricochets}\tag{john-calrson}\tag{lida-andre}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a character description of John Carlson and Lida André %
from the Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
2000s rock ballad with mid-tempo pulse, ringing electric guitars and steady tom-heavy drums; verse leans intimate and restrained, pre-chorus climbs with bass and open hi-hats, chorus opens wide with stacked harmonies and bent-note lead guitar, Gravelly male lead with soft female harmony on key lines, double-tracked choruses, delay throws on the last word of each hook, subtle crowd-like gang oohs in the final refrain, Tape hiss, reverse swell into the chorus, and a guitar harmonica-like fill between sections; warm, glossy, emotional mix with a big radio-ready center, warm, ballad, electronic, rock, piercing, light, smooth, natural, lilith
\end{style}
