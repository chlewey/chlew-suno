\songtitle{Vuelvo al sillón}

\tag*{2026}\tag{milonga-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{susana-bianchi}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on Susana Bianchi's monologue in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Milonga-rock with driving pulso criollo and electric guitar grit; verse rides guitarrón, handclaps, and brushed snare, pre-chorus narrows to voice plus bass pulse, chorus opens with bandoneón stabs and distorted guitars in a stomping milonga groove, Female Argentine vocals, intimate and dry in verses, doubled on hooks with shouted backing lines, Ear candy: reverse cymbal into choruses, vinyl crackle under the intro, a sharp guitar harmonic before the last refrain, Bright, punchy, earthy mix with arena-sized edges, rock
\end{style}
