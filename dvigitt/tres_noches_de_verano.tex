\songtitle{Tres noches de verano}

\tag*{2026}\tag{house}\tag{dubstep}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{spanish-lyrics}\label{sec:tres-noches-de-verano}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- three summer nights by the pool, three autumn days by the lake, three winter afternoons at home and three spring mornings in the garden, and nothing changes: still waiting for love. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 November 2026).
% TODO: year of the original lyrics unverified (tagged 2026)
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
House electronica, nasal, reedy mezzo-soprano vocals in a half-spoken talk-sing, then sustained chorus notes; spacious snare reverb and clean low end; steady four-on-the-floor pulse; shuffled closed hi-hats, pulsing synth bass, and dubstep drops with growling sub-bass and half-time snares.
\end{style}
