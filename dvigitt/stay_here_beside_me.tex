\songtitle{Stay Here Beside Me}

\tag*{2026}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\label{sec:stay-here-beside-me}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, as a duet between Thomas and Aurora -- a newly named relationship, the well-meant plans around it, and the wish just to be held until morning. \emph{Night-long Embrace} (page \pageref{sec:night-long-embrace}) sets the same night. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft-rock power ballad, intimate piano opening with restrained clean electric guitar and warm bass, steady live drums entering gradually, soaring but controlled chorus with broad electric-guitar sustain and subtle string pads. Male and female duet trading reflective verses, then close harmonies in the chorus; conversational phrasing, emotional restraint building into a heartfelt climax, polished organic studio mix.
\end{style}
