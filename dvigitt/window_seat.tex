\songtitle{Window Seat}

\tag*{2026}\tag{blues-rock}\label{sec:window-seat}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude, written in an attempt to mimic Carlos Thompson's voice and songwriting style following specific prompts about the overall subject: a puppy who wants to grow up to be the grumpy cat in the window. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Rock, lazy shuffle; overdubbed handclaps and warm, slightly rounded studio mix; warm baritone in a powerful full-voice belt, doubled on the hooks; one yawning slide guitar with relaxed blues bends, supported by a kid-choir chorus.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\annotation{Spoken intro}\\
There is a puppy.\\
There is a window.\\
There is a cat in the window.\\
Everything else is a detail.

\songpart{Verse 1}
The puppy runs the hallway twice before breakfast.\\
He greets the mailman like a long-lost brother.\\
The cat on the sill has not moved since Tuesday.\\
The puppy decides: that one has it figured out.

\songpart{Chorus}
When I grow up, I want to be grumpy.\\
When I grow up, I want to be stripes in the sun.\\
Not running, not wagging, not asking for anything,\\
just warm, and a little bit done.

\songpart{Verse 2}
He studies the cat the way I studied maps:\\
chin on paws, ears forward, taking notes.\\
Case one: the cat ignores the ball. Respect.\\
Case two: the cat ignores the dog. Respect.\\
Case three: the cat ignores the puppy.\\
That one is personal, but still.

\songpart{Chorus}
When I grow up, I want to be grumpy.\\
When I grow up, I want to be stripes in the sun.\\
Not jumping, not begging, not loving the vacuum,\\
just warm, and a little bit done.

\songpart{Verse 3}
He practices at night: one eye half open,\\
a sigh like someone else's tax return.\\
He lasts eleven seconds. Then the mailman comes.\\
He is out the door, and he is so glad.

\songpart{Bridge, spoken}
The cat watches him go.\\
Something moves in the tail. Possibly nothing.\\
Possibly a little envy\\
for a creature who can want that much.

\songpart{Chorus}
When I grow up, I want to be grumpy.\\
When I grow up, I want to be stripes in the sun.\\
Not sure I'll manage, but I'll tell you tomorrow,\\
after the mailman. After the run.

\songpart{Outro, instruments out}
The window is warm.\\
The puppy is asleep on the floor beneath it.\\
That counts.
\end{lyrics}
