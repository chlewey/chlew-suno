\songtitle{The Dream Stayed Out of Reach}

\tag*{2026}\tag{chamber-pop}\tag{saulon}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a substory of the novel draft \emph{Saulon} by Carlos Thompson (a fictional universe of his), extracted and translated by Claude -- Abdul wakes after fifteen years asleep, missing small pieces. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 12 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Theatrical chamber pop with a dramatic, expressive lyrical tenor lead; intimate piano and bowed strings in the verses, measured pizzicato pulse, swelling cello and violin harmonies into a broad, emotionally controlled chorus. Let the arrangement thin to piano and low strings before a sharply articulated, rhythmically nimble rap bridge, then return to full chamber-pop vocals for a restrained, unresolved ending. Clear diction, dynamic phrasing, organic acoustic ensemble, polished close vocal and natural room depth.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Abdul was twenty-seven when they closed the silver door,\\
Fifteen years beneath the hush, then footsteps on the floor.\\
He rose a little weary, like a night of broken sleep,\\
The bed he left was yesterday; the years were folded deep.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
His bones had settled; every measure said he’d barely changed,\\
One year for fifteen sleeping years—the numbers neatly ranged.\\
He knew the room he’d entered, knew the moment, knew the bed,\\
A dream remained behind him, with no picture in his head.

\songpart{Chorus}
What slips away when time stands still?\\
What comes back altered, what survives the chill?\\
A name, a word, a mark, a line—\\
Small missing pieces, measured one by one in time.

\songpart{Verse 2}
They asked about his mother; her name would not return.\\
“Wardrobe” held no furniture, no meaning he could learn.\\
An equation seemed uncertain; Arabic letters blurred,\\
Though once he’d read them fluently, each shape a familiar word.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The rest was there before them, as it had been before:\\
His history, sums, and movements matched the record’s score.\\
They called the scattered losses ordinary, small, precise;\\
He learned most things again with little trouble or advice.

\songpart{Chorus}
What slips away when time stands still?\\
What comes back altered, what survives the chill?\\
A name, a word, a mark, a line—\\
Small missing pieces, measured one by one in time.

\songpart{Bridge}
Fifteen years in suspension, but duration’s not the key;\\
The wound may live in leaving, in the return to memory.\\
The report says risk is tolerable—millions wait in store,\\
Five thousand years ahead, if they don’t wake them more.\\
No verdict on Abdul, no final page to show;\\
Just two lost doors inside the mind, and what the planners know.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
What slips away when time stands still?\\
What comes back altered, what survives the chill?\\
A mother’s name, a word he’ll learn,\\
A script once clear, now slow to turn.\\
No answer written, no account of where he’ll go—\\
The capsule shuts; the record leaves the question open.

\songpart{Outro}
He woke as if from restless sleep.\\
The dream stayed out of reach.
\end{lyrics}
