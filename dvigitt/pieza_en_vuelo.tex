\tag*{2026}\tag{milonga-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{susana-bianchi}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on Susana Bianchi's monologue in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Milonga rock with driving bombo legüero, nylon guitar and overdriven electric guitar accents; verse rides sparse and tense, pre-chorus opens with bass and handclaps, chorus lands wide with stacked female vocals and sharp gang responses, A smoky female Argentine lead stays close-mic in verses, pushed with doubled grit on hooks, Metal pick scrapes, reversed swells into lifts, and a final rhythmic stop before the last refrain, Bright, raw, and punchy mix, simple, rock
\end{style}
