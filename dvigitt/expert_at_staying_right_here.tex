\songtitle{Expert at Staying Right Here}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a ChatGPT summary of \emph{Too Smart for Your Own Good} (page \pageref{sec:too-smart-for-your-own-good}) -- color-coded charts of reasons to postpone, and excellent notes with no follow-through. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Intimate electronic pop with a restrained mezzo-soprano lead, dry conversational verses over clipped synth pulses and a steady club kick; build the pre-chorus with rising arpeggios and close harmonies, then open into a catchy, bittersweet chorus with rounded trance bass, crisp drums, and subtle distorted-guitar accents. A brief spoken-feeling bridge should turn the joke inward before the final chorus; polished but human, with clear vocals and sly dynamic contrast.
\end{style}
