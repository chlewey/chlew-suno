\songtitle{Manifest Fault}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag*{english-lyrics}\tag{buscando-culpables}
\begin{notes}
English-language adaptation by Claude of \emph{Buscando culpables}, by Carlos Thompson. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 20 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Singer-songwriter folk-rock with fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed acoustic drums, upright bass and restrained mandolin, plate reverb on vocals, male baritone lead vocals with plainspoken verses and fuller doubled choruses, steady mid-tempo 4/4 sway
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I hear myself blame you for it,\\
for where I've come to be.\\
Years spent living in the shadow\\
of who I'm not, not me.

A fragile teenage ego wound\\
that hasn't gone away,\\
it's not fair placing blame on you\\
for who I am today.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
You owed me nothing back then, true —\\
that much I've come to see.\\
What happened was a thing I chose,\\
a road that came from me.

Fixing on you, I thought the world\\
just worked the way it shows,\\
not stopping once to think what it\\
could mean to you, who knows.

\songpart{Chorus}
Talking about the past, I look\\
for someone else to blame,\\
how easy it becomes to point\\
a finger, just the same.

To blame you, or myself, or blame\\
a fate that wrote the plan,\\
a destiny that left me raw,\\
a vulnerable man.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I blame myself for thinking life\\
was simple as can be,\\
that saying wishes out loud, pulled\\
from pockets, wild and free,

would spark some chaos, or a dream,\\
or end the world outright,\\
but I'd endure — invincible,\\
my manifest birthright.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
It wasn't just the no that left\\
a hollow spot behind,\\
it was my own obsessive edge,\\
the spiral in my mind.

Knowing full well my actions caused\\
a hurt I couldn't face,\\
still hiding from myself the truth,\\
erasing any trace.

\songpart{Chorus}
Talking about the past, I look\\
for someone else to blame,\\
how easy it becomes to point\\
a finger, just the same.

To blame you, or myself, or blame\\
a fate that wrote the plan,\\
a destiny that left me raw,\\
a vulnerable man.

\songpart{Breakdown}
Sometimes I think I had no way\\
to know, to see it plain,\\
that circumstance alone would lead\\
me straight into that pain.

What happened left a wound so deep\\
it never can repair,\\
a hurt that lingers to this day,\\
a scar I still lay bare.

It's not your fault, you owed me not\\
a single thing at all.\\
It's not my fault to feel unsure,\\
not knowing how to call.

You don't find healing hunting down\\
the ones that we can blame,\\
it's only living every day\\
and waking up the same.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Talking about the past, I try\\
to understand it all,\\
how easy it becomes to blame,\\
whether you speak or stall.

To blame you, or myself, or blame\\
a fate that wrote the plan,\\
a destiny that left me raw,\\
a vulnerable man.

An empty fate can't bear the blame,\\
it holds no guilt at all.\\
To blame is just to miss the point,\\
to miss the lesson's call.
\end{lyrics}
