\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the same DeepSeek summary of five songs behind \emph{Five Rooms, One Key} -- \emph{Kvar i mig}, \emph{Mouth of the Machine}, \emph{Twenty Fourteen Scripts}, \emph{Between Worlds}, and \emph{Å finne noen andre å bebreide} -- reworked into a funk arrangement rather than a strict translation. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Funk; crisp syncopated bass, clipped rhythm guitar, dry electric piano, tight horn stabs, hand percussion and punchy drums; clear intimate female lead with sparse call-and-response verses, rising horn pre-choruses and stacked backing vocals; danceable midtempo groove; warm analog saturation, restrained room ambience, full-band bass-and-vocal bridge return
\end{style}
