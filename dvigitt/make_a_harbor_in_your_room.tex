\songtitle{Make a Harbor in Your Room}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sea-shanty}\tag{salsa}\label{sec:make-a-harbor-in-your-room}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a prompt written for Gemini Notebook about genre purism -- a work song learned from reruns and cartoons, still sung in a kitchen: pass it on. \emph{A Borrowed Beat} (page \pageref{sec:a-borrowed-beat}) comes from the same prompt. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Synthetic folk with a brisk, stomping work-song pulse, sailor-style call-and-response reframed through modern folk storytelling; acoustic strummed strings, handclaps, boot-stomp percussion, a bright squeezebox, and subtly synthetic bass and crisp electronic textures. Intimate mezzo-soprano lead with a wry, conversational delivery; small communal backing voices answer key phrases. Move from a spare, buoyant verse into a broad, chantable chorus, then a contrasting dramatic verse with a salsa-like rhythmic lift before a final full-ensemble chorus. Warm, handmade performance blended with clean contemporary production; playful and thoughtful, never parody.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Haul the rope, the captain cried,\\
Six hands pulled on the rising tide.\\
A shanty kept the hauling straight,\\
One voice called, the crew replied.

Now we know that wooden deck\\
From a rerun and a cartoon wreck;\\
Still we sing the working song\\
With a paper hat and a kitchen sink.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
We didn’t cross that ocean wide,\\
But we know how voices pull in time.

\songpart{Chorus}
Pass it on, pass it around,\\
New hands find the old song’s sound.\\
Change the tools, change the tune,\\
Make a harbor in your room.\\
No one owns the wind or sea—\\
Songs keep changing; let them be.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A salsa band can turn a tale\\
Of family, heartbreak, and a long night’s trail;\\
You can dance and still feel every word,\\
The brass keeps bright while the heart gets heard.

A borrowed step, a local string,\\
A drum made from whatever’s near;\\
That’s how yesterday’s strange new blend\\
Becomes the song your neighbors sing.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Every border lets a rhythm through,\\
Every meeting makes a something new.

\songpart{Chorus}
Pass it on, pass it around,\\
New hands find the old song’s sound.\\
Change the tools, change the tune,\\
Make a harbor in your room.\\
No one owns the wind or sea—\\
Songs keep changing; let them be.

\songpart{Bridge}
Who gets to say the song is pure?\\
The hands that made it crossed a shore.\\
A borrowed beat, a different tongue—\\
The next old song has just begun.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Pass it on, pass it around,\\
New hands find the old song’s sound.\\
Change the tools, change the tune,\\
Make a harbor in your room.\\
No one owns the wind or sea—\\
Songs keep changing; let them be.\\
Let them be, let them be—\\
Songs keep changing; let them be.

\songpart{Outro}
Six hands pulling, one voice calling,\\
New songs rise where old ones meet.
\end{lyrics}
