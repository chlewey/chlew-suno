\songtitle{El Air Jaguar}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}{susana-bianchi}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on Susana Bianchi's monologue in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Milonga rock with Argentine female vocals, sharp guitarrón pulse and driving electric guitar; verse rides dry and conversational over hand percussion, pre-chorus narrows to voice and claps, chorus opens with gritty gang doubles and a chantable refrain, Breathy low verses, firm chest-voice leads, small echo throws on key phrases, slide-guitar swells and a snare lift into the hook, Bright, punchy, smoky mix with live-room edge, rock, punta
\end{style}

El Air Jaguar es mío.\\

Thomas tiene los papeles,\\
pero yo sé la verdad.\\
Conozco cada arreglo,\\
cada mano al reparar,\\
cada técnico que dejó\\
su huella en el hangar.
