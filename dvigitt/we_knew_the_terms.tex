\songtitle{We Knew the Terms}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a section of Xumi's memoir, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- meeting Thomas in a London pub, and the friendship that followed. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Adult contemporary pop with a relaxed reggae groove and polished late-2010s production. Midtempo one-drop rhythm, tight rim-click and warm rounded bass, clean offbeat electric guitar, restrained keys, and subtle atmospheric pads. Verses stay conversational and intimate, opening into a memorable, emotionally assured chorus; add light harmony layers in the final chorus and a neat, understated outro. Female lead vocal with a natural British accent, clear diction, warm and poised delivery. Clean radio-ready mix with organic groove and controlled low end.
\end{style}
