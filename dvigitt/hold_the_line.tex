\songtitle{Hold the Line}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk}\tag{political}\tag{pass-the-bread}
\begin{notes}
Written by Suno after a prompt by Carlos Thompson -- a folk resistance anthem against tribal sorting, purity tests, and manufactured outrage, calling for solidarity across the lines a media/political machine draws between neighbors. Companion piece to \emph{Pass the Bread}. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
folk resistance anthem, driving acoustic guitar strum, foot-stomp percussion, warm bass, restrained electric guitar swells, communal gang harmonies, earnest lead vocal, dynamic verse-to-chorus lift, raw room energy, memorable singalong hook, grounded roots-rock production
\end{style}
