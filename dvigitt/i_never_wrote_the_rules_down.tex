\songtitle{I Never Wrote the Rules Down}

\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{punk-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{xumi}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a section of Xumi's memoir, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the unwritten rules: closeness without claim. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Late-2010s adult contemporary with a punchy punk-rock groove, female lead vocal with conversational verses and a big melodic chorus. Tight live-kit drums with brisk snare, melodic electric bass, bright crunchy rhythm guitars, and restrained piano accents. Keep verses close and candid, lift into wide harmonies and fuller guitars for the chorus, then strip back for a reflective bridge before a concise final chorus. Polished modern production with energetic dynamics and clear vocal focus.
\end{style}
