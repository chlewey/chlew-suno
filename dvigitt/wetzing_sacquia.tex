\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson -- a conlang chant (Wetzing, Sacquia, Dymbali, Vorkhan, Xujafo) built as a trance and dubstep anthem. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 4 October 2026).

\begin{style}
Trance and dubstep anthem, driving four-on-the-floor pulse with half-time drop sections; male falsetto lead with layered harmonies; polished, punchy festival-scale build and release; heavy growling sub-bass drops, wide supersaw chords, rising filtered arpeggios.
