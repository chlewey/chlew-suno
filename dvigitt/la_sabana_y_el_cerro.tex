\songtitle{La sabana y el cerro}

\tag*{2026}\tag{milonga-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{susana-bianchi}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on Susana Bianchi's monologue in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Milonga-rock with driving guitar strums, upright bass, and a dry bombo pulse; verse rides intimate and story-forward, pre-chorus opens with tighter handclaps and rising backing vowels, chorus lands on a chantable half-time hook with gritty electric accents, Female Argentine vocals, close-mic and smoky in verses, double-tracked on the hook, with short echo throws on key phrases, Ear candy: reverse guitar swells into each chorus, a bandoneón stab in the turnaround, and a cymbal swell before the final lift, Mix is warm, earthy, and punchy with a live-room edge, rock, punta
\end{style}

Y después está lo otro,\\

Bogotá tiene la Sabana,\\

Yo lo vi desde el aire\\
durante un año entero.

Lucy me llevó temprano,\\
la hermana de Thomas,\\
con sus botas con historia\\
y mi zapatilla en guerra.

Caminamos cuatro horas,\\
yo sin mapa en la piel,\\
ella sabiendo de memoria\\
cada piedra del sendero.
