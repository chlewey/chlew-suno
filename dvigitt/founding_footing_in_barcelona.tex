\songtitle{Founding Footing in Barcelona}

\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{bossa-nova}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{xumi}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a section of Xumi's memoir, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the freelance start in Barcelona in 2021. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Adult contemporary bossa nova with a relaxed Brazilian sway and polished 2020s production. Nylon-string guitar and understated syncopated percussion establish the groove; warm electric piano, rounded bass, and restrained brushed drums fill out the verses. Intimate female lead with a natural British accent, clear diction, and a measured, reflective delivery; lift into a memorable, graceful chorus with soft harmony layers. Keep the arrangement elegant and spacious, adding subtle strings in the final chorus and resolving with a gentle guitar-led outro.
\end{style}
