\songtitle{Autumn in Barcelona}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dream-pop}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{xumi}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on an excerpt from Xumi's memoir, from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson -- Barcelona dinners in the autumn of 2022, a relationship that runs to 2024 and ends plainly, and the life that fits her, with Recife, London and São Luís still calling. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
British dream pop, adult contemporary, measured mid-tempo sway with soft trip-hop pulse, intimate female lead vocal with clear conversational phrasing, flamenco nylon-string guitar solo in instrumental bridges, gang-choir echoes answering the lead in each chorus, verses kept spare with rounded bass and hazy electric guitar, choruses widening into layered harmonies, polished warm mix with gentle tape softness and spacious vocal ambience
\end{style}
