\songtitle{Smoke and Steel}

\tag*{2023}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{ricochets}\tag{fabian-muller}\tag{sophie-durand}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a character description of Fabián Müller and Sophie Durand %
from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Early-2000s alternative rock power ballad, midtempo 4/4 groove around 78 BPM, begins with intimate clean electric guitar and restrained piano, then expands into wide distorted guitars, live drums, melodic bass, and a soaring final chorus, Male lead vocal with a weathered but controlled lower register in the verses, rising into urgent sustained notes; female answering vocal enters in the pre-chorus and joins the final refrain, Use dynamic verse-to-chorus lifts, a brief guitar lead after the second chorus, tight punchy drums, glossy radio-rock compression, and a bittersweet cinematic mix, Keep the romance conflicted and the storytelling specific
\end{style}
