\songtitle{Two Hard Hearts}

\tag*{2026}\tag{industrial}\tag{folk-ballad}\label{sec:two-hard-hearts}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno following specific instructions, from the same prompt as \emph{The Door Left Open} (page \pageref{sec:the-door-left-open}) -- a feral dog and a grumpy old man holding their ground through a winter, until neither stands alone. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 1 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic industrial folk ballad with a very slow, deliberate pulse; metallic percussion and deep sustained bass under intimate piano, low strings, and restrained hammered dulcimer. Begin spare and wary, let a warm melodic refrain emerge as the dog earns trust, then build to a powerful but controlled emotional peak before a quiet resolved ending. Expressive mezzo-soprano lead, close and clear, with subtle low choral texture; raw, spacious production.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
You kept a chair against the door\\
And salt beside the stove\\
The winter worried at the boards\\
The pines bent under snow\\
Then came a scrape beneath the porch\\
A shape with burrs and scars\\
You raised your voice; it showed its teeth\\
Then vanished past the yard

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But come the dawn, there by the step\\
A muddy pawprint stayed\\
And half a heel of bread was gone\\
Though nothing had been paid

\songpart{Chorus}
You said, “Go on, I need no friend”\\
You said it to the wind\\
Still every night you left the latch\\
A finger’s width again\\
The dog would wait beyond the wood\\
Too proud to beg or run\\
Two hard hearts held their ground awhile\\
Till one of them gave in

\songpart{Verse 2}
You found it shivering by the shed\\
With wire around its leg\\
You cut the rusted snare away\\
It flinched, but did not beg\\
You set a bowl beside the stove\\
Then turned your face aside\\
It ate, and slept beneath your bench\\
While you kept watch all night

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Next day it stole your woolen glove\\
And buried it in snow\\
You cursed, then heard a thumping tail\\
And had nowhere to go

\songpart{Chorus}
You said, “Go on, I need no friend”\\
You said it less than then\\
Still every night you left the latch\\
Wide enough to let it in\\
The dog would wait beside your boots\\
And guard the door till dawn\\
Two hard hearts held their ground awhile\\
Till neither stood alone

\songpart{Bridge}
When fever pinned you to the bed\\
It brought your fallen shawl\\
It pressed its ribs against your hand\\
And answered every call\\
You woke to find the fire gone low\\
Its muzzle near your cheek\\
You touched the scar above its eye\\
And heard it breathe, not speak

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You said, “Stay close; the storm is bad”\\
As if that were the reason\\
It curled beside your chair at last\\
And made the cabin even\\
You set two bowls beside the stove\\
Two shadows by the flame\\
The man who swore he’d live alone\\
Was glad it knew his name

\songpart{Outro}
The wind still combs the pines at night\\
The old boards creak and groan\\
But now one paw keeps time beside\\
The steps that lead you home
\end{lyrics}
