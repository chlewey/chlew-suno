\songtitle{Call Me Xumi}

\tag*{2026}\tag{reggae}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{camila-alexander}\tag{xumi}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Xumi's memoir by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe. Character: Camila Eugenia Santos Alexander, Xumi -- born in São Luís in 1989, between a father from Birmingham and a Mineira mother. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 5 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Maranhão reggae blended with adult contemporary pop; organic, polished studio mix with spacious vocals and gentle dynamics; relaxed one-drop sway, warm rounded bass, crisp rim-click and shaker, clipped offbeat guitar, subtle hand percussion and occasional piano fills; mature female lead with a clear British accent, understated conversational phrasing, layered harmonies on the hook, narrative verses building to an expanded final chorus.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Born in São Luís, eighty-nine,\\
Island salt in the morning air.\\
Richard came from Birmingham,\\
A short contract, then he stayed there.\\
He taught his Portuguese with a Midlands turn,\\
And taxi drivers laughed with love.\\
Fátima brought her Mineira heart,\\
Pagode in her hands, and more than enough.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Two worlds talking over supper,\\
Football, books, and plans to roam.\\
A name too long for schoolyard tongues,\\
But every road still led me home.

\songpart{Chorus}
Call me Xumi, call me home,\\
I learned to carry more than one.\\
Red and black, my heart knows where,\\
Sport Recife, always there.\\
From Maranhão to Birmingham,\\
I keep both countries in my hands.\\
Call me Xumi, I belong\\
Where all my different roots grow strong.

At five, my father joined two names,\\

I learned the table, learned the teams,\\
And found there was room for every blue and red I knew.
