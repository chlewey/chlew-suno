\songtitle{Routes Keep Moving}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dubstep}\tag{industrial}\tag{thomas-entourage}\label{sec:routes-keep-moving}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the \emph{Stories and Arcs} section of a document by Claude analyzing the album covers of the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} songbook -- routes in pencil, records that outlast them, and who is watching whom. Not canon. Companion to \emph{Blank Scraps} (page \pageref{sec:blank-scraps}) and \emph{Torn-Out Spaces} (page \pageref{sec:torn-out-spaces}). Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 4 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Noise-heavy industrial dubstep with thick brown-noise sub layers, abrasive broadband static, distorted female soprano lead, and sharply contrasting half-time drops. Open with a grainy noise bed and close, tense vocal; build with metallic percussion and rising bass distortion; hit with huge syncopated wobble bass drops, then pull back to sparse documentary-like verses. Keep the soprano piercing and emotionally controlled, with fragmented doubles and clipped tails. Dense, deliberately degraded texture, powerful low end, abrupt edits, clear vocal placement.
\end{style}
