\begin{style}
Electronic folk story-song, midtempo brushed acoustic guitar and plucked dulcimer over rounded analog bass, crisp hand percussion, restrained four-on-the-floor pulse, airy synth pads, cinematic travelogue arrangement; intimate low male lead vocal with clear narrative diction, layered female harmonies on the chorus; verses stay sparse and detailed, pre-choruses add pulsing arpeggios, choruses widen with stacked harmonies and bright rhythmic synths, brief instrumental lift before the final chorus, polished warm mix with organic transients and controlled electronic low end
\end{style}
