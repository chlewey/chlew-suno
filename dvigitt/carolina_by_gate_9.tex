\songtitle{Carolina by Gate 9}

\tag*{2023}\tag{folk-ballad}\tag{ricochets}\tag{carolina-muller}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Diffusion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing character Carolina Müller from novel \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk ballad with gentle 3/4 sway and brushed acoustic guitar, upright bass, hand percussion, and a soft accordion drone; verse keeps it close and story-led, pre-chorus lifts with harmony answers, chorus opens into warm group vocals and a simple stomp-clap pulse, Lead vocal is intimate and close-mic with light doubles on the hook, a few breathy echoes, and a final chorus lift, Ear candy includes chair creaks, a distant boarding chime, and a bowed string swell into the refrain, Mix is warm, woody, and cinematic, light, tone, natural
\end{style}
