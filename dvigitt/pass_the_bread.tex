\songtitle{Pass the Bread}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk}\tag{political}\tag{hold-the-line}
\begin{notes}
Written by Suno after a prompt by Carlos Thompson -- companion piece to \emph{Hold the Line}, same folk-resistance register: refusing to be sorted into red/blue tribes, rejecting purity tests and performative virtue, choosing quiet neighborly solidarity over the shouting frame. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk resistance anthem with a weathered lead vocal and communal gang harmonies; acoustic guitar strums and stomping frame drum drive verses that build into a wide singalong chorus, with restrained fiddle fills; steady midtempo pulse; rootsy live-room recording with raw human dynamics.
\end{style}
