\songtitle{I Learned the Measure}

\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{synth-pop}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{camila-alexander}\tag{xumi}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Xumi's memoir by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe. Character: Camila Eugenia Santos Alexander, Xumi -- a friendship that gathered into a relationship after a 2015 City work event, and the choice not to fit its shape. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 5 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Adult contemporary synth-pop with polished 2010s production, British female lead vocal with clear understated phrasing, midtempo steady pulse, warm analog-style synth pads, rounded bass, restrained electronic drums, crisp piano accents. Begin intimate and conversational, gradually widen into a memorable, emotionally composed chorus; add subtle harmonies and fuller drums in later choruses, then close with a gentle synth-and-vocal resolve. Sophisticated, candid, bittersweet but self-possessed mix.
\end{style}
