\songtitle{Don't Be Evil Yourself}

\tag*{2026}\tag{candy-pop}\tag{synth-pop}\tag{partial-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Partial authorship: chorus, outro and part of each pre-chorus by Carlos Thompson; verses by Suno following specific instructions; the first line of each pre-chorus by Grok, asked to summarize the verses -- harms can be ranked, but lesser wrongs stay wrong and right views give no licence to harm. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Candy pop; close-miked breath detail over a sustained subbass drone, bright synth-pop chords and crisp electronic percussion; low, dark whispering vocals with stacked harmonies lifting the hooks; steady midtempo pulse opening into an anthemic chorus.
\end{style}
