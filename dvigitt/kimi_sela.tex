\songtitle{Kimi sela}

\tag*{2026}\tag{punk-rock}\tag{conlang-lyrics}\tag{sora-keli-muna-taro}\label{sec:kimi-sela}
\begin{notes}
Same invented-language lyrics by Grok as \emph{Sora Keli Muna Taro} (page \pageref{sec:sora-keli-muna-taro}), re-set as punk-rock. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 19 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Punk-rock, palm-muted power chords, brisk straight-ahead drive, fuzzy guitar bleed, gritty female vocals in a high, light upper register with call-and-response backing voices.
\end{style}
