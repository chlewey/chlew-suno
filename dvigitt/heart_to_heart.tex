\songtitle{Heart to Heart}

\tag*{2023}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{ricochets}\tag{sandra-roa}\tag{carolina-muller}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a character description of Sandra Roa and Carolina Müller %
from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Early-2000s alternative rock power ballad, midtempo 4/4 around 78 BPM, clean arpeggiated electric guitar and restrained piano in the verses, warm bass and brushed-to-full drums, then wide distorted guitars and crashing cymbals in the choruses, Build from intimate mother-daughter storytelling to a soaring final chorus, with a brief half-time bridge and a melodic guitar lead, Earnest female lead vocal with a mature, grounded lower register in the verses and a clear youthful harmony above the final chorus, Polished radio-rock mix, tight drums, broad guitars, modest plate reverb, emotional but not melodramatic
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Sandra pins her midnight hair\\
Behind the glasses she wears\\
White collar, black leather sleeve\\
A steady heart nobody sees\\
She signs the figures, takes the train\\
Carries old love like a quiet rain\\
But when her daughter calls her name\\
The whole long day begins again

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Carolina with her books held tight\\
Aachen winter, borrowed light\\
Three languages and a restless dream\\
Still she hears what her mother means

\songpart{Chorus}
You are the road beneath my feet\\
The room I run to when the world gets steep\\
No border, no distance, no turning away\\
I carry your voice through every foreign day\\
Sandra, Carolina, heart to heart\\
Even miles apart, we never start apart

\songpart{Verse 2}
A black beret and a shy half-smile\\
She makes a home in another mile\\
Brown eyes bright with a question inside\\
Learning which doors open from the other side\\
Her mother says, “Don’t lose your spark”\\
Then watches trains dissolve into dark\\
A pearl at her throat, a hand raised high\\
Two silhouettes beneath the platform sky

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
One knows the cost of standing tall\\
One is still learning how to fall\\
But every doubt she cannot name\\
Comes back answered just the same

\songpart{Chorus}
You are the road beneath my feet\\
The room I run to when the world gets steep\\
No border, no distance, no turning away\\
I carry your voice through every foreign day\\
Sandra, Carolina, heart to heart\\
Even miles apart, we never start apart

\songpart{Bridge}
If the numbers fail and the nights run long\\
If I forget where I belong\\
Say my name in Spanish, say it slow\\
I’ll find the place your hands still know\\
And if your courage starts to bend\\
I’ll be the daughter who won’t let it end

\songpart{Final Chorus}
You are the road beneath my feet\\
The reason I rise when the world gets steep\\
No border, no distance, no turning away\\
Your love is the language I bring every day\\
Sandra, Carolina, heart to heart\\
Different roads, but never worlds apart

\songpart{Outro}
Midnight ruby, winter brown\\
Two brave hearts, one beating sound\\
Wherever the next train starts\\
You carry me, I carry your heart
\end{lyrics}
