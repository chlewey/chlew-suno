\songtitle{Where the Tide Turns}

\tag*{2026}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\label{sec:where-the-tide-turns}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, as a duet between Aurora and Thomas -- back from Worcester, a walk on the beach below the house, tired but not broken, and the novel that has become his new hyperfocus. \emph{Not an Infrastructure Guy} (page \pageref{sec:not-an-infrastructure-guy}) and \emph{Stay a Little Longer} (page \pageref{sec:stay-a-little-longer}) set the same walk. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock power ballad, intimate piano opening with restrained clean electric guitar and warm bass, steady spacious drums entering gradually; verse lines trade between a thoughtful male tenor and a clear female mezzo, then join in close harmony on a broad emotional chorus. Build with sustained electric guitar, subtle strings, and fuller drums into a powerful final chorus, then resolve gently. Natural, expressive vocal performances, polished but warm studio mix, narrative storytelling, standard American English.
\end{style}
