\songtitle{The Quiet Tongue}

\tag*{2016}\tag{goth-metal}\tag{original-source}
% -> internal.tex (Essays?)
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on Carlos Thompson's blog entry \emph{Pensamientos claustrofóbicos sobre un futuro incierto} (May 2016). Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 15 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Goth metal, warm mid-range male growling vocals in call-and-response with backing voices; down-tuned guitars and tolling bells over sparse verses, widening into a swelling chorus; slow, unresolved burn; dark plate reverb with a shadowed, weighty mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I built a language out of night,\\
a secret tongue for no one’s mouth.\\
My father called it nothing,\\
so I buried it in doubt.\\
Somewhere in the wires, strangers\\
answered from the dark.

\songpart{Chorus}
Hold me at a distance,\\
warm but never near,\\
a candle in the window glass,\\
a voice I cannot hear.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Shy boy at the recess edge,\\
never one to call a friend.\\
Now I speak through glowing screens\\
to faces that won’t bend.\\
Kitchen bills and cat food,\\
the hum of all I’m not.

\songpart{Bridge}
Close it all, they say, close it all,\\
and the house will mend itself.\\
Four walls, the bills, the hush of the hall,\\
and a dream with no one to tell.

\songpart{Chorus (heavier)}
Hold me at a distance,\\
warm but never near,\\
a candle in the window glass,\\
a fire I can’t bring here.

\songpart{Verse 3}
No desk could hold me, no trade would stay,\\
no cause I joined took flight.\\
Only the forums, hourless, free,\\
let me be someone for the night.

\songpart{Bridge (stripped back)}
Close it all, they say, close it all,\\
and the quiet will be law.\\
Four walls, the bills, the hush of the hall,\\
and no one left to call.

\songpart{Final Chorus (fading, no resolution)}
Hold me at a distance,\\
warm but never near...\\
if I close the window,\\
who will answer here...
\end{lyrics}
