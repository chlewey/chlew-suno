\songtitle{Stay a Little Longer}

\tag*{2026}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\label{sec:stay-a-little-longer}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, as a duet between Aurora and Thomas -- the end of the same beach walk as \emph{Where the Tide Turns} (page \pageref{sec:where-the-tide-turns}): a wedding and old friends, moonrise, and the choice between dinner in the cabin or the tunnel to the house below. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft-rock power ballad with a slow, steady 6/8 sway; intimate piano and clean electric guitar open, warm bass and restrained drums enter gradually, broad melodic choruses with sustained guitar lines and subtle strings, then a quiet reflective outro. Male and female duet with conversational alternating verses, close harmonies that bloom in the chorus, expressive but controlled vocals, polished organic production and a cinematic dynamic build.
\end{style}
