\songtitle{Gabi in Focus}

\tag*{2023}\tag{ebm}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Diffusion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing a concept of a purple-haired, purple-eyed beauty %
that would be incorporated into the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe as Gabi (Gabriele Wiater).

See \emph{Purple Lace Stance}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
EBM with driving four-on-the-floor kick, metallic syncopated bass, and tightly gated industrial synth stabs; verses lock into a dry drum grid with clipped percussive keys and tense filtered bass, pre-chorus builds with risers, vocal chops, and expanding stereo width, then the chorus drops into distorted low-end weight, icy arpeggios, and sharp accent hits, Breathless, intimate vocal delivery with rhythmic ad-libs, cinematic room reverb on key phrases, and punchy, polished mixing that feels dark, defiant, and hyper-detailed, like a stylized fashion film in a cozy wooden room lit by dramatic spotlights, ebm
\end{style}
