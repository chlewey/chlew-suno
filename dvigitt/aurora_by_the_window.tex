\songtitle{Aurora by the Window}

\tag*{2023}\tag{sea-shanty}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Diffusion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing a concept of a femme fatale redhead psychotherapist %
that would be incorporated into the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe as Aurora Swansen.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Traditional maritime sea shanty with a steady heaving pulse, handclaps, stomping frame drum, concertina, fiddle, low whistle, and rough-hewn call-and-response, Verses feel intimate and narrative, then broaden into a loud crew chorus with stacked male and female group vocals, Acoustic room ambience, wooden-floor resonance, restrained low end, natural ensemble dynamics, and a brisk work-song tempo, The story centers on a red-haired woman named Aurora seated by a tall window, watching a harbor city and distant mountains while the crew sings of home, weather, and the next tide
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Aurora sits where the tall panes shine,\\
Red hair caught warm in the afternoon,\\
A harbor city below the line,\\
And blue peaks rising beyond the room.

\songpart{Chorus}
Heave away, boys, heave away,\\
Turn the rope and face the spray,\\
Aurora, keep the kettle warm,\\
We’ll find our road through wind and storm.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A golden medallion rests at her throat,\\
Green eyes fixed on the ferries below,\\
She marks each mast and passing boat,\\
And counts the clouds where the cold winds blow.

\songpart{Chorus}
Heave away, boys, heave away,\\
Turn the rope and face the spray,\\
Aurora, keep the kettle warm,\\
We’ll find our road through wind and storm.

\songpart{Bridge}
When the evening folds on the mountain side,\\
And the last red sail slips from view,\\
She’ll raise one hand from the window wide,\\
And we’ll pull the whole gray ocean through.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Heave away, boys, heave away,\\
Turn the rope and face the spray,\\
Aurora, keep the kettle warm,\\
We’ll find our road through wind and storm.\\
Heave away, boys, heave away,\\
Home is the song that the tide obeys,\\
Aurora, light in the harbor room,\\
We’ll come back home by the rising moon.

\songpart{Outro}
Heave away...\\
Heave away...
\end{lyrics}
