\songtitle{Grace in the Gale}

\tag*{2023}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{Erika Czajka}\tag{ai-portrait}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Stable Difussion prompt by Carlos Thompson describing a concept of a grey-eyes waifu with sidebangs %
that would be incorporated into the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe as Erika Czajka.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Operatic symphonic metal with a driving double-kick pulse, low distorted guitars, grand string and choir layers, and cinematic brass accents, Build from a tense wind-swept verse into a huge anthem chorus, with dynamic pauses before each vocal entrance and a soaring bridge, Powerful adult female mezzo-soprano vocals, classically trained vibrato, clear diction, confident and admiring delivery, Wide cinematic mix, crisp drums, dense but separated orchestration, dramatic outdoor atmosphere
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
By the lake where the tall grass bends,\\
You stand with the storm as your closest friend.\\
Dark hair gathered, your gray eyes bright,\\
A steady flame in the open light.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The wind takes hold of your blouse and skirt,\\
But you never bow, you never break, never hurt.\\
With a quiet smile and a fearless gaze,\\
You turn the restless air into praise.

\songpart{Chorus}
Erika Czajka, I honor your name,\\
Grace in the gale and a heart made of flame.\\
Erika Czajka, steadfast and clear,\\
The whole wide water grows still when you’re here.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Silver at your ears, lilac on your lips,\\
A blue clasp high where the silk collar slips.\\
Dimples rise as the dark clouds roll,\\
And courage wears beauty like a shining soul.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The hills may tremble, the cold may rise,\\
Still you meet the horizon with fearless eyes.\\
No crown is needed, no throne, no claim,\\
The wind itself remembers your name.

\songpart{Chorus}
Erika Czajka, I honor your name,\\
Grace in the gale and a heart made of flame.\\
Erika Czajka, steadfast and clear,\\
The whole wide water grows still when you’re here.

\songpart{Bridge}
Let the black clouds gather over the blue,\\
Let every wild current carry this truth:\\
You walk with poise where the hard winds run,\\
A living answer beneath the sun.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Erika Czajka, I honor your name,\\
Grace in the gale and a heart made of flame.\\
Erika Czajka, magnificent, free,\\
The lake holds your image for all eyes to see.

\songpart{Outro}
When evening descends and the wind moves on,\\
Your fearless light remains after dawn.
\end{lyrics}
