\begin{notes}
Lyrics by DeepSeek following structural instructions, narrowed themes and tone by Carlos Thompson, based on \emph{Fallstudie}, an original story by Carlos Thompson adapted to voice and tone by ChatGPT, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, narrated by Erika Czajka -- a call to Natalia, who had cut her stay short after the armed attack, and the darkness falling over Punta Espada. \emph{Från Stockholm till Punta Espada} (page \pageref{sec:fran-stockholm-till-punta-espada}) comes from the same story. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 18 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock, mid-tempo acoustic sway, warm smooth silky female mid-range vocals, tape-saturated production, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, strummed folk guitar, restrained drums and bass.
\end{style}
