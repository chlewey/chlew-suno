\songtitle{September Night}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-ballad}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the opening of a failed story by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic indie folk ballad in a minor key, slow 6/8 pulse, fingerpicked acoustic guitar and sparse felt piano opening, brushed drums entering in the second verse, low cello swells, distant bowed textures, and restrained electric guitar ambience, Build from close-miked intimate vocals into a fuller final chorus, with audible room detail, soft dynamic compression, and a cool gray mix that leaves space around the vocal, The arrangement should feel windy, chilly, and rainy without becoming overly dramatic
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
September pressed its cold hand to the glass\\
Rain drew crooked rivers down the pane\\
You stood barefoot beside the radiator\\
Counting every car that never came

The curtains lifted when the window slipped\\
A wet leaf landed on the floor\\
You said, “Stay until the kettle sings”\\
And I forgot what I was waiting for

\songpart{Chorus}
It was a September night\\
Coat sleeves dripping by the door\\
The wind kept worrying the house\\
But I wanted nothing more\\
Than your shoulder next to mine\\
And the kettle’s thin reply\\
It was a September night\\
And we let the whole world pass us by

\songpart{Verse 2}
The gutters filled with silver needles\\
Branches scratched along the roof\\
Your old blue mug was chipped beside me\\
Steam curled upward, disappearing too soon

We spoke about the train at seven\\
The job you might take up north\\
Then thunder shook the kitchen shelves\\
And neither of us reached for the door

\songpart{Bridge}
By morning, rain had found the garden\\
Puddles held the broken sky\\
You wrote your number on a receipt\\
Then folded it three times

\songpart{Final Chorus}
It was a September night\\
Cold enough to make us stay\\
The wind kept worrying the house\\
While the rain erased the day\\
And your shoulder next to mine\\
Made the narrow room feel wide\\
It was a September night\\
I still hear it when the weather turns outside

\songpart{Outro}
September pressed its cold hand to the glass\\
And somewhere, your kettle sings
