\songtitle{Blank Scraps}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dubstep}\tag{industrial}\tag{thomas-entourage}\label{sec:blank-scraps}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the \emph{Patterns} section of a document by Claude analyzing the album covers of the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} songbook -- six scraps pinned to a page in several scripts, and what won't cross over in translation. Not canon. \emph{Torn-Out Spaces} (page \pageref{sec:torn-out-spaces}) comes from the same section. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 4 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Noise-heavy industrial dubstep with thick brown-noise sub layers and abrasive broadband static; distorted female soprano lead, intimate and precise against the grit. Begin with paper-like static and a sparse voice, build through tense half-time verses, then slam into sharply contrasted dubstep drops with wobbling bass and clipped metallic percussion. Pull back to exposed vocal and noise between drops; finish with a sudden cut and a few decaying scraps of static. Dense, tactile mix with deliberate distortion and clear vocal presence.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}

Six scraps pinned above the page\\
One crossed out, one still unnamed\\
A line bends where the ink runs thin\\
A tune waits underneath the skin

Turn the sheet, the marks remain\\
New alphabets, the same old stain

\songpart{Chorus}
Translate what the paper keeps\\
Read the blank where the sentence sleeps\\
Every version shifts its shape\\
Every missing word escapes

\songpart{Verse 2}
A calligrapher’s hand trails off\\
Cyrillic curls beside the cloth\\
Arabic turns the margin round\\
Latin letters lose their sound

\songpart{Drop}
Blank scraps, blank scraps\\
What won’t cross over stays\\
Blank scraps, blank scraps\\
Held against the page

\songpart{Bridge}
At the table, four small faces\\
Wait behind speech bubbles, empty spaces\\
A pencil circles what we can’t decide\\
The crossed-out line is still inside

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Translate what the paper keeps\\
Read the blank where the sentence sleeps\\
From staff lines to a stained refrain\\
Spider caught in the ink again

\songpart{Outro}

Let the page keep still
\end{lyrics}
