\songtitle{The Woman from the Storm}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriel-thomas}\tag{susana-bianchi}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok based in a short story from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft but fast-paced operatic rock with a cinematic narrative arc: driving live drums around 150 BPM, piano and broad orchestral strings under distorted electric guitars, restrained verses opening into soaring theatrical choruses, dramatic dynamic swells, and a brief stripped-down hotel-bar bridge before the final anthem, Male baritone lead with clear storytelling diction, controlled vibrato, warm low register, and powerful operatic lifts on key lines, Polished modern rock mix, wide guitars, punchy kick and snare, piano audible beneath the vocal, rain-and-flight tension suggested through orchestral accents; emotional but unsentimental, ending with a reflective coda
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I read her résumé beneath a Madrid afternoon,\\
That photograph returned me to a battered little plane.\\
Patagonia below us, a partner building bridges,\\
A charter bird descending through the crosswind and the rain.

The cockpit opened wide into the cabin,\\
A child-faced pilot holding every trembling hand.\\
Some were praying, some were sick against the window,\\
But I watched her speak the storm into command.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
A sudden drop, a hard and crooked turn,\\
The clouds came down like iron on the wing.\\
Then rain became a silver curtain,\\
And her steady voice made everything behave.

\songpart{Chorus}
Susana, bring us softly to the ground,\\
With the whole wide sky trying to pull us down.\\
You held the fear where no one else could see,\\
And made a strip of earth our harbor be.\\
Susana, I remembered who you were—\\
The calmest voice inside the worst of weather.

\songpart{Verse 2}
The wheels touched down so gently I could hardly trust the sound,\\
Then on the apron, calm became a storm of words.\\
A masterclass in Porteño profanity,\\
Delivered to the crew who barely moved.

Nine years passed through airlines and the oceans:\\
Aerolíneas, Delta, Emirates in the frame.\\
The 787, long routes, a window opening,\\
And one more bird that needed someone just the same.

\songpart{Chorus}
Susana, bring us softly to the ground,\\
With the whole wide sky trying to pull us down.\\
The résumé was perfect on the page,\\
But I needed more than talent for the cage.\\
Susana, could you face a room of men\\
As fiercely as you faced that mountain weather?

\songpart{Verse 3}
In Madrid I built a wall of difficult questions,\\
Made the room more severe than I had planned.\\
The answer waited quietly inside me:\\
The captain’s seat was already in her hands.

The next day found us talking at the hotel bar,\\
Fernando there, her Emirates friend beside her.\\
We traded homes and families, broken marriages,\\
And switched to Spanish till the others lost their patience.

No talk of contracts, no talk of aircraft,\\
Just music, dinner, and a night that slipped its knot.\\
She wouldn’t let me pay, then let the rhythm\\
Carry us somewhere neither of us called love.

\songpart{Bridge}
Not romance—no promise made,\\
Just biology finding a safe place.\\
Past midnight, one cheek kiss at the doorway,\\
Thank you, thank you, and the distance back in place.\\
She left as if the world would close behind her,\\
As if we’d never meet again.\\
But I knew the storm had shown me\\
Who I wanted at the helm.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Susana, bring us softly to the ground,\\
Let the hard world rage beyond the cabin now.\\
You held your course when every voice broke loose,\\
You made a frightened sky feel almost true.\\
Susana, the résumé was only paper,\\
The photograph was only time.\\
I hired the woman from the storm,\\
The one who made survival sound refined.

\songpart{Outro}
A runway wet in Patagonia,\\
A bar in Madrid, a door before the dawn.\\
Some people enter like a passing weather system;\\
Some leave the captain’s light still burning on.
\end{lyrics}
