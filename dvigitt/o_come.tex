\songtitle{O Come}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:o-come}
\begin{notes}
Translated and adapted by Claude from the \emph{Gozos para todos los días} of the \emph{Novena de Aguinaldos}: the O antiphons -- Wisdom, Adonai, Root of Jesse, Key of David, Dayspring, King of Nations -- each answered by the refrain ``O come, nor tarry overlong''. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 7 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk carol, strummed acoustic guitars, shaking tambourines, harmonicas, and recorders; subtle tape hiss and a dry verse mix; unhurried processional sway; child vocals in solo verses with crooning legato phrasing and whispered ad-libs between lines, opening into gang choruses.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Chorus}
Sweet Jesus, Child of my delight,\\
come to our souls, I pray;\\
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 1}
O Wisdom of the Sovereign God,\\
who stooped to infant size,\\
O holy Child, come down and teach\\
us prudence, that our ways\\
may follow truth, and we may know\\
the wisdom of your grace.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 2}
O mighty Adonai, who spoke\\
to Moses long ago,\\
and gave your law to Israel,\\
come, ransom us, your own,\\
and let a weak and tender Child\\
a strong right arm to show.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 3}
O sacred Root of Jesse, raised\\
upon the heights to bear\\
your fragrant nard before the world\\
and fill the earth’s wide sphere,\\
O sweetest Child, the Lily called\\
of valleys, Fields’ fair flower.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 4}
O Key of David, you who ope\\
to exiles banished far\\
the closed and long-forbidden doors\\
of the king’s palace there,\\
come lead us, Child, with your white hand\\
from prisons sin has reared.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 5}
O Dayspring of the rising East,\\
O Sun of endless rays,\\
that through the darkness we may see\\
your splendor’s holy blaze,\\
O precious Child, the Christian’s joy,\\
let shine your smile always.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 6}
O spotless Mirror, Holy One\\
of holies, image rare,\\
beyond compare of God Most High,\\
blot out our guilt, and hear\\
the exile; as a little Child\\
give refuge to despair.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 7}
O King of nations, Emmanuel,\\
O Israel’s long desire,\\
O Shepherd of the flock, dear Child,\\
who feed with gentle care\\
now wayward sheep, with crook so soft,\\
or lamb, so meek, so fair.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 8}
Let heaven open, and from high\\
let kindly dew rain down,\\
to water all the thirsty earth;\\
O lovely Child, come soon,\\
come, God made man, bright shining star,\\
flower of the Field, now bloom.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 9}
Come, for now Mary readies arms\\
where soon her Child will lie,\\
and Joseph, with a holy zeal,\\
prepares his heart to be\\
a shrine to hold your love, O Child,\\
your own sanctuary.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 10}
Help of the weak, the mourner’s shield,\\
the comfort of the sad,\\
the exile’s light, my life of life,\\
my Lord, my love, adored,\\
my constant Friend, divine, my own,\\
my brother, and my God.

\songpart{Chorus}
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come quickly, no delay!

\songpart{Verse 11}
May eyes enamored look on you\\
and kiss your feet and hands;\\
prostrate upon the earth, I stretch\\
my arms to you; no words,\\
but more than any phrase can say,\\
I weep, and speak in tears.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Come, Savior, whom we sigh to see,\\
come to our souls, and stay;\\
O come, nor tarry overlong,\\
come soon, do not delay.
\end{lyrics}
