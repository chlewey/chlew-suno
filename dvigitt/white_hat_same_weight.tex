\songtitle{White Hat (Same Weight)}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:white-hat-same-weight}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on \emph{Christmas 2024} and \emph{Full House} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- to Sylvia, wearing the blue hat: the white one weighs the same, nobody is finished, keep your own handwriting; Christmas Eve, the novena and the grandmother's tamale recipe, and the fireworks barge at midnight. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock / pop-rock, around 112 BPM in D major, with a warm, a little weathered, and gently encouraging pulse; fingerpicked acoustic guitar and low harmonica open, brushed snare enters verse 2, full drums, electric rhythm guitar and a spring-reverb slide guitar line lift the chorus; mandolin rings the offbeats in the second chorus, a voice-guitar-harmonica bridge rises to a whole-step final chorus; older male vocals, gritty, strained, powerful full-voice belt with a close verse presence.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1 (acoustic guitar, harmonica, a bare kick drum)}
I flew in from London with a head full of drawings,\\
Landed on a dust strip where the desert meets the sea.\\
There’s a city that’s a rumor, there’s a blueprint and a promise,\\
And a lot of folks who’ve put their trust in me.

\songpart{Verse 2 (brushed snare enters)}
I read a paper once on burying highways,\\
Thought I’d give Bogotá its skyline back.\\
I’ll probably drop it in a couple of days, I always do,\\
Ideas come easy, it’s the staying that’s the track.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus (building, electric guitar swells underneath)}
And you ask me when it happens, when you finally feel grown,\\
Like there’s a door, and a doorman, and a key.\\
Well I’ve worn the white one for years now, kid,\\
And I’m still figuring out who’s under it. Me.

\songpart{Chorus (full band, big open strum, slide guitar answering each line)}
Oh, the hat’s just a color, the weight’s the same,\\
Blue or white, you carry it the same way.\\
Nobody’s finished, nobody’s arrived,\\
We’re all just building as we go, building as we go.\\
So keep your pen and your paper, keep your own handwriting,\\
Let the machines learn to follow you.\\
The city’s gonna rise whether we’re ready or not,\\
Take your time becoming what you do.

\songpart{Verse 3 (drop back to acoustic, intimate)}
Christmas Eve, the novena, the long table,\\
My father talking stars till the small hours burn.\\
There’s a tamale recipe my grandmother left him,\\
Finally cooked, and I swear I tasted her return.\\
A drawing set for a kid, and the spark in her eyes,\\
Worth every gift I never picked.\\
And I walk past the places where the ground remembers,\\
Don’t know what the chill means, just know where it sits.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus 2 (same build, a little more urgent)}
And somebody’s father walks the site beside you,\\
Trying to see what you’ve been building all this time.\\
You don’t have to be finished to show him,\\
Just be honest about the line.

\songpart{Chorus (repeat, add harmony vocals and a mandolin or banjo ring on the off-beats)}
Oh, the hat’s just a color, the weight’s the same,\\
Blue or white, you carry it the same way.\\
Nobody’s finished, nobody’s arrived,\\
We’re all just building as we go, building as we go.\\
So keep your pen and your paper, keep your own handwriting,\\
Let the machines learn to follow you.\\
The city’s gonna rise whether we’re ready or not,\\
Take your time becoming what you do.

\songpart{Bridge (drums cut out, voice, guitar and harmonica only, then a slow crescendo)}
Midnight on the water, a barge and a fuse,\\
I worried all night about weather and tides.\\
But the sea behaved within parameters,\\
And the whole sky opened wide.\\
Then the planes take everyone different directions,\\
Home to the island, onward to the sea.\\
Some people leave, and some people stay behind,\\
And that’s how it’s meant to be.

\songpart{Final Chorus (key change up a whole step, stomp-clap, group vocals)}
So take the blue hat, wear it proud,\\
One day it’ll turn white, and you’ll laugh out loud,
’Cause it never felt the way they said it would.\\
The hat’s just a color, the weight’s the same,\\
Build it your way, and build it good.
\annotation{(slow down, harmonica outro, hold the last chord)}\\
Build it good.
\end{lyrics}
