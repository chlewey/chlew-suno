\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a DeepSeek summary of five songs and the thematic intersections between them -- \emph{Kvar i mig}, \emph{Mouth of the Machine}, \emph{Twenty Fourteen Scripts}, \emph{Between Worlds}, and \emph{Å finne noen andre å bebreide}. Truths kept locked away in separate rooms, each with its own weight -- what's buried doesn't leave, it just keeps moving under the skin. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dark introspective trap, male vocals, restrained half-time groove, deep 808 sub-bass, clipped hi-hats with occasional triplet rolls, sparse minor-key piano fragments, distant granular vocal texture, intimate close-mic verses, tense rising pre-chorus, wide layered hook, stripped bridge, unresolved outro, polished low-end with dry drums and selective reverb
\end{style}
