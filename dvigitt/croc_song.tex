\songtitle{Croc song}

\tag*{2026}\tag{children-rock}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno following instructions by Carlos Thompson -- a patient crocodile by the river bend who lets the gnu and the zebra pass. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Playful children’s rock, bouncy mid-tempo backbeat, bright crunchy electric guitars, rounded bass, lively kit with handclaps, little stop-start breaks for comic timing; clear warm lead vocal with a friendly sing-along chorus and light group responses, storybook energy, crisp uncluttered mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
By the muddy river bend\\
Croc sits still and waits again\\
Gnu comes trotting, zebra too\\
He watches what they’re going to do

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Nearer, nearer, hoofbeats tap\\
Croc just blinks beneath his cap

\songpart{Chorus}
Wait, wait, patient Croc\\
Keeping quiet by the rock\\
If they’re close, he takes a look\\
Then lets them pass beside the brook\\
Wait, wait, there they go\\
No big fuss, just nice and slow

\songpart{Verse 2}
Zebra pauses, stripes in sun\\
Gnu says, “Croc, are you having fun?”\\
Croc gives one polite little grin\\
“Not close enough,” he says, “come in—”

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Nearer, nearer, splash and plop\\
Croc decides to simply stop

\songpart{Chorus}
Wait, wait, patient Croc\\
Keeping quiet by the rock\\
If they’re close, he takes a look\\
Then lets them pass beside the brook\\
Wait, wait, there they go\\
No big fuss, just nice and slow

\songpart{Bridge}
He waits all day, he waits all night\\
He likes the river, cool and bright\\
But when they’re near, he doesn’t chase\\
He gives them room and keeps his place

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Wait, wait, patient Croc\\
Keeping quiet by the rock\\
Gnu and zebra wander through\\
Croc says, “Have a lovely day, you two!”\\
Wait, wait, there they go\\
Friends can pass, and Croc says so

\songpart{Outro}
Still as stone, with a sleepy grin\\
Patient Croc lets the world roll in
\end{lyrics}
