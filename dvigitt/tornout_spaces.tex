\songtitle{Torn-Out Spaces}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dubstep}\tag{experimental}\tag{thomas-entourage}\label{sec:torn-out-spaces}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the \emph{Patterns} section of a document by Claude analyzing the album covers of the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} songbook -- translation as torn-out spaces, a voice rearranged that still remains. Not canon. \emph{Blank Scraps} (page \pageref{sec:blank-scraps}) comes from the same section. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 4 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Aggressive experimental dubstep with heavy broadband static and deep brown-noise beds, distorted female soprano lead, clipped intimate verses opening into huge half-time drops, snarling wobble bass, metallic impacts, granular noise cuts, abrupt breakdowns and a final drop that collapses into hiss; dense, abrasive, deliberately lo-fi texture with clear vocal presence.
\end{style}
