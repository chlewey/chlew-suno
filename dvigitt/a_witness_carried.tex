\songtitle{A Witness Carried}

\tag*{2026}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{christian-music}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a ChatGPT summary of the lives of a list of modern Christian martyrs and witnesses -- Wilberforce, Kolbe, Bonhoeffer, Jägerstätter, Sophie and Hans Scholl, Romero, Dorothy Day, Berrigan and Dorothy Stang. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
"layered vocals, chamber hip hop", narrative chamber hip-hop with intimate piano ostinato, bowed cello and viola, restrained dusty breakbeat, rounded low bass, occasional woodwind counterline; verses move through distinct historical portraits, chorus opens into layered harmonies, bridge strips to voice and piano before a fuller final refrain; close, articulate lead vocal, warm cinematic mix with clear lyric focus
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Wilberforce carried names into the chamber,\\
A bill returned, a decade more to fight.\\
He spoke for those the ships had made invisible,\\
And kept the question in the public light.

Kolbe stepped forward in the crowded yard,\\
And gave his place to Gajowniczek.\\
One life offered where a sentence fell;\\
A stranger’s name held close against the end.

\songpart{Chorus}
When the hour asked, they answered with their lives,\\
Not all with swords, not all beneath a flag.\\
A hand held out, a word that would not bend,\\
A witness carried when the world turned black.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Bonhoeffer named the lie inside the church,\\
Then joined the risk beyond the pulpit rail.\\
Jägerstätter would not wear the uniform;\\
His faith outweighed the threats inside the jail.

Sophie and Hans left paper in the city,\\
Small folded pages passing hand to hand.\\
A quiet voice can still confront a kingdom;\\
They read the truth and faced the state’s command.

\songpart{Chorus}
When the hour asked, they answered with their lives,\\
Not all with swords, not all beneath a flag.\\

\songpart{Verse 3}
Romero spoke the names the powerful erased,\\
Then fell beside the altar where he prayed.\\
Day opened doors and set a place for strangers,\\
While Berrigan stood where the bombs were made.

Dorothy Stang walked forest paths with farmers,\\
Defending land no deed could rightly steal.\\
Their courage was no legend made of marble;\\
It was a daily choice, a wound made real.

\songpart{Bridge}
A vote, a vow, a leaflet passed along,\\
A bowl set down, a refusal to obey.\\
The cost was never distant from the body;\\
The witness lives in what we choose today.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
When the hour asks, may we answer with our lives,\\
With open hands and words that will not bend.\\
A name remembered is a charge to carry;\\
Let courage travel farther than its end.

\songpart{Outro}
A place made free.\\
A truth spoken plain.\\
A door left open.\\
A forest standing.
\end{lyrics}
