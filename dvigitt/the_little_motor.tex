\songtitle{The Little Motor}

\tag*{2026}\tag{chamber-pop}\tag{saulon}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a substory of the novel draft \emph{Saulon} by Carlos Thompson (a fictional universe of his), extracted and translated by Claude -- a night in Cali, a mother's blessing, and the choice of the dark. Retrieved from Dvigitt's Suno page (V6-MINI, 12 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber pop with a theatrical lyrical mezzo-soprano lead; intimate, story-forward verses over felt piano, pizzicato strings, cello, and restrained brushed percussion. Build each chorus with swelling chamber strings and close harmonies, then pivot into a salsa montuno-style bridge: syncopated piano montuno, hand percussion, lively bass tumbao, and answering vocal phrases. Return to a hushed, emotionally resolved outro. Detailed, warm acoustic production with clear vocal presence and dynamic arrangement.
\end{style}
