\songtitle{Reading Too Much}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the narrative universe of \emph{Thomas Entourage} by Carlos Thompson. %
Written by Carlos Thompson with editions by Grok and Gemini. %
First person narration by Gabriele Wiater.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
EBD, fast-paced female vocals, almost spoken, texture vocals, close-mic, 2020s club sound, industrial flavor, 132 BPM, 808, metallic percussion, sub-bass with dubstep
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Seventeen, and Thomas moved her across continents,\\
a curfew complication—he called her\\
a colleague's niece just visiting Tallinn\\
when the world shut down.

And Thomas being Thomas couldn't keep his pants on,\\
then took her on as his protégée,\\
with her family's blessing back in Sardinia.\\
Tallinn to London, Bogotá, New York.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Was she a victim of an older, richer, wiser man?\\
Was she an opportunist playing my clueless polymath?\\
Is Thomas the one I have to protect?\\
Or is Sylvia the one I should guard?

\songpart{Chorus}
She looked innocent enough,\\
young, pretty, but self-possessed.\\
She looked harmless enough,\\
teal pixie cut, enigmatic smile.

My own judgment I could not trust,\\
not for Thomas, not for me, not for her.

Or maybe I just read too much.

\songpart{Verse 2}
New York was in the middle of the second wave,\\
not the best moment to visit the Big Apple.\\
I took Sylvia under my wing, not knowing what to do—\\
protect her? empower her? guide her? fall for her?

She was innocent the way sheltered people are,\\
but Sylvia knew how to wield that innocent charm.\\
No one was innocent in Tallinn.\\
No one is innocent here in New York.\\
Not Thomas, not Sylvia. Not myself.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Was I acting to protect Thomas as my property?\\
Was I trying to keep Sylvia out of the adult world?\\
Or were my motives far more selfish—\\
did I simply want Sylvia for myself?

\songpart{Chorus}
She looked innocent enough,\\
young, pretty, but self-possessed.\\
She looked harmless enough,\\
teal pixie cut, enigmatic smile.

My own judgment I could not trust,\\
not for Thomas, not for me, not for her.

Or maybe I just read too much.

\songpart{Bridge}
And then I let my guard down.\\
One triggered memory she seized upon.\\
Four years ago, in my studio,\\
I almost neglected myself to death.

And right there in that same studio\\
she looked right through me, and I broke.\\
For just a few moments I was at her mercy,\\
and I knew she knew.

I finally got my act together,\\
pretended everything was cool again.\\
I fulfilled my duty, sent her home,\\
and confessed to Thomas how I'd failed.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
She looked innocent enough,\\
young, pretty, but self-possessed.\\
She looked harmless enough,\\
teal pixie cut, enigmatic smile.

My own judgment failed me,\\
and then one thing became clear:\\
Thomas is clueless sometimes,\\
but he is also a rock.

A rock for Sylvia, a rock for me.

Or maybe I just read too much.
\end{lyrics}
