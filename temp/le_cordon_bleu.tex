\songtitle{Le Cordon Bleu}

\tag*{2026}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the studies section of Diana Naji's profile, within the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dream-pop with chanson française elements, slow swung groove and brushed percussion; verse stays close-mic over glimmering keys and muted guitar, pre-chorus opens with accordion haze and a rising string pad, chorus lands on a floating minor-key lift with stacked French-leaning harmonies and a simple pulse, Whispered doubles, soft falsetto echoes, and short delay throws on key phrases; reversed swells, vinyl texture, and a bell-like chime between lines, Intimate, glossy, and airy in the mix, pop
\end{style}
