\songtitle{Sin Retorno}

\tag*{2026}\tag{doo-wop}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a conversation between Carlos Thompson and NotebookLM.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Doo-wop with handclaps and upright bass walking in a swung mid-tempo groove; verse rides sparse piano, snapped fingers, and brushed drums, pre-chorus lifts with stacked “ooh” harmonies, chorus blooms with girl-group belts and a playful key change feel, Female lead vocal with sweet doubles, tight backing trio responses, and a few teasing delay throws on the hook, Tape hiss, tambourine pickups, and a vinyl-pop turn into the chorus, Bright, glossy, retro mix with punchy low end, empowering
\end{style}
