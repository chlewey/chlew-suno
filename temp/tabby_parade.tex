\songtitle{Tabby Parade}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ska}\tag{cats}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a descripiton by Carlos Thompson on his cats.

A portrait of Carlos Thompson's cats: Tobías, Matías, Tequila, Pimienta, Amaretto, Jiji, Lúcifer, Valentín, Mongkut, and Capuccino.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Ska with bouncy offbeat guitar, brisk brass punches, and dubstep swirls that bloom into wobbling bass drops; verse keeps the skank tight and playful, pre-chorus strips to voice and rim clicks, chorus hits with horn stabs and gang replies, Delicate female vocals up front, doubled softly, with airy ad-libs, tape-delay throws, filter sweeps, and a bright punchy mix with glossy low-end, dubstep, ska, female vocals
\end{style}
