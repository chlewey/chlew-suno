\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude-generated prompt based on archetypes of Stable Difussion prompts by Carlos Thompson
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Hip-hop hymn with grand orchestral swells over a slow boom-bap swing; verse rides brushed drums, upright bass, and clipped piano; pre-chorus lifts with strings and timpani rolls; chorus lands in full choir, brass stabs, and handclap accents. Lead vocal is close-mic and intimate with stacked doubles on the hook, ad-lib echoes panned wide, and a few whispered asides. Amber vinyl crackle, reversed string swells into transitions, and a final horn rise keep it cinematic and bright, light, world, warm, hip-hop, blues, soft
\end{style}
