\begin{notes}
Part of Gabriele Wiater's biographical series in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe. Lyrics by SUNO based on a section of Gabi's biography co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop with mid-tempo 127 BPM pulse, tight syncopated drum machine and metallic accents; verse stays sparse and close-mic, pre-chorus opens with layered pulse and rising noise, chorus lands with punchy low end and stacked doubles on the anchor, Vocal is restrained female lead with fast clipped phrases, intimate whispers on key turns, short delay throws on the last word, Ear candy: filtered risers into the chorus, scraped-metal textures between lines, a brief half-time pull in the bridge, Mix is bright, dry, and hard-edged with polished impact, vocal, raw, mellow, pop, industrial, dynamic, talented
\end{style}
