\songtitle{Jersey Summers}

\tag*{2026}\tag{acoustic-punk}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{fiona-levit}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the second segment of the story \emph{A New York Night} by Carlos Thompson, %
within the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Acoustic punk with fast downstroked guitar, handclaps on the backbeat, upright bass thump, and loose group shouts; verse one stays lean and story-driven, pre-chorus lifts with stacked oohs, chorus hits in gang vocals with a sing-back hook, bridge strips to one guitar and close-mic lead before the final chorus swells with stomps, tambourine, and short delay throws on the title; bright, raw, and road-trip warm, acoustic, theme, uplifting, punk, beats
\end{style}
