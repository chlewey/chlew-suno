\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a story by Carlos Thompson for the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} project.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
World music chamber rock with mid-paced electronic pulse, hand percussion and bowed strings; verse rides sparse frame drum, plucked guitar, and soft synth haze; pre-chorus lifts with layered harmonies and rising strings; chorus opens with full rhythm section, low cello drones, and repeated vocal hook, Mellow female lead with intimate doubles, airy backing echoes, brief delay throws on names, and a restrained but cinematic mix with organic room warmth, female vocals, country, punta, world, rock, mellow
\end{style}
