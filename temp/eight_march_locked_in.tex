\songtitle{Eight March Locked In}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}
\begin{notes}
Based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas' Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with brisk acoustic strumming, walking bass, brushed snare, and nimble mandolin; verse keeps a close-mic baritone on dry room tone, pre-chorus lifts with handclaps and stacked thirds, chorus opens into gang hums and a stomp pattern, Bridge strips to voice and guitar, then final chorus adds harmonies, a banjo run, and a wheezy accordion tag, Warm, immediate mix with tape crackle, lifted pick noise, and light crowd-room sparkle, rock, folk, light, warm, drama
\end{style}
