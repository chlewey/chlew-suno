\songtitle{Memoria de humo}

\tag*{2026}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Gemini Notebook summary of chapter 2 (1993--2000) of Carlos Thompson's memoir \emph{A Life in World Events}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Rock en español with punk-theatre drive and rapped verses; brisk driving drums, chugging guitars, handclap accents, and dramatic stop-start turns, Verse 1 rides tight guitar stabs and spoken-rapped phrasing; pre-chorus strips to bass and toms; chorus opens with gang vocals and sharp melodic lift, Bridge flips to a half-time rap with tense octave guitar, then final chorus returns bigger with crowd shouts, short delay throws on the hook, and a bright, punchy mix, cumbia, rock en espanol, punk, grupera
\end{style}
