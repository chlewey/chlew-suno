\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a biography of character Gabriele "Gabi" Wiater from \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop with a mid-tempo 127 BPM pulse, tight syncopated drum machine hits, metallic percussion, and a restrained female lead in close-mic fast phrases; verse stays sparse with dry kick, muted bass, and workshop clanks, pre-chorus opens with filtered noise and rising synth scrape, chorus lands punchy with stacked doubles and short gang ad-libs, Ear candy: weld-spark tick loops, reversed metal swell into the hook, tape-stop cut before the bridge, Bright, punchy, industrial-leaning mix with intimate vocal detail, dynamic, industrial, vocal, pop, mellow, world
\end{style}
