\songtitle{Before That Blows}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ricochets}
\begin{notes}
Based on character Khamid Khaled from \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}

\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Born in Sidon, raised in Springfield —\\
an American at heart.\\
From a small town he joined Marines\\
to prove his loyal part.

Kenya liaison before the heat,\\
with languages and grace.\\
Marine-trained in calm discretion,\\
he reads the hidden face.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The blast was past, he left the corps,\\
He walked from fields of fire;\\
A quiet guard, a private shield,\\
Became his new attire.

\songpart{Chorus}
Khamid Khaled, his job's to understand\\
where the threats come, where the heat goes.\\
Aware of the situation, sharp and quiet.\\
To protect, to shield, before that blows.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A Colombian man in Miami’s trade,\\
Who walked a narrow line;\\
His new boss balanced darkened tides,\\
With risks between each sign.

A sudden storm of gunfire came,\\
His training took control;\\
He pulled him out, away from death,\\
And kept the chaos whole.

He knew the law, the games it played,\\
The rules behind the fight;\\
Not one to run from consequence,\\
But seek the proper light.

Müller in Miami,\\
Through shadows he drove;\\
A steady right hand,\\

Protect first, escape fast,\\
Remove him from fear.

From roads through the city,\\
To meetings unknown;\\
Khamid stayed beside him,\\
Never alone.
