\songtitle{La frazada}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}{susana-bianchi}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on Susana Bianchi's monologue in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Milonga-rock with driving guitar stabs and pulsing bombo legüero, straight 4/4 and a dusty milonga swing; verse stays sparse with voice, acoustic guitar, and low bass, pre-chorus opens with handclaps and rising electric scrapes, chorus lands fuller with distorted guitars and group responses, Female Argentine vocals, close-mic in verses, gritty doubles on hooks, a few whispered asides, tape flutter and wind-like swells between sections, warm and raw mix, punta, rock
\end{style}
