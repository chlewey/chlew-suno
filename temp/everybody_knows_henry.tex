\songtitle{Everybody Knows Henry}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soul}\tag{ricochets}\tag{henry-little}
\begin{notes}
Based on characters and scenes from \emph{Ricochets: When destinies overlap} by Carlos Thompson.\\
Character: Henry Little
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
70s soul, cinematic soul, 84 BPM, baritone lead vocals, close-mic vocals, stacked backing vocals, muted electric bass, electric piano comping, wah guitar fills, brush drum kit, bass clarinet counterlines, hand percussion accents, plate reverb, tape saturation, warm analog mix, sly confidence, world-weary resolve
