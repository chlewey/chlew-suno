\begin{lyrics}
\annotation{In the manner of an 11th-century scop, alliterative verse with caesura}\\
Hwæt! In þām ārīdum eardum, þǣr regn is rǣre and wæter wēl weorþ,\\
se mǣsta mægen manna is gāst-gifu, glēaw and gōd.\\
Abraham æt his heorþe, hālignes heold,\\
and fremdan fēran fægere frēode, folde and fōd.

Swā Lot on Sodoma, sēlesta sinces weard,\\
englas under īren-hūse in-fēng, fæste fægnode.
Þā burh-waras, bealwe and brōgan full,\\
cuman cȳðan woldon, cwealm and unriht rǣran.

Nā for hæmed-hete, ac for giefu-gylt grim,\\
for brecan beorhte bēagas blētsunge,\\
for weorþan wēl-cuman tō wræcce and wīte.\\
Lot, lēof and lār-fæst, his dohtor dēore\\
bead on beornas blīðe, þæt gystas gescylde.

Þā fȳr from heofenum, fǣr and fǣrlic,\\
brim-stān and bǣl, burga forbærnde.\\
Dēad-sǣ dūne līð, dēop and drēorig,\\
tācen tō tǣcanne þæt giefu is gōd.

Ne is þis sōþ spell of stāne and strǣle,\\
ac sǣd and sōþ-sagu, sīð and lar.\\
On ealdum ēðum, on īsigum eardum,\\
healdan giefu gystes, gōd and glēawlic.

\annotation{Gied-refrain}\\
Giefu gystes glēawa, gōd under heofenum!\\
Se þe breceþ bēagas blīðra manna,\\
se sceal in fȳre fēran, fǣrlic wīte þolian.\\
Healdan hūs and hlāf, hāl and gesund!
\end{lyrics}
