\tag*{2026}\tag{musical-theater}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Gemini Notebook summary of chapter 4 (2008--2013) of Carlos Thompson's memoir \emph{A Life in World Events}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Jazz musical theater with brushed drums, upright bass, muted piano clusters, and sly horn stabs; verse rides a wry walking swing, pre-chorus tightens into spoken-sung tension, and chorus opens into theatrical held notes and ensemble-like doubles, Close-mic male baritone with intimate breath, occasional scooped phrases, and short delay throws on key words, Ear candy includes page-turn clicks, a soft cymbal swell into the chorus, and a final piano turnaround, Mix is warm, smoky, and front-and-center, with crisp articulation and velvet low end, jazz
\end{style}
