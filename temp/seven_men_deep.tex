\begin{style}
Dancehall with a rolling dembow groove, sharp snare snaps, bouncing sub bass, and skank guitar on the offbeat; verse rides sparse with clipped patois-inflected lines, pre-chorus lifts with stacked chants and rising percussion, chorus drops wide with gang vocals and a chantable hook, Lead vocal is gritty and close-mic with doubles on key words, ad-lib puns panned wide, plus reverse swells, rim-roll fills, and a horn stab into the final chorus, Bright, punchy mix with warm low end and club-ready space, world, theme, dancehall, distinct, emotional, melancholy
\end{style}
