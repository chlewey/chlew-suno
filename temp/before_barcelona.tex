\songtitle{Before Barcelona}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabi-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a biography of character Gabriele "Gabi" Wiater from \emph{Thomas Entourage} universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop, mid-tempo at 127 BPM with a locked mechanical pulse, syncopated claps, churning synth bass, and restrained female lead vocals; verse keeps it close and conversational, pre-chorus tightens with filtered percussion and rising noise, chorus opens into gang choir echoes and stacked doubles on the hook, Reversed swells, metallic ticks, and a soft impact before each chorus; sleek, tense, glossy mix with an intimate center and wide choir edges, mellow, pop, industrial
\end{style}
