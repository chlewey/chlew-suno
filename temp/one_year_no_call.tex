\songtitle{One Year No Call}

\tag*{2026}\tag{country-rock}\tag{ricochets}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on an excerpt from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Country-rock with a steady mid-tempo backbeat, twangy electric guitar, acoustic strums, pedal steel fills, and a driving bass line; verse stays conversational and close-mic, pre-chorus lifts with harmony stacks and snare build, chorus opens wide with gang vocals on the hook and short delay throws on the name, Bridge drops to just acoustic and vocal, then final chorus comes back bigger with lead-guitar licks, handclaps, and a warm, radio-bright mix, rock, country
\end{style}
