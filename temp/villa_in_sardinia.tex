\songtitle{Villa in Sardinia}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{tallinn-arc}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO, based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas’ Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with a fast driving acoustic strum, upright bass bounce, brushed snare, and bright mandolin accents; verse stays close-mic and conversational, pre-chorus lifts with harmonies and handclaps, chorus opens wider with gang vocals on the hook, Light tambourine on the backbeat, occasional fiddle fills between lines, guitar pick noise and room tone kept upfront for an intimate, energetic mix, folk, light, rock
\end{style}
