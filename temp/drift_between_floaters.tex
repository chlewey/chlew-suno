\songtitle{Drift Between Floaters}

\tag*{2022}\tag{symphonic-ballad}\tag{bachees}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a small essay on the Bachees planet and biology. The Bachees are a fictional alien species that live in a gas giant.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Symphonic ballad with layered vocals, slow-evolving strings, harp pulses, low brass swells, and female vocals; verse stays intimate with solo piano and cellos, pre-chorus opens into wordless choir, chorus blooms with stacked harmonies and broad orchestral rises, bridge strips to winds and close-mic lead before a final lift; subtle reversed swells, distant chimes, and shimmering choir tails; wide, cinematic, and luminous mix, ballad, dynamic, dynamics, light
\end{style}
