\songtitle{Seven Archetypes}

\tag*{2026}
\begin{notes}
Written by SUNO based on a summary by Claude by examining image generating prompts by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Hip-hop over a scratched symphonic loop with dusty strings, hard boom-bap drums, and a tense head-nod swing; verse rides chopped violins and tight kick-snare, pre-chorus strips to bowed bass and vinyl crackle, chorus opens with stabbed orchestra hits and a gang-vocal anchor, Lead vocal is sharp and conversational with doubled punchlines, ad-lib echoes, and brief filter sweeps; ear candy includes record scratches, reversed string swells, and a final brass lift, Mix is gritty, cinematic, and front-facing, drama, romantic, world, emotional, hip-hop, light, warm, beautiful, sensual
\end{style}
