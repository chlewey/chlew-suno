\songpart{Verse 1}
Peter walked down the street\\
singing loud with steady beat\\
he saw a red car drive by\\
with a pretty girl who catched his eye

Peter reached the store and walked in\\
the man in the counter had a wide grin\\
some apple juice and a candy bar\\
Peter bought and then a peanut jar

\songpart{Chorus}
Peter, peter, city walk\\
some adventures some mundane\\
become the king of a rock\\
and spot some distant train

\songpart{Verse 2}
Peter walk out into the sunny day\\
and headed to the park to play\\
one dog, two dogs, three he counted\\
before in the monkey bars he mounted

Peter played trhough the afternoon\\
still daylight but up was the moon\\
now was time to go back home\\
before it was dark in the sky dome

\songpart{Chorus}
Peter, Peter, city walk\\
some adventures some mundane\\
become the king of a rock\\
and spot some distant plane

\songpart{Outro}
Peter came back home by sunset\\
counting the adventures he met\\
thinking about the girl in the red car\\
and wishing her to the first star
