\songtitle{What Thomas Gave}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Part of Gabriele Wiater's biographical series in the \emph{Thomas Entourage} narrative universe. Lyrics by SUNO based on a section of Gabi's biography cowritten between Carlos Thompson and Claude.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop with a mid-tempo 127 BPM pulse, clipped four-on-the-floor kick, metallic hand percussion, and a rubbery synth bass; verse stays restrained and close-mic, pre-chorus opens with rising noise and stacked fragments, chorus lands on gang-choir echoes and tight doubled lead, Reversed swells, chain-rattle fills, and a glassy top-line keep it sleek, tense, and glossy, pop, industrial, mellow
\end{style}
