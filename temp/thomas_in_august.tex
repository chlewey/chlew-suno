\songtitle{Thomas In August}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Part of Gabriele Wiater's biographical series in the \emph{Thomas Entourage} narrative universe. Lyrics by SUNO based on a section of Gabi's biography cowritten between Carlos Thompson and Claude.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop with a mid-tempo 127 BPM pulse, syncopated machine-kick and metallic clanks; verse stays close-mic and restrained over a sparse pulse, pre-chorus opens with filtered synth lift and clipped handclap tension, chorus hits with stacked gang choir echoes and a surging hook line, Female lead stays mellow and refrained, with fast flowing phrasing, delay throws on key words, reversed swells into each section, Bright, punchy, and cold-glossed with a hard-edged club sheen, natural, mellow, industrial, pop
\end{style}
