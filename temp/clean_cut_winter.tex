\songtitle{Clean Cut Winter}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Part of Gabriele Wiater's biographical series in the \emph{Thomas Entourage} narrative universe. Lyrics by SUNO based on a section of Gabi's biography cowritten between Carlos Thompson and Claude.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop with a mid-tempo 127 BPM pulse, tight syncopated kick patterns, metallic percussion ticks, and a sparse verse-to-lift arrangement; verses stay close and restrained, pre-chorus opens with filtered noise and low synth pressure, chorus hits with stacked stabs and a wider hook, Female lead vocal is intimate and fast-phrased with doubled hook lines, whispered ad-libs, and short delay throws on key words, Reversed swells, cold room-tone, and tape-stop glitches bridge sections; punchy, glossy, industrial-tinged mix with sharp low-end focus, industrial, low, vocal, simple, dynamic, romantic, pop, mellow, clean
\end{style}
