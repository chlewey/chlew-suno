\songtitle{Seidenstraße}

\tag*{2026}C\tag{roads}
\begin{notes}
Part of the geography cycles series, lyrics by SUNO, on summaries by Grok, from Claud collaborations with Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dubstep hymn with female lead and deep throat-sung chants, built on half-time wobble, ritual drums, and Tibetan horns, dungchen, and dranyen accents; verse rides sparse sub pulses and frame drum, pre-chorus strips to voice and drone, chorus hits with huge bass, stacked female harmonies, and monk-gang chants, Bridge opens to wind chimes, reversed gongs, and a low throat-sung call, Bright-cinematic low end with sacred, echoing space, black death, throat singing, theme, dubstep
\end{style}

Not one road,\\
not one thread.\\
A web of markets,\\
rivers, dust, and bread.

From Mediterranean stone\\
to China’s western gates,\\
cities breathed the trade\\
for fifteen hundred years.
