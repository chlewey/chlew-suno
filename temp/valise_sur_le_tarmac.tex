\songtitle{Valise sur le tarmac}

\tag*{2026}\tag{chanson-francaise}\tag{ricochets}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on an excerpt from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chanson française with mezzo vocals, a swaying mid-tempo groove and brushed drums; verse stays close and conversational over accordion, muted piano, and upright bass, pre-chorus opens with strings and a rising backing hum, chorus lands on a singable refrain with doubled lead and soft gang harmonies, Airport ambience, suitcase wheels, and a short string lift between sections; intimate, glossy, and cinematic, light, low, chanson
\end{style}
