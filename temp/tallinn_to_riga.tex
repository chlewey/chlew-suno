\songtitle{Tallinn To Riga}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{tallinn-arc}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO, based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas’ Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with brisk acoustic strum, tambourine snap, upright bass pulse, and handclaps on the backbeat; verse rides close-mic baritone with dry-eyed storytelling, pre-chorus opens into stacked oohs, chorus hits with gang vowels and a stomp-along anchor, Bridge drops to guitar and voice, then final chorus lifts with mandolin runs, ferry-noise swells, and warm room reverb; bright, intimate, road-worn mix, rock, natural, light, folk
\end{style}
