\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on section X of \emph{Rotterdam Leg}, a story from \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative unisverse by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial art-pop with female lead; jittery metallic percussion, pulsing synth bass, and clipped arpeggios; verse rides sparse machine ticks and close-mic storytelling, pre-chorus tightens with rising filter noise and detuned bell shards, chorus opens into a blunt chant with stacked doubles and sharp delay throws on the last word; bridge drops to dry vocal and conveyor-belt clanks before the final chorus returns bigger, Intimate, dry, bright-and-hard mix with cold steel sheen, faint, pop, industrial
\end{style}
