\songtitle{Copper-Fire Crown}

\tag*{2026}
\begin{notes}
Written by SUNO based on a summary by Claude by examining image generating prompts by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber pop, fast-paced and rapid-fire with clipped pizzicato strings, harpsichord snaps, tight kick pulses, and darting woodwind riffs; verse races in near-spoken syllables, pre-chorus opens into stacked harmonies and rising cello, chorus lands on a bright unison hook with wordless ooohs, Quick harp glissandos, reverse string swells, and sparkling percussion fills between lines; crisp, academic, and gleaming in the mix, rich, chamber pop, rapid, beautiful, emotional
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
She walks in copper-fire\\
Not hair, but a blaze with a name\\
Pale skin like a struck bell\\
Lit from some ancient flame\\
Her eyes hold a ruined kingdom\\
And don't even look away\\
She wears her grief like velvet\\
And makes it look like praise

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Beauty, then danger\\
Then beauty again\\
A hand on the sword\\
And a wound in the same breath\\
You lean in close\\
She does not retreat\\
She is the hush\\
Before a myth learns to speak
