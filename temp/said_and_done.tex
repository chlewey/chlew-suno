\songtitle{Said and Done}

\tag*{2026}
\begin{notes}
Adaptation to country-blues by Claude of \emph{Secreto de padre}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
blues country, outlaw country, slow blues shuffle, driving quarter-note chorus, honky-tonk two-step, spoken intro, weathered baritone, slide guitar licks, brushed snare, steel guitar bends, fiddle leads, upright bass walk, room mic vocal, tape saturation, plate reverb, gated reverb snare, dynamic tempo shifts, defiant resolve, male baritone vocals
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro – spoken, low and slow, guitar resting on one chord}
We need to talk, son — you, the new boy at my table,\\
the one I trained myself, the one I trust.

\songpart{Verse 1 – slow blues shuffle, brushed snare, slide guitar, loose meter}
I see her come home late, that dreamy kind of smile,\\
eyes lit up like Sunday, gone for a long while.\\
I raised that girl myself, boy, I know her every tell,\\
I know the sound of trouble dressed up as something swell.\\
You know she's got a fella, some kid from the choir hall,\\
young love she don't take serious, not serious at all.\\
She's got her little secrets, I've always known that's true,\\
and in this game of hide-and-seek… that other man is you.

\songpart{Chorus – tempo lifts, drums switch from shuffle to driving quarter-note pulse}
She's grown now, she's gonna fly,\\
don't you dare make my baby cry.\\
I see it all, I ain't no fool,\\
her secret's safe — but so are you.\\
So watch your step and watch your hands,\\
a father's patience only stands\\
so long, so far, so close to the line —\\
don't you cross it, boy, you'll know it's mine.

\songpart{Verse 2 – back to slow blues, heavier, vocal more weathered}
It hurts me some to watch this dance I can't allow,\\
don't sit right with me, but I've made my peace somehow.
'Cause you're the one I trained, son, the one I call my own,\\
and she's my blood — she's always known how to hold her own.\\
I've got to loosen this old grip, let her fly on her own wings,\\
even though my gut's still screaming, and my heart still stings.\\
So I'm asking one thing only, boy, before we shake on this —\\
treat her like she's something precious, don't you break what shouldn't be missed.

\songpart{Chorus – tempo lifts, drums switch from shuffle to driving quarter-note pulse}
She's grown now, she's gonna fly,\\
don't you dare make my baby cry.\\
I see it all, I ain't no fool,\\
her secret's safe — but so are you.\\
So watch your step and watch your hands,\\
a father's patience only stands\\
so long, so far, so close to the line —\\
don't you cross it, boy, you'll know it's mine.

\songpart{Bridge – honky-tonk two-step, brighter key, fiddle and upright bass, almost a different song}
Now I ain't her warden, I ain't her judge,\\
I ain't gonna stand here holding a grudge.\\
She's a grown woman, she'll learn as she goes,\\
fall on her face and get up, I suppose.\\
But hear me good now, hear me clear —\\
there's a difference between a chance and a tear.\\
You give her the first, I'll look away,\\
you give her the second, you won't see another day.

\annotation{Spoken-sung breakdown – stripped to just kick drum and voice, climax}\\
I know she writes you letters that she don't dare to send,\\
I know you clear your phone out every time we're in.\\
I know she wears your jacket when she thinks I ain't around,\\
but she shakes your hand all formal when I'm standing on this ground.\\
If you look at her the way I see you look at her,\\
how could I not know exactly what's occurring?\\
So if one day she comes home crying,\\
and I find out that you're the reason why —\\
I swear this partnership dies out in the open road,\\
and this secret won't stay quiet, it'll be the only thing folks know.

\songpart{Final Chorus – full tempo, full band, both feels colliding — drums driving, slide guitar wailing underneath}
She's grown now, she's gonna fly,\\
don't you dare make my baby cry.\\
I see it all, I ain't no fool,\\
her secret's safe — but so are you.\\
So watch your step and watch your hands,\\
a father's patience only stands\\
so long, so far, so close to the line —\\
and what's been done is done… but the line's still mine.

\songpart{Outro – instrumental fade, steel guitar, hard stop}
\annotation{Spoken, low:} That's the deal.
\annotation{sharp cut, two seconds of silence, one last hanging chord}
\end{lyrics}
