\songtitle{Semestermap in Tallinn}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{tallinn-arc}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO, based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas' Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with fast acoustic strumming, driving kick drum, handclap lifts, and a close-mic baritone lead; verse rides a nimble four-on-the-floor pulse, pre-chorus opens with stacked harmony and tom rolls, chorus hits with gang vocals and bright mandolin churn; bridge drops to voice, bass, and a brushed snare before the last lift, Warm, punchy mix with room air, tape flutter, and little accordion and whistle fills between lines, rock, medieval, light, folk
\end{style}
