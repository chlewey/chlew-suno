French fast-paced pop-rap with driving syncopated percussion and tight bass pulses; verse races through documentary-style detail, pre-chorus tightens with rising vocal stacks and clipped syllables, chorus hits with gang-style unisons and short chantable lines, Use doubled lead in verses, call-and-response backing on key names, quick risers, reversed swells, and sharp stop-start edits, Bright, punchy, urgent mix with a gritty edge
