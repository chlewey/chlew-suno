\begin{style}
Bossa nova with brushed percussion, warm nylon-string guitar, and supple bass in a swaying 2/4 feel; verse stays intimate with airy tenor lead and soft chord changes, pre-chorus lifts with layered vocal oohs and a bass walk, chorus opens with gentle gang harmonies and a hummed guitar reply, Add shell-like shaker accents, a tide-swell riser into the hook, and a brief flute lick between lines, Mellow, sunlit, close-mic mix with glossy warmth, male vocals, bright, light, mellow, sensual, deep, harmony, simple, warm, tropical
\end{style}
