\songtitle{Bachees of the Deep}

\tag*{2022}\tag{bachees}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a small essay on the Bachees planet and biology. The Bachees are a fictional alien species that live in a gas giant.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Symphonic ballad with sweeping strings, harp arpeggios, low brass pulses, and layered choral vocals; verse stays intimate with solo voice and soft timpani, pre-chorus lifts through rising woodwinds and stacked harmonies, chorus blooms in wide strings and wordless choir, bridge drops to distant piano and haloed vocal doubles before a final orchestral swell, Rich cinematic mix, glossy and vast, with glimmering transitions, reversed swells, and celesta sparkles between phrases, light, ballad, dynamic, dynamics
\end{style}
