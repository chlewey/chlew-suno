\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO, based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas’ Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with fast acoustic strums, upright bass push, and a crisp kick-snare backbeat; verse rides close-mic baritone over sparing guitar and hand percussion, pre-chorus opens with stacked harmonies and a climbing mandolin figure, chorus hits with gang vowels and a fuller drum kit, bridge drops to voice and picked guitar before the final lift, Claire-style scene details, short double-tracked lines, delay throws on place names, vinyl crackle and a reversed swell into the last chorus, bright but earthy mix with intimate room tone, loud, folk, light, world, warm, rock, reading
\end{style}
