\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a biography of character Gabriele "Gabi" Wiater from \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} universe by Carlos Thompson.

\begin{style}
Industrial pop, mid-tempo 127 BPM with clipped four-on-the-floor kicks, metallic percussion, and a pulsing synth bass; verse rides restrained and close, pre-chorus tightens with rising noise and snapped claps, chorus blooms into gang choir echoes and stacked doubles, Airy female lead, soft but rapid phrasing, bright punchy mix, with reversed swells and hard filter lifts into each drop, pop, mellow, industrial
\end{style}
