\tag*{2026}\tag{english-lyrics}\tag{musical-theater}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a summary by Gemini Notebook of Carlos Thompson's memoir "A Life in World Events."
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie-rock musical theater with driving mid-tempo strum, tom-heavy build, and a stomping piano under close-mic male baritone vocals; verse stays conversational and detailed, pre-chorus lifts with harmony stacks and a restrained drum push, chorus opens into theatrical gang vocals and a memorable chant, bridge pulls back to fingerpicked guitar and spoken confessional phrasing before the final chorus returns bigger, Ear candy includes reverse cymbal swells, short delay throws on key names, and a tape-worn synth pad under the last lift, Warm, present, and slightly theatrical mix with punchy drums and intimate vocal close-up, country, musical, rock
\end{style}
