\songtitle{Tucanito to Tallinn}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{tallinn-arc}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO, based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas' Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with brisk strummed acoustic guitar, driving kick drum, and bouncing bass line; verse rides close-mic baritone on tight picked chords, pre-chorus opens with handclaps and a rising harmony, chorus lands with gang vocals and wide electric guitars, Bridge drops to voice and fingerpicked guitar, then final chorus adds mandolin fizz, tambourine lift, and a short instrumental turnaround, Intimate, warm, and punchy with room tone and crisp vocal doubles on the hook, light, world, rock, folk
\end{style}
