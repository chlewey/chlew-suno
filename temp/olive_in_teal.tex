\begin{style}
1970s disco with four-on-the-floor kick, syncopated bass guitar, clipped rhythm guitar, conga pops, and a bright string section; verse rides smooth and watchful, pre-chorus strips to bass and handclaps, chorus opens with stacked female group vocals and brass hits. Lead vocal is sleek and close-mic with cheeky ad-libs, short delay throws on the hook, and glossy crowd responses. Mirror-ball shimmer, filtered string risers, and a tambourine lift into each chorus; mix is warm, polished, and punchy, disco, romantic, clean, warm, light, contemporary
\end{style}
