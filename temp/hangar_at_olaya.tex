\songtitle{Hangar at Olaya}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ricochets}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on an excerpt from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chanson française with a waltzing mid-tempo pulse, brushed drums and accordion-guitar interplay; verse 1 paints the tarmac in close detail, pre-chorus tightens with rising strings and a pulsing bass line, chorus opens into a singable refrain with mezzo vocal doubles and soft group responses, Add airport PA static, a delayed piano figure, and a small horn swell before the final chorus, Mix is intimate, velvety, and slightly cinematic, light, chanson, low
\end{style}
