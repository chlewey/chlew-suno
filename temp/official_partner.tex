\songtitle{Official Partner}

\tag*{2026}\tag{industrial-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Part of Gabriele Wiater's biographical series in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe. Lyrics by SUNO based on a section of Gabi's biography co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop, mid-tempo 127 BPM with a clipped four-on-the-floor pulse, metallic percussion accents, rubbery sub-bass, and a restrained female lead that moves in tight fast phrases; verses stay sparse and intimate, pre-chorus opens with filtered noise and stacked breath doubles, chorus lands with gang-choir echoes on the hook phrase, sidechain swells, and a cold synth lift, Bright, punchy mix with an industrial edge and close-mic vocal detail, industrial, pop, mellow, dynamic
\end{style}
