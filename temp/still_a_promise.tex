\songtitle{Still A Promise}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabi-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a biography of character Gabriele "Gabi" Wiater from \emph{Thomas Entourage} universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop with a mid-tempo 127 BPM pulse, tight syncopated kick patterns and metallic percussion; verse stays sparse and intimate with close-mic female lead and clipped phrases, pre-chorus adds filtered synth pressure and rising machine hiss, chorus opens into a punchy hook with stacked doubles and short gang responses, Bridge pulls back to dry drums and a lone bass pulse, then final chorus returns with sharper claps, glitch sparks, and a cold bright mix with industrial edge, dynamic, mellow, industrial, vocal, football, pop, punta, world
\end{style}
