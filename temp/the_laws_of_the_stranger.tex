\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro - spoken}
Where parched winds howl through barren stone,\\
And whisper dread of old,\\
A binding code is etched in bone,\\
By ancient tongues foretold.\\
Beneath the veil of virtue's guise,\\
The laws of hearth remain,\\
To guard the guest beneath the skies,\\
From shadow and from stain.

\songpart{Verse 1 - The Sacrilege of the Twin Cities - sung, warning}
Not by the sins of flesh alone,\\
Did fire consume the plain,\\
But that the sacred seeds were sown,\\
With sacrilege and pain.\\
Within those towns where shadows danced,\\
In dark, malevolent glee,\\
The desperate stranger stood entranced,\\
Before man's cruelty.\\
They broke the bond, they scorned the rite,\\
And mocked the ancient crown,\\
Invoking from the realms of night,\\
A wrath to burn them down.

\songpart{Verse 2 - The Grotesque Offering - concern, advisory}
Behold the host, a shadowed ghost,\\
Beset by storm and fate,\\
Who stood against a raging host,\\
Before his shaking gate.\\
To save the stranger from the knife,\\
And keep the pact divine,\\
He offered up his daughters' life—\\
The branches of his vine.\\
Oh, dark and grim the altar stood,\\
Where ancient customs bleed,\\
A grotesque price of flesh and blood,\\
To sate a primal need.

\songpart{Verse 3 - The Cosmic Void - dreath, urgent}
Peer down into the black abyss,\\
If your frail heart so dare,\\
No mortal spite could spawn all this,\\
But horrors breeding there.\\
For cosmic laws were rent in twain,\\
Beneath the sulfur sky,\\
And truths that warp the human brain,\\
In deeper darkness lie.

\songpart{Verse 4 - The Ashen Lesson - calmetr contained}
The Dead Sea stirs with bitter brine,\\
Where judgment left its scar,\\
A harrowing and grim design,\\
To warn us from afar.\\
No idle myth, no phantom dream,\\
But warnings carved in stone:\\
Abide the laws, or hear the scream,\\
Of entities unknown.\\
For those who break the stranger's bread,\\
Then turn their blade to smite,\\
Shall join the lost, forgotten dead,\\
In everlasting night.
\end{lyrics}
