\songpart{Verse 1}
He read the file before he spoke —\\
a witness's word was all it took.\\
Not quite the smuggler, not quite clean,\\
a man too smart to ask what it could mean.

He stepped outside and let it sit,\\
left the lawyer to explain the bit.\\
Came back in to lay the terms:\\
walk out free, and pay in names.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No promise here of absolution,\\
no pretending he was pure —\\
just a chance to change the ending,\\
just a bargain with a cure.

\songpart{Chorus}
Armando Perez called him an asset first —\\
the mission always came before the man.\\
But every piece has blood and history,\\
every number left its mark on the hand.

You give us what the world is hiding,\\
we give you what we still defend.\\
It's not mercy, and it's not justice —\\
it's the word Perez keeps till the end.

\songpart{Verse 2}
A girl was gone, MDPD moved fast —\\

The room had eyes, but the room had no ears —\\
they spoke in code about what no one could hear.

He left with words of cooperation,\\
no claim, no custody, no name to clear.\\
But one loose thread kept pulling at him —\\
evidence that made no sense at all.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}

just let the evidence unravel on its own.\\
No fingerprint of the agency behind it —\\
the charges dropped, and the asset walked alone.

\songpart{Bridge}
Someone came asking to settle a score —\\
a voice connected to that old case before.\\
He wouldn't trade the asset for a grudge,\\
not for a war that wasn't his to judge.

The asset's years had paid in kind,\\
the new voice brought intel of its own design.\\

\songpart{Outro}
He kept the money that ran clean,\\
kept the name still standing in between.\\
The company that hid the trade —\\
that was the only price he paid.

"Thank you. This is over now" —\\
the closing words, and nothing more.\\
Not a clean walk, not fully free,\\
just a debt called settled, let it be.

Perez signed his part and walked away,\\
the file passed to someone else's hands.\\
What came after wasn't his to watch —\\
the asset's story, never clean.
