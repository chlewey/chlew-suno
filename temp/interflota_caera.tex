\begin{style}
Cinematic pop-rock trailer theme with driving mid-tempo drums, pulsing guitars, and layered gang vocals; verse rides tense low-register guitars and toms, pre-chorus strips to snare pulse and rising synths, chorus hits with stacked octave harmonies and big chant hooks, Bridge drops to a half-time vocal lead with distant choir, then final chorus returns with brass hits, reverse swells, and a wide, punchy arena mix, rock, theme, pop
\end{style}
