\songtitle{Lilac in Utrecht}

\tag*{2026}\tag{vispop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{erika-czajka}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the profile of Erika Czajka in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Vispop with bright nyckelharpa motifs and soaring violin lines over a steady midtempo pulse; verse stays intimate with fingerpicked rhythm and spare plucks, pre-chorus lifts into stacked female harmonies and a rising bow figure, chorus opens wide with layered lead doubles and chanty oohs, Transitions use reversed string swells, soft hand percussion, and a glimmering top-line, Clear, polished, airy mix, environmental, warm, dynamics
\end{style}
