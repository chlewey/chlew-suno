\begin{style}
Chamber pop ballad, mid-tempo with brushed drums, piano, string quartet swells, and a melodic sax solo outro; verse stays intimate and restrained, pre-chorus opens with rising cello and suspended harmony, chorus blooms with lush strings and breathy double-tracked vocals, bridge drops to piano and low viola before the final lift, Close-mic female vocal, tender vibrato, soft doubles on the hook, sax answers the last line with smoky delay throws, polished cinematic mix with warm mids and a wide, glowing low end, dramatic, ballad, deep, flat, simple
\end{style}
