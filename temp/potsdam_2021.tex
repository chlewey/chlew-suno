\songtitle{Potsdam 2021}

\tag*{2026}\tag{industrial-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{gabriele-wiater}
\begin{notes}
Part of Gabriele Wiater's biographical series in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe. Lyrics by SUNO based on a section of Gabi's biography co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial pop, mid-tempo 127 BPM with a restrained female lead, clipped syncopated percussion, rubbery bass pulses, and a continuous vocal flow; verse stays close-mic and controlled, pre-chorus opens with rising harmonies, chorus lands on gang-choir echoes and stacked doubles, Add filtered machine sweeps, metallic ticks between phrases, and a glossy-but-tense mix with a clean low end, pop, industrial, healing, mellow, minimal
\end{style}
