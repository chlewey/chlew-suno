\songtitle{The Man I Taught You To Be}

\tag*{2026}
\begin{notes}
Adaptation to country-blues by Grok of \emph{Secreto de padre}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
country blues, spoken intro, low male voice, acoustic guitar fingerpicking, fiddle counterlines, banjo rolls, player piano accents, brushed snare, upright bass, porch percussion, tape saturation, plate reverb, live room ambience, slow blues shuffle, half-time bridge, full band chorus, bittersweet restraint, father-son tension
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro - spoken, low male voice, acoustic guitar, slow blues shuffle}

Son, I ain't here to fight you,\\
and I ain't here to judge.\\
But there's something you and me\\
we gotta talk about.

\songpart{Verse 1}

I saw her coming home late,\\
with that smile she couldn't hide,\\
that look a father recognizes\\
when there's someone on her mind.

I know my girl, I raised her,\\
I know the way she moves,\\
I know when she's keeping secrets,\\
I know when she's got something to lose.

You know she had a boyfriend,\\
that college kid she used to see,\\
a young love she was holding onto,\\
but it wasn't where her heart would be.

I watched the two of you pretending,\\
like I couldn't read the signs,\\
but I know my daughter better\\
than she knows I read her eyes.

\songpart{Chorus}

She ain't a child I can hold back,\\
she's a woman making her own way.\\
I ain't got the right to choose her roads,\\
or tell her who she should love today.

And I ain't standing here as a preacher,\\
Lord knows I got my own scars too.\\
I'm just asking you, son,\\
remember who believed in you.

Don't break her heart,\\
don't make her cry.\\
Don't turn something beautiful\\
into something she survives.

\songpart{Verse 2}

I remember when you started,\\
when you were learning the trade,\\
I showed you every door I could,\\
gave you every chance I made.

You were the kid with a hungry heart,\\
trying to find your place,\\
and I was proud to watch you grow,\\
proud to see you earn your name.

So don't think I don't understand it,\\
don't think I don't know desire.\\
Some things happen before we know it,\\
some sparks turn into fire.

But she's my blood, she's my daughter,\\
and she's stronger than you think.\\
I can't keep her from the world,\\
but I can ask you how you treat it.

\songpart{Chorus}

She ain't a child I can hold back,\\
she's a woman making her own way.\\
I ain't got the right to choose her roads,\\
or tell her who she should love today.

And I ain't standing here as a preacher,\\
Lord knows I got my own scars too.\\
I'm just asking you, son,\\
remember who believed in you.

Don't break her heart,\\
don't make her cry.\\
Don't leave her picking up pieces\\
of a love that passed her by.

\songpart{Bridge - stripped down, voice and guitar}

I know she chose you,\\
and I know she chose me too.\\
One of us is the man she loves,\\
one of us is the man who raised her through.

Maybe I can't protect her forever,\\
maybe I never could.\\
But if you ever loved her,\\
you better love her like you should.

\songpart{Final Chorus - full band, drums enter}

She ain't a child I can hold back,\\
she's a woman standing tall.\\
I won't build a cage around her,\\
I won't be the wall.

But if you hurt her,\\
if you make her regret,\\
the boy I taught and trusted\\
is a man I won't forget.

So take care of her heart,\\
take care of what you hold.\\
Some things are worth more\\
than anything you can own.

\songpart{Outro - spoken}
I don't need you to be perfect.

I just need you to be worthy.
\end{lyrics}
