\songtitle{Tallinn By January}

\tag*{2026}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{tallinn-arc}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO, based on the story line of Thomas García's Tallinn Arc, from Carlos Thompson's narrative universe \emph{Thomas' Entourage}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with brisk acoustic strum and upright bass drive, light drums on the backbeat, close-mic baritone lead, warm gang harmonies in the chorus; verse rides fingerpicked guitar and walking bass, pre-chorus opens with tambourine lift and rising harmony, chorus hits with full strum and handclaps, Add short fiddle fills between lines, a brushed snare turn into each section, and a final chorus with doubled vocals and a small room reverb glow, bright and intimate mix, folk, rock, light
\end{style}
