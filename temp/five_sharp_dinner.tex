\tag*{2026}\tag{acoustic-punk}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{fiona-levit}\tag*{english-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on the fourth segment of the story \emph{A New York Night} by Carlos Thompson, %
within the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Acoustic punk with sophisti-pop sparkle, brisk swung strum and tight brushed percussion; female lead, verse stays close-mic and mellow over palm-muted guitar and warm electric color; pre-chorus opens with stacked harmonies and a rising bass walk; chorus hits with gang-vocal replies and chiming accents, Vinyl crackle into the first verse, a reversed swell before each chorus, airy delay throws on key lines, Bright, intimate, lightly glossy mix, pop, light, loud, flat, mellow, vocal, reading, punk
\end{style}
