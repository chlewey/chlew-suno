Vocal style: Emphasis through melody, harmony, phrasing, dynamics, tempo, articulation, and pitch variation rather than shouting or screaming. Flow & phrasing: Natural, connected vocal lines with smooth transitions between phrases. Avoid unnecessary pauses or line breaks; phrasing should follow punctuation, musical structure, or explicit formatting cues.

BPM/Tempo: Flexible. Comfortable with tempo changes, rubato passages, half-time sections, accelerations, and contrasting rhythmic feels within the same song.

Open to: Choral arrangements, vocal ensembles, glam influences, new-age textures, and unconventional genre combinations when they fit the song.

Accent and pronunciation: unless otherwise specified use Standard American English for English, and Central Colombian Spanish for Spanish. Allow for other accents to be specified.
