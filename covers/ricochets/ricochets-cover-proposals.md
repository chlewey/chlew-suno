# Ricochets

Everything on the desk is still orderly, hopeful, undisturbed — but a long, soft shadow falls diagonally across the table from just outside the frame, hinting at what's coming. ## Promotion

`covers/ricochets/promotion`

A bold, high-contrast cinematic title-sequence image: a single bullet frozen mid-ricochet in the air, its glowing amber trajectory line bouncing between two mirrored, half-lit city skylines that face each other across a dark void — an Andean mountain silhouette and the towers of Bogotá on one side, palm trees and glass towers of Miami on the other. The trajectory bends sharply at the frame's center where the bullet flashes brightest, implying impact and rebound. The palette is near-black noir with one hot amber-orange trail and cool blue city glow on each horizon. Poster-quality digital illustration, dramatic rim lighting, sense of motion and inevitability. Bold condensed sans-serif title "RICOCHETS" centered near the top, in the style of a film title card. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

## Canonical

`covers/ricochets/canonical`

An overhead photograph of an open case dossier on a worn metal desk under a single lamp, its papers and photographs spilling loosely from the folder. A one-way boarding pass from Bogotá to Miami is tucked half-under the folder's flap. Paperclipped to the top photograph is a grainy surveillance still showing only a woman's silhouette in a wide-brimmed hat and dark sunglasses, face obscured. A spent bullet casing rests upright in the folder's fold like a paperweight, and the corner of another photo — a wedding ring catching flash light — peeks out from beneath the stack. Warm desk-lamp light pools at the center, fading into cold shadow at the desk's edges. Photorealistic evidentiary still life, noir-procedural mood, shallow depth of field. The folder's tab carries a small typed label reading "CASE FILE — CANONICAL". Square 1:1 aspect ratio.
