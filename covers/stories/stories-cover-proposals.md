# Named Fictional Works

## Drift

`covers/stories/drift`

A moody overhead flatlay photograph of a kitchen table at night in a modest Midwestern American home, softly lit by a single warm pendant lamp from above. Two wineglasses sit on the worn wooden table — one full of red wine catching the light, the other nearly empty with a faint lipstick mark on the rim. Beside them, a smartphone lies face-up showing a half-typed text message on its glowing screen, and a plain gold wedding ring rests on a neatly folded cloth napkin. At the very edge of the frame, slightly out of focus, a stairwell descends into a basement where warm amber workshop light spills upward against the cool blue-gray tone of the kitchen. The composition is quiet and tense, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame camera with shallow depth of field, film grain, muted desaturated color grading evoking restrained domestic melancholy. No text or title overlay. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

## Eiran

`covers/stories/eiran`

A richly detailed candlelit character portrait of Eiran, an ancient slate-furred, silver-bearded cartographer-archer with clawed hands, hunched intently over a spread black-stone map at a wooden drafting table in a stone tower. A yew bow and a quiver of feathered arrows lean against his chair. His clawed fingertip touches the map, which glows faintly with cold teal-green ink at one torn edge, the lines doubling and bleeding into a ghostly translucent image of a cracked modern highway and salt flat breaking through from another world. Small partial sketch-portraits and map fragments are pinned loosely around the outer edge of the frame like a contact sheet, referencing a series of curated images. Warm sepia and amber candlelight dominate, contrasted with the eerie cold glow from the map. Painterly photorealistic fantasy illustration style, intricate textures on fur, parchment, and stone, cinematic depth. A small stamped serif title reading "EIRAN" at the top edge. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

## African Empire -- Monukae

`covers/stories/monukae`

A warm, candlelit overhead still-life photograph on a dark wooden table: a West African kora (harp-lute) resting diagonally across an open leather-bound merchant's ledger filled with handwritten tallies and ink sketches of ships. Beside it, a small ornate crown sits deliberately outside the main pool of warm light, half in shadow, unclaimed. Beneath the ledger, the edge of a hand-drawn trade map is visible, inked with routes connecting the Orinoco river, the Andes, and the Cantabrian coast. Deep gold, umber, and bronze tones dominate the palette, with a single cool silver highlight catching the crown's rim. Photorealistic, rich textures on aged leather, wood grain, and metal, dramatic single-source lighting reminiscent of Dutch still-life painting. A carved serif title stamped at the top reading "MONUKAE". Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

## Saulon

`covers/stories/saulon`

A cinematic interior shot inside a generation sleeper ship: a single open cryo-pod in the foreground, its curved viewport glowing with soft amber emergency light, looking out into a small round window at a distant, dust-veiled Earth dimming in the blackness of space. Nearby, a hand-lettered star chart is taped to a metal console, labeled "SAULON I" and "SAULON II" in worn ink. The ship's interior is cold blue-black metal and glass, condensation on surfaces, sparse indicator lights blinking faintly. The overall palette is dominated by deep space blacks and cold blues, broken only by the warm amber glow of the pod light. Photorealistic science-fiction concept art quality, moody and elegiac, vast sense of scale and isolation. No text overlay. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

## Scholer

## Bachees

`covers/stories/bachees`

A vast, awe-inspiring science-fiction concept-art image of a gas giant's atmosphere seen from within: immense swirling cloud bands in amber, rust, and cream, layered like a storm-wracked ocean of sky. Scattered throughout the cloud layers, at wildly different scales, drift the translucent silhouettes of enormous winged "floater" creatures — some tiny, some as large as moons — their forms glowing faintly from within. Several pale moons are dimly visible through the haze in the upper distance. The composition emphasizes overwhelming cosmic scale and quiet biological wonder, photorealistic digital matte-painting style, warm golden-hour lighting diffused through thick clouds. No text overlay. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.
