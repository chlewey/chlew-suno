\songtitle{Kasha Call}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk}\tag{instrumental}\label{sec:kasha-call}
\begin{notes}
Instrumental. An adaptation of a sound fragment of a theatrical representation of the Yonna dance: kasha drums answered by gaita macho and gaita hembra. \emph{Call of Lore} (page \pageref{sec:call-of-lore}) is a looser adaptation of the same fragment. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Traditional folk percussion and wind call-and-response; unprocessed acoustic recording; female-male unison calls; a measured pulse building into a kasha drum crescendo, then a sudden silence; kasha drums lead, answered by gaita macho and gaita hembra, with the drums pressing over the melody before a varied response and another drum call.
\end{style}
