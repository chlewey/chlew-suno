\begin{style}
Alternative R\&B with restrained post-punk arrangement; close-miked, dry production; dry drum kit, wiry melodic bass, clipped clean guitar; steady midtempo pulse; low conversational female mezzo, opening melody on “I can stay,” a full-beat pause before “I can wait,” stacked hook harmonies and layered octave doubles, final chorus voice brought forward, bass and drums dropped before the last lines.
