\songtitle{Keep Your Hands Kind}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{latin-jazz}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{zahra-moradi}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\label{sec:keep-your-hands-kind}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Lines Not Crossed} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, around Zahra's visit to Punta Espada in December 2026 -- Thomas, glad she is well with Mihai and Jan-Alan in Kraków, naming a want he will not act on; Gabi, who read his shoulders in the cabin dark and needed no confession. From the same story as \emph{Don't Mistake the Bridge for Home} (page \pageref{sec:dont-mistake-the-bridge-for-home}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock song colored by Latin jazz: steady acoustic-guitar strum and warm electric bass, syncopated hand percussion, brushed kit, piano montuno accents, lyrical trumpet and tenor sax fills, occasional fiddle answering the vocal. Medium-tempo, conversational verses opening into a harmonized duet chorus; a restrained bridge builds with layered percussion, then returns to a close, dry final refrain. Two distinct, intimate voices with natural phrasing, clear English diction, organic room sound and warm tape-like mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1: Thomas}
Michael steadies Gabi in New York,\\
Michael waits for Natalia at the door.\\
Mihai is Zahra’s husband now—\\
Funny how the names line up, no more.

\songpart{Verse 2: Thomas}
She teaches in Kraków, takes her seat\\
With those who speak for northern Iran.\\
A playful child, a hand in hers;\\
I hope her days are kind as they can.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus: Thomas}
I mean that, and I know it’s true;\\
Some guilt lets go when she is well.\\
Then comes the small, familiar ache:\\
I won’t know her taste again.

\songpart{Chorus: Duet}
I can want what I can’t have,\\
I can name the want and let it pass.\\
You can hold a wish without a claim,\\
You can feel the fire and keep your hands kind.\\
I’m not a machine, I’m not a saint;\\
I choose what love will never ask.

\songpart{Verse 3: Thomas}
I used to think detachment could be signed\\
At the threshold, neat and understood.\\
Estonia, fall and winter, nineteen—\\
A warm routine became a pillar of wood.\\
Then one bitter, accidental break\\
Put failure in the frame of every room.

\songpart{Verse 4: Gabi}
I read your shoulders in the cabin dark,\\
No question needed, no confession due.\\
I knew who stood behind your faraway eyes;\\
I knew a part of you was reaching through.\\
It didn’t lessen what we had that night.\\
Desire can be rough and still be true.

\songpart{Bridge: Duet}
Aurora taught me not to flinch\\
When the buried self comes into view.\\

They’re part of me, not orders to pursue.\\
And you can see me as I am,\\
Without pretending I’m untouched.
