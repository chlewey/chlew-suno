\songtitle{Lines Not Crossed}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{gaita}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{punta-espada}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{zahra-moradi}\label{sec:lines-not-crossed}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by DeepSeek based on \emph{Lines Not Crossed} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- Zahra visiting Thomas's project at Punta Espada in La Guajira with her husband Jan-Alan (December 2026): the old romance, a Wayuu council, a detour into the desert, and a kiss that stops short of the line. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 9 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock, yonna or chichamaya percussion opens alone before kasha drums join gaita macho and gaita hembra, with drum kit, electric guitars and electric bass; wide layered guitar tracks and tight percussion; mixed lead-and-chorus vocals build through the pre-choruses into a fuller final chorus; steady midtempo backbeat.
\end{style}
