\songtitle{Abulón (The Shadow Crown)}

\tag{african-empire}\tag{monukae}\tag{abulon}

\begin{notes}
Based on the \emph{Monukae} and \emph{Black Empire} production notes by Carlos Thompson, and particularly on the Character Abulón from \emph{Monukae}.

Lyrics by DeepSeek in interaction with Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Genre: Epic world / Orchestral folk / Griot ballad\\
Tempo: 75 BPM, builds to 90 BPM\\
Key: D minor → F major at final chorus\\
male vocals
\end{style}

\begin{lyrics}
\annotation{Genre: Epic world / Orchestral folk / Griot ballad}\\
\annotation{Tempo: 75 BPM, builds to 90 BPM}\\
\annotation{Key: D minor → F major at final chorus}

\songpart{Intro – spoken, low drums, kora}
He could have been king. %
He chose the shadow.

\songpart{Verse 1 – solo voice, sparse percussion}
From the fertile belt of the Sahel\\
Where the grain grows tall and the horses run\\
He held more souls than the emperor's bell\\
But never reached for the golden one\\
His father wed a duchess land\\
A general's sword turned noble hand\\
At fourteen he learned the steward's art\\
At seventeen the blade became his heart

\songpart{Chorus – full percussion, brass swell}
Abulón, Abulón, shadow of the throne\\
Stronger than a king but he stands alone\\
He moves the world with a merchant's loan\\
Abulón, Abulón, the power unknown

\songpart{Verse 2 – steady groove, lower register}
He sailed to save the colonies\\
Where the Plate River runs wide and cold\\
At nineteen, chains and iron knees\\
To the Inca's mountain hold\\
But a noble's cage is a gilded page\\
He spoke, and the captors listened long\\
At twenty-three he wed their blood\\
And returned with a peace song

\songpart{Chorus}
Abulón, Abulón, shadow of the throne\\
Stronger than a king but he stands alone\\
He moves the world with a merchant's loan\\
Abulón, Abulón, the power unknown

\songpart{Verse 3 – tension rising, strings enter}
Home to find his father's grave\\
His mother caged behind stone wall\\
Usurpers digging the ducal grave\\
The emperor's court refused his call\\
But Inca spears and Malian friends\\
Broke the thieves at Monukae's gate\\
He stood before the golden throne\\
And made the vultures wait

\songpart{Bridge – sudden silence, then low drums}
The emperor saw a sharper mind\\
Than any noble ever bred\\
Five years as governor of Minorca\\
Then I sign your lands, he said

\songpart{Verse 4 – faster, marching rhythm}
His firstborn son, a pirate's prize\\
On the cold Cantabrian sea\\
Pay the gold, the Irish laughed\\
Abulón's reply was glee

\annotation{Spoken over drums}\\
I do not pay. %
I punish.

A treasure ship with double the weight\\
Then twenty-three hundred swords at night\\
Cork burned down to the harbor stone\\
The gold left burning in the light

\songpart{Chorus – louder, brass dominant}
Abulón, Abulón, shadow of the throne\\
Stronger than a king but he stands alone\\
He moves the world with a merchant's loan\\
Abulón, Abulón, the power unknown

\songpart{Verse 5 – slower, tragic turn}
Old Carthage fell to Roman siege\\
He broke their lines upon the land\\
Chased their fleet to Sicily\\
Two ports turned to sand\\
He called on Egypt, called on Arabs\\
Bound their ships into one fleet\\
On the Aegean, fire found him\\
His hull became his heat

\annotation{Final Chorus – key change to F major, full choir}\\
Abulón, Abulón, shadow on the wave\\
No king was ever so proud a knave\\
He left no throne but a world he gave\\
Abulón, Abulón, beyond the grave

\songpart{Outro – solo kora, fading drums}
He could have been king.\\
He chose the shadow.\\
And the shadow chose him back.

\end{lyrics}
