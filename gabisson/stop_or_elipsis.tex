\songtitle{Stop\_or\_Ellipsis}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Gemini in interaction with Carlos Thompson, produced in two sittings.

Based on a message in Mastodon, by Carlos Thompson, inspired by a WhatsApp interaction. %
One first draft was written by Gemini and a sample was produced.

Second sitting, lyrics were fixed and a whole song co-written between Carlos and Gemini, and first sample was used in Suno as base for remix with the extended and corrected lyrics.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Indie pop with a lo-fi aesthetic. %

A dry, mid-tempo drum kit features a tight snare and a steady kick. %
A melodic bass guitar follows the chord progression with simple root notes. %
The male vocals are dry, intimate, and delivered in a conversational, rhythmic style. %
The arrangement is sparse, focusing on the interplay between the vocal melody and the rhythmic guitar. %
100 BPM, Key of G Major, 4/4 time signature.
