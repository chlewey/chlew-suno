\songtitle{Learning How to Stand Here}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:learning-how-to-stand-here}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Sylvia's diary entries in \emph{Christmas 2024} by Carlos Thompson (December 2024 to January 2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Sylvia Lai -- a blue hard hat on the building site, meetings in Spanish, notes kept by hand, and the family arriving from Sardinia; a line to her father in Italian in the bridge. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie-pop / it-pop with a youthful female lead vocal, intimate and observant delivery. Mid-tempo loping groove, crisp dry kit and handclaps, rounded bass, bright muted guitar, small piano accents, occasional airy woodwind answers. Verses stay close and conversational, pre-chorus gradually opens, chorus lifts with stacked harmonies and a memorable repeated phrase; bridge strips back to voice and keys before a full final chorus. Warm, detailed modern production with human timing and clear storytelling.
\end{style}
