\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the Grok summary of an autobiographical piece by Carlos Thompson. %

\begin{style}
Dubstep hymn with half-time stomp, sub drops, and swung snare snaps; verse rides sparse organ stabs and low pulses, pre-chorus strips to voice and ticking hats, chorus hits with stacked chants and wobbling bass. %
Male vocals close-mic in verses, wider doubles on hooks, ad-lib echoes on Bella. %
Risers, reversed bells, and a final sub-bloom. %
Wide, dark, punchy mix., dubstep, world, sleep, beautiful, happy
\end{style}
