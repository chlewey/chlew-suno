\songtitle{Close Enough to Hear Him}

\setin{2026-10}
\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{aurora-swansen}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Steven Swansen}, a story by Carlos Thompson narrated by Aurora Swansen, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- in Aurora's voice, who called about her father and who did not, and staying close while he slowly recovers. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 25 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Adult contemporary jazz-pop with a relaxed swing-leaning midtempo groove; piano and Rhodes trade warm extended chords, brushed kit and upright bass keep a supple pulse, with restrained saxophone replies between vocal phrases. A conversational New England female lead carries detailed, narrative verses, opening into a memorable, gently rising chorus; add soft layered harmonies late, then resolve with a small piano-and-sax outro. Intimate, clear mix with warm saturation, natural room depth, and the voice forward.
\end{style}
