\songtitle{Liberation, Mostly}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\label{sec:liberation-mostly}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude, written in an attempt to mimic Carlos Thompson's voice and songwriting style following specific prompts about the overall subject: a liberation anthem against societal norms. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Folk-rock with punchy brass stabs, strummed acoustic guitar, bass, and stomping drums; deadpan baritone, clipped rhythmic spoken verses and a gang-vocal chorus; dry close-mic vocals up front, tight studio mix; brisk, chant-ready stadium pulse.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\annotation{Spoken intro}\\
This is a liberation anthem.\\
An anthem should say what it liberates from.\\
Societal norms. There, I said it.

\songpart{Verse 1}
I was handed a ladder:\\
school, college, marriage, kids, nine to five.\\
I climbed two rungs and read the map.\\
The map was drawn for somebody else.

\songpart{Chorus}
Free me from what they expect,\\
and from what I expect they expect.\\
I'll keep the rules I can defend.\\
The rest I'm leaving at the door.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I was handed a list of wants to be ashamed of.\\
My church wrote the first half.\\
My good politics wrote the second.\\
So I became ashamed of being ashamed,\\
which is, I admit, a very efficient system.

\songpart{Chorus}
Free me from what they expect,\\
and from what I expect they expect.\\
I'll keep the rules I can defend.\\
The rest I'm leaving at the curb.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Bogotá, a Tuesday, the bus doesn't move.\\
Nobody honks. Everyone agrees this is how it works.\\
In my head the city has trains, parks, a plan,\\
and nobody asks me to explain the plan.

\songpart{Bridge, spoken}
Case one: a norm that protects someone. Keep it.\\
Case two: a norm that only protects itself. Drop it.\\
Case three: I can't tell which. Ask a friend.\\
Ask a spreadsheet.

\songpart{Chorus}
Free me from what they expect,\\
and from what I expect they expect.\\
I'll keep the rules I can defend.\\
The rest I'm leaving at the church.

\songpart{Outro, spoken, instruments out}
This song is a norm too:\\
verses, a chorus that keeps coming back,\\
a bridge where I get serious.\\
Free me from the anthem.\\
I'll finish early.
\end{lyrics}
