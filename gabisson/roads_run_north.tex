\songtitle{Roads Run North}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-metal}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{liis-sepp}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\tag{erika-czajka}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{diana-naji}\label{sec:roads-run-north}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the first section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Liis's last goodbye and a Telegram meme, a night with Kadri, then the summer road with the children and Erika from Tallinn through Riga, Stockholm and Jönköping to Hamburg, Utrecht and Rotterdam. \emph{Where Your Yes Begins} (page \pageref{sec:where-your-yes-begins}) continues it. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Nordic folk metal with driving mid-tempo stomp, kannel and nyckelharpa carrying modal hooks over distorted bass and tight double-kick drums; verses stay lean and story-forward, choruses open into broad harmonized folk melodies, with a brief sparse instrumental passage before the final refrain. Male baritone lead, gritty and close in the verses, forceful and melodic in the chorus. Organic, cold-edged production with clear string detail and a heavy low end.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Liis said, “Don’t come to the ceremony,”\\
Her mother, her boyfriend—reasons in the air.\\
I dressed anyway, kissed her at the doorway,\\
Then walked away as if I didn’t care.\\
That Wednesday was the last time I saw her;\\
A week later, Telegram sent a joke.\\
One little meme, no words to answer—\\
The final thread between us broke.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The days kept opening, one by one,\\
With names and plans beneath the summer sun.

\songpart{Chorus}
Roads run north, the miles roll on,\\
A map of goodbyes from dusk to dawn.\\
I carried the plans, I carried the past,\\
And every new country went by too fast.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I sent my semester’s final report,\\
Walked home while the weather stayed kind.\\
Kadri wrote; I asked her over that night,\\
Let the work and the hours unwind.\\
A shower, a cab, her door, her bed;\\
The children would come in a couple of days.\\
A Dallas investor waited in my files,\\
But I chose her arms and the room’s slow haze.

\songpart{Verse 3}
At a café, breakfast, then home on foot,\\
Taking the long way into summer’s ease.\\
I opened the laptop: trips, children, Erika,\\
Gabi, and all the careful what-ifs.\\
Telegram pulled me down its winding hall;\\
Diana and I wrote, but made no date.\\
After midnight sleep, six o’clock wake—\\
Tallinn at one-thirty. No room to be late.

\songpart{Chorus}
Roads run north, the miles roll on,\\
A map of goodbyes from dusk to dawn.\\
I carried the plans, I carried the past,\\
And every new country went by too fast.

\songpart{Verse 4}
Three nights in Tallinn, old stones in the rain,\\
Then Riga, three nights, and the ferry’s dark wake.\\
Stockholm and the roads that curl around it,\\
Then Erika joined for the turns we would take.\\
Jönköping, one night, her father and brothers,\\
Their years not far from the children beside me.\\
Malmö, Copenhagen, each stop falling behind us,\\
Hamburg at last, three nights by the sea.

\songpart{Bridge}
I sent them back to Bogotá,\\
Then shut the hotel door on the world.\\
Thirty-six hours, no grand design,\\
Just two lives folded close and still.\\
Bremen was written down in the plan;\\
We left too late to follow that line.\\
Straight on to Utrecht for her business,\\
Rotterdam for my flight the next day.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Roads run north, the miles roll on,\\
Tallinn to Hamburg, then onward alone.\\
I carried the plans, I carried the past,\\
And every new country went by too fast.\\
Some messages end with a joke sent late;\\
Some journeys close at a boarding gate.\\
Roads run north, and the map folds shut—\\
A life in motion, a heart catching up.

\songpart{Outro}
Rotterdam, morning, my flight waiting there.\\
The road kept going. The room stayed somewhere.
\end{lyrics}
