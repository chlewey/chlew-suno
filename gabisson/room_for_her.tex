\begin{style}

\end{style}

\begin{lyrics}

\songpart{Verse 1}
The show didn't land\\
the way they'd planned —\\
she caught it in Gabi's eyes\\
before the car stopped.\\
No words in the elevator,\\
just the weight of the week\\
and Gabi's shoulder\\
finding hers in the dark.\\
Sylvia at the door,\\
Thomas in the library —\\
the penthouse swallowed them in,\\
soft lights, familiar rooms.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
She always knows\\
before it's said,\\
reads the room\\
before it's read —\\
this is how we love,\\
this is what she gives,\\
knowing everything\\
and asking nothing back.

\songpart{Chorus}
There's always room for her\\
at Mariain's table,\\
a key that fits,\\
a light she leaves on late.\\
There's always room for her,\\
the bag by the closet,\\
the guest room that knows her name —\\
this is how we love.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Low music, easy talk,\\
Manhattan spread below,\\
four people loose\\
in the end of something hard.\\
She kissed Gabi at the door,\\
then turned and kissed Thomas too —\\
unhurried, warm,\\
the way you kiss what you know.\\
Then out into the Village night,\\
the street receiving her,\\
the particular quiet\\
of walking back alone.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
She's always the one\\
who leaves last,\\
who pulls the door\\
and hears it catch —\\
this is how we love,\\
this is what she keeps,\\
the going-out\\
so someone else can stay.

\songpart{Chorus}
There's always room for her\\
at Mariain's table,\\
a key that fits,\\
a light she leaves on late.\\
There's always room for her,\\
the bag by the closet,\\
the guest room that knows her name —\\
this is how we love.

\subsection{Breakdown}
Morning.\\
A text: on my way.\\
Two minutes: at the gallery now,\\
might miss you.\\
The bag was already packed.\\
The room already made.\\
She got there just in time\\
to find it empty.\\
They met outside the café —\\
arms, fabric, thirty seconds —\\
safe flight,\\
I'll message when we land.\\
Then taillights\\
into the afternoon.\\
Then the Village,\\
just the Village.

\songpart{Chorus - Outro}
There's always room for her\\
at Mariain's table,\\
a key that fits,\\
a light she leaves on late.\\
There's always room for her —\\
the bag by the closet,\\
the guest room still holding her shape —\\
this is how we love.\\
But the light stays on\\
for no one now,\\
the room too still,\\
the city loud —\\
there's always room for her,\\
there's always room,\\
and she is gone,\\
and this is how we love.

\end{lyrics}
