\songtitle{A Cabin Waiting Past the Palms}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:a-cabin-waiting-past-the-palms}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Sylvia's diary entries in \emph{Christmas 2024} by Carlos Thompson (December 2024 to January 2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Sylvia Lai -- Christmas Eve at Villa Jaguar: Santa in borrowed clothes, dinner at midnight, the gifts, and a small secret slipped out to the cabin past the palms. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie-pop, intimate young female lead vocal, conversational and lightly breathy, diary-like storytelling with crisp melodic phrasing. Mid-tempo lilting groove, chiming electric guitar, rounded bass, restrained live drums and handclap accents, soft keyboard color. Verses stay close and detailed; lift into a memorable warm chorus, then a quieter bridge and gentle final chorus. Clear modern production with warm organic texture and clean vocal-forward mix.
\end{style}
