\songtitle{Overrated}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok from a draft by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Aurora -- her mother's affair, weeks split between her father in Worcester and her mother in Lincoln, Rhode Island, Clark as the way out at eighteen, and love as overrated. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 21 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Alternative R\&B, husky smoky female mezzo with powerful full-voice belts; post-punk instrumental production, wiry bass guitar, angular clean electric guitar, taut live-sounding drums and spare keys; tape saturation and soft clipping; mid-tempo pulse.
\end{style}

\songpart{Verse 1}
My mother cheated; that's the tale I knew.\\
They split my weeks in halves, and so I grew\\
between my dad's in Worcester and the town\\
of Lincoln, Rhode Island, where Mom moved down.

At eighteen, Clark was how I got away\\
from Mom and stepdad's house; I chose to stay\\
with Dad, whose girlfriend, kind, was fine with me:\\
a quiet house, a place to simply be.
