\songtitle{Enough Sun to Share}

\tag*{2024}\tag*{2026}\tag{acoustic-punk}\tag{encounters}\tag{felicia-campbell}\tag{gabriel-moreno}\label{sec:enough-sun-to-share}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Encounters} vignette series by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on the Stable Diffusion prompts (2024) for Felicia Campbell and Gabriel Moreno -- a park bench, a winded cyclist, a shared earbud and a bottle of water. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Acoustic summer punk, brisk strummed acoustic guitar driving a buoyant straight-ahead punk pulse, tight handclaps and light kit, warm melodic bass, occasional jangly electric-guitar accents. Scene-setting narrative verses build into a shout-along repeated chorus; raw, conversational male lead vocal with energetic gang harmonies on the hook. Sunlit, playful, slightly scrappy live-band feel, clear punchy mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Felicia on the park bench, red hair in the breeze\\
One white earbud in, a grin she can't conceal\\
Ruby at her nostril catching afternoon\\
She taps her blue-sweatpant knee and hums a tune

\songpart{Verse 2}
Gabriel's down on the grass beside his bike\\
Helmet tipped back, catching his breath in the light\\
Fifty-two and cooked from chasing every hill\\
She slips one earbud out: “Hey, you doing okay still?”

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
He laughs, says, “Give me just a minute or two”\\
She offers her water; he says, “That'll do”\\
The park keeps moving, but they take their time\\
Two strangers sharing shade beneath the lime

\songpart{Chorus}
Hey, slow down, the day's not going anywhere\\
Take a seat, catch your breath, there's enough sun to share\\
You came in racing, now you're laughing with me\\
Summer's a little better when we let each other be

He shakes his head, then gives the chorus a try\\
