\songtitle{Australian Daughter, Colombian Name}

\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{victoria-ospina}\tag{cosmopolitan-pop}

\begin{notes}
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Contemporary cosmopolitan pop; mid-tempo (~ 105 bpm); sophisticated groove; organic-electric hybrid instrumentation; warm, optimistic, urban; reflective without nostalgia; movement rather than longing.\\
Lead: femenine young vocals, Australian registry.\\
Chorus: rich choir harmonies that feel communal and panoramic, contrasting with the intimate first-person verses
\end{style}
