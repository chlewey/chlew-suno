\songtitle{Fallen}

\tag*{2026}\tag{blues}\tag{original-lyrics}\label{sec:fallen}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, as a response to \emph{Fallen Messenger} (page \pageref{sec:fallen-messenger}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 7 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Slow minor blues, sparse arrangement with space between parts; tape-saturated mix and plate reverb, tremolo electric guitar, sliding fretless bass, Wurlitzer organ, brushed snare backbeat and ghost-note hi-hats; 12-bar form at 69 BPM, half-time turnaround; female vocals, close and restrained, ending in a sparse spoken outro.
\end{style}
