\songtitle{Stopped Before the Edge}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{latin-jazz}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{zahra-moradi}\label{sec:stopped-before-the-edge}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Lines Not Crossed} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, a duet of Thomas and Zahra during her visit to Punta Espada in December 2026 -- a hand found on the road back to the villa, a turn off the convoy's line, and the kiss they stopped short of going further. From the same story as \emph{Don't Mistake the Bridge for Home} (page \pageref{sec:dont-mistake-the-bridge-for-home}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Intimate folk-rock duet colored by Latin jazz: gently loping 6/8 groove with brushed percussion, upright bass, nylon-string guitar, piano montuno touches, warm trumpet and a lyrical violin line. Story-led verses trade between a low, restrained male voice and a close, expressive female voice; harmonize briefly on the refrain. Build from sparse guitar and bass into a restrained ensemble swell, then fall back to a quiet, unresolved ending. Organic room sound, rounded tape warmth, clear vocals and nuanced dynamics.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1: Female}
Your hand found mine on the road back home,\\
The villa waiting past the bend.\\
We named the life we might have made,\\
Then let the sentence end.

\songpart{Verse 2: Male}
You said the years had changed the shape\\
Of what we could forgive.\\
I kept my eyes upon the road,\\
And held the hand you gave.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus: Female}
A small turn off the convoy’s line,\\
A path where no one came.

\songpart{Chorus: Duet}
We stopped before the edge of it,\\
Before the old ache learned to speak.\\
One kiss, one breath, one quiet choice—\\
We let the moment leave.

\songpart{Verse 3: Female}
“Not all the way,” I said to you.\\
You answered, “Not out here.”\\
Your thumb moved softly on my leg;\\
The rest was close and clear.

\songpart{Verse 4: Male}
“We’d better stop, or we won’t stop.”\\
You nodded once, and then\\
I drove us back to waiting cars,\\
And joined the road again.

\songpart{Chorus: Duet}
We stopped before the edge of it,\\
Before the old ache learned to speak.\\
One kiss, one breath, one quiet choice—\\
We let the moment leave.

\songpart{Bridge: Female}
At first, a handshake, measured, neat;\\
A week of shared meals, open doors.\\
Jan-Alan chasing grown-up games,\\
And laughter through the airport floors.

\songpart{Bridge: Male}
Then arms around you at goodbye,\\
So ordinary to the crowd.\\
Gabi never saw the difference.\\
Pilar kept her silence loud.

\songpart{Final Chorus: Duet}
We stopped before the edge of it,\\
Though neither heart was free.\\
A warm embrace, a careful road,\\
And all we didn’t say.\\
We stopped before the edge of it,\\
And carried on that way.

\songpart{Outro: Duet}
The villa fell behind us.\\
The road went on ahead.
\end{lyrics}
