\songtitle{Every Step, Every Stripe}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-rock}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{pilar-venegas}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Pilar's dossier by Carlos Thompson and Claude, from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe. Character: Pilar Venegas -- from law school and the Administrative Corps to army captain. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 6 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop-rock with country-style verses, female lead vocal, conversational country phrasing over acoustic guitar and steady live drums; open up into a bold electric-guitar chorus with a strong melodic hook; switch to a tightly rhythmic, clearly articulated rap bridge, then return to the full-band final chorus. Polished, grounded, story-forward production with crisp vocals and dynamic section contrast.
\end{style}
