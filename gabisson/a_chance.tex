\songtitle{A Chance}
\begin{style}
Rapid-fire Tarantela with soul and rap bridges. Musical theater pop with a driving, syncopated rhythm. A bright, clear male tenor vocal leads with precise diction. The arrangement features a rhythmic acoustic piano playing staccato chords and melodic fills, a clean electric bass guitar playing syncopated eighth notes, and a drum kit with a crisp snare and active hi-hat patterns. A string section provides staccato accents and sustained harmonic support. The track is in the key of G Major with a tempo of 128 BPM in 4/4 time.
\end{style}
