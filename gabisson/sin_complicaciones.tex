\songtitle{Sin complicaciones}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop}\tag{spanish-lyrics}\tag{english-lyrics}\tag{italian-lyrics}\tag{campidanese-lyrics}\tag{catalan-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on a screenplay by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Sylvia, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- Christmas in Carbonia, New York with Gabi and Thomas, and an uncomplicated New Year in Colombia. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 14 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Euro pop, single female mezzo with a Sardinian Italian accent and careful pronunciation, clear multilingual phrasing with a Campidanese chant intro, stereo-widened polished mix, festive 98 BPM mid-tempo backbeat, palm-muted electric guitar, bright keyboard stabs, drum kit and hand-percussion breaks, family-dinner warmth and road-trip lift.
\end{style}
