\songtitle{Le phases de Thomas}

\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}

\begin{notes}
A reflective song about the evolving phases of Thomas Garcia’s life, narrated in Interlingua. %
It traces his passage from timidity and observation toward a quieter acceptance of love, memory and the slow changes that shape him.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
folk rock, alt-pop storytelling, 132 BPM, allegro drive, solo acoustic guitar, brushed kick pulse, harmonica lead, male tenor vocals, close-mic intimacy, group handclaps, full-band lift, electric bass ostinato, drum kit backbeat, tape saturation, sidechain pumping, bittersweet nostalgia, observational tone, IALA Interlingua narrative, wordless intro
\end{style}
