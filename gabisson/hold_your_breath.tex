\songtitle{Hold Your Breath}

\tag*{2026}\tag{electro-rock}\tag{ricochets}\tag{la-dorada}\tag{john-carlson}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Claude summary of chapter 10 \emph{Heat} from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematographic power electro-rock ballad, minor-key pulse at a restrained midtempo, tense low synth ostinato and sub-bass beneath sparse piano, distant metallic percussion, then wide distorted guitars and explosive drums in the choruses, Verses should feel stealthy and claustrophobic with intimate, breath-controlled male vocals; choruses rise into a forceful anthemic delivery without becoming celebratory, Use abrupt dropouts for patrol warnings, filtered radio-like vocal fragments for broken indoor reception, and a dramatic near-silence before the final extraction, Polished film-score scale, hot dry close vocal, controlled saturation, deep low end, spacious but not glossy mix
\end{style}
