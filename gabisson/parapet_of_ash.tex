\begin{style}
Symphonic dark pop with a slow half-time pulse, bowed low strings and distant tom hits; verse stays intimate and sparse with close-mic female vocals, double-tracked on key lines, while the pre-chorus opens into rising harmonies and filtered choir pads. %
Chorus lands wider with big sustained notes, stacked oohs, and short delay throws on the hook phrase. %
Glassy risers, reversed cymbal swells, and a final bell shimmer lift each transition; mix is cinematic, glossy, and heavily atmospheric., deep, light
\end{style}
