\songtitle{Arrow Throwing Rat}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the Grok summary of an autobiographical piece by Carlos Thompson. %
Names of characters changed in the input text.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Fast-paced folk with driving acoustic strums, stomp-clap rhythm, bright fiddle lines, and a brisk 6/8 feel; verse rides nimble storytelling, pre-chorus tightens with handclaps and rising harmony, chorus opens on gang vocals with a chantable hook. %
Lead vocal stays close and conversational, with doubled chorus lines, quick call-and-response ad-libs, banjo fills between phrases, and a punchy, earthy mix with lively room tone., low, folk
\end{style}
