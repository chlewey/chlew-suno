\songtitle{Sincere}

\tag*{2026}\tag{metal-ballad}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{zahra-moradi}\label{sec:sincere}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Thomas, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the start of his relationship with Zahra in 2019: a Farsi mix-up breaking the ice in a conference room, coffee dates turning into weekends, and the agreement that changed in her mind while he never asked. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 1 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft metal ballad, slow D-minor sway, close-mic baritone vocals with clipped rhythmic staccato phrasing and whispered ad-libs between lines; fingerpicked acoustic guitar, low distorted bass, brushed percussion, restrained chorus lift, plate reverb on the lead.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I’ve already been asked if I spoke Arabi\\
several times in Europe, part of my look.\\
It was just time someone asked about my Farsi.\\
I’m not Middle Eastern at all but that I took.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
We took the mistake with ease and little laughs,\\
an icebreaker in that sterile conference room.\\
A small connection between hard data and infographs.\\
An early warning of things we should not assume.

\songpart{Chorus}
I never lied when I embraced you, Zahra (Zahra)\\
my moments with you were sincere.\\
I really liked you, my dear Zahra (Zahra)\\
It’s a shame I failed to be your peer.

\songpart{Verse}
The progression seemed so natural I never stopped to ask\\
if our initial agreement had morphed in your mind.\\
Coffee dates became weekends, weekends a task;\\
from warm greetings into shared intimacy, this aligned.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I should’ve listened that first night we shared a bed.\\
You asked me if I was sure and this would be a deal.\\
I didn’t ask what it was, if you meant something instead\\
of the initial agreement you were about to repeal.

\songpart{Bridge}
That night in Helsinki, your toothbrush by my sink,\\
your books on my night table, signs clear as a guide.\\
You knew I didn’t say forever, but we both failed to think\\
how our actions were building something I couldn’t provide.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I never lied when I embraced you, Zahra (Zahra)\\
my moments with you were sincere\\
I really liked you, my dear Zahra (Zahra)\\
But I failed to realize that you being here\\
meant to you not what it meant for me.
