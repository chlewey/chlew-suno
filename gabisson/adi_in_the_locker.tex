\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the Grok summary of an autobiographical piece by Carlos Thompson. %
Names of characters changed in the input text.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Fast-paced Dominican merengue with güira drive, bright brass punches, and accordion riffing over a propulsive two-step groove; verse keeps the kick and tambora lean, pre-chorus lifts with stacked handclaps and a rising horn figure, chorus hits with shouty gang vocals and short brass stabs. %
Lead vocal is playful and breathy in verses, doubled on the hook, with ad-lib responses and a few delay throws on "Adi." Crisp, punchy mix with a lively dance-floor sheen., merengue
\end{style}
