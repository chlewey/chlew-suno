Lyrics by DeepSeek following structural instructions, narrowed themes and tone by Carlos Thompson, based on the press release and news reports on the inauguration of the Condor railway, an original story by Carlos Thompson adapted to voice and tone by ChatGPT, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the first passenger train, moved from Tuesday to Wednesday 13 April in Holy Week while the president was at the UN: two carriages for guests and press, four for those who bought their tickets. \emph{Ceremonia} (page \pageref{sec:ceremonia}) tells the same day.
