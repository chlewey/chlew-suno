\songtitle{Seat 14C}

\tag*{2024}\tag{industrial-dance}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{the-leg}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{thomas-garcia}

\begin{notes}
Based on the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} fictional universe by Carlos Thompson.\\
Character: Gabriele Wiater, Gabi, and Thomas García.\\
Lyrics by Grok based on the work \emph{The Leg}.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Industrial dance at 132 BPM with hard four-on-the-floor kick, metallic clanks, syncopated bass stabs, and servo-like percussion; verse rides clipped synth pulses and spoken-sung lines, pre-chorus strips to kick, hiss, and rising filters, chorus hits with a chantable hook and stacked doubles. %
Vocals are close-mic and cool in verses, lightly distorted on hooks, with ad-lib panning, delay throws, and a few breathy asides. %
Ear candy: reversed cabin swells, turbine whooshes into transitions, and a burst of glassy arps at the final lift. %
Bright, punchy, steel-edged mix., light, simple, playful, industrial, theme
\end{style}
