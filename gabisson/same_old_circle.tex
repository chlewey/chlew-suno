\songtitle{Same Old Circle}

\begin{notes}
Based on a companion text from \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson.\\
Companion text \emph{The Cohort} by Carlos Thompson expanding the lore of Fabián Müller and Sandra Roa, and other minor characters.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Electronic pop with half-time dubstep bridges, crisp four-on-the-floor verses, rubbery synth bass, clipped handclaps, and a driving club pulse; verse keeps it intimate with sparse keys and ticking percussion, pre-chorus lifts with filtered risers and stacked harmonies, chorus hits wide with an anthem hook and group vocals, bridge drops into wobbling bass and chopped vocal flashes before the final lift. %
Close-mic lead vocal with doubles on the hook, airy delays on key names, and bright, punchy mix with glossy top end., dubstep, theme, industrial, electronic
\end{style}
