\songtitle{Evi Sem Yike (single)}

\tag*{1992}\tag{rock}\tag{original-source}\tag{evi-sem-yike}
\begin{notes}
Adapted from \emph{Evi Sem Yike} (see above) for a more traditional song structure
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Rock with a rhythmic 4/4 mid-tempo drive, bluesy storytelling verses, punchy punk choruses, and a metal-leaning guitar riff intro; verses ride bass and drums with fiddle accents, pre-chorus tightens to handclaps and gang shouts, chorus opens wide with stacked gang vocals and shouted hook repeats. %
Lead vocal stays gritty and conversational, with group echoes on key lines, a short guitar break into the bridge, and bright punchy mix with lively live-band energy., metal, blues, punk, rock, electric
\end{style}

Peeling off the damp fabric, letting it drop by the door\\

Just a hollowed-out animal staring up from the bed.
