\songtitle{Export Snow}

\tag*{1989}\tag{post-punk}\tag{original-source}\tag{bill-pi}
% -> stories.tex (Named Fictional Works?)
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude synopsis of Carlos Thompson's 1989 story \emph{La historia de Bill Pi}, part one \emph{El regreso}. Same story as \emph{La historia se corta}, in English. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 15 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sophisticated post-punk with melodic punk drive, taut bass guitar, clipped live drums, wiry angular guitar, brief melodic metal accents, and restrained analog synth texture. Fast, tightly articulated English lead vocal with youthful female character, cool narrative delivery that opens into an urgent chorus. Verses move in brisk, accumulating action; pre-choruses build tension; choruses hit with a concise repeatable hook; bridge strips back to voice and bass before a sudden unresolved ending. Raw-edged but carefully arranged, clear vocal-forward mix, dynamic rather than glossy.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Nineteen ninety, dust on the stair\\
William Pérez finds his old room there\\
Two years gone, Swiss snow in his coat\\
A studded grin and a chain at his throat\\
He calls Diego: “I came back broke—\\
Except for the money I shouldn’t have.”\\
A hundred grand in Nassau sleeps\\
While Bogotá counts the promises cheap

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Jane Smith, Antoine, a kitchen crew\\
Encrypted numbers, a debt gone through\\
Paris behind him, Bern shut tight\\
He won’t say what happened that night

\songpart{Chorus}
Hide, get organized, export snow\\
That’s the map he draws for everyone he knows\\
Hide, get organized—say it like a plan\\
But the story keeps outrunning the man

\songpart{Verse 2}
At Diego’s house, the old names arrive\\
Sandra takes notes, the room comes alive\\
Germán, Gerardo, María Paula too\\
A thousand for repairs—real money, real news\\
By morning he’s gone across the eastern sky\\
New York, Liliana, the old goodbye\\
Juana at the door, flowers, dinner arranged\\
French plates, old hurt, and a face rearranged

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
They sleep together; he pulls away:\\
“I’m not the same as I was yesterday.”\\
Then London calls with a box and a key\\
“If I don’t come back, let her read for me.”

\songpart{Chorus}
Hide, get organized, export snow\\
That’s the map he draws for everyone he knows\\
Hide, get organized—say it like a plan\\
But the story keeps outrunning the man

\songpart{Bridge}
Prague: Jane is dead. Antoine wants me gone.\\
Three notebooks, one cassette, one sealed envelope.\\
Don’t break the seal unless I’m dead.\\
The tape says money crossed borders in the dark;\\
The mistake was Jane—not you.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Diego comes home; Germán translates by hand\\
Sandra stops where the pages cut too deep\\
March second, New York: Liliana begins\\
The French diary opens; the hatred begins\\
One line, then another, then no way to know\\
What name is waiting beneath the next fold

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Hide, get organized, export snow—\\
The plan sounds clean till the pages unfold\\
Hide, get organized—who knew what, when?\\
She turns to the next line—\\
And the story stops there.

\songpart{Outro}
She reads.\\
She hates.\\
The page turns—\\
Cut.
