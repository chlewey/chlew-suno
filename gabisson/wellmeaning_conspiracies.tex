\songtitle{Well-Meaning Conspiracies}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alt-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{aurora-swansen}\label{sec:well-meaning-conspiracies}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok (interacting on structure) based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Claire and Gabi, now best friends, bringing his secret with Claire into daylight, and Aurora the only one he can tell. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Alternative / art-rock, mid-tempo restless pulse, layered guitars shifting between clean arpeggios and slight distortion in stereo, elegant arrangement with gradual textural movement, androgynous midrange lead with restrained phrasing and close, clear vocals.
\end{style}
