Lyrics by SUNO based on the profile of Natalia Kyou Park in the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.

\begin{style}
K-pop with female lead vocals, layered harmonies, fast syncopated verses, bright four-on-the-floor chorus lift, crisp electronic drums, rubbery bass, sparkling synth arps, and pre-chorus tension built with filtered claps and rising pads; verses stay nimble and percussive, pre-chorus narrows to voice and handclaps, chorus opens wide with stacked hooks and crowd-style ad-libs, bridge drops to close-mic intimacy before the final surge, Glossy, punchy mix with vivid top-end and polished vocal layers, uplifting, k-pop
\end{style}
