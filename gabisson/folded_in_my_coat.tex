\songtitle{Folded in My Coat}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:folded-in-my-coat}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Sylvia's diary entries in \emph{Christmas 2024} by Carlos Thompson (December 2024 to January 2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Sylvia Lai -- the days after Christmas at Villa Jaguar and on the farm, New Year's fireworks from a barge, an old suspicion let go, and the flight back to Barcelona. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie-pop, It-pop, young female lead vocal, intimate conversational phrasing with bright youthful tone, gently propulsive midtempo groove, clean chiming electric guitar and rounded bass, crisp restrained drums, small keyboard details; verses stay close and diary-like, pre-chorus gradually opens, chorus lifts into a memorable melodic refrain with soft layered harmonies, bridge turns quieter before the final chorus; polished warm production with clear vocal focus and natural dynamics
\end{style}
