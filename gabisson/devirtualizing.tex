\songtitle{Devirtualizing}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{fiona-levit}\label{sec:devirtualizing}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the \emph{Devirtualizing} section of \emph{Dubai} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- meeting Fiona face to face in London after knowing her online, the next day's Eurostar, and Dubai slipping into the conversation. \emph{Just Out of Range} (page \pageref{sec:just-out-of-range}) follows on the flight. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 26 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock, male baritone lead, drum-kit, strings, piano; narrative and gently wry, with scene-setting piano and strings that swell around the vocal. A measured, conversational lead delivery.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Funny name on a North London sign,\\
I’d lost it before I crossed the line.\\
Fiona waited, small and self-possessed,\\
Tortoiseshell frames, an eclectic dress.\\
Hello? A handshake? Cheek to cheek?\\
Three little signals, none in sync.\\
We’d banned the subject of our work;\\
The silence found that rule and smirked.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
If it had been a date, I’d have called it a disaster.\\
But we weren’t there for that—we’d just made the distance shorter.

\songpart{Chorus}
Devirtualizing, face to face,\\
Two old names in a brand-new place.\\
No grand reveal, no perfect start,\\
Just a second meeting and a little more heart.\\
Devirtualizing, step by step,\\
A strange first drink, then the train we’d get.\\
The screen went dark; the story stayed alive—\\
We’d meet tomorrow, see what came outside.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Island Gardens, weather first,\\
Then the ticket, as rehearsed.\\
Two minutes in, the talk took flight:\\
The Park, the crowd, the day’s headlines.\\
Biden, Johnson, Myanmar,\\
Covid rules and vaccines—there we are.\\
The ticket waited in the wings;\\
We talked through everything but things.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And Dubai slipped between the sentences somehow.\\
She said she’d come; it sounded simple then, somehow.

\songpart{Verse 3}
St Pancras, Eurostar platform,\\
The greeting found a gentler form.\\
The train pulled out; the words ran wide,\\
A vivid world beyond the glass outside.\\
Then quiet gathered, mile by mile;\\
She leaned her head against my shoulder awhile.\\
I let the moment settle there,\\
And checked my networks, taking care.

\songpart{Bridge}
A little window, a little rain,\\
A hundred signals in the moving train.\\
The last thing said was never the whole;\\
Some conversations travel without control.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Devirtualizing, face to face,\\
Two old names in a brand-new place.\\
No grand reveal, no perfect start,\\
Just a second meeting and a little more heart.\\
Devirtualizing, mile by mile,\\
A shoulder, a window, a borrowed quiet.\\
The screen went dark; the story stayed alive—\\
And somewhere down the line, Dubai.

\songpart{Outro}
Funny name, gone from my mind.\\
Her head on my shoulder.\\
The network can wait this time.
