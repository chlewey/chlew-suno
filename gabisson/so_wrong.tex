\songtitle{So Wrong}

\tag*{2024}\tag{punk-soul}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{tallinn}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{thomas-garcia}

\begin{notes}
A risky romance between Claire Jones and Thomas Garcia, told from the moment when professional boundaries blur and desire overrides caution. %
The lyrics describe the dangerous thrill of falling for someone you know you shouldn’t.
\end{notes}
