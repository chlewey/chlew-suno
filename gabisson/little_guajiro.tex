\songtitle{Little Guajiro}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ebm}\tag{rock}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{natalia-kyou-park}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{erika-czajka}\tag{punta-espada}\label{sec:little-guajiro}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the short story \emph{Embotado} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- the night the meeting at the main house came under attack, Venegas taking the room to the bunker, Uriana reading the dead, and the numbness that held until he broke down on the Baltic with Erika beside him. \emph{Villa Jaguar} (page \pageref{sec:villa-jaguar}) tells the same assault as a news report. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
EBM rock instrumental foundation with a steady, mechanical four-on-the-floor pulse, clipped bass synth, dry live rock drums, tense electric guitar accents, and spare industrial textures. Keep the verses controlled and spacious beneath a deadpan baritone lead; build pressure through denser percussion and guitars at the attack, then shift the outro into a more expressive, rough-edged country blues delivery with blues guitar bends and a looser pocket. Gritty, clear mix, grounded dynamics, narrative focus.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
We planned to close by five that day,\\
So the Wayuu could head back home.\\
At six-thirty I asked them to dinner,\\
At the main house, keep the work going.

Park had only held the lead a few months,\\
Since Foreman died and left the chair.\\
Murphy was my first call, unavailable,\\
And pointed us to Park instead.

Murphy, McLean, Jiménez at the table,\\
Ipuana, Epinayú speaking for home.\\
We traced the roads and beach access;\\
The corridors stayed unspoken.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Park kept circling the question,\\
Jiménez finally named the thing.\\
Then supper ended quiet,\\
And we settled in the salon.

\songpart{Chorus}
Three points on the outer line,\\
Three trucks and four armored rides.\\
“Under attack,” Venegas said,\\
While distant thuds turned into fire.\\
We held the door, we held our ground,\\
The inner walls stayed standing.\\
Lots of white, lots of black,\\
Little Guajiro, Uriana said.

\songpart{Verse 2}
She came in with two armed guards,\\
Rifles, vests, helmets, all in view.\\
They’d watched the inner perimeter,\\
Meant to pass unseen until they had to.\\
The guests thought she might be the danger;\\
I told them she was security.\\
Epinayú’s men were there as well,\\
And the room went still around us.

Down to the basement, she said,\\
Leave them here; I’ll take the bunker.\\
“How serious?” “Very serious.”\\
Three breaches on the outer ring.\\
She couldn’t rule out someone turning,\\
Couldn’t disarm the men inside.\\
So we split the room in two,\\
And waited while the defenses held.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The assault never reached the middle.\\
No one crossed the inner line.\\
Two more hours below the house,\\
After the last shot died away.

\songpart{Chorus}
Three points on the outer line,\\
Three trucks and four armored rides.\\
“Under attack,” Venegas said,\\
While distant thuds turned into fire.\\
We held the door, we held our ground,\\
The inner walls stayed standing.\\
Lots of white, lots of black,\\
Little Guajiro, Uriana said.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Uriana knew the clan wars well,\\
And said those men were not Guajiro.\\
The violence looked imported,\\
From farther down the coast.\\
The Army came close to eleven;\\
Venegas argued with the captain.\\
No one was taken, no one disarmed,\\
The troops stayed outside the citadel.

CTI arrived from Bogotá,\\
First in on a charter flight.\\
At dawn Uriana knew one dead man,\\
A disaffected Guajiro tied to Gaitanistas.\\
A surrendered leader from Córdoba\\
Was confirmed by fingerprints.\\
All day the clans rejected blame:\\
No Wayuu community sent them.

\songpart{Bridge}
They said the clan violence was over.\\
They could not answer for the Gaitanistas.\\
In the afternoon we met again;\\
Park looked calm, still taking it in.\\
Murphy had lived through the Troubles.\\
McLean had served in the British Army,\\
And something else he wouldn’t discuss.\\
I had the least experience there.

\songpart{Outro - Blues}
I stayed calm for five long days,\\
Numb enough to pass for fine.\\
Then the Baltic trip, Erika beside me,\\
And the quiet finally came apart.\\
I kept my hands around the cup,\\
I couldn’t make the words behave.\\
Lots of white, lots of black,\\
Little Guajiro, Uriana said.\\
And I broke down on the Baltic,\\
With Erika beside me.
\end{lyrics}
