\begin{style}
Indie art-pop rock, late 2010s New York City aesthetic. %
Moderate tempo, around 100 BPM. %
D minor, resolving to D major on the extended final chorus. %
Guitar-forward arrangement: clean electric rhythm guitar,\\ 
occasional arpeggiated lead, understated bass. %
Warm keyboard pads underneath, not dominant. %
Sparse percussion in verse, fuller drum kit entering on chorus. %
Two distinct female lead vocal timbres alternating\\ 
by section — one more literary and cool (mid-west registry), one warmer and more direct (slight German accent) — converging on the bridge and extended chorus. %
Male vocal appears briefly in verse 3, low and understated.\\
Dry, intimate production with controlled reverb. %
Song builds in density from verse 1 through the extended chorus\\
Emotional register: observational, precise, warm\\ 
without being sentimental. %
Brooklyn apartment party atmosphere — indoor intimacyo, late summer, adult social complexity.
\end{style}
