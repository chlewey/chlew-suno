\songtitle{Never Fall}

\tag*{2026}\tag{eurodance}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude under Carlos Thompson's specific guidance on theme and purpose -- a defiant anthem against manufactured division, naming the systems that profit from keeping people split rather than the people themselves. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Eurodance with gritty rhythmic male rap and big anthemic female vocal, hard-gated kick and bass, driving 140 BPM four-on-the-floor pulse, bright synth leads, piano breakdown, rising snare builds, layered chorus harmonies and a 90s dance-pop mix
\end{style}
