\songtitle{Night-long Embrace}

\tag*{2026}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\label{sec:night-long-embrace}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok (interacting on structure) based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- back in the cabin after the beach, asking Aurora to drop the usual clinical script and simply stay the night. \emph{Stay Here Beside Me} (page \pageref{sec:stay-here-beside-me}) sets the same night as a duet. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Neo-soul R\&B, mellow close-mic baritone with fluid, clean enunciation and clipped rhythmic staccato phrasing; warm Rhodes, soft brushed drums, soft harmony pads beneath the chorus; tape-saturated production; quiet, unhurried groove.
\end{style}
