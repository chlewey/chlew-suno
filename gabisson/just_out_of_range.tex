\songtitle{Just Out of Range}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{fiona-levit}\tag{xumi}\tag{camila-alexander}\label{sec:just-out-of-range}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{The Flight} section of \emph{Dubai} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Claire, Fiona and Xumi talking in the cabin while he reads and drifts out of range. Follows \emph{Devirtualizing} (page \pageref{sec:devirtualizing}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 26 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock male baritone lead with drum kit, strings, piano, and the hum of a jet cabin; Latin Pop, Pop Rock, Rock en Español. A shoomy string bridge and a sax bridge frame an observant, cinematic cabin narrative.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I tell myself I’ve talked enough for one flight\\
Claire keeps a Welsh lilt tucked inside each phrase\\
Fiona laughs, and Xumi leans toward her\\
I take my book and move behind their voices

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
They loosen up, word by word\\
I know the sound, not every turn

\songpart{Chorus}
Let them talk, let them talk\\
I’ll give my little battery a while\\
Let them talk, let them talk\\
I’m still here, just out of range

\songpart{Verse 2}
Their careful vowels turn familiar at the edges\\
North London, maybe—two paths through the same sentence\\
I miss the thread; I don’t mind missing it\\
I finish reading, pull my headphones on

\songpart{Chorus}
Let them talk, let them talk\\
I’ll give my small battery a while\\
Let them talk, let them talk\\
I’m still here, just out of reach

\songpart{Bridge: shoomy strings}
No need to solve their conversation\\
No need to join every little turn

\songpart{Bridge: saxophone}
I close my eyes\\
Still counting voices

\songpart{Outro}
The cabin keeps its even breathing\\
I let the words go on without me\\
When I open my eyes, will they still be laughing?
\end{lyrics}
