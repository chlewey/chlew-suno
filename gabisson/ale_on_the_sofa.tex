\songtitle{Ale On The Sofa}

\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the Grok summary of an autobiographical piece by Carlos Thompson. %
Names of characters changed in the input text.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Fast-paced country with driving acoustic strums, snappy kick-drum stomp, fiddle runs between lines, and a walking bass pushing the groove; verse 1 stays tight and conversational, pre-chorus opens with held harmony and rising pedal steel, chorus hits with gang-vocal repeats of the name. %
Lead vocal is dry and close, with doubled hooks, quick ad-libs, and a handclap lift into each chorus. %
Bright, punchy mix with a little room grit and lively barn-lean energy., sleep, country, theme
\end{style}
