\begin{style}
Dark cinematic fantasy score with slow 6/8 pulse, bowed low strings and frame drum beneath a glassy synth drone, now featuring intimate ethereal vocals; verses paint hyper-detailed bioluminescent forest imagery with sparse hand percussion and distant choir oohs, a pre-drop swell of reversed cymbals and brittle chimes heightens tension as the elf senses unseen movement, the main theme arrives with bold brass, harp flourishes, and layered choral harmonies, then a misty pad bridge narrows to breath-close whispers before a final lift where full orchestra, choirs, and shimmering electronics merge into a cold, electric, mythic climax.
\end{style}
