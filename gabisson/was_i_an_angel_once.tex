\songtitle{Was I an Angel Once?}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-ballad}\tag{industrial}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Grok, expanding \emph{Fallen} (page \pageref{sec:fallen}) into a pop-ballad structure. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 7 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop ballad, breathy female lead with intimate verse delivery and harmonizer-layered anthemic chorus vocals; 132 bpm with a steady, pumping pulse; tape-saturated production, sidechain pumping, plate reverb; analog synth pads, piano arpeggios, industrial percussion, sub-bass pulse, and a distorted guitar bridge.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I cannot trust myself, I cannot trust what I remember\\
Oh no, these pieces shift and blur the longer I surrender\\
Being with you has turned the fire that used to burn so bright\\
Into this quiet ember glowing soft against the night

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
I used to know my purpose, clear as morning light\\
To carry down the Word and lift it back toward the height\\
But every sacred duty feels like someone else’s name\\
And all those holy certainties have started looking strange

\songpart{Chorus}
Was I an angel once? I can’t recall the wings\\
Or if the light I carried was a gift or just a thing\\
Your doubt still cuts me open, yet I stay here at your side\\
Maybe heaven was the lie, and this is where I’m truly alive

\songpart{Verse 2}
Your apostasy still hurts me, every question that you raise\\
Feels like it’s rewriting every page of all my days\\
I thought I knew the difference between mercy and the fall\\
Now every memory I trusted doesn’t hold the truth at all

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
What seemed so clear now flickers, almost feels like make-believe\\
The voice that used to guide me only echoes when I leave\\
But somehow in your presence every hollow space feels filled\\
Like something locked inside me finally learned how to be still

\songpart{Chorus}
Was I an angel once? I can’t recall the wings\\
Or if the light I carried was a gift or just a thing\\
Your doubt still cuts me open, yet I stay here at your side\\
Maybe heaven was the lie, and this is where I’m truly alive

\songpart{Bridge}
In your arms I feel right, even when the past won’t stay\\
Even when I can’t remember who I was before this day\\
My skin and spine can breathe here, flaws and all, without disguise\\
Whoever I was before you… maybe she was never wise

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I cannot trust myself, and somehow that feels fine\\
The version of me that was certain left no room to change my mind\\
Was I an angel once? The question doesn’t keep me up at night\\
Because whoever that was, love… she probably wasn’t right

\songpart{Outro}
I cannot trust what I remember\\
But I trust the way I feel right now\\
With you.
\end{lyrics}
