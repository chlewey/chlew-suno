\songtitle{Line of Credit}

\tag*{2026}\tag{road-rock}\tag{blues-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:line-of-credit}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Thomas -- a sequel to \emph{Wheels Up} (page \pageref{sec:wheels-up}): other people's millions on his watch and nothing in his own name, from Barranquilla to Houston and the Bay, until an email on the fourteenth of March. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 20 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Road rock with blues-tinged shuffle: featured slide guitar, warm bass, Hammond organ, overdriven rhythm guitar; polished FM rock production; low male baritone, hushed close-mic conversational verses, doubled hooks and anthemic gang-vocal chorus; mid-tempo shuffle at 112 BPM in A major.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro – slide guitar, sparse shuffle, close low vocal}
No salary, no title, nothing in my name,\\
Just a line of credit and a seat on the plane.\\
The rent gets paid, the fridge is never bare,\\
Not by me. I'm somewhere in the air.

\songpart{Verse 1 – drums and bass enter, relaxed shuffle}
Barranquilla Monday, Medellín by Friday,\\
Cali on a Sunday, every city's got a highway.\\
Houston, then the Bay, my calendar's all gates,\\
Chasing down a future that's learning how to wait.\\
One July for old friends: a favor, no angle, no pay.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus – building, organ swell, snare}
Other people's millions, all of it on my watch,\\
I set it on its way, not a peso would I touch.\\
Big money, slow money, years before it grows:\\
I plant it, I protect it, and I'm broke. Nobody knows.

\songpart{Chorus – full band, gang vocals, swaggering}
Millions on my watch, a limit in my name,\\
Rich in other people's trust, broke all the same.\\
Boarding pass, a credit line, a plane that's always leaving,
(Wheels up!) Nothing in my pockets, and still believing.

\songpart{Verse 2 – palm-muted guitar, warmer reverb, easy swagger}
Medellín, a Twitter crush, a dinner and a smile,\\
Houston, an investor's friend with a lazy kind of style.\\
Every hotel bar's a door, every door's a night:\\
I left the guilt in Tokyo. It never made the flight.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus – building, one line cracks}
Other people's millions, all of it on my watch,\\
Not a peso of it mine, not a peso would I touch.\\
But the only thing I'm good at is the leaving, not the stay,\\
Funny how the freedom feels a lot like running away.

\songpart{Chorus – full band, gang vocals}
Millions on my watch, a limit in my name,\\
Rich in other people's trust, broke all the same.\\
Boarding pass, a credit line, a plane that's always leaving,
(Wheels up!) Nothing in my pockets, and still believing.

\songpart{Bridge – half-time, electric piano, intimate vocal}
Home on a Sunday, the kids asleep upstairs,\\
The kitchen light is burning, and somebody's still there.\\
I stand in the hall with my bag still on,
"Guilt-free," I tell myself. Then why's the quiet so long?

\songpart{Final Chorus – stripped, then rebuilds, doubled vocal, stunned not triumphant}
Fourteenth of March, an email, nothing more,\\
Cards back to zero, the limit's out the door.\\
Refresh the screen. It holds. Nothing in the sky.
(Wheels up!) Something in my name, and still I don't know why.

\songpart{Outro – slow, band drops out, voice and one guitar, fade out}
Read it twice, refreshed it once, closed the lid real slow,\\
Dialed home, she picked up on the second ring.
\annotation{spoken} "It's real," I say. "It's real."
\annotation{End}
\end{lyrics}
