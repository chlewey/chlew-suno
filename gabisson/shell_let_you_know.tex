\songtitle{She'll let you know}

\tag*{2025}\tag{new-age}\tag{original-source}

\begin{notes}
First part is a piece written by Carlos Thompson based on a fictional character. %
The second part is a lyrification by Carlos Thompson of a conversation about how the original text could be applied to other contexts.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
epic, choral, synthesizers, new age
\end{style}

\begin{lyrics}
When she's in the mood she just surrenders\\
 until it is time for her to take the initiative.

When she isn't in the mood she'll let you know.

you will know. 

When she wants it, she'll let you know with some fake disdain.

the fake disdain.

You need to read her carefully\\
to tell this fake disdain\\
from her unmoodiness.

her unmoodiness

could be your lover\\
could be your cat\\
your teenage daughter\\
or your old car

your printer, your project\\
or just your mind

she'll just surrender\\
then take the lead

or just stop you\\
at your own feet

\end{lyrics}
