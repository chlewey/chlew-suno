\songtitle{Wheels Up}

\tag*{2026}\tag{road-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:wheels-up}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Thomas -- the start-up years: no job and a baby down the hall, a pitch sent to strangers, then Tokyo, the Bay and San José, the hospitality he didn't walk away from, and landing back in Bogotá. \emph{Line of Credit} (page \pageref{sec:line-of-credit}) continues it. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 20 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Road rock; soft, firm low male baritone storyteller in conversational verses, call-and-response backing voices and doubled hooks, only the chorus opens into anthemic gang vocals; driving drums, overdriven guitars, jangly arpeggios, Hammond organ; 126 BPM in E major; polished FM-rock production.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro – sparse clean electric guitar, close vocal}
No job, no badge, a baby down the hall,\\
Rent on the table, back against the wall.\\
Typed up a dream on a late-night screen,\\
Sent it to strangers I had never seen.

\songpart{Verse 1 – drums enter, driving bass}
Twenty-first of May, the replies roll in,\\
Strangers say maybe, and maybes begin.\\
Tokyo's waiting, the Bay on the line,\\
A map full of pins and a suitcase of time.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus – building, snare roll, organ swell}
Visa's still pending, the clock's running down,\\
Passport in limbo, one foot out of town.\\
What if they stamp it with a big red no?\\
Then it clears. Wheels up. Time to go.

\songpart{Chorus – full band, anthemic, gang vocals}
(Wheels up!) Wheels up, heart racing, headlights on a dream,\\
Starting up from nothing, nothing's what it seems.\\
They say the jackpot's waiting down the road,\\
But is it real, or a mirage that never shows?

\songpart{Verse 2 – palm-muted, darker, more reverb}
Tokyo neon, a boardroom full of suits,\\
Polite little bows, and the pitch takes root.\\
Then a courtesy from the hosts, a card on the tray,
"Part of the service," and I don't walk away.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus – building, uneasy}
Business is breathing, the numbers look right,\\
One hand on the future, one out of sight.\\
Guilt in one pocket, relief in the other,\\
Can't tell the sinner from the working father.

\songpart{Chorus – full band, gang vocals}
(Wheels up!) Wheels up, heart racing, headlights on a dream,\\
Starting up from nothing, nothing's what it seems.\\
They say the jackpot's waiting down the road,\\
But is it real, or a mirage that never shows?

\songpart{Bridge – half-time, electric piano, intimate vocal}
San José sunrise, the valley's all gold,\\
Meetings run long and the yeses unfold.\\
A second chance across a candlelit glass,\\
Less guilt than before, and that's what stings:\\
It goes down easy, and it goes down fast.

\songpart{Final Chorus – biggest, doubled vocals, key-lift feel}
(Wheels down!) Wheels down, heart racing, the signs are turning green,\\
The jackpot's looking real now, the realest I have seen.\\
But every good road ends where the headlights fade:\\
What if it's all over before the dream is made?

\songpart{Outro – slow, band drops out, voice and one guitar, fade out}
Landed in Bogotá, September light,\\
She's waiting by the door: "How did it go? Were you all right?"
\annotation{spoken} "Productive," I say.\\
And every word is true...
(just not all of it.)
\annotation{End}
\end{lyrics}
