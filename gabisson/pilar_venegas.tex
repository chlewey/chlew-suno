\begin{style}
Bambuco with lilting Andean 6/8 rhythm, crisp guitar and tiple arpeggios, gentle bombo pulse, and verses that breathe like a mountain walk; verse one frames her rise from Ibagué to army captain, pre-chorus lifts on legal and intelligence details, chorus blooms with warm unison melody and tight backing thirds, bridge softens to intimate close-mic confession before the final chorus returns fuller, Lead vocal is clear and noble with selective harmony swells, short delay throws on the name line, and subtle string flourishes, flute pickups, and rain-stick transitions for ear candy; mix is warm, bright, and acoustic-polished, minimal
\end{style}
