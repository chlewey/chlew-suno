\songtitle{Stay A Little}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\label{sec:stay-a-little}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Sylvia's diary entries in \emph{Christmas 2024} by Carlos Thompson (December 2024 to January 2025), from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Sylvia Lai -- arriving at Villa Jaguar by the Caribbean, a walk on the public shore with Jairo, the cranes drawing the future port, Susana thinking of a flat nearby, and the family flying in. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 11 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie-pop / it-pop, young female lead vocal, buoyant mid-tempo groove with crisp handclaps and tight live drums, rounded bass, bright jangly electric guitar and small piano accents. Begin with a close, conversational verse over sparse guitar; open into a catchy, layered chorus with light harmonies, then pull back for a reflective bridge before a final fuller chorus. Clean, warm, polished mix with an intimate vocal up front and a breezy coastal feel.
\end{style}
