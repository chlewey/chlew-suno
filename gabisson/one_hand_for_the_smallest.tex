\songtitle{One Hand for the Smallest}

\setin{2026-09}
\tag*{2026}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{aurora-swansen}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Steven Swansen}, a story by Carlos Thompson narrated by Aurora Swansen, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- in Aurora's voice, her father as she remembers him, the distance kept since she left for Bogotá, and his stroke. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dark singer-songwriter ballad, intimate New England female lead with clear regional cadence and unvarnished storytelling, slow measured pulse, low piano and muted acoustic guitar, restrained cello entering beneath the verses, subtle brushed percussion only in later sections, arrangement gradually widens around the refrain then falls back to voice and piano for the closing thought, close dry vocal with natural breaths, warm grain and understated room ambience, mix keeps the words forward and avoids melodrama.
\end{style}
