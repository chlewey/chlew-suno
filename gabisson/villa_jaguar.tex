\songtitle{Villa Jaguar}

\tag*{2026}\tag{nu-metal}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{punta-espada}\label{sec:villa-jaguar}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a news clip written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- the November assault on Villa Jaguar, near Punta Espada, and its toll. \emph{Little Guajiro} (page \pageref{sec:little-guajiro}) tells the same night from inside. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 27 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic nu-metal with jagged drop-tuned guitar riffs, syncopated half-time drums, distorted bass, and forceful low male vocals shifting between tight rhythmic verse delivery and a broad anthemic chorus. Layer bold, tense brass stabs and deep tambora patterns into the groove; open with ominous brass and hand percussion, build through clipped verses, hit a crushing chorus, drop to a sparse percussion-and-voice bridge, then return with full-band weight. Dark, urgent, documentary-like storytelling, modern punchy mix with raw vocal edges and wide cinematic low end.
\end{style}
