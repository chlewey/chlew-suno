\songtitle{Where Your Yes Begins}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folktronica}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\label{sec:where-your-yes-begins}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the second section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Rotterdam on the eighth of July, two queen beds and Claire's line, a kiss that was hers to give, and the flights on to Teterboro and Dallas: disappointed in the story in his head, not in her. Follows \emph{Roads Run North} (page \pageref{sec:roads-run-north}); \emph{Just the Road} (page \pageref{sec:just-the-road}) continues it. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Electronic Welsh folk with a steady, measured pulse; modal fiddle and harp phrases threaded through restrained synth bass and crisp electronic percussion, with subtle Welsh folk ornamentation. Male baritone in natural American English pronunciation, close and conversational in the verses, opening into a more melodic refrain. Build from a spare, intimate opening into a fuller but controlled chorus, then strip back for a reflective bridge and resolve with a quiet final refrain. Clear, detailed mix with organic strings against precise electronic rhythm.
\end{style}

Rotterdam, the eighth of July\\
Same arrival, same late sky\\
Two queen beds, one careful line\\
“Don’t touch me unless I ask this time”\\
You let the silence hold its shape\\
Then crossed the room and slipped away\\
Bare as daylight, calm and sure\\
I stayed beside the closed-off door

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Morning came; you came to me\\
Your kiss was yours to freely give\\
I reached too far, you drew the line\\
And I heard what you had said that night

\songpart{Chorus}

Not in you, but in the story in my head\\
You owed me nothing, not a thing\\

So I’ll meet you where your yes begins\\
And leave the rest where it has been
