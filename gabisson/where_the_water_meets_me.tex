\songtitle{Where the Water Meets Me}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-rock}\tag{latin-jazz}\tag{original-source}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{zahra-moradi}\label{sec:where-the-water-meets-me}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on \emph{Lines Not Crossed} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, a duet of Thomas and Zahra during her visit to Punta Espada in December 2026 -- on the beach with her family nearby: out of time and out of step, no answers asked, and each walking a different way along the water. From the same story as \emph{Don't Mistake the Bridge for Home} (page \pageref{sec:dont-mistake-the-bridge-for-home}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 3 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Intimate folk-rock with Latin jazz; steady mid-tempo sway; organic live-room feel, warm tape texture, clear vocal intimacy, understated dynamics; male tenor and female voices trade close, conversational verses, then join for lightly stacked, doubled hook harmonies that widen at the climax; acoustic guitar pulse, warm upright bass, brushed kit, hand percussion, syncopated piano montuno, mellow tenor sax and muted trumpet answering the vocal lines, occasional fiddle; final return to guitar, soft percussion, and a lingering horn phrase.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1: Male}
I was afraid you'd come back here,\\
Afraid the old words would still be true.\\
I turn a page and tear the corner;\\
I meant something deep to you.\\
And if I still mean something, Zahra,\\
I don't know what that asks us to do.

\songpart{Verse 2: Female}
You know I'm not expecting answers,\\
I won't ask the past to make a plan.\\
I thought you might be passing through me,\\
A brief warmth in a foreign land.\\
Gray days, a city I was learning,\\
A story I could fold by hand.

\songpart{Chorus: Both}
We were out of time, out of step,\\
Two hearts reading different lines.\\
You can keep what mattered, I can go,\\
No one has to call it right.\\
The tide keeps drawing, tide keeps lifting,\\
And the water takes the words we couldn't place.

\songpart{Verse 3: Female}
You were kind enough to make me notice\\
I needed someone steady by my side.\\
If you'd been cruel, I'd have closed the chapter,\\
Never asked where all your other loves might lie.\\
But you were good, and that was harder;\\
I thought you'd be the one who'd stay.

\songpart{Verse 4: Male}
I keep turning over what went wrong,\\
Trying to hear the shape beneath your words.\\
Your eyes, your mouth, the space between us—\\
A thought I won't turn into more.\\
Your family laughs beyond my shoulder;\\
I let the quiet have the shore.

\songpart{Chorus: Both}
We were out of time, out of step,\\
Two hearts reading different lines.\\
You can keep what mattered, I can go,\\
No one has to call it right.\\
The tide keeps drawing, tide keeps lifting,\\
And the water takes the words we couldn't place.

\songpart{Bridge: Male}
You turn toward the sand and join them,\\
I turn toward the open sea.\\
Not every love becomes a promise;\\
Some stay deep and still set you free.

\songpart{Outro: Both}
No answer, no demand,\\
Just the waves around our feet.\\
You have your people in the sand,\\
I walk where the water meets me.
\end{lyrics}
