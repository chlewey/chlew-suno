\songtitle{Pizza on the Table}

\tag*{2024}\tag{narcocorrido}\tag{ricochets}

\begin{notes}
Based on a companion text from \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson.\\
Lyrics by Suno based on companion text \emph{The Deal} by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
English-language narcocorrido with brisk tom-driven pulse and accordion lead, swung with a tense borderland bounce; verse rides sparse bass, guitar, and snare taps, pre-chorus tightens with handclaps and rising trumpet, chorus hits with gang shouts and doubled lead, bridge drops to lone accordion and spoken grit before the final lift. %
Close-mic male vocal, gritty and direct, with vinyl crackle and sharp brass stabs in a punchy, dry mix, low, sophisticated
\end{style}
