\songtitle{Not a Label, Not a Part}

\tag*{2026}\tag{industrial-metal}\tag{never-fall}
\begin{notes}
Adaptation by Claude of \emph{Never Fall} -- the same defiant anti-division message, reworked into a harsher industrial-metal register with new lyrics rather than the same words. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 24 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Industrial metal with tape saturation, stereo-wide mix, and plate-reverb snare; driving 138 BPM pulse; clipped half-shouted verses, barked mechanical unison chorus, flat processed spoken breakdown, layered final shouts; seven-string drop-tuned palm-muted chugs, syncopated double kick, industrial drum machine, programmed noise bed, distorted static loop, and filtered voice samples.
\end{style}
