\songtitle{Watching the Play}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sophisti-pop}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{sylvia-lai}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{erika-czajka}\tag{victoria-ospina}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\label{sec:watching-the-play}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson in the voice of Sylvia, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, extracting a thesis from the diary entry behind \emph{Different Maps} (page \pageref{sec:different-maps}): Erika and Vicky are right for each other. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Euro pop, sophisti-pop with glossy synth sheen and 80s radio-pop polish; 132 BPM four-on-the-floor drive; close-mic youthful female vocals, wide-ranging with legato crooning that climbs into top notes; palm-muted electric guitar, sharp keyboard stabs, muted bass guitar, gated snare.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I won't die on this hill, but I insist this makes sense.\\
I'm not finding hidden truths, but reading with another lens.\\
Don't get me wrong, Erika and Thomas stay just friends—\\
close and intimate, but not each other's romantic ends.

\songpart{Chorus}
Or maybe I want things to secretly be this way.\\
I won't die on this hill, I'm just watching the play.\\
Erika and Vicky are right for each other, despite\\
they are still denying what's so clear in this light.

\songpart{Verse 2}
This is not what is latent now, just a speculative what-if:\\
what if Thomas wasn't in love with his cabin chief.\\
Of the women in his life, Erika is who he's most compatible with,\\
and that connection is sincere, mutual, clear — no myth.

\songpart{Chorus}
Or maybe I want things to secretly be this way.\\
I won't die on this hill, I'm just watching the play.\\
The hidden connections neither of them would admit,\\
the underlying camaraderie they won’t submit.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Erika could have been to Thomas both what Claire and Gabi are,\\
but that door was already closed, no place for no cigar.\\
And for Erika, Thomas is so clearly her perfect right—\\
the one who gets her cravings and energy into the light.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Or maybe I want things to secretly be this way.\\
I won't die on this hill, I'm just watching the play.\\
Erika and Vicky, my sisters and partners in crime,\\
Thomas my lover, my sugar, and Gabi my sky.\\
This thesis is likely what is still romantic in mine.
\end{lyrics}
