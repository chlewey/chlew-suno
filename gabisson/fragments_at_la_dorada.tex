\songtitle{Fragments at La Dorada}

\tag*{2026}\tag{country-folk}\tag{ricochets}\tag{la-dorada}\tag{john-carlson}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Claude summary of chapter 10 \emph{Heat} from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Tense country-folk story song with vallenato instrumentation: steady acoustic guitar, dry caja vallenata percussion, melodic button accordion fills, warm upright bass, restrained fiddle, and a dusty nocturnal groove, English-language male lead vocal with clear narrative diction, intimate verses that build into a memorable cautionary chorus, Arrangement moves from sparse stealthy opening to fuller accordion-and-fiddle tension, drops nearly bare during the patrol wait and microphone failure, then resolves with a quiet dawn outro, Organic roots mix, close vocal, room ambience, controlled dynamics, no glossy pop polish
\end{style}
