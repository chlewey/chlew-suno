\songtitle{Not an Infrastructure Guy}

\tag*{2026}\tag{indie-folk}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{aurora-swansen}\label{sec:not-an-infrastructure-guy}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok (interacting on structure) based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- on the beach with Aurora: infrastructure is what he ended up doing, not what he is, and the only thing he wants to build now is a story. \emph{Where the Tide Turns} (page \pageref{sec:where-the-tide-turns}) sets the same walk. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Indie folk singer-songwriter, sparse acoustic guitar with gently picked strings and open pauses, unhurried sway, slightly raspy male baritone in a mellow close delivery, stacked roomy unprocessed harmonies on the hooks, soft waves beneath the vocal, natural intimate production with space for silence.
\end{style}
