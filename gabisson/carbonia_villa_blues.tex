\begin{style}
Blues shuffle at a mid-tempo sway with brush drums, upright bass, greasy electric guitar fills, and a smoky Hammond bed; verse rides story-heavy and conversational, pre-chorus tightens with stop-time hits, chorus opens on a chantable line with gang responses and short guitar answers, Close-mic lead vocal with dry doubles on the hook, a few delay throws on names, turnarounds marked by harmonica sighs and room-tone swells, Warm, worn, intimate mix with a dusty club glow, blues, light, world
\end{style}
