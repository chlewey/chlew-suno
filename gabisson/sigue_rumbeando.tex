\songtitle{Sigue Rumbeando}

\tag*{2026}\tag{salsa}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Translated and adapted to salsa by Claude from \emph{Keep On Rolling} (page \pageref{sec:keep-on-rolling}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 8 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Salsa puertorriqueña, piano guajeo driving the arrangement over conga tumbao, campana bell pattern, bass tumbao, trombone stabs and punchy horn hits; 180 BPM syncopated clave groove with four-on-the-floor energy; projected male lead with call-and-response group shouts and montuno vamp; live club ambience, plate reverb, tape saturation.
\end{style}
