\begin{style}
Mid-tempo British alternative rock with restrained art-pop detail, around 108 BPM, Female lead vocal with polished upper-class British pronunciation, controlled and observant in the verses, opening into a quietly devastating chorus, Fingerpicked acoustic bass movement and dry hand percussion underpin clean electric guitar arpeggios; add restrained overdriven guitar swells in the chorus, a brief darker bridge, and a spacious but intimate mix, Keep the arrangement taut, literate, and emotionally unsentimental, with clear dynamic growth from sparse opening to layered final refrain
\end{style}
