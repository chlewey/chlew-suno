\songtitle{A Quiet Kindness}

\tag*{2024}\tag*{2026}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{encounters}\tag{brittany}\tag{thomas-bauer}\label{sec:a-quiet-kindness}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Encounters} vignette series by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on the Stable Diffusion prompts (2024) for Brittany Summers and Thomas Braun -- two strangers at a café table, close enough not to be alone. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Adult contemporary Americana, gentle mid-tempo brushed roots groove, fingerpicked acoustic guitar and rounded piano, restrained pedal steel answering the vocal, soft bass and understated drums; scene-setting verses unfold like a quiet café vignette, chorus opens into a memorable melodic lift, bridge pares back to voice and guitar before a warm final chorus. Intimate, clear lead vocal, natural phrasing, organic close-miked production with spacious but restrained room tone.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Brittany at the tall café table,\\
coffee cooling by her hand,\\
flowered green and yellow cotton,\\
straw hat shading where she stands.\\
Freckles gathered on her cheekbones,\\
silver chain against her skin,\\
her eyes lowered to the saucer,\\
like she’s weighing where to begin.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Outside, the afternoon keeps moving,\\
inside, the little moments stay.

\songpart{Chorus}
Two lives sharing the same small room,\\
two cups, two separate afternoons.\\
She holds her coffee, he checks his phone,\\
close enough to not be alone.\\
A quiet kindness, a place to land,\\
a warm cup passing from hand to hand.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Thomas turns his latte slowly,\\
purple sleeves and tired eyes,\\
gray beard softened by the window,\\
while his screen keeps asking why.\\
The day has left its mark around him,\\
he lets the silence do its part;\\
Brittany’s hat tips toward the table,\\
a little garden in the heart.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No one needs to say a thing here,\\
no one has to leave just yet.

\songpart{Chorus}
Two lives sharing the same small room,\\
two cups, two separate afternoons.\\
She holds her coffee, he checks his phone,\\
close enough to not be alone.\\
A quiet kindness, a place to land,\\
a warm cup passing from hand to hand.

\songpart{Bridge}
Maybe it’s the chair beside you,\\
maybe it’s the door held wide,\\
maybe being seen for one brief moment\\
is enough to turn the tide.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Two lives sharing the same small room,\\
two cups, a softer afternoon.\\
She lifts her eyes; he sets his phone down,\\
and kindness moves without a sound.\\
A quiet kindness, a place to land,\\
a warm cup passing from hand to hand.

\songpart{Outro}
The cups go empty, the day goes on,\\
but something gentle lingers when they’re gone.
\end{lyrics}
