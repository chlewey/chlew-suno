\songtitle{A Little More Welcome}

\tag*{2024}\tag*{2026}\tag{synth-pop}\tag{encounters}\tag{uneekwa}\tag{charly-manos}\label{sec:a-little-more-welcome}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Encounters} vignette series by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on the Stable Diffusion prompts (2024) for Uneekwa Johnson and Charly ``Chuck'' Manos -- a wave on a downtown corner that makes the afternoon lighter. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Uplifting synth-pop with a buoyant four-on-the-floor pulse, crisp claps, rounded bass, bright arpeggiated synths, and glossy melodic hooks. Scene-setting verses build into an expansive, optimistic chorus; a brief stripped-back bridge gives way to a full final refrain. Warm expressive lead vocal, polished modern mix, energetic and self-assured.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Uneekwa steps out, green satin catching noon,\\
Braids across her shoulders, walking to her tune.\\
Phone in one hand, purse swinging at her side,\\
A grin that says she’s got the whole day on her side.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
At the corner, Charly sees her coming through,\\
Brown hoodie, easy smile, and a little nod hello.\\
Two paths cross where the busy avenues meet,\\
And the afternoon feels lighter underneath their feet.

\songpart{Chorus}
Walk on, Uneekwa, let the whole town know,\\
You carry your own kind of sunlight wherever you go.\\
Charly joins the moment, smiling as you pass,\\
One bright hello can make a good day last.\\
Walk on, walk on, sure and free,\\
A little more welcome on this downtown street.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Silver chain and earrings, maroon on her smile,\\
Boot heels mark a steady rhythm, mile after mile.\\
Charly holds the corner, shoulders loose and kind,\\
She waves; he waves back, and they fall into stride.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No grand introduction, no need to explain,\\
Just a shared small kindness in the middle of the day.\\
The crosswalk turns green, the traffic rolls away,\\
And they head on smiling in their separate ways.

\songpart{Chorus}
Walk on, Uneekwa, let the whole town know,\\
You carry your own kind of sunlight wherever you go.\\
Charly joins the moment, smiling as you pass,\\
One bright hello can make a good day last.\\
Walk on, walk on, sure and free,\\
A little more welcome on this downtown street.

\songpart{Bridge}
For a breath, the crowd makes room,\\
A friendly look can change the view.\\
Then onward, easy, unafraid—\\
The city opens as they make their way.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Walk on, Uneekwa, let the whole town know,\\
You carry your own kind of sunlight wherever you go.\\
Charly joins the moment, smiling as you pass,\\
One bright hello can make a good day last.\\
Walk on, walk on, sure and free,\\
A little more welcome on this downtown street.

\songpart{Outro}
Uneekwa walks on, Charly turns the corner,\\
Two smiles in the city, and the day feels warmer.
\end{lyrics}
