\songtitle{We're Drifting}

\tag*{2026}\tag{heartland-rock}\tag{adult-contemporary}\tag{drift}\tag{damon}\tag{fran}\tag{raoul}
\begin{notes}
Adapted into English by Grok from \emph{Vamos a la deriva} (page \pageref{sec:vamos-a-la-deriva}). Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 12 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Adult contemporary heartland rock, analog tape saturation with plate and spring reverb, subtle delay throws; strained-chest male tenor lead with female alto harmony; mid-tempo stumbling groove with a steady chorus lift; electric guitar arpeggios and fiddle countermelody over warm bass guitar, brushed snare, and muted kick.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1—Damon}
The spark of college and that young love flame,\\
your years at Purdue and the quiet that came,\\
your hunger to take on the world and to win,\\
still showing your trophies while the race isn’t in—\\
feeling what’s yours is yours, not someone else’s claim,\\
and still, with it all, feeling you’re not the same.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The fire of beginning turns to everyday grind,\\
hiding down in the basement, leaving your truth behind,\\
feeling the life that you wanted you don’t want anymore,\\
and whatever’s still missing you’ll never restore.

\songpart{Chorus}
And we’re drifting, just letting things slide,\\
and we’re drifting, no longer putting up a fight,\\
and we’re drifting, with nothing left on our mind.

Damon and Fran, Damon and Fran, life keeps moving on.\\
Raoul, Raoul, you don’t have a map.\\
And we’re drifting, with nothing left on our mind.

\songpart{Verse 2—Fran}
The spark of college and the world at your feet,\\
your years at Purdue and the life that came after that heat.\\
From the rust of Gary to the suburbs of Dayton, Ohio,\\
family, good job, solid paycheck—nothing to show\\
but the spark is gone and you feel ungrateful inside,\\
a husband you still love and a silence that won’t subside.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And the spark lights up again where it never should,\\
you don’t know what you want or if it’s just breaking the wood.\\
Thoughts spinning in circles, secret smiles and fear,\\
you can’t be yourself—that was never the creed you held dear.

\songpart{Chorus}

and we’re drifting, with nothing left on our mind.

Damon and Fran, Damon and Fran, life keeps moving on.\\

\songpart{Breakdown—Raoul}
Son of the South, born in Detroit,\\
raised on New Orleans nights,\\
French-Canadian blood in your veins,\\
Texas for college and the quiet it brings.\\
Transplanted to the Midwest,\\
with its tighter, closed-in ways,\\
and this woman in the office—\\
you click, but she’s already claimed.\\
You don’t know what’s the wise move\\
or if wisdom is just another word for fear.\\
But there’s no real urgency—\\
it’s still early here.

\songpart{Outro}
Damon—a life you no longer understand,\\
one that got lost somewhere along the road.\\
Fran—a spark that’s fading out,\\
and an office temptation that won’t let go.\\
Raoul—a culture that still costs you,\\
and the forbidden you refuse to own.

And a drift that never ends.
