\songtitle{Evi Sem Yike}

\tag*{1992}\tag{post-rock}\tag{original-source}

\begin{notes}
Based on an original 1992 narrative. %
The song follows a deeply troubled protagonist haunted by an old love and the self-inflicted pain of obsession, building toward a cathartic post-rock climax.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
post-rock, indie rock, 118 BPM, minor-key acoustic arpeggio, rain foley, baritone vocals, close-mic whisper, syncopated bassline, jangly rhythm guitar, live drum break, distorted guitar wall, echoing piano chord, plate reverb, tape saturation, sharp snare cut, whispered confessional, desperate belt, bleak catharsis, smooth vocal transitions
\end{style}

\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Movement I: The Frame (0:00 – 0:45)}
\annotation{Sonic Environment: A lonely, repeating acoustic guitar arpeggio in a minor key. %
The distant, muffled sound of rain hitting a windowpane. %
The vocals are delivered in a flat, exhausted whisper, right up against the microphone.}

Cold water dripping from my hair to the floor\\
Peeling off the damp fabric, letting it drop by the door\\
The mattress is freezing, the sheets feel like lead\\
Just a hollowed-out animal staring up from the bed.

The quiet is heavy, the silence is loud\\
Why does the dark feel so much like a shroud?\\
I close my eyes, but the ceiling won’t fade\\
And I’m back in the room where the comedy played…

\songpart{Movement II: The Shift (0:45 – 2:15)}
\annotation{Sonic Environment: The rain fades. %
A sudden, jarring tempo change. %
A bouncy, syncopated indie-rock drumbeat and a driving bassline kick in. %
The guitar switches to jangly, bright chords. %
The vocals become faster, rhythmic, and laced with cynical energy.}

Look at me now, what a wonderful guest!\\
Laughing at jokes, putting my mask to the test.\\
A room full of warmth, a rare invitation,\\
But I’m just an actor simulating conversation.\\
A total hypocrite, blending right in,\\
Hiding the sickness under my skin.

Because I’m watching the floor, and I’m watching the door,\\
And my mind is a predator wanting much more.\\
There’s Gredë Mau Ilemb, she’s laughing with friends,\\
I’m tracking her shadow where the hallway ends.\\
And Yerdë Fansë, and Inë Te Savi—\\
The air in this party is getting too heavy.

I want them, I hate them, I feel so slighted,\\
By sparks in my head that were never ignited.\\
They don’t even know! They are totally blind\\
To the monstrous trial taking place in my mind.\\
They’re completely innocent, living their lives,\\
While I sit in the corner, sharpening knives.

\songpart{Movement III: The Collapse (2:15 – 3:30)}
\annotation{Sonic Environment: The driving rhythm section abruptly vanishes. %
The tempo drops significantly. %
The bright guitar vanishes, replaced by a solitary, echoing piano chord that rings out into emptiness. %
The vocals slow down, becoming raw, trembling, and deeply mournful.}

\annotation{The music breathes... %
a long pause}

No... %
it’s not them. %
It was never them.\\
I’m just rewriting the script to condemn.\\
Because the floor dropped out years ago,\\
In a fracture so deep that it refuses to grow.

Viel Derë...

Your name is the rust in my veins.\\
Your ghost is the only thing guiding the reins.\\
When you walked away, the cosmos went black,\\
Every mirror I look at is shattered and cracked.\\
I’m trying to bleed onto anyone new,\\
Just to see if the color reminds me of you.

\songpart{Movement IV: The Climax / Epiphany (3:30 – 4:15)}
\annotation{Sonic Environment: A massive post-rock crescendo begins. %
Thundering live drums enter. %
Layers of heavily distorted electric guitars swell, building a suffocating wall of sound. %
The vocals rise into a passionate crescendo, desperate belt, without screaming or shouting, over the noise.}

And it’s a crime! It’s a self-inflicted sin!\\
To tear down the house just to see what’s within!\\
I’m committing an injustice, a beautiful lie,\\
Feeding on ghosts while the living pass by!

I can’t use Grede Mau to fix what you broke!\\
I can’t breathe their air while I’m trying to choke!\\
It’s a phantom! A shadow! A conceptual cell!\\
I am building the walls of my own private hell!

\songpart{Movement V: The Return / Burnout (4:15 – 5:00)}
\annotation{Sonic Environment: The wall of sound instantly cuts out on a sharp snare hit. %
A vacuum of silence follows. %
Then, the minimalist acoustic guitar loop from the intro slowly creeps back in. %
The ambient rain returns. %
The vocals return to a defeated, breathless whisper.}

\annotation{The sound of heavy, shaky breathing}

So I choke back the moan... %
turn my face to the wall.\\
My brother is sleeping just down the hall.\\
He knows how I get. %
He leaves me alone.\\
A statue of salt, turning back into stone.\\
No more chasing the girls. %
No more fanning the spark.\\
I’ll just pull up the blankets… and stay in the dark.

\annotation{Sonic Environment: The guitar loop plays one final time, dissolving into the rhythmic sound of rain against the window. %
Fade to total silence.}
\end{lyrics}
