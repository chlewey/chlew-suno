\songtitle{Run, Little Benny}

% -> stories.tex (Cats); generos.tex (Montuno & Brass)
\tag*{2026}\tag{death-metal}
\begin{notes}
Written by Suno after a specific prompt by Carlos Thompson on style and plot -- Benny the cat outrunning a pack of dogs across town to reach Lola, set to a death-metal-plays-salsa genre collision. Retrieved from Gabriel's Suno page (V6-MINI, 25 September 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Death metal attempting to play salsa, dramatic guttural male lead with clear narrative phrasing, frantic syncopated Latin dance groove, downtuned distorted guitars, blast-beat bursts, bright brass punches, busy hand percussion, stalk-forward verses erupting into catchy danceable choruses, abrupt stop-start breaks, raw chaotic live-room energy with deliberately unruly genre collision
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Benny slipped past the fish shop door,\\
One torn ear and a paw gone sore.\\
Across the square, on a balcony rail,\\
Lola was waiting with a ribbon tail.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
But the mutts by the bakery lifted their heads,\\
Three sets of teeth and a hunger for strays.\\
Benny tucked low by the barrels and crates,\\
One careful step, then he raced.

\songpart{Chorus}
Run, little Benny, cut through the town,\\
Past the brass shop, keep your whiskers down.\\
Lola's upstairs where the blue pots lean,\\
One brave cat crossing the dogs' whole scene.\\
Run, little Benny, quick as a spark,\\
Love's on the landing; the lane's gone dark.

\songpart{Verse 2}
He ducked through the laundry, slid under a cart,\\
A dog hit the puddle and barked at the start.\\
He crossed by the fountain when wheels rolled by,\\
Then climbed up the wall where the ivy ran high.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The pack hit the corner, paws drumming stone,\\
Benny kept moving, determined alone.\\
One leap to the awning, one jump to the stair,\\
Lola leaned down and called from up there.

\songpart{Chorus}
Run, little Benny, cut through the town,\\
Past the brass shop, keep your whiskers down.\\
Lola's upstairs where the blue pots lean,\\
One brave cat crossing the dogs' whole scene.\\
Run, little Benny, quick as a spark,\\
Love's on the landing; the lane's gone dark.

\songpart{Bridge}
The dogs reached the doorway--too late, too late.\\
Benny was safe by the iron gate.\\
Lola pressed noses; the whole street sighed.\\
He brought her a sardine he'd saved inside.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Run, little Benny, you made it through,\\
The town can keep all its barking and noise.\\
Lola's upstairs where the blue pots lean,\\
Two little cats and a night kept clean.\\
Run, little Benny, home from the chase,\\
A ribbon-tail welcome, a warm hiding place.

\songpart{Outro}
One torn ear, two purring hearts,\\
Tomorrow they'll choose a different start.
\end{lyrics}
