Not Even Mine
Ricochets, ch2 Routine Breaking - Henry Little's backyard, Long Island, 2005-06-25 (scene undated in the text)
Source: ch2, the barbecue conversation between Fabián Müller and Henry Little about the Miami garage shooting
Voice: Fabián Müller, first person; Henry Little's questions as a second, deeper voice
Style: swampy blues-rock, slow heavy groove, dirty slide guitar and resonator, fuzz bass, stomping kick and tambourine, Hammond organ swells, humid reverb; smooth, controlled male baritone with a faint Latin accent, half-sung and half-spoken; second voice a deep, gravelly bass-baritone; call-and-response; 72 BPM, E minor

[Intro]
[slide guitar, crickets, a grill hissing, a beer cap popping]

[Verse 1]
[Müller]
Long Island Saturday, the smoke is in the trees,
big man in a Nirvana shirt hands another beer to me.
Gray coming through his hair cut short, black denim, easy grin,
but his eyes ain't on the barbecue, his eyes are looking in.

[Little, spoken]
"I haven't wanted to ask, but I'm worried now:
what the heck happened in Miami that night?"

[Verse 2]
[Müller]
I went to meet a lawyer, a contract, nothing more,
found Gunther and his gunners waiting there before.
A black Lincoln rolling in, the Diablos at the wheel,
then a lady in a Town & Country, and the air turned into steel.
She asked which one was Roberto,
and the shooting started then.
Khamid shoved me in the truck,
and the cops boxed us in.

[Pre-Chorus]
[Little]
"I read the report, Mr. Müller."
[Müller]
Well, you know how those reports are made.
[Little]
"DEA says nothing happened."
[Müller]
Nothing happens when it's paid.

[Chorus]
Reports can't be trusted, not even mine,
I tell it how I recall it, and I read between the lines.
I tell it to who's asking, and I tell it how it pays,
you can trust what I remember, not the things I didn't say.
Not even mine, no,
not even mine.

[Instrumental break]
[slide solo over stomp and tambourine]

[Verse 3]
[Little]
"So what do you think happened?"
[Müller]
No deal was on the table, no powder changing hands,
they called us to that parking lot to finish other plans.
They wanted Gunther dead, and me dead or framed,
and the lawyer in the minivan, I'm sure they wanted the same.
Someone in Miami PD planted what they found,
someone in the DEA told Spitz how to knock it down.

[Bridge]
[slow, organ swells]
[Little]
"A lawyer and an eight-year-old?"
[Müller]
Sarah Carlson was her name.
The girl, I don't think they wanted her,
but a bullet don't take aim.
Same firm, Nash-something, as the man I came to meet,
and the firing started right after she spoke,
the moment she spoke, in that heat.
Tense with Gunther and the Diablos, yeah,
but not that kind of tense.

[Chorus]
Reports can't be trusted, not even mine,
I tell it how I recall it, and I read between the lines.
I tell it to who's asking, and I tell it how it pays,
you can trust what I remember, not the things I didn't say.
Not even mine, no,
not even mine.

[Outro]
[groove drops to slide and kick; a voice calls their names from far off]
[Little, spoken, suddenly friendly]
"Well, well, well, Mr. Müller.
The barbecue is ready, and let's not make Mrs. Little wait."
[Müller, half-sung]
Not even mine...
[End]
