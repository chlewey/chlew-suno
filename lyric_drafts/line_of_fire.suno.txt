Line of Fire
Ricochets, ch13 The Glades - Miguel Morocho's fifth-floor office, Cali, est. 2005-08-31
Source: ch13, the plastic bag on Morocho's desk ("A passport. Credit cards. A Motorola Razr."), taken by petty muggers after the April 2004 garage shooting and recovered by Morocho's people (the passport seized in Panama from someone trying to use it); Morocho's rum against Carlson's Coke; "She put herself in the line of fire… The daughter… no one anticipated"; "Let's get some lunch."
Voice: Miguel Morocho, first person, singing to John Carlson; coro in Spanish
Style: minor-key Cali salsa bolero, slow son montuno feel, velvet piano tumbao, muted trumpet, soft congas and bongó, güiro, warm upright bass; opens as a bolero with nylon guitar and rises into a restrained salsa in the coro, never fully breaking loose; smooth, velvety male baritone, courteous and unhurried, a host's warmth over bad news; coro of male voices answering in Spanish; 92 BPM, A minor

[Intro]
[nylon guitar bolero, a ceiling fan, ice dropped into a glass]

[Verse 1]
[male:]
Siéntese, John, the fifth floor is cooler,
the river's out there doing what it does.

I remember how you take it, como era.
On the desk, a plastic bag, nothing fancy,
I'd rather hand it over than explain.
A passport. Credit cards. A Motorola Razr.
Go on. Take your time. I'll pour again.
