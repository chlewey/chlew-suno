# The Issue

If you ask my daughter, she'd probably say I don't notice what she is into. I am too busy in my own affairs, building and maintaining a company, dodging threats of the trade, spending time in the local jet-set and reaching out to the international stage. Before college, she only visited a few days a week if I was in town. How can a father be in her everyday life? Now, she is in college, in a faraway town.

I am not the kind of parent that keeps asking. I feel interrogations are too intrusive. I am used to other ways to be aware of what matters. I have always kept an eye on her. Her mother shares with me most relevant details. Her teachers knew how and when to share things with me. My little time with her I use to observe her interests and her moods.

Long ago I decided I didn't want to be the intrusive parent. But the parent she can rely on when something really bad happens. And one part of this decision was to approach maturely her sexual awakening. Not an easy task. There is a social conditioning I grew up in that fathers should guard their daughter's virginity until marriage, and guard fiercely which man will be worth to be her husband.

But at the same time I have always thought that's toxic.

So I knew she had a school sweetheart at 14, sex about 16 with some other kid, leading to breaking up with her then boyfriend. No. I don't know the details, just the patterns. What she told and didn't tell her mother. What her phone call habits revealed. And something else I have noticed: she owns her own decisions. She is a heart-breaker, not a broken heart.

I wouldn't say she is mature. I don't agree, I am not okay, with many of the things she does. But I will protect her whenever she needs, even when it is a fault of her own. But letting her make her own mistakes is my form of love.

And she went to college overseas. Outside of the supervision of her mother. Outside of my supervising reach. Aachen is a very nice and historical town but outside of my usual cycle.

And I can tell she is the same. The same spirit outside parental supervision. If she refused to be contained living with her mother and attending high school, now in another city, in another country, pretending her mother or I have control would be utopic.

I don't interrogate, when I know I can be lied to. I don't have spies. I just know who she is. What the different sparks in her eyes mean. I can't know details, but there is something I know. She broke with most of her local flings before leaving. She partied hard once out of town, and then she settled on one stable guy. A guy I was never presented to but who often was just out-of-frame when teleconferencing with her.

And there is one more thing I know. She didn't break with all her flings here. She kept two upon departure. A consummated one and the unconsummated one.

She eventually cut the former.
The latter...
She completed her journey during these vacations home.

Of course, this is something I don't have to tell you. You know the details better than I do. I infer. You are the one she tells things. And you are that fling. I know the spark in her eyes. I also know you. Almost eight years working for me. I know when you stopped seeing her as a child, and when you stopped talking to her as a child. And I know the prudent distance you kept with her.

I haven't said anything before. Somehow I am more worried about you than about her. She is learning but she has been owning her lessons. You are resilient too. Discretion is your trade. But my daughter is currently the only one in your circle, isn't she? There is a line between caring for a lover and falling in love, and you have no real guardrail preventing you from crossing that line.

I was worried you would cross the line of disrespect, or break her heart. But it won't happen. Not that way. I know you did break her heart because of me. For what I asked you to do. But she will forgive you. I don't think she will forgive me.
