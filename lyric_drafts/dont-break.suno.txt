**Don't Break Her Heart, Old Friend**
- alt-country blues, narrative ballad, slow blues shuffle -

[Intro – Spoken, low & weary, just acoustic guitar + soft bass]
Come here, son… we need to talk.
You’ve been spendin’ a lot of time around my house lately.

[Verse 1 – Slow bluesy shuffle]
She slips in late with that dreamy little smile,
Eyes shinin’ brighter than the dashboard lights for miles.
I know my daughter, raised her since she was small,
I can read that girl like faded writin’ on the wall.
She’s got that college boy she keeps on the side,
But everybody knows that ain’t no forever ride.
This new fire burnin’ in her veins…
The man behind it, brother… I know it’s you, son.

[Chorus – Steady backbeat, steel guitar weeping]
She’s grown now, barely twenty-two,
I ain’t no preacher, I’ve done things I regret too.
I’ve seen the way you look at her when you think I ain’t around,
She thinks her daddy’s blind, and that secret makes her proud.
Her mama and me ain’t supposed to know what’s goin’ on,
So don’t you tell her I know… just keep singin’ that same ol’ song.

[Verse 2 – Slightly faster, more tension in the delivery]
You’re half my age, son — late twenties, full of fire,
I’m forty-four and tired, watchin’ this dangerous desire.
You’re my right-hand man, the one I brought up through the years,
Now you’re stealin’ glances at the girl I held through her tears.
It cuts me deep to watch this dance I wish I hadn’t seen,
But I’m tryin’ to swallow pride and let her live her dream.
She’s my blood, you’re damn near like a son I never had,
So I’m askin’ man to man — don’t make me regret that.

[Chorus]
She’s grown now, barely twenty-two,
I ain’t no preacher, I’ve done things I regret too.
I’ve seen the way you look at her when you think I ain’t around,
She thinks her daddy’s blind, and that secret makes her proud.
Her mama and me ain’t supposed to know what’s goin’ on,
So don’t you tell her I know… just keep singin’ that same ol’ song.

[Bridge – Half-spoken, building intensity, call-and-response feel with background vocals]
[Lead:] I see the texts she hides when she thinks I’m sleepin’,
[BG:] (Don’t break her heart, boy…)
[Lead:] She wears your shirt like armor when nobody’s peepin’,
[BG:] (Don’t break her heart…)
[Lead:] You look at her the way I looked at her mama way back when,
[BG:] (Don’t break her heart, boy…)
[Lead:] If she comes home cryin’, this friendship ends right then.
I ain’t lockin’ her down, she’s got the right to choose,
She’ll fall and she’ll rise, win some and lose.
But if you treat her wrong and leave her torn apart,
That pain’s comin’ back on me… straight to the heart.

[Final Chorus – Full band, urgent and soulful]
She’s grown now, barely twenty-two,
I ain’t no preacher, but I’m warnin’ you…
I’ve seen the way you look at her when you think I ain’t around,
She thinks her daddy’s blind, but I see everything in this town.
What happened… happened… I’ll learn to live with the truth,
Just don’t you break her heart, son — that’s the only thing I ask of you.

[Outro – Slow fade, lone guitar picking]
[Spoken, low:]
You hear me? …Take care of her.

[Last ringing chord fades into silence]

alt-country blues, narrative ballad, spoken intro, low gravelly baritone, steel guitar weeping, acoustic guitar fingerpicking, soft upright bass, brushed snare shuffle, background choir vocals, call and response, plate reverb, tape saturation, gated room reverb, slow blues shuffle, mid-tempo backbeat, bittersweet tension, urgent restraint, 1970s studio sound
