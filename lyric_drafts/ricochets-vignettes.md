# Ricochets — singable vignettes

Scenes from *Ricochets* by Carlos Thompson that could each carry one song: one moment, one place, a concrete image, an emotional turn and a clear singer. Story order. Dates come from `reference/timeline.json` in the Ricochets project; "est." marks its low-confidence estimates. Quotes are from the novel text.

Status: **NEW** = no song centered on the scene; **DRAFT** = lyrics drafted (`.suno.txt`) but not yet rendered and fetched from Suno; **PARTIAL** = touched by a song but not its subject; **COVERED** = already sung.

## Vignettes

### 1. Pizza, Soda and Paperwork
- **Chapter / date:** companion *The Deal*, 2001-10-26
- **Where / who:** DEA interview room, Miami; Fabián Müller, Armando Perez, Josh Spitz
- **Scene:** Müller is arrested over a 250 kg container. "Perez left the room to get pizza, soda, and paperwork," and Spitz advises his client to negotiate. Müller ends up taking immunity in exchange for informing.
- **Song angle:** Müller weighing the deal in an empty room. Antioquian trova or corrido.
- **Existing songs:** *Pizza on the Table*, *¿Aceptás el trato?*, *Inter-Flota Caerá*

### 2. Two Bottles of Wild Turkey
- **Chapter / date:** ch13 The Glades (flashback), 2004-04-19
- **Where / who:** "A small, polished bar in Boynton Beach"; John Carlson, Miguel Morocho
- **Scene:** "Two bottles of Wild Turkey. One already empty." A drunk Carlson sketches the garage set-up: "Not targeted. That's the point." Morocho slides him a burner phone: "Whatever you decide-- dump it on Wednesday."
- **Song angle:** Carlson's slurred plan, heard in hindsight as a confession. Slow southern-gothic outlaw country.
- **Existing songs:** *Two Bottles of Wild Turkey* (rataflechera, 2026-11; outlaw country). *Morocho* (gabisson) also gives it four lines ("entre copas se pactó").

### 3. The Road to Plantation
- **Chapter / date:** ch6 Flashbacks, 2004-04-22
- **Where / who:** a bar in West Palm Beach, then Jackson Memorial, then a car to Plantation; John Carlson, Miguel Perea, Andrew Gonzalez, Marta (Sarah's sister)
- **Scene:** "Assuming everything is okay, John Carlson doesn't call Sarah back." He goes out to celebrate with his team instead. Then comes Gonzalez's call. On the drive, Marta blames him, then asks his forgiveness in tears and embraces him.
- **Song angle:** Marta's voice, from anger to forgiveness within one car ride. Acoustic Americana ballad.
- **Existing songs:** *The Road to Plantation* (rataflechera, 2026-11; Americana ballad), from `lyric_drafts/road_to_plantation.suno.txt`. *Town and Country* covers the day in broad strokes.

### 4. ¡No pasó nada!
- **Chapter / date:** ch6 Flashbacks, 2004-04-22/23 (the text doesn't fix whether this is that night or the next day)
- **Where / who:** Sandra's home, Bogotá; Sandra Roa, Carolina Müller Roa
- **Scene:** A call comes from Spitz's office. Sandra yells "¡No pasó nada!", "a fake angry face; but Sandra's eyes reveal she is crying." She sinks to the floor against the coffee table. Carolina, hurt by the scream, sits beside her instead of asking.
- **Song angle:** Carolina, about seventeen, choosing comfort over answers. Spanish-language Colombian pop ballad.
- **Existing songs:** *¡No pasó nada!* (rataflechera, 2026-11; pop ballad). *Heart to Heart* and *Lo que queda* are profiles.

### 5. Ally or Foe
- **Chapter / date:** ch6 Flashbacks, 2004-04-23
- **Where / who:** MDPD interrogation room, with no audio; Fabián Müller, Armando Perez
- **Scene:** "Are you an ally or a foe?" "You choose, Mr. Muller."
- **Song angle:** a cat-and-mouse duet. Noir.
- **Existing songs:** *The Lens Cannot Know*

### 6. Forgetting Her Face
- **Chapter / date:** ch1 The Hangover, 2005-06-21
- **Where / who:** Señora Rosario's boardinghouse, La Candelaria; John Carlson, Camilo Cáseres
- **Scene:** A notebook entry: "I am even forgetting Lilith's face and her voice."
- **Song angle:** Carlson. Rock.
- **Existing songs:** *Ricochets Hangover*, *Mémoire en miettes*, *Los rebotes*

### 7. Vous ?! — Ça *te* dérange ?
- **Chapter / date:** ch1 The Hangover, 2005-06-21
- **Where / who:** hotel bar with the Old King Cole mural, New York, then 55th Street; Fabián Müller, Sophie Durand
- **Scene:** By the second cocktail they are on "tu". When he slips back to "vous" she fakes offence ("Vous ?!"), and he corrects himself ("Ça te dérange ?"). She waves away the contract talk: "I want fun. And a famous New York pizza."
- **Song angle:** a flirtation duet that turns on the switch from vous to tu. Written as 2000s sophisti-pop with chanson elements, French lyrics (first proposed as French-English cabaret swing).
- **Existing songs:** *Vous ?! — Ça te dérange ?* (rataflechera, 2026-11). *Smoke and Steel* and *Le Rêve des Autres* are profiles; *Ricochets Hangover* mentions the pizza in passing.

### 8. Coffee and Bread
- **Chapter / date:** ch1 The Hangover, 2005-06-22
- **Where / who:** a New York street to an artisan bakery; Fabián Müller, Barys Sobal
- **Scene:** "Gunther... he was a man of his word." The walk ends with the bodyguards eating pastries.
- **Song angle:** tension that fizzles out. Noir.
- **Existing songs:** *A Narrow Way*

### 9. Not Even Mine
- **Chapter / date:** ch2 Routine Breaking, 2005-06-25 (the scene itself is undated; see timeline issue T-002)
- **Where / who:** Henry Little's backyard barbecue, Long Island; Fabián Müller, Henry Little
- **Scene:** Little, in a black Nirvana T-shirt, asks what happened that night. Müller: "Reports can't be trusted. Not even mine." They wanted Gunther dead, he says, and the lawyer too. Then Little calls him to the grill: "let's not make Mrs. Little wait."
- **Song angle:** a fixer's backyard confession in Müller's voice. Swampy blues-rock.
- **Existing songs:** *Not Even Mine* (rataflechera, 2026-11; blues-rock). *Everybody Knows Henry* and *Henry Little* are profiles.

### 10. Even Paranoids Have Enemies
- **Chapter / date:** ch2 Routine Breaking (folder, undated) and ch7 A Pause (meeting, 2005-07)
- **Where / who:** Nash-Ocampo-Wayne; a walk on Miami Beach; David Harris, Marion Gebhart
- **Scene:** The note "Even Paranoids have Enemies"; the wide-brimmed hat; "This was a mistake."
- **Song angle:** Harris. Noir.
- **Existing songs:** *Under That Wide Brim* (which also covers Keaton's card in ch8)

### 11. Lucky Bastard
- **Chapter / date:** ch3 Take-offs, 2005-06-27
- **Where / who:** Parque de la 93, Bogotá, near noon; John Carlson
- **Scene:** Going over his notes, Carlson writes "A family guy?", then "Or just a facade?" He remembers how at ease Müller and Sandra were together, more than he ever was with Sarah, and writes "Lucky bastard!" Then he looks at the clouds and grins, "realizing how he is starting to think like a local."
- **Song angle:** envy turning into an unexpected sense of belonging. Alt-country that moves from sun into rain.
- **Existing songs:** *Lucky Bastard* (rataflechera, 2026-11; alt-country), from `lyric_drafts/lucky_bastard.suno.txt`

### 12. Alright, Soldier
- **Chapter / date:** ch4 All Roads, 2005-07-01
- **Where / who:** Müller's empty house above the Bogotá Savanna; John Carlson, Lida André (by phone)
- **Scene:** Carlson stands at the window in latex gloves when Lida calls. "I'm out in a walk," he lies, and she signs off with "Alright, soldier." Afterwards he finds the Outlook folder *Nerviger Kripo*.
- **Song angle:** a burglar's lie to the one person he doesn't want to lie to. Spy-noir trip-hop.
- **Existing songs:** *Alright, Soldier* (rataflechera, 2026-11; trip-hop), from `lyric_drafts/alright_soldier.suno.txt`

### 13. Gang, bitte / Muller, un paso al costado

### 15. Can You Come Get Me?
- **Chapter / date:** ch8 High Stakes, 2005-07 (one week; window 2005-07-07/07-28)
- **Where / who:** a tienda in Normandía, then the sidewalk, Bogotá; John Carlson, Lida André, Óscar Julián
- **Scene:** "Una uva y una empanada," then a boxed half-bottle of aguardiente: "He doesn't like it. Never has. The taste should be enough to stop him. It isn't." The phone shows "John Amerikaan": "I'm not feeling well. Can you come get me?" Lida wipes his lip. The shop owner had kept his backpack: "Y también dejó este cuaderno sobre la mesa."
- **Song angle:** Lida's voice answering the call; a relapse and a rescue. Bogotá rock ballad.
- **Existing songs:** *Can You Come Get Me?* (rataflechera, 2026-11; Bogotá rock ballad), from `lyric_drafts/can_you_come_get_me.suno.txt`. The other Lida songs cover her stay in general.

### 19. Demasiado rola
- **Chapter / date:** ch12 Vertrek, est. 2005-08-22
- **Where / who:** the kitchen of Müller's house, Bogotá; Fabián Müller, Carolina, Alma Quinchía
- **Scene:** Carolina gets changua with almojábanas, and Fabián gets a calentado montañero. "Her bowl. Pale. Soft. Bogotano. His plate. Heavy. Decisive. Paisa." "Me saliste demasiado rola," he says, and she steals a piece of his chorizo. "Me tienes todo el día." That afternoon she asks in German whether he deals drugs, and he writes "Es könnten sich Mikrofone im Haus befinden" in the newspaper margin.
- **Song angle:** Fabián on their last easy morning, before the question. An Andean bambuco or pasillo that darkens at the end.
- **Existing songs:** *Demasiado rola* (rataflechera, 2026-11; bambuco / pasillo), from `lyric_drafts/demasiado_rola.suno.txt`. One stanza of *Carolina* also covers the afternoon.

### 20. After I Cross That Gate
- **Chapter / date:** ch12 Vertrek, est. 2005-08-22
- **Where / who:** a taxi to El Dorado, then international departures; John Carlson, Lida André
- **Scene:** "Something real... at least until you cross that gate." "It'll be real *after* I cross that gate." At the airport he spots the Müllers. "Please… forget about him. Just tonight."
- **Song angle:** a duet that refuses to be either a romance or a goodbye. Dream-pop ballad.
- **Existing songs:** partial. The outro of *The Healer and the Hurting* touches the farewell, and the boardinghouse goodbyes ("Vete antes de que me hagas llorar") are in *Señora Rosario's House*. ### 21. Line of Fire
- **Chapter / date:** ch13 The Glades, est. 2005-08-31
- **Where / who:** Morocho's fifth-floor office, Cali; John Carlson, Miguel Morocho
- **Scene:** A plastic bag sits on the desk: "A passport. Credit cards. A Motorola Razr." Morocho pours himself rum while Carlson sticks to Coke. "She put herself in the line of fire… The daughter… no one anticipated." Five minutes later: "Let's get some lunch."
- **Song angle:** Morocho as a velvet-voiced host delivering the worst truth. Minor-key Cali salsa or bolero.
- **Note:** the items were taken by petty muggers after the April 2004 garage shooting and later recovered by Morocho's people (the passport was seized in Panama from someone trying to use it).
- **Existing songs:** drafted in `lyric_drafts/line_of_fire.suno.txt` (not yet rendered).

## Notes on the text

- **Vuelta a Bal Harbour:** the scene is in ch20 (est. 2005-09-27/28), not ch21; the lyrics match ch20 line for line. Its `\setin` is currently `2005-09-28/2005-09-29`.
- **Two weeks vs. ten days:** in ch13 Morocho says "Two weeks ago…", but the gap from ch11 (about 08-21) to ch13 (about 08-31) is about ten days (timeline issue T-005).
- **Montoya in ch5:** Fabián says Montoya "se accidentó", but Óscar says "Juan Pablo llegó como de décimo". The two lines contradict each other, and neither matches the race: on 2005-07-03 Montoya retired on lap 46 from third place (hydraulics per Wikipedia, engine per Racing-Reference), without a crash. Alonso won. The ch5 date still holds through Wimbledon and Alonso.
- **Names that vary in the text:**
  - "Ernesto Mojica" in ch17, where the canonical name is Néstor.
  - "Rey Piros" in ch18–19, against "Rey Pilos" elsewhere.
  - "Lidewejn" in ch12, where the canonical spelling is Lidewijn.
  - "call me Noemie" in the ch1 footnote.
