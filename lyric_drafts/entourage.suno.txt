Here are the quatrains. I've aimed for an ABCB ballad rhyme scheme throughout, with physical detail in the first line, role in the second, emotional weight in the third or fourth.

---

[Gabriele Wiater /Gah-bree-ELL-eh VEE-ah-ter/]
Her eyes look purple in the light,
a sculptor, restless, brave;
Gabriele Wiater holds a door
no other woman gave.

[Aurora Swansen]
With auburn hair and measured words
she coached him back to ground;
Aurora Swansen crossed the line—
and Thomas paid the round.

[Victoria Ospina]
She trains the body, sets the pace,
dark-eyed and built for speed;
Victoria Ospina chose the work—
and gave him what he'd need.

[Erika Czajka /EH-ree-kah CHIGH-kah/]
On ice she carves, in desert heat
she draws the greening plan;
Erika Czajka keeps it clean—
the rarest thing she can.

[Natalia Kyou Park]
Green eyes, green streaks, a Californian cool,
she drafts the master plan;
Natalia Kyou Park designs the ground—
he found her in Milan.

[Diana Naji /DEE-ah-nah NAH-zhee/]
Terracotta on dark skin,
she played the longer game;
it was Diana Naji's move—
he couldn't say the same.

[Sylvia Lai /SIL-vya Lah-ee/]
A teal pixie cut, hazel-eyed,
she grew from ward to more;
Sylvia Lai became the glass
he hadn't seen before.

[Susana Bianchi]
She flew the Jaguar through the rain,
dark-haired and sure of hand;
Susana Bianchi shared one night—
and kept the sky as planned.

[Camila Alexander — Xumi /SHOO-mee/]
Dark eyes, a London-Maranhão blend,
she moves his capital through;
Camila Alexander — Xumi —
the friendship always held true.

[María del Pilar Venegas]
Gray eyes and freckles, steady build,
she reads the room and stands;
María del Pilar Venegas broke
one night in other hands.

[Mariain Schumer]
Blue-eyed, she drifted through a door
that Gabi held ajar;
Mariain Schumer stayed because
the orbit felt like hers.

[Fiona Levit]
Black wavy hair, a butterfly,
hazel eyes behind the glass;
Fiona Levit fixed a ticket—
and made the channel last.
