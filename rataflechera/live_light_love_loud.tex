\songtitle{Live Light, Love Loud}

\tag*{2026}\tag{stadium-rock}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene and style description by Carlos Thompson -- a singalong chant for the little joys. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 23 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Arena-sized stomping chant with a buoyant, joyful pulse; lead vocal carries the rhythmic labor in punchy call-and-response phrases, answered by a large mixed crowd chorus. Heavy unison vocal claps and shouted refrains over driving bass and drums, ringing cowbells, and bright brass stabs that punctuate rather than dominate. Verses stay lean and percussive with the lead out front; choruses widen into massed voices, with a brief brass lift before the final chant. Raw, human crowd energy, clear upfront vocals, roomy stadium ambience, strong singalong hook.
\end{style}
