\songtitle{My Kind of Friend}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alt-rock}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{xumi}\tag{thomas-garcia}\label{sec:my-kind-of-friend}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, in the voice of Xumi, based on the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} lore -- a road trip through Liège, Maastricht, Aachen, Cologne and Bonn, and a friend to share a bed with expecting no more. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 12 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
British alt-rock with a female lead vocal, clear midrange phrasing and a chorus-lifted bridge; driving 132 BPM road-trip pulse, melodic guitar hooks over a driving bassline, punchy live drums with gated snare, distant reverbed guitar and a dance instrumental break; sidechain pumping, tape saturation, plate reverb and wide stereo guitars.
\end{style}

You talked about history with such passion\\
like your nerdish themes drafted.

A part of me said caution, beware.\\

And I’m glad I came with you there.
