\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene by Carlos Thompson from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- who sleeps where at the Melgar villa over New Year, and why Sylvia ends up in the spare bed in Lucía's room. Compare \emph{Sin complicaciones}. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 18 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
2020s synthpop, plain corporate delivery, female vocals, midtempo straight pulse, clipped analog bass, cool glassy synth chords, restrained electronic percussion, clean office-bright mix, emotionally neutral close female lead, concise verse-chorus structure with subtle chorus lift and a dry, matter-of-fact finish.
\end{style}
