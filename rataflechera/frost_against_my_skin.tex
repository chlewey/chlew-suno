\begin{style}
Dream-pop, 90 BPM with a slow, measured pulse; soft pad drones, distant reverb guitar, analog synth arpeggios, brushed snare and a subtle sub-bass pulse; tape saturation, plate reverb, wide stereo chorus and restrained sidechain pumping; the user's own recorded female voice, faintly hummed in the intro, layered harmonies building through the choruses, with cinematic restraint.
\end{style}
