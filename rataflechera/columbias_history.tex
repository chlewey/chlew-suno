\songtitle{Columbia’s History}

\tag*{2026}\tag{country-folk}\tag{original-source}\tag{kingdom-of-columbia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Kingdom of Columbia} alternate history by Carlos Thompson (2021). Lyrics by Suno based on a paragraph of a historical summary by Carlos Thompson -- the state church, religious freedom, and Columbia's wars and alliances, as a children's hymn. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Country folk children’s educational hymn, gentle two-step sway with a steady acoustic-guitar strum, warm upright bass, brushed snare, fiddle and simple piano. Clear, friendly group vocals with a lead voice and children’s chorus answering key phrases. Storytelling verses move chronologically through the history; a memorable repeated chorus sums up tolerance and peace; brief fiddle interludes; finish with a calm communal refrain. Natural, uncluttered acoustic production, easy melody and diction.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
By Columbia’s rivers, by the fields and hills,\\
A church stood at the center of the country’s will.\\
From eighteen thirty-seven through the years ahead,\\
The Baptist Church was state church, history tells us plain.

\songpart{Chorus}
Learn the days and learn the way,\\
How a nation changes with the years.\\
Peace and faith, and room to grow,\\
Columbia’s story we can know.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Eighteen eighty brought a law that opened up a door,\\
Non-Protestants could worship, though their rights were fewer.\\
Public service stayed for Protestants alone,\\
And high officials recognized the church upon the throne.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Then nineteen sixty-four brought freedom broad and clear,\\
The Religious Freedom Act removed each limit there.\\
Now every faith could practice without that old restraint,\\
A turning point in Columbia’s story we can name.

\songpart{Chorus}
Learn the days and learn the way,\\
How a nation changes with the years.\\
Peace and faith, and room to grow,\\
Columbia’s story we can know.

\songpart{Verse 4}
Since independence, Columbia has kept from distant wars,\\
Though border troubles, Indian wars, and insurgencies left scars.\\
In thirty-six she joined the Allies in the Great War’s fight,\\
Against the Austro-Russo axis, standing for the right.

\songpart{Verse 5}
Her expeditionary force crossed over to Italy,\\
And helped in its liberation—a part of history.\\
She joined more alliances as the years rolled on,\\
And NADI-IDAN membership carries that work on.

\songpart{Chorus}
Learn the days and learn the way,\\
How a nation changes with the years.\\
Peace and faith, and room to grow,\\
Columbia’s story we can know.

\songpart{Outro}
Remember every turning year,\\
Ask, learn, and listen well.\\
Columbia’s story, told with care,\\
Has many chapters still to tell.
\end{lyrics}
