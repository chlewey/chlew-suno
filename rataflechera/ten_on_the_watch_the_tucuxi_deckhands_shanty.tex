\songtitle{Ten on the Watch (The Tucuxi Deckhand’s Shanty)}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sea-shanty}\tag{ai-based}\tag{panamax}\label{sec:ten-on-the-watch}
\begin{notes}
From \emph{Panamax}, a 2026 document by Claude based on a 2013 description of a yacht by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions -- a new deckhand on the quiet watch and the drills for the day that never comes. See also \emph{The Tucuxi} (page \pageref{sec:the-tucuxi}) and \emph{Cast Off from Puerto Colombia} (page \pageref{sec:cast-off-from-puerto-colombia}). Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 25 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sea shanty with Afro-Caribbean beats, rolling hand percussion and fiddle carrying a brisk nautical melody; low male bass shanty lead with close-miked gang vocals; steady swaying pulse, easing into a slow final verse before the final chorus surges back to full tempo.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I signed aboard the Tucuxi beneath Cartagena’s wall,\\
A deckhand, lowest on the list, the newest of them all,\\
Two hundred eighty-eight meters and a crew of twenty-five,\\
And on the open ocean, boys, she’s quiet as a hive!

\songpart{Chorus}
So it’s ten on the watch and the sea is wide,\\
Nothing to do but drill and abide!\\
Muster, mark and man the boat,\\
For the day that never comes will keep us afloat!

\songpart{Verse 2}
The sun hangs on her panels and the diesel sleeps below,\\
The biogas hums, the garden grows, the desalter’s running slow,\\
I walk my round on the weather deck, I count the hatches twice,\\
I log the swell, I watch the sky, and the sea says nothing twice.

\songpart{Chorus}
So it’s ten on the watch and the sea is wide,\\
Nothing to do but drill and abide!\\
Muster, mark and man the boat,\\
For the day that never comes will keep us afloat!

\songpart{Verse 3}
Six hours on and six hours off, my cabin’s up on eight,\\
Right above the owners’ suite, where the quiet keeps the gate,\\
I wash my cup, I mend my line, I read a tattered book,\\
And the bridge is one deck up from me, so I give a lookout’s look!

\songpart{Chorus}
So it’s ten on the watch and the sea is wide,\\
Nothing to do but drill and abide!\\
Muster, mark and man the boat,\\
For the day that never comes will keep us afloat!

\songpart{Verse 4}
But Tuesday brings the muster bell, and I know my lifeboat well,\\
Twenty boats of thirty souls, and six hundred bound to tell,\\
We count the cooks, we count the guests, we count the VIPs,\\
We drill it till we do it, boys, in our sleep and on our knees!

\songpart{Chorus}
So it’s ten on the watch and the sea is wide,\\
Nothing to do but drill and abide!\\
Muster, mark and man the boat,\\
For the day that never comes will keep us afloat!

\songpart{Verse 5}
Then it’s fire in the galley, and it’s smoke below the line,\\
Man overboard on the starboard side, and the minisub’s in time,\\
The helicopter’s winching, boys, the crane swings out the boat,\\
And a deckhand’s just a deckhand till the day he keeps her afloat!

\songpart{Chorus}
So it’s ten on the watch and the sea is wide,\\
Nothing to do but drill and abide!\\
Muster, mark and man the boat,\\
For the day that never comes will keep us afloat!

\songpart{Final verse (slow)}
So here’s to the watch of the quiet sea, and the ten who keep her true,\\
We’ll drill for the storm that may not come, and the one that might break through,\\
For the day she needs us, boys, we’ll be ready, every one,\\
Ten on the watch, and the Tucuxi rolls on!

\songpart{Final chorus (slow, then full)}
So it’s ten on the watch and the sea is wide,\\
Nothing to do but drill and abide!\\
Muster, mark and man the boat,\\
For the day that never comes will keep us afloat!
\end{lyrics}
