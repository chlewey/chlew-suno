\songtitle{Unopened Door}

\tag*{2026}\tag{dream-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{liis-sepp}\tag*{english-lyrics}

\begin{notes}
Based on the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} fictional universe by Carlos Thompson.\\
Character: Liis Sepp.\\
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Dream pop with slow-swung pulse and airy harmonic motion; verse rides a sparse kick, soft hand percussion, and glassy keys, pre-chorus opens with filtered pads and a rising shimmer, chorus blooms with wide synth chords and a rounded bass line. %
Breathy stacked vocals, short delay throws on key phrases, reversed swells into transitions, and tiny chime accents between lines. %
Polished, intimate, and cinematic with a deep velvet sheen., warm, refined, simple, smooth, deep, bright, soft
\end{style}

She watched from the edge, quiet and curious.
