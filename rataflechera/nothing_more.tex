\begin{notes}
\emph{My Kind of Friend} (page \pageref{sec:my-kind-of-friend}) with its meter and rhyme tightened by Grok. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 12 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Bedroom singer-songwriter ballad, tender conversational lead in a close-miked natural delivery, subtle room ambience and warm imperfect home-recorded texture with a clear natural mix, restrained unhurried pulse, gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed percussion entering gradually, occasional muted upright piano.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
The heartland of western Europe, you called it,\\
where the Frankish Empire once was crafted.\\
You spoke of history with such pure passion,\\
like those nerdish themes you’d long drafted.

Our schedules matched—I said, why not?\\
One part of me whispered, caution, beware.\\
Another said don’t think, just go ahead.\\
And I’m so glad I went with you there.

\annotation{PreChorus}\\
Liège, Maastricht, Aachen, Cologne, and Bonn—\\
not routes I ever would have chosen on my own.

\songpart{Chorus}
And I knew right there you were my kind of friend:\\
the one who speaks with fire of things I never knew,\\
who listens with real interest to my nerdish blend,\\
with whom I share a bed and ask for nothing more.
