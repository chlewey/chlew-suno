\songtitle{Weird}

\tag*{2026}\tag{chamber-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{victoria-ospina}\tag{aurora-swansen}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{erika-czajka}\label{sec:weird}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok (interacting on structure) based on a scene written by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe -- dinner after the beach, Vicky's one-word answer for a relationship that has just been given a name, and Thomas leaving the room with Aurora a little after ten. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 22 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Chamber-pop, intimate vocal in a close, natural delivery; unhurried brushed pulse; soft tape saturation; soft piano, muted strings, and brushed drums.
\end{style}
