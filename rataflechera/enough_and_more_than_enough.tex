\songtitle{Enough, and More Than Enough}

\tag*{2026}\tag{synth-pop}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude, as a response to \emph{One Real Hand} (page \pageref{sec:one-real-hand}), itself a response to \emph{A Someone or a Someone} (page \pageref{sec:a-someone-or-a-someone}), -- accepting the part that is offered, one real hand and open doors. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 11 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Synth-pop ballad, tape saturation and plate reverb with sidechain-pumped dynamics; arpeggiated synth chords, detuned pad beds, distant reverbed guitar, gated snare, sub-bass support, and offbeat hi-hats; intimate male baritone lead; 109 BPM four-on-the-floor pulse, bittersweet restraint and a gradual lift.
\end{style}
