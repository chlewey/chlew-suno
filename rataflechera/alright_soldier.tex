\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson -- chapter 4 (\emph{All Roads}): Carlson's Friday break-in at Müller's empty house above the Bogotá Savanna, Lida André's call (``I'm out in a walk''; ``Alright, soldier''), and the Outlook folder \emph{Nerviger Kripo} found at dusk. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 16 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Spy-noir trip-hop; dusty vinyl crackle, radio static and tower chatter; deep sub bass, muted trumpet lead, tremolo guitar, cold Rhodes chords; 78 BPM slow broken beat in D minor; low hushed male baritone crooning in legato, close-mic and nearly whispered, hooks doubled; warm, light female phone-filtered voice with a soft Dutch accent.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
\annotation{vinyl crackle, a bus pulling away, boots on a gravel path}

\songpart{Verse 1}
\annotation{Carlson}\\
Portal del Norte, last stop on the red line,\\
an intercity bus to just before the toll.\\
Then up the trekking path with the city at my shoulder,\\
notebook full of cipher, ten o'clock on the dial.\\
Friday, the housekeeper's ride is on schedule,\\
Mrs. Alma at the door and then the door is closed.\\
A jammer in my pocket, a pick for the service lock,\\
and he never changed the code. Nobody ever does.

\songpart{Verse 3}

Ninety minutes.\\
A backdoor in the machine,\\
every cushion where I found it,\\
every surface wiped clean.\\
Arm the alarm, step out,\\
and the dark hill takes me down.
