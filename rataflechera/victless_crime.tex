\songtitle{Victless Crime}

\tag*{2026}\tag{singer-songwriter}\tag{original-lyrics}\label{sec:victless-crime}
\begin{notes}
Original lyrics by Carlos Thompson. Adapted by Claude as \emph{Thoughts Are Not Crimes} (page \pageref{sec:thoughts-are-not-crimes}) and by Grok as \emph{Something to Chew On} (page \pageref{sec:something-to-chew-on}). Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Singer-songwriter; unhurried, speech-led pulse; half-spoken vocal in the user's own recorded voice, close and natural; roomy analog tape ambience with soft saturation; upright piano carrying spare chords and pauses.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\annotation{Stanza 1}\\
Feeling unapologetic\\
and yet wrong in so many ways,\\
knowing that your desires\\
ain't wrong 'cause thought crimes\\
are not crimes at all.

\annotation{Stanza 2}\\
A religious upbringing\\
that constantly taught sex was a sin,\\
desires to be ashamed of,\\
self-joy regarded a wrongful thing—\\
victimless crime\\
claiming a victim in the shame.

\annotation{Stanza 3}\\
Progressive-leaning,\\
telling every person deserves respect,\\
consent taught and agreed,\\
and keep proving you're not a creep.\\
Objectifying people:\\
an ideological sin.

\annotation{Stanza 4}\\
Unmet desires are nothing\\
compared to your partner's headache,\\
and you know it, can't object.\\
They're nothing compared to famine—\\
your dirty little cravings\\
just something to chew on.
\end{lyrics}
