\songtitle{Petrovna}

\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
\annotation{clean guitar motif, single line, repeated — 8 bars}

\songpart{Verse 1}
\annotation{sparse, close vocal, light percussion}\\
Born between two cities, two tongues, two kinds of cold\\
father's blood from somewhere east, mother's hands from further south\\
learned to carry all of it before I learned to speak\\
learned to hold the weight of names inside my mouth

Tallinn kept me, others left\\
I built a home from what remained\\
two ways of knowing who I am\\
and neither one of them explained

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
\annotation{guitar firms up, percussion enters properly}\\
I read the ones who come here lost\\
I know the shape before they speak\\
I built my walls from what I found\\
and what I found was all I need\\
all I need

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{full band, melodic, driven but not loud}\\
I don't need just one\\
I keep several close\\
not from fear of empty rooms\\
but that's how I'm made\\
warmth without an anchor\\
closeness without cost\\
I know what I am\\
and I haven't lost\\
I haven't lost

\songpart{Verse 2}
\annotation{sparse again, vocal precise}\\
I chose the ones who come undone in cities not their own\\
I read the file, I know the name before I cross the room\\
the jacket worn to ruin, the neckline torn just so\\
not hiding — saying look, and look again

I'll show you what I want you to know\\
The shields aren't wounds\\
they're architecture\\
every wall I built, I built with open eyes\\
a room for me\\
and rooms for others\\
doors that open only when I decide

\songpart{Pre-Chorus 2}
\annotation{builds as before}\\
I read the ones who come here lost\\
I know the shape before they speak\\
I built my walls from what I found —\\
and what I found was all I need\\
all I need

\songpart{Chorus}
I don't need just one\\
I keep several close\\
not from fear of empty rooms\\
but that's how I'm made\\

closeness without cost\\
I know what I am\\
and I haven't lost\\
I haven't lost

\annotation{guitar drops to single line; vocal near-spoken, unhurried; slight reverb — the only atmospheric effect in the song}\\
but sometimes someone looks\\
not through me\\
at me\\
and I'm not ready for that\\
not the way they see the file\\
the way they see the wall —\\
the way they see the thing\\
behind the wall\\

nothing more

\annotation{single guitar line, the intro motif returning; no resolution chord; fades on motion}
