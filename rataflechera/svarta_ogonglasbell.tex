\begin{notes}
A distinctive arrangement that supports the song's storytelling and emotional arc. %
The lyrics begin with “Och drömarna går till slut”, giving a quick sense of the song’s tone.
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Alt-pop ballad with slow swaying pulse and sparse hand percussion; verse stays intimate with muted bass and plucked clean guitar, pre-chorus opens into layered pads and soft tom lifts, chorus lands on a wide hook with stacked harmonies and delayed vocal tails. %
Breathy close-mic lead, doubled on key lines, with subtle gang-vocal echoes on the anchor. %
Hearth crackle, reversed swells, and a glassy bell turn into the drops. %
Dark, polished, and intimate., deep, moody, faint, raw, warm
\end{style}
