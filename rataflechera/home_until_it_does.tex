\songtitle{Home Until It Does}

\tag*{2026}\tag{singer-songwriter}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a thematic prompt by Carlos Thompson -- a bittersweet comfort song about waiting out a rainy day with two cats, a tabby and a calico. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page, 14 September 2026.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Intimate bedroom singer-songwriter ballad, gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed percussion entering gradually, occasional muted upright piano, close-miked tender lead vocal, conversational verses opening into a quietly aching chorus, subtle room ambience, warm imperfect home-recorded texture, restrained dynamics, bittersweet but comforting, clear natural mix
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Rain writes crooked lines on the asphalt below\\
Silver at the curb where the tired cars go\\
I've left the blinds half-open, let the gray come through\\
But every drop keeps pointing me back to you

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The kettle clicks, the old floor bends\\
Two small hearts keep me company again

\songpart{Chorus}
I'm waiting for a sunny day\\
For yellow spilling through the glass\\
While my tabby guards the windowsill\\
And my calico curls against my arm\\
They don't ask the clouds to go\\
They just stay close and let time pass\\
I'm waiting for a sunny day\\
But I'm not alone while the rain comes down

\songpart{Verse 2}
My tabby bats a paper receipt across the floor\\
Then settles like a question by the bedroom door\\
My calico is purring in a patch of worn-out blue\\
As if she knows the light will find its way here too

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
The room is small, the afternoon is long\\
Their breathing makes a quiet song

\songpart{Chorus}
I'm waiting for a sunny day\\
For warm dust rising from the lane\\
While my tabby watches every moving shape\\
And my calico sleeps through the rain\\
They don't promise skies will clear\\
They only make this waiting soft\\
I'm waiting for a sunny day\\
With two little reasons not to feel lost

\songpart{Bridge}
Maybe hope is not a window wide\\
Maybe it's a striped tail by my side\\
Maybe brightness starts this way\\
A steady purr against the gray

\songpart{Final Chorus}
I'm waiting for a sunny day\\
For gold across the asphalt cracks\\
While my tabby keeps the morning watch\\
And my calico brings the quiet back\\
When the clouds have had their say\\
And the first dry sparrow calls\\
I'll still remember rainy rooms\\
And the company that held me through it all

\songpart{Outro}
Rain on the asphalt, soft and slow\\
Two warm bodies, letting me know\\
The sun can take its time to come\\
I've got home until it does
