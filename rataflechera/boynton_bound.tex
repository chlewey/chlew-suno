\begin{style}
Country rap with driving acoustic strums, snap-clap backbeat, and a fast two-step feel; verse rides tight storytelling with fiddle fills, pre-chorus tightens on muted bass and handclaps, chorus opens with stacked harmonies and a chantable hook, rap bridge drops to half-time with dry kick and talk-sung cadence, final chorus adds gang vocals and a steel-guitar lift. %
Lead vocal is close-mic and gritty, with doubled hook lines, reply shouts, and a few delay throws on key words. %
Ear candy includes banjo turnarounds, reverse cymbal swells, and a radio-style stop before the last lift. %
Mix is bright, punchy, and road-dust clear., rap, emotional, country
\end{style}
