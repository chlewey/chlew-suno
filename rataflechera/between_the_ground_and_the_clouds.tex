\songtitle{Between the Ground and the Clouds}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{punk-rock}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{pilar-venegas}\tag{lucia-perez}\label{sec:between-the-ground-and-the-clouds}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the fifth section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- the flight from Teterboro to Bogotá with Claire and Gabi, Pilar's spare jacket on the tarmac, lunch with his parents and Lucía, and Claire's question on the way back that he couldn't answer. Follows \emph{Going with the Tenderness} (page \pageref{sec:going-with-the-tenderness}); \emph{Make a Room Your Own} (page \pageref{sec:make-a-room-your-own}) continues it. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Narrative soft rock with brisk punk-leaning verses and a hip-hop bridge. Driving live kit and overdriven guitar stabs push the verses; open ringing guitars and broad melodic bass widen the chorus; bridge drops to a tight half-time kick, clipped bass, and conversational rhythmic male vocal, then builds back into full-band soft rock. Intimate, clear English lead vocal, restrained and reflective rather than theatrical; polished organic mix, strong dynamic contrast, memorable repeated chorus.
\end{style}
