\songtitle{Efficient and Charming}

\begin{notes}
Written by Carlos Thompson based on characters and events from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
British contemporary folk-pop, singer-songwriter, male baritone vocals, 92 BPM, acoustic guitar fingerpicking, piano arpeggios, cello countermelody, brushed snare kit, muted bass guitar, room mic intimacy, tape saturation, plate reverb, 1970s analog warmth, bittersweet longing, narrative build, chorus lift

\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I liked you that first day I interviewed you for my cabin crew,\\
and I don't mean physical attraction, although that happened too.\\
I liked your piercing presence and your command of the room,\\
how you fit all what I from your résumé could assume.\\
I wasn't looking for someone who delivered snacks onboard,\\
but who knew operations and details not to be ignored.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And you fit that profile,\\
efficient at paperwork,\\
who could command the aisle\\
from Rotterdam to New York.\\
I knew that first day\\
you were the right gear\\
to keep my bay and way,\\
to draw my life clear.

\songpart{Chorus}
Efficient and charming, Claire,\\
green eyes, freckles, and ginger hair,\\
commanding the galley,\\
fixing my plans,\\
wrecking my lifeplan\\
with just a glance.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Falling in love was very easy, the days moved so fast,\\
I didn't notice at first, it's a pattern from my past.\\
I'm attracted to beauty, to wit, to intelligence, to charm,\\
all that and efficiency — you've got it all, you could disarm.\\
But there was something else, something I couldn't quite define,\\
partly care, but no, that's your job — something else did align.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Chemistry, some say,\\
butterflies in your guts.\\
I didn't know that day\\
a sharp knife that cuts,\\
and it was odd —\\
I'm not made for commit,\\
at what I'm flawed:\\
being singular at it.

\songpart{Chorus}
Efficient and charming, Claire,\\
green eyes, freckles, and ginger hair,\\
commanding the galley,\\
fixing my plans,\\
wrecking my lifeplan\\
with just a glance.

\songpart{Bridge}
And a storm got you grounded,\\
too much time just being you,\\
with no professional assignment,\\
and damn! I liked that too.\\
So wrong, and yet felt so right,\\
you said we'd figure out,\\
making love through that night,\\
and since everything was doubt —\\
power imbalance, my main concern,\\
my lack of singularity too.\\
I can't lose you, I can't burn\\
what was working with me and you,\\
and an add-on clause, you see,\\
a clumsy attempt at naming it,\\
to protect you and to keep me\\
from my guilt and lack of commit.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Efficient and charming, Claire,\\
green eyes, freckles, and ginger hair,\\
my trusted aide commanding the galley,\\
fixing my plans, keeping my tally,\\
my lover, wrecking my lifeplan,\\
my loved one, with just a glance.
\end{lyrics}
