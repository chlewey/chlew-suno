\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude based on \emph{Ricochets} by Carlos Thompson -- chapter 12 (\emph{Vertrek}): a duet of John Carlson and Lida André in the taxi to El Dorado and at international departures, ``Something real... at least until you cross that gate'' against ``It'll be real after I cross that gate'', and the Müllers spotted at the airport. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 24 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Dream-pop ballad, 68 BPM slow sway; female alto with a light Flemish accent, breathy and close, rising into a powerful full-voice belt with layered octave doubles; male low baritone, tired and half-spoken in the verses. Their voices overlap without resolving in the final chorus. Shimmering reverb guitars and slow-washed synth pads sit against wipers, city rain, and a distant airport chime in the bridge; soft brushed drums and round sub bass carry the tense pulse as E major drifts to C\# minor.
\end{style}
