\songtitle{Steady Hands}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sophisti-pop}\tag{ricochets}\tag{felicia}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Gemini Notebook summary of character Felicia from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sophisti-pop with polished electronic texture: midtempo four-on-the-floor groove, crisp programmed drums, warm electric piano, muted funk guitar, tasteful fretless bass, glassy analog synth accents, and close-miked expressive female lead vocals, Keep verses restrained and conversational, lift into a richly harmonized chorus, then use two dramatic dubstep-inspired drops with sub-bass wobble, syncopated stutters, chopped vocal fragments, and sharp low-end impact, Elegant, cinematic, radio-ready mix with controlled dynamics, wide choruses, dry intimate verses, and a clean resolved outro
\end{style}
