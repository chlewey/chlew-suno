\songtitle{A New Map Turns}

\tag*{2026}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{original-source}\tag{kingdom-of-columbia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Kingdom of Columbia} alternate history by Carlos Thompson (2021). Lyrics by Suno based on a paragraph of a historical summary by Carlos Thompson -- colonial representation, the wars of the empires, and Columbia's independence. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Anthemic hip hop with ceremonial fanfare brass, forceful boom-bap drums, deep sub-bass, and layered communal choir vocals. Mid-tempo marching groove with crisp snare and handclap accents; verses deliver the historical narrative in clear, rhythmic rap, while broad choral refrains make the political turning points feel monumental. Brass announces each section, pulling back to sparse percussion beneath key dates, then building into a full-ensemble final chorus. Bold, resolute, cinematic production with intelligible lead vocals and wide, tightly arranged choir.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
Hear the bells across the water,\\
Hear the meeting-house doors swing wide.\\
A new bargain takes its place:\\
A voice on either side.

\songpart{Verse 1}
Seventeen seventy—crisis at the gate,\\
The Crown and the colonies renegotiate.\\
British subjects, counted in the common right,\\
Seats for their voices, no more kept from sight.\\
Williamsburg, eighteen eighty-one, Columbia takes its name,\\
Boston follows, eighty-three—New England joins the frame.\\
Two continental parliaments, each province sends its own,\\
And seats in London’s Commons link the ocean to the throne.

\songpart{Chorus}
Two chambers rise, two banners fly,\\
Three votes carried across the tide.\\
Home rule held, the old ties stayed,\\
Then Columbia chose another way.\\
Raise the standard, mark the year,\\
The people’s claim is ringing clear.\\
From Williamsburg to Boston town,\\
A new map turns, a new voice sounds.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Seventeen seventy-nine, the rival fleets collide,\\
Ninety-three, then eighteen-five—both empires test the tide.\\
Eighteen-oh-seven, sixteen, the contest circles wide,\\
For colonies and sea lanes, with the world held in the sides.\\
No war matched fifty-six in scale or in its flame,\\
Yet each new season shifted a border and a name.\\
In eighteen-oh-two, Barbary’s coast saw Britain, France unite,\\
A strange collision shared before they turned again to fight.\\
Spain stood with France in ninety-three and oh-seven’s campaign,\\
Florida returned in ninety-five; Louisiana changed hands again.\\
By eighteen-ten, its rule was lost—old settlements undone,\\
The map kept moving westward underneath a distant sun.

\songpart{Chorus}
Two chambers rise, two banners fly,\\
Three votes carried across the tide.\\
Home rule held, the old ties stayed,\\
Then Columbia chose another way.\\
Raise the standard, mark the year,\\
The people’s claim is ringing clear.\\
From Williamsburg to Boston town,\\

\songpart{Verse 3}
Eighteen thirty-two: Columbia’s chamber makes its claim,\\
Independence declared—the break now has a name.\\
France and Spain give aid; in thirty-four they join the fight,\\
The war redraws the boundary through the long Atlantic night.\\
Thirty-seven: Britain recognizes Columbia standing free,\\
Louisiana’s territory divided by decree.\\
New Spain’s borders marked against the British, Columbian line,\\
While New England and Canada keep faith with Crown and sign.

\songpart{Bridge}
One parliament chose a different road,\\
One kept the ancient colors flown.\\
Across the border, lines were drawn;\\
Across the sea, the votes went on.\\
History is not a single voice—\\
It turns at every people’s choice.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Two chambers rose, two banners flew,\\
Three votes crossed the ocean blue.\\
Home rule held, the old ties swayed,\\
Then Columbia chose another way.\\
Raise the standard, mark the year,\\
The people’s claim is ringing clear.\\
From Williamsburg to Boston town,\\
A new map turned, a new voice found.

\songpart{Outro}
Thirty-two: the claim.\\
Thirty-four: the war.\\
Thirty-seven: recognized.\\
And the map was changed once more.
\end{lyrics}
