\songtitle{The Tucuxi (Heave Away, Grey Dolphin)}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sea-shanty}\tag{ai-based}\tag{panamax}\label{sec:the-tucuxi}
\begin{notes}
From \emph{Panamax}, a 2026 document by Claude based on a 2013 description of a yacht by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions -- the ship herself, from her cargo deck to the owners' suite. \emph{Ten on the Watch} (page \pageref{sec:ten-on-the-watch}) and \emph{Cast Off from Puerto Colombia} (page \pageref{sec:cast-off-from-puerto-colombia}) follow her crew. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 25 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sea shanty, gravelly deep, low female lead with a firm chant, wooden percussion resonance, fast-paced pounding pulse, bodhran driving the verses into a broad, louder final chorus.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Come gather round, you sailors, and hear the tale I tell,\\
Of a ship the size of Panamax, and built to serve us well,\\
Two hundred eighty-eight meters long, thirty-two abeam,\\
She’s the grey dolphin of the ocean, she’s the Tucuxi of the stream!

\songpart{Chorus}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!

\songpart{Verse 2}
At her bow she swallows motorcars, thirty in a row,\\
Two double-trucks roll up her ramp and rumble down below,\\
Sixty forty-footers, stacked up three high,\\
And her own bridge crane to lift them to the sky!

\songpart{Chorus}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!

\songpart{Verse 3}
There’s a minisub in her belly and two small boats beside,\\
Two helicopters in the hangar and a pad for them to ride,\\
Twenty lifeboats on her flanks, thirty souls apiece,\\
Six hundred bound for shore in peace!

\songpart{Chorus}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!

\songpart{Verse 4}
Four hundred fifty passengers in two hundred cabins fine,\\
A hundred twenty crew aboard, but twenty work the line,\\
Ten men keep the watch, me boys, the rest serve, one and all,\\
Stewards, jesters, even lawyers, hear them call!

\songpart{Chorus}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!

\songpart{Verse 5}
Aft the owners keep their suite with a view three-sixty round,\\
Bridge above and five suites under, where the VIPs are found,\\
Casino, spa, and theatre, and a pool upon the deck,\\
And a dance hall for the sailors on the swell!

\songpart{Chorus}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!

\songpart{Verse 6}
She grows her greens in water, she hauls her fish by line,\\
She drinks the sea made sweet again, and sips the sun to shine,\\
Thirty days of larder for six hundred hungry souls,\\
And the compost and the biogas feed her fires!

\songpart{Chorus}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!

\songpart{Verse 7}
The children build their robots and learn the ship’s own ways,\\
Eighty thousand hours of film to fill the sailing days,\\
And if you’re needed home, me lads, the chopper’s on the wing,\\
To Bogotá in forty-eight, hear the Tucuxi sing!

\songpart{Final chorus (slow, then louder)}
So heave away, Tucuxi! Roll and run!\\
Through the locks and out to the open sun!\\
With the diesel low and the sunlight high,\\
She’s a dolphin grey beneath the sky!
\end{lyrics}
