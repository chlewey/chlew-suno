\songtitle{Just the Road}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{country}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{diana-naji}\label{sec:just-the-road}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the third section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Texas: Dallas with Amanda, Bob's ranch near Wichita Falls, Austin alone, and a sticker sent to Diana. Follows \emph{Where Your Yes Begins} (page \pageref{sec:where-your-yes-begins}); \emph{Going with the Tenderness} (page \pageref{sec:going-with-the-tenderness}) continues it. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock with country delivery, mid-tempo steady 4/4 groove, warm acoustic guitar strums with restrained electric guitar fills, rounded bass and natural drums, intimate conversational male lead vocal with subtle country phrasing, clear narrative verses that build into a memorable open-hearted chorus, understated organ support, polished but organic studio mix, reflective and road-worn mood.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}

Claire caught the next flight back to London\\
Fernando had cousins down in Houston town\\
So I took the wheel of a rented RAV4\\
GPS blinking, trying to find my way around\\
Dallas came up mile by mile\\
A new town, a long way from home

\songpart{Verse 2}
Amanda knew the streets and the places\\
David Brown’s assistant, kind enough to show\\
