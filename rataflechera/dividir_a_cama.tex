\begin{notes}
Adapted by Grok into Brazilian Portuguese and bossa nova from \emph{My Kind of Friend} (page \pageref{sec:my-kind-of-friend}). Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 12 October 2026).

\begin{style}
Bossa nova with fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed percussion entering gradually, muted upright piano and layered guitar-percussion textures building into a full, dense arrangement; close-miked tender lead vocal with conversational verses opening into a quietly aching chorus; laid-back, gentle sway; subtle room ambience, warm imperfect home-recorded texture, restrained dynamics, clear natural mix.
\end{style}
