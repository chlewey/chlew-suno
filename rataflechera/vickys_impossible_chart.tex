\songtitle{Vicky's Impossible Chart}

\begin{notes}
Co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude, based on characters from \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe.
\end{notes}

\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I walked into your open house, looking to place my girl —\\
you warmed to her spirit, and to my Bogotá world.\\
I don't let it pass me, when someone easy on the eye\\
leans in close to hear what I have to say, and why.

She never did enroll, but the three of us kept talking,\\
and somewhere in that hallway, something else was starting.

\songpart{Chorus}
Vicky,\\
Australian Vicky with a Colombian line,\\
that easy smile, that fearless shine,\\
Vicky, Victoria Ospina,\\
gym trainer with a physical therapist's heart,\\
playful — impossible to chart.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I had a trip to Australia, asked if you'd come along,\\
London to Sydney on a private bird, mid-haul and long.\\
You saw a free ride home to go and see your folks,\\
didn't expect the stopovers — Dubai, Medan, Darwin's coast.

It was fun, you fought the friction, then you let it go —\\
camaraderie flourished somewhere down below.

\songpart{Chorus}

\songpart{Verse 3}
Sydney in December heat, I met your mom and dad,\\
tracing old connections to a life we might have had.\\
Five months of low-key flirting — we agreed it earned a test,\\
then it was back to London for the holidays, but first:\\
Bali, Chennai, Abu Dhabi, one more loop to fly\\
before the year was over, before we said goodbye.

\songpart{Bridge}
You said the body needs its care —\\
physical therapy, as needed as the training.\\
You told me your plans, your next design,\\
I told you I'd be glad to see them shine.

\songpart{Chorus — extended}
