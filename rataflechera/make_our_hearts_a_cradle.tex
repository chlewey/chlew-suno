\songtitle{Make Our Hearts a Cradle}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gospel}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:make-our-hearts-a-cradle}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Claude's English translation of the \emph{Oración para todos los días} of the \emph{Novena de Aguinaldos}. \emph{Dwell in Us} (page \pageref{sec:dwell-in-us}) comes from the same translation. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 7 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
High-energy contemporary gospel with a deep male baritone solo lead in the verses, exuberant mixed gospel choir echoing the ends of phrases, and crisp handclaps driving a lively, swinging groove. Open with claps and warm piano, build with Hammond organ, rounded bass, punchy live drums, and bright brass accents; let the choir answer each verse, then lift into a full-throated, memorable refrain. Add dynamic call-and-response, a brief organ-and-clap turnaround, and a triumphant choir-led ending. Rich, organic church ensemble, clear intimate lead vocal, wide choir, polished live-room energy.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Most loving God, whose mercy has no end,\\
You gave your Son, our promise and our friend.\\
Made flesh within a Virgin’s tender care,\\
Born in a manger, healing hope to share.\\
For such a gift, with every heart and breath,\\
We thank You, Lord, for life that conquers death.

\annotation{Choir Echo}\\
Thank You, Lord! (Thank You, Lord!)\\
For love so wide! (For love so wide!)

\songpart{Chorus}
Come, make our hearts a cradle, Jesus,\\
Come and make Your home within.\\
With humble hands and love awakened,\\
Let Your life in us begin.\\
We lay the world’s proud treasures down;\\
Your mercy is our song.\\
Newborn King, abide within us,\\
Make this dwelling Yours for evermore.

\songpart{Verse 2}
We bring the poverty Your Son embraced,\\
The quiet strength, the virtues of His grace.\\
By every hardship of that holy night,\\
By tender tears beneath the manger light,\\
Teach us humility; set love aflame,\\
And loosen every earthly grip we claim.

\annotation{Choir Echo}\\
Set love aflame! (Set love aflame!)\\
Make room for Him! (Make room for Him!)

\songpart{Chorus}
Come, make our hearts a cradle, Jesus,\\
Come and make Your home within.\\
With humble hands and love awakened,\\
Let Your life in us begin.\\
We lay the world’s proud treasures down;\\
Your mercy is our song.\\
Newborn King, abide within us,\\
Make this dwelling Yours for evermore.

\songpart{Bridge}
In the stillness, in the morning,\\
In the work our hands must do,\\
Let our hearts be poor and open,\\
Let them beat with love for You.\\
Every room and every promise,\\
Every joy and every tear—\\
Jesus, born among the lowly,\\
Make Your cradle here.

\annotation{Choir Call and Response}\\
Leader: Make Your cradle here!\\
Choir: Make Your cradle here!\\
Leader: Dwell within us, Lord!\\
Choir: Dwell within us, Lord!\\
Leader: Now and evermore!\\
Choir: Now and evermore!

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Come, make our hearts a cradle, Jesus,\\
Come and make Your home within.\\
With humble hands and love awakened,\\
Let Your life in us begin.\\
We lay the world’s proud treasures down;\\
Your mercy is our song.\\
Newborn King, abide within us,\\
Make this dwelling Yours for evermore.

\songpart{Outro}
Abide within us, Jesus,\\
Abide within us evermore.
\end{lyrics}
