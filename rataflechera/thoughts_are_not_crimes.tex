\songtitle{Thoughts Are Not Crimes}

\tag*{2026}\tag{pop-ballad}\label{sec:thoughts-are-not-crimes}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Claude from \emph{Victless Crime} (page \pageref{sec:victless-crime}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, recast as a rhymed pop ballad. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Pop ballad, slow 72 bpm pulse, warm reverb, piano-led verses with soft strings entering and swelling through the pre-choruses, restrained arrangement opening into a building emotional chorus; stripped piano-and-voice bridge, half-step lift into a full final chorus with soaring strings and layered harmonies, then solo-piano fade. Intimate, breathy lead in the user's own recorded voice, opening to a more open, vulnerable delivery.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
\annotation{Soft piano, intimate vocal}\\
They taught me young that wanting was a sin,\\
A quiet fire to bury deep within.\\
I learned to flinch at what my heart would say,\\
And hang my head and wash the thoughts away.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
\annotation{Strings enter, building}\\
But who is hurt by what I'm dreaming?\\
Just the one who keeps believing\\
That a thought's a crime at all,\\
That a heart should have to fall.

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Full band, emotional, open vocal}\\
Unapologetic, and yet I'm so unsure,\\
Wanting what I want, and wanting to be pure.\\
But thoughts are not crimes, they're not crimes at all,\\
So I'll lay down the shame and I'll let the guilt fall.

\songpart{Verse 2}
\annotation{Piano returns, restrained}\\
Now I've found a gentler creed:\\
Respect for all, consent agreed,\\
But objectifying is the newer sin,\\
Same old guilt in a different skin.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
\annotation{Strings building}\\
It's nothing when your partner's aching,\\
Nothing next to famine, lives in the breaking,\\
Just a dirty little craving,\\
Something to chew on, quietly waiting.

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Full band, emotional, open vocal}\\
Unapologetic, and yet I'm so unsure,\\
Wanting what I want, and wanting to be pure.\\
But thoughts are not crimes, they're not crimes at all,\\
So I'll lay down the shame and I'll let the guilt fall.

\songpart{Bridge}
\annotation{Stripped back, piano and voice, rising slowly}\\
And I keep on proving I'm not a creep,\\
Keep my wanting quiet, buried deep,\\
But I'm not a monster for what I feel,\\
Wanting isn't wrong, and the wanting's real.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
\annotation{Key change up a half step, full power, strings soaring, layered harmonies}\\
Unapologetic, and I'm finally sure,\\
Wanting what I want, and I'm learning to be pure.\\
For thoughts are not crimes, they're not crimes at all,\\
So I lay down the shame and I let the guilt fall.
\annotation{Slow fade, solo piano}\\
They're not crimes at all.

\annotation{End}
\end{lyrics}
