\songtitle{Come to Our Souls}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gospel}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:come-to-our-souls}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Grok, adapting the O Antiphons from the original Latin into ballad quatrains, with the refrain ``Come to our souls, little Jesus''. \emph{Ero Cras} (page \pageref{sec:ero-cras}) sings the Latin originals. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 10 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Theatrical gospel, uplifting and festive with a high energy handclap-and-piano stomping groove; rolling piano chords, Hammond organ swells and tambourine shake through the refrains; male baritone and female mezzo-soprano alternate half-spoken talk-sing verses with whispered ad-libs between lines, then a gospel choir answers with ad-libs; close-miked lead vocals and loud ensemble refrains.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1 - male}
O Wisdom from the Highest’s mouth,\\
Who spans from end to end,\\
And orders all things strong and sweet:\\
Come, teach the prudent way.

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
Come, show the light of day.

\songpart{Verse 2 - female}
O Adonai, of Israel’s house\\
The captain and the guide,\\
Who in the bush-fire spoke to Moses,\\
And law on Sinai gave:

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
Stretch forth Thy arm to save.

\songpart{Verse 3 - male}
O Root of Jesse, sign to all,\\
Before whom kings grow dumb,\\
Whom every nation seeks and prays:\\
Come free us—do not stay.

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
Come break the night away.

\songpart{Verse 4 - female}
O Key of David, Israel’s rod,\\
Who opens, none can shut;\\
Who shuts, and none can open wide:\\
Lead captives from the dark.

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
Unlock the prison’s mark.

\songpart{Verse 5 - male}
O Dayspring, everlasting light,\\
And Sun of justice, rise:\\
Shine on the ones who sit in shade\\
And death’s long shadow-land.

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
And light the darkened strand.

\songpart{Verse 6 - female}
O King of nations, their desire,\\
The corner-stone of peace,\\
Who makest Jew and Gentile one:\\
Save man from dust’s release.

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
And grant the captive ease.

\songpart{Verse 7 - male}
O Emmanuel, our King and Law,\\
The nations’ hope and stay,\\
Their Saviour and their longing’s end:\\
Come save us, Lord, today.

\annotation{Refrain - choir}\\
Come to our souls, little Jesus,\\
Come take our fears away.
\end{lyrics}
