\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based in a short story from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Tropipop with punk texture, driving mid-tempo groove, palm-muted electric guitar chops over bright tropical percussion and punchy live drums; verse rides tight bass and clipped rhythm guitar, pre-chorus opens with rising toms and stacked harmonies, chorus hits with gang vocals and a chantable anchor; bridge strips to voice and hand percussion before a final hook with gritty doubles, delay throws, and a glossy-but-rough mix, punta, medieval, punk
\end{style}
