\songtitle{The Night Say What It Knew}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{estonian-folk}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{kadri-kuznetsova}\label{sec:the-night-say-what-it-knew}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the seventh section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Gabi's ``I'm in'' before take-off, a Belgian seatmate he lets go by, a night with Claire in Tallinn held without a script, and the new semester opening with coffee with Kadri. Follows \emph{Make a Room Your Own} (page \pageref{sec:make-a-room-your-own}). Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 31 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock with Estonian folk texture, piano and saxophone. Measured mid-tempo pulse; piano carries a warm, steady chord pattern while subtle Estonian kannel-like plucks and restrained fiddle phrases thread through the verses. Intimate male lead vocal, conversational and clearly enunciated. Let saxophone answer the vocal at line endings, then open into a gentle, memorable chorus with light harmony. Build from sparse piano and folk strings to a fuller but unshowy band, then settle into a quiet piano-led outro. Organic, close-miked mix with natural room warmth.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Before the plane, a message came:\\
“Forever, or as long as we can bear.\\
I’m in. Have a nice flight.”\\
I read it twice, then put it there.

Claire held out her phone to me:\\
“Take care of him.” From Gabi.\\
She wore that careful little smile\\
That leaves a space where words might be.

\songpart{Verse 2}
They’d set the seats; across the aisle,\\
A Belgian accountant told her tale—\\
A holiday of wrong turns, missed plans,\\
And every small mishap that wouldn’t fail.\\
She was easy company, and kind.\\
I might have asked to stay in touch.\\
But nothing in me leaned toward it;\\
I didn’t want the deal that much.

\songpart{Chorus}
Some things don’t ask to be decided\\
On a runway, in a crowded room.\\
We let the quiet ride beside us,\\
Let the night say what it knew.\\
No grand promise, no performance,\\
Just a place to lay our heads.\\
Morning came with honest words,\\
And left the rest unsaid.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Rotterdam to Tallinn, fewer names;\\
I took my seat and let the list go by.\\
I asked Claire back to my apartment—\\
Guest room, if she’d rather, either way.\\
In the cab, the silence fit us well.\\
She changed, then said, “Just lie beside me.”\\
We held each other without a script.\\
The night could tell us what it might be.

\songpart{Bridge}
“I’m still finding out what this all means,\\
If I still have the shape for it.\\
I don’t need to take care of you—\\
You take care of yourself,” she said.\\
“For her, for me, for your kids.”\\
Then goodbye, a flight, the campus walk;\\
The new semester waiting there.\\
Kadri and I exchanged the usual words,\\
As if last week had brought us here.

\songpart{Outro}
That night: “Coffee tomorrow? Off campus.”\\
“Three thirty, same place?” I wrote.\\
A thumbs-up, and I marked the hour.\\
One small square around a note.
