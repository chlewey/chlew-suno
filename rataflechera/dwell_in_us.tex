\songtitle{Dwell in Us}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gospel}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:dwell-in-us}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on Claude's English translation of the \emph{Oración para todos los días} of the \emph{Novena de Aguinaldos}. \emph{Make Our Hearts a Cradle} (page \pageref{sec:make-our-hearts-a-cradle}) comes from the same translation. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 7 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Gospel hymn with a warm, resonant baritone lead and responsive mixed choir echoes, steady handclaps, gentle piano chords, rounded bass, restrained organ, and subtle choir swells. Begin intimate and prayerful; build through each verse into a broad, uplifting refrain, with choir answers between lead phrases and a final held choral cadence. Human, devotional delivery, clear diction, rich natural room sound, polished but not glossy.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Most gracious God, whose love has no end,\\
You gave your Son, your dearest gift,\\
Made human in a Virgin’s womb,\\
Born in a manger for our healing.\\
For such a mercy, Lord, we thank you,\\
In every heart and every tongue.

\songpart{Chorus}
We give you thanks, we give you praise,\\
For love made near in Jesus’ name.\\
Make room within us, make us new;\\
Be born in us, and dwell there still.

\songpart{Verse 2}
I bring before you, in all our names,\\
The humble life your Son embraced:\\
His poverty, his gentle strength,\\
His patient heart, his holy love.\\
By all he bore, by all he gave,\\
Let mercy shape the lives we lead.

\songpart{Chorus}
We give you thanks, we give you praise,\\
For love made near in Jesus’ name.\\
Make room within us, make us new;\\
Be born in us, and dwell there still.

\songpart{Bridge}
Give us humility, deep and true;\\
Set love within us, burning bright.\\
Turn us from all that fades away;\\
Let Christ be cradled in our lives.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
We give you thanks, we give you praise,\\
For love made near in Jesus’ name.\\
Make room within us, make us new;\\
Be born in us, and dwell there still.\\
Be born in us, and dwell there still.
\songpart{Outro}
Dwell in us forever, Lord.
\end{lyrics}
