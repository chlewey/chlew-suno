\songtitle{Indigo Portrait (Gray-Blue Eyes)}

\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{liis-sepp}\tag{ia-portrait}\tag{dream-pop}\tag{english-lyrics}

\begin{notes}
Based on the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} fictional universe by Carlos Thompson.\\
Character: Liis Sepp.\\
\end{notes}

\begin{style}
Dream pop ballad with slow-swung pulse and airy harmonic motion; verses float over sparse kick, soft hand percussion, and glassy keys that mirror gentle camera moves, pre-chorus opens with filtered pads and a rising shimmer like lights blooming on porcelain skin, chorus blooms with wide synth chords and a rounded bass line that frames the emotional close‑up. %
Breathy, intimate female vocals with subtle layered harmonies, short delay throws on key phrases, reversed swells into transitions, and tiny chime accents that sparkle like jewelry. %
Polished, cinematic, and romantic with a deep velvet indigo sheen and warm, soft-focus glow.
\end{style}
