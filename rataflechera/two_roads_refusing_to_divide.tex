\songtitle{Two Roads Refusing to Divide}

\tag*{2026}\tag{alt-rock}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{zahra-moradi}\tag{mihai-rusu}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Suno based on a Claude summary of Zahra Moradi's post-Tallinn character development, guided by Carlos Thompson. Characters: Zahra Moradi, Mihai Rusu.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
polished alternative rock fused with mazurka folk motion; brisk lilt in 3/4 and occasional 2/4 turns, live punchy drums, melodic electric bass, wide distorted guitars, Tembûr drone and rhythmic plucks, oud answering phrases, bandura arpeggios and bright hammered accents; verses restrained and conversational, choruses expansive with stacked harmonies, a brief instrumental folk interlude, dynamic builds from intimate clean guitars to full-band release; clear emotionally grounded English lead vocal, warm natural room ambience, detailed modern mix, controlled low end, crisp transients, organic human performance
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Tallinn wore its winter mask in twenty-twenty\\
Doors half-closed, but the clinic still received\\
You came with a small concern and stayed for coffee\\
A longer answer than either meant to give

Mihai had crossed from Poland to the Baltic\\
With Budjak stories folded in his coat\\
A nursing degree, a life remade in Kraków\\
And one more city learning how to hold

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No one was promising tomorrow\\
No one had a map across the sea\\
But your names kept finding one another\\
In the narrow hours left to breathe

\songpart{Chorus}
Zahra and Mihai, two roads refusing to divide\\
Sanandaj to Kraków, with the distance at your side\\
What the borders couldn't settle, what the documents couldn't name\\
You built a home from every mile, and carried on the same

\songpart{Verse 2}
By summer, Tallinn faded into weather\\
You went back where the old familiar streets began\\
He returned to Kraków, back toward the work he'd gathered\\
And the life he'd learned to make with his own hands

A year of calls and ordinary resistance\\
Different mornings, different weight in every day\\
Still the thread survived the missed trains and the silence\\
Still the promise found a quieter way

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Then late twenty-two broke open\\
And you crossed the distance one more time\\
No grand parade, just two lives meeting\\
At the border of a shared design

\songpart{Chorus}

Sanandaj to Kraków, with the distance at your side\\

\songpart{Bridge}
Moradi on one passport, Rusu on another\\
Two offices that never learned to speak\\
One says the daughter, one records the wife\\
Both are true each time you sign your name each week

Jan-Alan sleeps, and all the papers lose their power\\
Jan for one new home, Alan open to the rest\\
A little boy with two directions in his breathing\\
A future no border has rehearsed yet

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Zahra and Mihai, now the distance has a face\\
A child between the histories, a home inside the grace\\
What the borders couldn't settle, what the years could not erase\\
You built a life from every mile, and gave the miles a place

\songpart{Outro}
And somewhere, Thomas keeps a photograph\\
A small bright proof that life moved on\\
From Tallinn's narrow winter window\\
To the morning where your son was born
