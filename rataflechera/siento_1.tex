\songtitle{Siento}

\tag*{1996}\tag{glam-metal}\tag{original-lyrics}\tag{spanish-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
New glam-metal version of \emph{Siento} (1996), same lyrics. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 6 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Glam metal, tape-saturated mix with plate reverb and sidechain pumping; power-chord guitars, harmonized lead guitar, tom-heavy fills and double-kick drums; driving 132 BPM; projected tenor belts from a central Colombian vocal identity, opening into arena gang vocals on the Spanish heartbreak chorus.
\end{style}
