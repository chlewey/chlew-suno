\songtitle{Joseph, Bring Us Close to Jesus}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gospel}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:joseph-bring-us-close-to-jesus}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Novena de Aguinaldos}: lyrics by Suno based on Claude's translation into KJV-style English of the prayer to Joseph (\emph{Oración a San José}). \emph{Mary, Prepare Us} (page \pageref{sec:mary-prepare-us}) and \emph{By the Grace of Your Holy} (page \pageref{sec:by-the-grace-of-your-holy}) set the other prayers. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 13 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
High-energy contemporary gospel with a joyful, driving handclap groove and a warm, commanding baritone lead in clear call-and-response with a full, diverse mixed choir. Begin with a brief piano-and-clap pickup; build verses with piano, Hammond organ, bass, punchy live drums, and bright brass accents; open into a soaring choir-led refrain with stacked harmonies, fervent ad-libs, and strong rhythmic claps. Add a dynamic breakdown that spotlights the soloist and choir answering, then rise into a triumphant final refrain. Organic, communal, polished live-room energy, crisp vocal-forward mix.
\end{style}
