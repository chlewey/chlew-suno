\begin{lyrics}

\songpart{Verse 1}
The show closed quiet, not quite the win they'd planned,\\
Mariain caught it in Gabi's eyes before the car stopped.\\
No words in the back of a stranger's machine,\\
just her shoulder finding Gabi's in the dark.\\
The Upper East Side waiting, soft and indifferent,\\
the week dissolving somewhere on the bridge.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Elevator slow, they leaned without speaking,\\
fatigue like a second coat, heavy and thin.\\
Sylvia glowing at the door, already reading the room,\\
Thomas somewhere in the library blue —\\
the penthouse swallowed them in,\\
low lights, familiar air, Manhattan spread below.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
She always knows before it's said,\\
reads the weather in a glance —\\
the soft machinery running quiet\\
underneath the circumstance.

\songpart{Chorus}
The unspoken rhythm,\\
no map, no name,\\
just the way they move together\\
through the ordinary strange.\\
The unspoken rhythm —\\
this is how they live,\\
this is what they give.

\songpart{Verse 3}
Gabi found him at the desk, wrapped her arms around him,\\
kissed his neck and breathed him in —\\
it wasn't great, but it was ours.\\
Low music, easy talk, the four of them loose,\\
drinks and small plates and the city below,\\
nobody performing, nobody keeping score.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
She kissed Gabi at the door,\\
then turned and kissed Thomas too —\\
unhurried, warm, the way you kiss\\
what you already know is true.\\
Then out into the Village night,\\
the one who always walks back alone.

\songpart{Chorus}
The unspoken rhythm,\\
no map, no name,\\
just the way they move together\\
through the ordinary strange.\\
The unspoken rhythm —\\
this is how they live,\\
this is what they give.

\songpart{Bridge}
Morning came with bacon\\
and Sylvia's gentle knock —\\
Gabi groaned into the pillows,\\
hair wild from the clock.\\
Cab to Mariain's,\\
a text: might miss you —\\
the bag already packed,\\
the room already knew.\\
Outside the gallery,\\
thirty seconds, arms and fabric —\\
safe flight —\\
I'll message when we land —\\
then taillights into the afternoon,\\
six hours of southern sky,\\
Bogotá lights like broken glass\\
scattered on the plateau ground,\\
Alicia rushing up the stairs,\\
arms open, spinning round.

\songpart{Verse 4}
SUV climbing through the thin cool air,\\
gummies and New York sweets passed around.\\
Dropped Alicia at her mother's, waited till she was in,\\
then three more blocks to home.\\
Gabi turned the lights on, checked the rooms,\\
Sylvia claimed his left shoulder,\\
Gabi settled on his right —\\
Thomas wrapped an arm around each,\\
the fireplace low,\\
the city quiet on the other side of the glass.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
No grand declarations,\\
just the weight of arms and breathing slow —\\
the soft machinery of love\\
that only insiders know.

\songpart{Chorus - Outro}
The unspoken rhythm,\\
no map, no name,\\
just the way they move together\\
through the ordinary strange.\\
The unspoken rhythm —\\
exhibitions come and go,\\
but this is what survives.\\
The unspoken rhythm,\\
low and sure,\\
the particular light,\\
the particular door —\\
Mariain walking back through the Village,\\
Gabi on his shoulder,\\
the city holding all of it,\\
the way it always has.

\end{lyrics}
