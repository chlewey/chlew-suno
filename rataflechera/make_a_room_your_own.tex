\songtitle{Make a Room Your Own}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{r-and-b}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{mariain-schumer}\label{sec:make-a-room-your-own}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the sixth section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- a working week in New York with Gabi coming and going, a careful lunch with Claire, and the Carroll Gardens party where he watches Gabi in her own world among her friends and Mariain, before flying back to Tallinn. Follows \emph{Between the Ground and the Clouds} (page \pageref{sec:between-the-ground-and-the-clouds}); \emph{The Night Say What It Knew} (page \pageref{sec:the-night-say-what-it-knew}) continues it. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 31 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock with subtle R\&B instrumental texture, intimate narrative ballad, relaxed mid-tempo pocket with understated brushed drums and warm bass. Piano leads the verses with spacious chord voicings; mellow tenor sax answers vocal phrases and grows into a lyrical interlude. Gradually add restrained electric guitar and soft harmony layers for the chorus, then return to piano and sax for a reflective outro. Close, conversational male lead vocal, emotionally observant rather than dramatic; warm, clear studio mix with gentle tape character and room around the sax.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Most of the work was words on a page\\
Reports and advances from my apartment office\\
Appointments waited, the week moved on\\
And you came by whenever the hour allowed\\
A text first, then lunch or dinner\\
Sometimes morning found you still there\\
Three times that week, the door opened\\
And the days took their shape from us

\songpart{Verse 2}
Claire stayed across the river in New Jersey\\
With Fernando and the copilot there\\
Tuesday she came into the city\\
We shared a table, the three of us and you\\
Nothing sharp, nothing said amiss\\
Just careful manners, measured space\\
A polite distance passing between us\\
Like a coat no one thought to take off

\songpart{Chorus}
I watched you make a room your own\\
While I stood trying to find my place\\
You knew each face, each old connection\\
I knew the doorway and the hour\\
We had said love could hold more than one\\
In Tallinn, Bogotá, plain as truth\\
So I stayed when your people gathered\\
And let the evening show me you

\songpart{Verse 3}
Friday, Carroll Gardens, your apartment\\
I’d been there once the week before\\
A trace of smoke stayed in the rooms\\
Though you never touched a cigarette\\
You said there’d be a crowd, some artists\\
Promoters, drinks, the Brooklyn scene\\
I said yes because it mattered to you\\
And soon the rooms were full of voices

\songpart{Verse 4}
Susan, Michael, José—I knew them\\
Then Mariain, your former love and guide\\
I saw what I had sensed in Tallinn:\\
She still held a place no guest could take\\
You moved through every conversation\\
Hosting, greeting, slipping out of reach\\
I knew enough to ask a question\\
Not enough to enter what they shared

Then Michael stayed, and Mariain\\

The next day I flew back to Tallinn\\
