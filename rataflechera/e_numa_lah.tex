\songtitle{E numa lah}

\tag*{1996}\tag{trance}\tag{original-source}\tag{conlang-lyrics}\tag{english-lyrics}
\begin{notes}
Adaptation by Gemini of the 1996 texts of \emph{Noche Larga} and \emph{E Numa Lah}, fed without changes: the conlang lines around an English rendering of the Spanish text on the long night, with a new ``Surrender to the night'' chorus. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 5 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Trance with atmospheric pads, pulsing sub-bass, rolling bassline, cascading synth arpeggios and crystalline plucks; euphoric supersaw drops over a massive kick, with stripped-back percussion in the breakdown. Full chorus layering, warm synth echoes and gentle breathy delay. Driving 150 BPM festival pulse; the user's recorded male baritone leads the builds, opening into female soprano vocals in the drops.
\end{style}
