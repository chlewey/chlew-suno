\songtitle{The Storage Unit}

\tag*{2026}\tag{ricochets}\tag{john-carlson}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Claude through an interactive session with Carlos Thompson. %
Based on a fragment from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Americana, alt-country ballad, 82 BPM, baritone lead vocals, close-mic intimate phrasing, pedal steel swells, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed snare kit, upright bass, piano counterlines, harmonica fills, gang-vocal chorus, chamber strings, tape saturation, plate reverb, slapback delay, hushed grief, slow-burn resolve
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
I found the box behind the winter coats,\\
the tape still holding, the label in her hand.\\
I opened it slow like a door in an old house,\\
afraid of what I'd wake up if I did.\\
There's a photograph of a girl on a porch swing,\\
and I forgot, for a minute, how to stand.

\songpart{Chorus}
Sarah, Sarah, you're still smiling in the light,\\
your hand on her shoulder, your ring catching bright.\\
I used to know exactly what I saw —\\
now I'm not so sure of anything at all.

\songpart{Verse 2}
Six years of birthdays, a whole life in a shoebox,\\
before the cameras all went and turned to screens.\\
I lingered longer on your face than on hers,\\
which tells me something I don't want to know.\\
There's a second folder underneath the first one —\\
another man's handwriting on a coat I don't recognize.

\songpart{Chorus — variant}
Sarah, Sarah, you're still smiling in the light,\\
his hand near your shoulder, your ring catching bright.\\
I used to know exactly what I saw —\\
now I'm going through it frame by frame by frame.

\songpart{Bridge}
Every photograph, I hold it to the window,\\
looking for the thing that isn't there.\\
His face gives you away — plain as daylight.\\
Yours gives nothing. Yours was never scared.\\
No proof. No touch caught out of place.\\
Just a ring that never left your hand.

\songpart{Verse 3 — hushed}
I put the folder back the way I found it,\\
the album too, the tape pressed back down.\\
I carry out the bag I came here for,\\
and leave the rest exactly where it was.

\songpart{Outro / Chorus — variant, spoken-low}
Sarah, Sarah, wherever the light still finds you,\\
I was wrong about the shape of what I lost.\\
I used to know exactly what I saw —\\
I'll never know now. I let it go.
\end{lyrics}
