\songtitle{Cast Off from Puerto Colombia (The Tucuxi Deckhand’s Shanty)}

\tag*{2026}\tag{sea-shanty}\tag{electronic}\tag{ai-based}\tag{panamax}\label{sec:cast-off-from-puerto-colombia}
\begin{notes}
From \emph{Panamax}, a 2026 document by Claude based on a 2013 description of a yacht by Carlos Thompson. Lyrics by Claude following specific instructions -- boarding day at Puerto Colombia and the crossing to Lisbon, seen by the deckhands. See also \emph{The Tucuxi} (page \pageref{sec:the-tucuxi}) and \emph{Ten on the Watch} (page \pageref{sec:ten-on-the-watch}). Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 25 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Sea shanty, roomy handclap reverb, pulsing modern synthesized beats with bright synth bass and crisp electronic percussion, steady hauling stomp, androgynous midrange lead with a doubled refrain.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
At dawn on the old Colombia pier, the gangway’s swung and down,\\
Lines are doubled, fenders set, the Tucuxi’s tied to town,\\
The stewards smile and greet the guests, that’s theirs to do, not ours,\\
We stand the lines and watch the quay through the early morning hours!

\songpart{Chorus}
So haul away and hold her fast, and mind the lines below!\\
The band is on the quarterdeck and Lisbon’s where we go!\\
Count them in and count them out, and keep the watch in line,\\
We’ll dance the Atlantic over, boys, and see the stars align!

\songpart{Verse 2}
Up the ramp from the Puerto quay a handful of cars roll in,\\
The guests who drove down to the bay, and the garage takes them in,\\
Two big trucks sit idle, parked, they’re only riding free,\\
And the crane swings stores aboard, boys, for the crossing of the sea!

\songpart{Chorus}
So haul away and hold her fast, and mind the lines below!\\
The band is on the quarterdeck and Lisbon’s where we go!\\
Count them in and count them out, and keep the watch in line,\\
We’ll dance the Atlantic over, boys, and see the stars align!

\songpart{Verse 3}
The helicopter’s dropping in with the owners and their kin,\\
The private lift goes straight to seven, and the family slips right in,\\
We stand at the rail, eyes front, hands neat, as the owners go by,\\
And a deckhand sees them come aboard and never meets their eye!

\songpart{Chorus}
So haul away and hold her fast, and mind the lines below!\\
The band is on the quarterdeck and Lisbon’s where we go!\\
Count them in and count them out, and keep the watch in line,\\
We’ll dance the Atlantic over, boys, and see the stars align!

\songpart{Verse 4}
Then four hundred guests come streaming in, all laughter, bags and song,\\
The stewards take the glasses and the cabin lists along,\\
We hold the gangway, log the count, and keep the quay in sight,\\
Then walk our rounds on the lifeboats, checking all is right!

\songpart{Chorus}
So haul away and hold her fast, and mind the lines below!\\
The band is on the quarterdeck and Lisbon’s where we go!\\
Count them in and count them out, and keep the watch in line,\\
We’ll dance the Atlantic over, boys, and see the stars align!

\songpart{Verse 5}
The bay slides by in the morning haze and the old pier falls astern,\\
Tucuxi dolphins ride the bow as the ropes are made to turn,\\
Ten of us stand the watch, me boys, while the dance hall’s loud and bright,\\
And the party’s in the stewards’ hands, and the ship’s in ours tonight!

\songpart{Chorus}
So haul away and hold her fast, and mind the lines below!\\
The band is on the quarterdeck and Lisbon’s where we go!\\
Count them in and count them out, and keep the watch in line,\\
We’ll dance the Atlantic over, boys, and see the stars align!

\songpart{Final verse (slow, then full)}
Ten days of blue from Colombia’s coast to Lisbon’s river light,\\
The music rolls below us, boys, while we keep the watch at night,\\
Let the guests go dancing, let the owners sleep up high,\\
For the sea belongs to the crew, boys, and the Tucuxi’s rolling by!

\songpart{Final chorus}
So haul away and hold her fast, and mind the lines below!\\
The band is on the quarterdeck and Lisbon’s where we go!\\
Count them in and count them out, and keep the watch in line,\\
We’ll dance the Atlantic over, boys, and see the stars align!
\end{lyrics}
