\begin{notes}
Re-recorded arrangement of \emph{Si pienso en ti}, same lyrics, in contemporary Arabic / melismatic pop rather than the original's house-pop. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 17 September 2026).
\end{notes}

Si pienso en una persona,\\
