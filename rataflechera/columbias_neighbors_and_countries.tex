\songtitle{Columbia’s Neighbors and Countries}

\tag*{2026}\tag{doo-wop}\tag{hip-hop}\tag{original-source}\tag{kingdom-of-columbia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Kingdom of Columbia} alternate history by Carlos Thompson (2021). Lyrics by Suno based on a paragraph of a historical summary by Carlos Thompson -- the neighbouring realms and the kingdom's countries and counties, as a children's song. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 28 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Children’s educational doo-wop blended with friendly hip-hop, buoyant mid-tempo swung groove, handclaps and warm upright bass, brushed snare with crisp backbeat, playful piano and small brass accents. Lead vocal is clear, inviting, and easy to sing along with; children’s-style group responses and stacked close-harmony syllables. Call-and-response verses name the neighboring realms counterclockwise, then a repeating chorus teaches the subdivisions; brief instrumental turnaround, tidy unison ending. Bright, warm, classroom-friendly mix with crisp diction.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
Shoo-bop, shoo-bop, gather near,\\
Columbia’s neighbors, let’s make it clear!

\songpart{Verse 1}
Starting north, then turning round,\\
Five neighbors all around are found:\\
Dominion of New England leads the way,\\
Dominion of Canada joins the display.\\
Kingdom of Oregon, next in line,\\
New Kingdom of Leon, then we find\\
Realm of Santo Domingo—round we go,\\
Counterclockwise, now you know!

\songpart{Chorus}
Eight countries, one special county,\\
Name them all and count them proudly!\\
Virginia, North Carolina,\\
South Carolina, Georgia shine-a;\\
Mississippi, Transilvania,\\
Arkansas and Missouri—sing!\\
Saint Louis is the special county,\\
Clap and learn the whole thing!

\songpart{Verse 2}
New England, Canada, Oregon belong\\
To the British Commonwealth—yet act on their own.\\
Independent countries, each with its say,\\
A commonwealth connection along the way.\\
New Leon and Santo Domingo stand\\
Independent counties under Spain’s crown land.\\
Different ties, now the map is bright,\\
We can name each neighbor right!

\songpart{Chorus}
Eight countries, one special county,\\
Name them all and count them proudly!\\
Virginia, North Carolina,\\
South Carolina, Georgia shine-a;\\
Mississippi, Transilvania,\\
Arkansas and Missouri—sing!\\
Saint Louis is the special county,\\
Clap and learn the whole thing!

\songpart{Bridge}
Each country has counties inside,\\
Cities and boroughs, side by side.\\
Country, county, city, borough—say!\\
That’s how the kingdom’s laid away.

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Eight countries, one special county,\\
Name them all and count them proudly!\\
Virginia, North Carolina,\\
South Carolina, Georgia shine-a;\\
Mississippi, Transilvania,\\
Arkansas and Missouri—sing!\\
Saint Louis is the special county,\\
Columbia’s map is in our memory!

\songpart{Outro}
Shoo-bop, shoo-bop, neighbors all around,\\
Counties, cities, boroughs—now the map’s been found!
\end{lyrics}
