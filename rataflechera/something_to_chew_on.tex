\songtitle{Something to Chew On}

\tag*{2026}\tag{blues}\label{sec:something-to-chew-on}
\begin{notes}
Adapted by Grok from \emph{Victless Crime} (page \pageref{sec:victless-crime}), original lyrics by Carlos Thompson, recast as AAB Delta-blues stanzas. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 17 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Delta blues, gritty deep baritone in the user's own recorded voice, close and raw; 71 BPM heavy-footed shuffle with a sparse backbeat; dobro slide over fingerpicked resonator, walking bass, wailing harmonica and swelling church organ; tape saturation, mono room mic, sepia analog warmth, vintage 1960s mix.
\end{style}
