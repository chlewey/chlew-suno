\songtitle{Mary, Prepare Us}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gospel}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:mary-prepare-us}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Novena de Aguinaldos}: lyrics by Suno based on Claude's translation into KJV-style English of the prayer to Mary (\emph{Oración a la Santísima Virgen}). \emph{Joseph, Bring Us Close to Jesus} (page \pageref{sec:joseph-bring-us-close-to-jesus}) and \emph{By the Grace of Your Holy} (page \pageref{sec:by-the-grace-of-your-holy}) set the other prayers. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 13 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
High-energy contemporary gospel, brisk driving groove with handclaps and stomping tambourine, rich piano chords, warm organ, buoyant bass, and bright brass accents. Mezzo-soprano lead solo with expressive gospel phrasing, answered by a full diverse mixed choir in bold harmonies; build from intimate opening prayer to a soaring communal refrain, drop to a tender reflective bridge, then rise into a joyful final call-and-response and resolved amen. Live-room warmth, clear lead vocal, wide choir, punchy rhythmic mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Intro}
O sovereign Mary, hear our prayer\\
Prepare our souls with tender care

\songpart{Verse 1: Lead}
For all thy virtues, for thy humility\\
God chose thee to be the mother of His Son\\
Prepare my soul, and every soul\\
Who keeps this novena in this season\\
For the spiritual birth of thy beloved Son

\annotation{Call and Response}
\annotation{Lead} Mary, prepare us!
\annotation{Choir} Prepare us, prepare us!
\annotation{Lead} Make our hearts ready!
\annotation{Choir} Make our hearts ready!
\annotation{Lead} For the birth of thy Son!
\annotation{Choir} We wait in faith and love!

\songpart{Verse 2: Lead}
O sweetest Mother, share with us\\
That deep recollection, divine tenderness\\
With which thou waited for thy Son\\
Teach us to behold Him, love Him, worship Him\\
Now and throughout eternity

\songpart{Chorus}
\annotation{Lead} Make us ready, make us worthy!
\annotation{Choir} Ready, worthy, hearts made new!
\annotation{Lead} Let us behold Him, love and worship!
\annotation{Choir} Through all time, our praise to You!
\annotation{Lead} Mary, guide us to thy Son!
\annotation{Choir} Amen, amen!

\songpart{Bridge: Lead}
In quiet wonder, keep us near\\
In tender faith, replace our fear
\annotation{Choir} Prepare our souls, prepare our souls
\annotation{Lead} That we may welcome Him with love
\annotation{Choir} Prepare our souls, prepare our souls

\annotation{Final Call and Response}
\annotation{Lead} Who prepares our hearts?
\annotation{Choir} Mary, Mother, lead us on!
\annotation{Lead} Who shall we behold?
\annotation{Choir} Jesus, God's beloved Son!
\annotation{Lead} Who shall we love and worship?
\annotation{Choir} Him through all eternity!
\annotation{All} Amen! Amen! Amen!
\end{lyrics}
