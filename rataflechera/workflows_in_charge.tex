\songtitle{Workflow's in Charge}

\tag*{2026}\tag{country-rap}\tag{electronic}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on a scene and style description by Carlos Thompson -- a workday spent proving the job gets done instead of doing it: Jira, Mantis, Teams and the SLA clock. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 20 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Rapid-fire country rap with a brisk train-track shuffle, twangy banjo chops and steel-guitar flicks over punchy electronic kick, crisp claps, elastic synth bass, and short digital bleeps. Tight, witty male sing-rap with clear consonants and conversational bite; verses packed with quick internal rhymes, a broad melodic chant-hook, brief half-time breakdown, then a full-band final chorus. Polished modern mix with the acoustic twang upfront and electronic low end tight.
\end{style}
