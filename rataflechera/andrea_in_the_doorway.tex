\songtitle{Andrea in the Doorway}

\tag*{2024}\tag{power-ballad}\tag{ricochets}\tag{andrea-roa}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by SUNO based on a Gemini Notebook summary of character Andrea Roa from Carlos Thompson's novel \emph{Ricochets}.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft metal power ballad with a slow 6/8 pulse, clean piano and fingerpicked electric guitar in the verses, restrained toms and warm bass, then broad distorted guitars and sustained lead lines in the choruses, Start intimate and observational, build toward a cathartic but compassionate final chorus, with a short melodic guitar solo before the bridge, Clear expressive female lead vocal, close-miked in verses and soaring in choruses; polished 2000s-style rock mix, wide guitars, controlled low end, dramatic cymbal swells, no screaming
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Thirty-nine with the morning folded neatly in her hands\\
Chevrolet at the gate, two fourteen-year-old sons beside her\\
Roberto on one side, Nicolas on the other\\
Names carried through a house that never learned their weight

Pamela sits close, twenty-two and almost finished with her degree\\
A young heart counting semesters, trying not to take up space\\
Carolina sees her turn quiet beneath the family table\\
And feels the room grow colder than the coffee in the cup

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
Nobody says the wound out loud\\
But every silence knows her name

\songpart{Chorus}
Andrea, hold your family in the doorway\\
Hold your sons, hold the hand they won’t explain\\
When the old house measures love by years and approval\\
Will you still let tenderness remain?\\
Andrea, there is no clean way through a Sunday\\
No perfect word to make the distance disappear\\
But the ones who came together in that Chevrolet\\
Are a family, if you keep them near

\songpart{Verse 2}
Samuel watches from seventy-five years of certainty\\
Francina keeps her disapproval underneath the lace\\
They love the daughter they remember from another chapter\\
But cannot bless the future standing in her place

Sandra leaves a little room for what she cannot answer\\
Fabian looks less frightened by the shape of something new\\
Carolina builds a reason for the discomfort at the table\\
Says it’s the age, though prejudice is breathing too

\songpart{Pre-Chorus}
And Pamela studies every plate\\
As if permission might be served

\songpart{Chorus}
Andrea, hold your family in the doorway\\
Hold your sons, hold the hand they won’t explain\\
When the old house measures love by years and approval\\
Will you still let tenderness remain?\\
Andrea, there is no clean way through a Sunday\\

But the ones who came together in that Chevrolet\\

\songpart{Bridge}
Roberto and Nicolas, two young names in the margin\\
Fourteen years old, watching every face\\
They know a home can change its language\\
They know love can enter and rearrange the place

And Andrea, youngest daughter, tired of being translated\\
By the eyes that think they know what she should be\\
Let the table keep its questions for another morning\\
Let Pamela sit beside you, openly

\annotation{Instrumental}

\songpart{Final Chorus}
Andrea, walk your family through the doorway\\
Let the room decide what it can understand\\
Samuel and Francina may be waiting with their silence\\
Still, your future is already in your hands\\
Andrea, no one gets to call your love a mistake\\
No one gets to turn your tenderness to shame\\
Roberto, Nicolas, Pamela at your shoulder\\
And the woman you became beyond their names

\songpart{Outro}
The plates are cleared, the afternoon grows wider\\
The car waits quietly beyond the gate\\
Andrea carries everyone together\\
And leaves before the room can choose her fate
\end{lyrics}
