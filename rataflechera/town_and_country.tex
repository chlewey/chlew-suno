\begin{notes}
A distinctive arrangement that supports the song's storytelling and emotional arc. %

\begin{style}
Country rap with fiddle licks and muted banjo over a stomping kick; verse rides tight acoustic strums and finger snaps, pre-chorus narrows to bass and brushed snare, chorus opens with gang-vocal hook and steel guitar sighs. %
Rap bridge cuts to half-time with talk-sung lines, then final chorus adds handclaps, a grit-laced electric lead, and wide harmony on the last line. %
Bright, punchy mix with close-mic vocals and delay throws on names., rap, lilith, light, country
\end{style}
