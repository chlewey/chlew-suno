From \emph{Thomas' Entourage}, character Zahra Moradi
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Stranbêj with Govend/Halparke energy, mid-tempo and pulsing with a handclap-driven folk groove; verse rides a tight frame drum, low drone, and dry acoustic plucks; pre-chorus opens with rising harmonies and a lifted tambourine pattern; chorus lands on stacked group chants, stomps, and a danceable call-and-response. %
Close-mic lead vocal with warm doubles, bright unison shouts on the hook, short delay throws on key lines, and crisp room-reverb tails; pencil taps, chair creaks, and a reversed swell into each chorus. %
Clean, photoreal, wide-but-intimate mix with polished low end., soft, natural, warm, clean
\end{style}
