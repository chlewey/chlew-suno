\songtitle{Put Out the Little Fires}

\tag*{2026}\tag{folk-pop}\label{sec:put-out-the-little-fires}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno following specific instructions, inspired by the lives of Thomas García (\emph{Thomas' Entourage}) and Carlos Thompson -- slow mornings in a dream apartment with the cats, interrupted by the urgent little fires of remote work. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 6 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Cinematic acoustic folk-pop with a clear contrast between intimate, slow morning verses and urgent, fast-driving choruses. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, warm piano, restrained brushed percussion in the verses; choruses surge with brisk kick and snare, pulsing bass, strummed guitars, and concise brass lifts. A sunlit, gently playful bridge eases into the final chorus. Male lead vocal, plainspoken and expressive, close-miked verses opening into energetic, rhythmic high-register choruses. Warm organic production with subtle electronic accents. End with a brief, clearly spoken reflective coda over sparse piano.
\end{style}
