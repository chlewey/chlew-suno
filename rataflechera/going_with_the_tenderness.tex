\songtitle{Going with the Tenderness}

\tag*{2026}\tag{soft-rock}\tag{disco}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{thomas-garcia}\tag{gabriele-wiater}\tag{claire-jones}\tag{erika-czajka}\label{sec:going-with-the-tenderness}
\begin{notes}
Lyrics by Suno based on the fourth section of \emph{Summer 2019} by Carlos Thompson, from the \emph{Thomas' Entourage} narrative universe, in the voice of Thomas -- Claire back through Dallas, a week in New York finding Gabi in her studio, and the evening that turned into ``come with me to Bogotá''. Follows \emph{Just the Road} (page \pageref{sec:just-the-road}); \emph{Between the Ground and the Clouds} (page \pageref{sec:between-the-ground-and-the-clouds}) continues it. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 30 October 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Soft rock with a disco drive, mid-tempo four-on-the-floor groove, rounded melodic bass, crisp live kit with open hi-hat, clean electric piano and bright restrained guitar, subtle string accents in the chorus. Narrative male lead vocal, conversational and intimate in the verses, opening into a warm memorable chorus. Arrangement builds from a lean verse to a wider chorus, with a brief instrumental lift before the final verse; polished organic studio mix, bittersweet romantic tension.
\end{style}
