\songtitle{Meet Me at Punta Espada}

\tag*{2026}\tag{persian-pop}\tag{thomas-entourage}\tag{ai-based}\tag{zahra-moradi}\tag{thomas-garcia}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson. Based on an email exchange between Zahra Moradi and Thomas Garcia. Characters: Zahra Moradi, Thomas Garcia. Co-written by Carlos Thompson and Claude. Lyrics by Suno.
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Persian pop with a warm cinematic arrangement, midtempo groove, expressive female lead vocal, English lyrics delivered with subtle Persian melodic ornamentation, santur and nylon-string guitar accents, rounded electric bass, restrained darbuka and modern pop drums, wide strings entering in the pre-chorus, intimate verses opening into a memorable chorus, polished radio mix with clear vocal presence and gentle coastal warmth
\end{style}
