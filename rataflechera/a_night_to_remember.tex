Lyrics by SUNO based on a short story from the \emph{Thomas’ Entourage} narrative universe by Carlos Thompson.

\begin{style}
Late-1990s/early-2000s Eurodance anthem at 138 BPM: four-on-the-floor kick, bright piano stabs, elastic synth bass, crisp clap, gated pads, and a soaring minor-key hook, Male lead vocals with clear narrative diction in the verses, rhythmic urgency in the pre-choruses, and a huge singalong chorus, Arrange as cinematic story chapters: hotel-bar opening, Milan conversation, intimate midnight interlude, morning departure, then a dramatic final reveal; use brief instrumental lifts and a final chorus with wider harmonies, Polished club mix, punchy low end, bright sidechained synths, tasteful vocal delay, confident but bittersweet tone
\end{style}
