\songtitle{By the Grace of Your Holy}

\tag*{2026}\tag{gospel}\tag{christmas-carol}\label{sec:by-the-grace-of-your-holy}
\begin{notes}
From the \emph{Novena de Aguinaldos}: lyrics by Suno based on Claude's translation into KJV-style English of the prayer to the Child Jesus (\emph{Oración al Niño Jesús}). \emph{Mary, Prepare Us} (page \pageref{sec:mary-prepare-us}) and \emph{Joseph, Bring Us Close to Jesus} (page \pageref{sec:joseph-bring-us-close-to-jesus}) set the other prayers. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 10 November 2026).
\end{notes}
\begin{style}
Southern gospel hymn, measured 6/8 sway with a steady backbeat, warm piano and Hammond organ supporting acoustic guitar and rounded bass; scene-setting verses build into a broad, memorable congregational chorus, with a brief devotional bridge and a resolved final refrain. Baritone lead delivered with earnest clarity, close harmony quartet and small mixed choir answering key phrases. Church-room warmth, gentle tape saturation, natural dynamics, clear vocal-forward mix.
\end{style}
\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Remember, sweet Child Jesus, the promise that You gave,\\
To Margaret, and through her, to every heart that prays:\\
“Ask by the merits of My infancy, and trust in Me;\\
No prayer laid down before My throne shall turned away be.”

\songpart{Chorus}
By the grace of Your holy childhood,\\
By the love that brought You near,\\
Take our need and hear our pleading,\\
Hold us fast and calm our fear.\\
Jesus, tender Child, we trust You;\\
Let our hope in You abide.\\
Lead us on to life eternal,\\
Keep us close beside Your side.

\songpart{Verse 2}
You know the weight we carry, the sorrow and the strain;\\
We open all our weakness and bring to You our pain.\\
Make holy all our living; prepare our hearts for home,\\
And grant the grace we need to walk wherever we may roam.

We give ourselves to You, Lord,\\
