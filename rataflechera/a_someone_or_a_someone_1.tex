Adapted from a previous version of the same title (2023) as a synth-pop ballad, with a spoken pre-chorus. Retrieved from rataflechera's Suno page (V6, 6 October 2026).

\begin{style}
Synth-pop ballad with your own recorded male baritone, a spoken pre-chorus, and a steady 118 BPM four-on-the-floor pulse; gated snare, programmed hi-hats, sidechain pumping, tape saturation, plate reverb and 1980s digital reverb sheen; analog arpeggios, polyphonic synth chords, Moog bass, and dramatic brass stabs lifting into the chorus.
\end{style}
