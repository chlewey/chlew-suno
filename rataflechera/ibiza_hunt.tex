\begin{lyrics}
\songpart{Verse 1}
Casablanca, I clocked her — Naji's girl, twenty-two,\\
soft voice, sharp eyes, close to a man I needed to impress.\\
I looked away on purpose.\\
Told myself that kept me safe.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus 1}
Three years gone, I lost the edges of her face —\\
static where a picture used to be.\\
Playing a part I'd half-designed myself,\\
some nights worth keeping, most forgettable.\\
Rooms and faces, rooms and faces —\\
none of them stayed.

\songpart{Chorus}
She moved through that Ibiza floor like she owned the room,\\
I played it cool, she read me anyway.\\
I made my move, she turned it back around —\\
I was the hunter, I became the prey.

\songpart{Verse 2}
She led me to her orbit, and she stayed — one I won't forget.\\
Almost felt a face I knew I'd carry.\\
Fresh off London, MBA in hand,\\
but her heart was pointed somewhere warmer — Cordon Bleu.

\songpart{Pre-Chorus 2}
Influencer, she said — travel, finance, haute cuisine,\\
she left her number, told me: wait.\\
Next morning on the balcony outside her door,\\
I checked.

\songpart{Chorus}
She moved through that Ibiza floor like she owned the room,\\

\songpart{Bridge}
Naji's daughter — the name clicked back,\\
her sleeping off a night I wouldn't trade.\\
That girl from Casablanca I made myself forget —\\
was it chance? Or had she known all along?

\songpart{Chorus — extended}
